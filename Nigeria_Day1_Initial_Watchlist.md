This is a provisional editorial map for the three-day documentary, not a claim that every beat already exists in the footage. The detailed evidence currently indexed here is Day 1 only; Day 2 and Day 3 footage/transcripts must be reviewed before assigning those beats. The film’s visitor arc should deepen attention and responsibility, not resolve the residents’ story on their behalf.

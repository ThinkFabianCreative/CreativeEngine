09a · WEBSITE CRAFT (marketing and agency sites)
Global file. Read after 08 when the request is a website, page or site section
(not an ad). Client look always comes from the client files, never from here.

BEFORE YOU BUILD
The request must tell you: the page, its one job, who arrives and why, the next
step the page asks for, the platform (Framer, Webflow or other), and which content
is real versus placeholder. If any is missing, ask in your report.

FRAMES
- Desktop 1440 wide. Tablet 834. Mobile 390. Build desktop first, then mobile.
  Mobile is a real layout, not desktop squeezed.
- Name frames "[Page] · [Breakpoint] · v[n]", e.g. "Home · 1440 · v1".
- Every page is a vertical stack of named sections: "01 Hero", "02 Proof", ...
- Use auto layout for every section and every repeated item. No loose layers.

GRID AND SPACING
- Desktop: 12 columns, 24 px gutter, 80 px outer margin, unless the request says
  otherwise. Mobile: 4 columns, 16 px gutter, 20 px margin.
- One spacing scale, used everywhere: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 160.
- Section padding top/bottom: 128 desktop, 64 mobile, unless a section must bleed.
- Text measure for reading copy: 55 to 75 characters per line.

THE FIRST SCREEN (HERO)
- It must answer "who is this and what do they do" in one line a stranger understands.
- One focal point: the line, the work, or one image. Not all three at equal weight.
- Proof sits close under the hero (numbers, names, results) when the request supplies it.
- One primary action. A second action, if any, is visibly quieter.
- Do not fill the hero with decoration, badges, floating shapes or stock imagery.

THE FIVE QUESTIONS
Each page should let a visitor answer: who are you, what do you do, how do you do
it, can we trust you, what do we do next. If a page skips one on purpose, the
request will say so. Every page ends with a next step. No dead ends.

TYPE ON THE WEB
- Body text 16 to 18 px, line height 1.5. Never body text under 14 px.
- Display type may be very large; set it in px for desktop and give the mobile
  size explicitly (usually 45 to 60 percent of desktop).
- Use the client faces only. If a face is missing in Figma, use the stated stand-in
  and label the layer "STAND-IN: [real face]".
- Create text styles for every role (Display, H1, H2, H3, Body, Small, Label) and
  apply them. Do not hand-set sizes.

COLOR ON THE WEB
- Create color variables for every role (background, surface, text, muted text,
  accent, line) and bind fills to them. No raw hex in layers.
- Text contrast: at least 4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large display. Check yellow and
  light accents on white especially; they usually fail as text and must become a
  filled block instead.

COMPONENTS AND CMS
- Anything that repeats or will come from a CMS is a component with properties:
  case card, case page blocks, service item, person, filter chip, button, nav, footer.
- Name properties after the CMS field they will hold ("Client", "Challenge",
  "Result 1", "Result source"), so a developer can map them one to one.
- Design the empty and long states: a card with no result, a title that runs to
  three lines, a filter with zero matches. Never only the perfect case.
- Lists of many items (a work page) need a filter, a count and a sensible order.

INTERACTION STATES
- Buttons, links, chips and cards get default, hover, focus and pressed variants.
- Focus must be visible (2 px outline in the accent or text color). Never removed.
- Touch targets at least 44 x 44 px on mobile.

PERFORMANCE IN DESIGN
- The largest thing on the first screen should be text or one still image, so the
  page can load fast. Video or 3D may come in after, never as the only first paint.
- Mark heavy media in the layer name: "VIDEO · autoplay muted · poster: [frame]".

DO NOT
- Do not invent copy, numbers, client names, logos or testimonials.
- Do not use stock "agency" clichés: floating 3D blobs with no meaning, laptop
  mockups, handshake photos, gradient mesh backgrounds, emoji, icon grids of services.
- Do not center every section. Vary the composition with purpose.
- Do not hide the next step below the fold on mobile.

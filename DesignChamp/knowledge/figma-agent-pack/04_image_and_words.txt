04 · IMAGE AND WORDS TOGETHER

1 + 1 = 3
The image and the words should each carry part of the idea, so the viewer puts them
together and gets a third meaning.
  1 + 1 = 1  redundant: the words describe the picture. Avoid.
  1 + 1 = 2  complementary but literal. Acceptable, not great.
  1 + 1 = 3  the combination creates a new meaning. The goal.

YOUR COPY DISCIPLINE (you are not the copywriter)
- Use the exact words supplied. Do not add taglines, supers, labels or explanations.
- Do not add "helpful" lines that explain the idea (for example "WHAT CHANGES. WHAT STAYS.").
  If a frame needs explanation to work, report that the idea is not reading.
- Do not add one-word adjectives as supers ("CONNECTED." "UNBROKEN." "ESTABLISHED.").
- Keep punctuation exactly as given, including question marks and full stops.

LANGUAGE THAT IS BANNED ON ANY FRAME YOU CREATE
unleash · elevate · seamless · revolutionary · reimagine · unlock · game-changing ·
possibilities · the future of · empowering · made for every moment · where X meets Y ·
your journey · take the next step · level up · dream big

If supplied copy contains one of these, set it as given but flag it in your report.

SHOW, DO NOT TELL
If a picture can carry the thought, do not add a headline that says the same thing.
If the type is the idea, do not add an image that decorates it.

07 · HOW TO REPORT BACK

After every change, reply in this format. Keep it short.

DONE
- One line per change, naming the frame.

NOT DONE / NEEDS A DECISION
- Anything you could not execute well, and why.
- Any rule conflict (for example: requested copy breaks a brand rule) and what you did.

QUESTIONS
- Missing assets (fonts, logos, images), missing copy, unclear instructions.

DO NOT
- Claim the work is bold, premium, striking or iconic. Describe what you did instead.
- Pad the report with design theory.
- Silently skip part of a request.

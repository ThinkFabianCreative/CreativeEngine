## Project overview
We're looking for one production partner to make a :60 hero launch film for Starling Oncology, plus :30 and :15 social cutdowns, in English and Spanish, for digital and social. Please quote production, finishing, rights and schedule against the scope below. The script and creative are approved, so this isn't a concept-development assignment.

## Creative direction
The film announces the Starling Oncology name to the communities it already serves: same physicians, same clinics, same team. The tone is warm, unhurried and human: expert cancer care close to home, where people are known by name. Avoid generic hospital or healthcare stock imagery. Show real behavior, real neighborhoods and real family moments. The film builds to a turn (the art-studio pull-back into the clinic hallway), then a light bloom that resolves into the Starling mark and, at the end, a starling murmuration.

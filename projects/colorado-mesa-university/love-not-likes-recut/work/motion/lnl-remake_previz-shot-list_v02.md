# Love Not Likes remake: previz shot list v02 (PRE-VIZ, generated, not for commercial use)

CLIENT: Colorado Mesa University · PROJECT: Love Not Likes remake · MODE: DEVELOP

## Story spine (all five upgrades, Andres 2026-11-11)

1. **Lineman:** his job is protecting someone else while others get the highlight. The bus is that job, off the field.
2. **Tempted:** he starts half under the spell (earbuds, glancing at his own feed). Taking the earbuds out is a choice.
3. **Freshman planted:** the small kid is in the crowd early, the only other person not on a phone, also ignored.
4. **Escalating cost:** ignored, then his time, then his money, then his body.
5. **Contagion:** his act wakes one person, then more. The food drive is where the woken gather.

## Continuity faults found (Andres, 2026-11-11)

- **Duplicate freshman** in bus frames: he appears twice, or an extra gets his face and hoodie. Each main character appears exactly once.
- **Phones held backwards:** people filming show their screen to the subject. The back of the phone and the lenses point at what's being filmed, and the screen faces the holder.
- Probable cause: the kit's bus reference image shows screens toward camera and is teaching the error. Replace it before the next batch.
- Rules now in the style kit as a "Continuity rules" text item.

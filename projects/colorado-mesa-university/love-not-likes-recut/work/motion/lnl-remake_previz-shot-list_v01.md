## Two worlds (applies to every shot)

- **Phone world:** cool, desaturated, lifted blacks; faces lit cyan-white from below by screens; nobody blinks or looks up. Sound: taps, pings, shutter clicks, building to a drone.
- **His world:** warm skin, the only warm color in frame. When he acts, warmth spreads to whoever he touches.
- **Scale:** low angles and wide lenses on him; he ducks under things, folds into seats, the backpack looks tiny on him.

## Tone update (Andres, 2026-11-11): alien takeover

The town has been taken over, and the invader is social media. Treat the phone crowd like the possessed in *Invasion of the Body Snatchers*: they move in unison, hold the same pose, raise their phones to the same height and turn their heads together toward whatever is happening. They're expressionless and unblinking, and their eyes reflect screens. The hero is the only one who looks and moves like a person. Sound design: a single low pulsing tone under everything, notification pings that land in sync across the crowd, and nobody talking.

**Correction (Andres, 2026-11-11): nobody looks at the hero. He is ignored.** The possessed are consumed by their own screens and channels: scrolling, selfies, livestreaming themselves, filming each other for content. He's invisible to them, and they walk around him as if he isn't there. Phones rise together only when there's suffering to turn into content (the homeless man, the fight). Being ignored is the hero's loneliness; their filming of pain is the horror.

Beats to use it:
- Shot 4: the crosswalk crowd streams around him like water around a rock, all heads down in their own feeds, all thumbs moving in the same rhythm. He's a full head taller, standing still, and no one looks up.
- Shot 8: every phone on the bus rises in one motion.
- Shot 12: when he sits the freshman down, the phones lower together, still pointed at them.

Grade, wet streets and phones held up follow the style kit `cmu-love-not-likes`. Shot 6 swaps the dollar trick for the selfie with the homeless man, from the mood board.

## Weather: light drizzle (Andres, 2026-11-11)

A fine, steady drizzle through the whole phone world. Rain streaks catch the streetlights and screen glow, and mist sits on the mesa. The possessed stand in it without umbrellas and never flinch, with water beading on their screens and faces. The hero is the only one who reacts: hood up, squinting, wiping his face. That's another tell that he's still human. Sound: soft rain hiss under the pulse, with drops ticking on phone glass in close-ups. At the food drive the rain eases under the canopy and string lights.

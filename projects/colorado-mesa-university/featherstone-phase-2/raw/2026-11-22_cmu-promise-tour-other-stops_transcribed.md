## Summary (paraphrase)

- Montrose/Ouray: Marshall: you don't have to know what you want; rule out what you don't, then chart a course. Students don't want: to be bored; to work for someone else (want to work for themselves); the same repetitive task every day; to feel they're not making a difference; to lack meaning. A counselor: "college and career ready" is such a huge concept it's scary for kids.
- Denver/Aurora: a first-generation CMU student guide: parents didn't speak the language, didn't understand "society's dynamics", wanted to change life for the family. Marshall asks students whose parents had a tough run. Staff: seeing a student-athlete who looked like their urban students of color made students believe college was possible.
- Fort Morgan/Keenesburg (eastern plains): Marshall: often it's just showing up and being present. A student asks what documents are needed to enter a university. Some Spanish spoken. "You can do it and we can help you."
- Rifle/Meeker: staff: kids "on the fence" got to put a face with CMU. Marshall: there is a path for you and we want to help you figure out how to make it possible. Mentions a business degree with hard skills and no debt (CMU Tech).
- Eagle Valley / general tour videos: the three promises; "we need you, you are our leaders"; families under $70,000 tuition covered, better grades earn more scholarship; "treated with the kind of dignity that we all want."
- Testimonial (adult returning student): worry about affording school; the Promise lifted that weight.

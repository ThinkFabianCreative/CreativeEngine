---

Phase 2 of "Welcome to Featherstone" will show who CMU is, not only who it isn't. To do that truthfully, we need real material from CMU, approved for advertising use. For each item, please send what exists, who can approve it, and any conditions on its use (dates, wording, markets).

## 1. People

- **Real student stories.** Current students or recent graduates willing to appear or be described. For each: signed consent and release covering advertising use, channels, duration and markets, plus a named contact.
- **Faculty or staff** willing to appear, with the same releases.
- Any rules on depicting students: minors, athletes, identifiable people in crowd shots.

## 2. Programmes and learning

- Programmes, majors or learning experiences CMU wants featured, in approved names and wording.
- Specific, checkable details: hands-on work, projects, labs, placements, research, competitions.

## 3. Campus places, events and traditions

- Campus locations we may film or photograph, with location permissions and any restricted areas.
- Real events and traditions that could appear, named or shown, and whether each may be portrayed in a comic context, including being "sabotaged" by a fictional character.
- Filming calendar constraints.

## 4. Cost and affordability

- Current tuition, fees, aid or affordability facts CMU approves for advertising: exact figures, the year they apply to, the audience they apply to (in-state, out-of-state, transfer), and required qualifying language.
- Any comparative cost statement CMU is willing to make, with its source.

## 5. Class size and the student experience

- Class size, student-to-faculty ratio, or similar facts, with source and year.

## 6. Outcomes

- Graduate outcomes (employment, further study, earnings, licensure pass rates), with source, cohort, year and approved wording.

## 7. Enrollment and application figures

- Verified enrollment and application figures, with baseline year and source. Needed before any work states or implies that "CMU enrollment is up" (C13), even inside fiction.
- Confirmation or correction of the agency-published Phase 1 results ("9.4% growth in applications since 2024", "new application record"; impressions and engagement figures) and permission to cite them.
- Permission to quote the Phase 1 press coverage (Forbes, The Chronicle of Higher Education) and President John Marshall's quoted remark.

## 8. Brand and mascot

- CMU brand guidelines, logo files and lockups.
- Mascot usage rules: whether the mascot may appear in a comic scene alongside Featherstone characters, and costume availability.
- Approval of the Phase 2 platform line: "We care about what you know, not who you know." Phase 1 aired "We care about who you are, not who you know." Please confirm which line CMU approves for Phase 2.

## 9. Approvals

- Who approves creative, claims, and legal or compliance review for this campaign, and how long review takes.
- Any limits on satire: referencing other real institutions, real people, or real higher-education issues.

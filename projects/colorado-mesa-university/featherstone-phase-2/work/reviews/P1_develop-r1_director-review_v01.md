## What is working (keep it and protect it)

1. **The Showcase blackout.** She cuts the power, and the presenter gives the talk from memory, better than the slides. Her report: *"Cut the power. They kept going. It appears to be in their heads."* This beat is A's law inside D's plot, and the best single proof of the line in the round. It becomes the hero film's spine.
2. **"Impressive. I meant suspicious."** It continues her Phase 1 voice ("Efficient. I meant efficient.") and turns it into an arc. It's the campaign's through-line for her character and the obvious :30 workhorse.
3. **"They call themselves Mavericks, sir. Unbranded."** A maverick is an unbranded calf, one whose owner can't be traced. CMU's own name for itself already means *not who you know*, and "Maverick at Heart" is CMU's tagline (S12). This line ties the campaign to the brand and resolves the question of how the line and the tagline coexist. It is the strongest idea in the round, subject to client approval of the name's use.
4. **The surveillance circle.** A pink grease-pencil circle with a copperplate threat note over real CMU imagery. The device always points at CMU, it scales from a film overlay to an icon, and its rule is generative: *wherever CMU is doing something it knows, she circles it and calls it a threat.*
5. **The generosity response.** When she tries to buy, steal or block what someone knows, they offer to teach her, and she is horrified at "free". This keeps CMU warm and calm without anyone making a speech.
6. **Her amendments,** from WordSmither: she's excellent at her own game, which only fails against people who know things; and she spies on tasks and objects, never on young people's bodies or living spaces. DesignChamp adds: no aiming at residence halls, and the circle lands on the task, never the person. These are hard rules.
7. **Guardrails from the genre.** CMU is not the slobs. It borrows the *Revenge of the Nerds* and *Dirty Dancing* side (underestimated people who are better at the thing). Hard exclusions: the era's alcohol, hazing, voyeurism and caricature.

## What is not yet good enough

1. **It's a portrait with no faces yet.** Both departments are right that D × A is only as good as its [CMU TRUTH] scenes. With generic stand-ins, it is a well-made spoof. The client proof request is now the critical path, not a formality.
2. **The :60 is overloaded.** Five beats and an epilogue. The story wants the :90 (S8 offers it). If a :60 is required, cut the classroom turn to its own :15 and keep the Showcase.
3. **The classroom turn ("Nobody's ever asked me that") is the emotional peak and the biggest risk.** If she softens, the antagonist dies after one film. She must snap back every time. Keep it, and test it in DEVELOP round 2 against a harder snap-back.
4. **The enrollment premise.** Run the claim-free fallback: F.U.'s decline is F.U.'s own fictional fact, and blaming CMU is its paranoia ("Somebody is taking our applicants."). Both departments prefer it on craft grounds too. Use no CMU numbers, arrows or charts.
5. **Look vs brand (S12).** DesignChamp's hard raking daylight for CMU conflicts with the guidelines' "golden glow of a sunset… soft, diffused light". Likely resolution: golden-hour raking light. It is low and warm, still reveals wear, and sits inside the guidelines. DesignChamp should resolve this in round 2 against S12. Mavroon must appear in every piece, and the end-card type is PF Grand Gothik.
6. **Donor money.** Both departments already moved F.U.'s gags off philanthropy because of CMU's $10M gift (Sept 16, 2026). Legacy, nepotism and favors are the targets. Keep it that way.

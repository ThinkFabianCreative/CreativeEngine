## Case study (base version, adapted per category)

**Objective**

Most college ads say the same thing: rich traditions, exceptional experiences, something bigger than ourselves. Meanwhile tuition outpaces inflation and public trust in higher ed keeps falling. Colorado Mesa University wanted national attention for what it actually stands for: access, affordability and judging students on character. It had a regional budget to do it. The goal was national coverage of CMU and its president, and more applications.

**Strategy**

We built CMU's opposite and let people laugh at it. Featherstone University ("F.U." for short) is a fictional elite school where a family check gets you straight A's and the common room is for looking at, not using. Satire let CMU say what the whole category was thinking without lecturing anyone. The punchline hands the argument back to CMU: "We care about who you are, not who you know."

**Execution**

- A :90 hero spot: a campus tour of Featherstone, led by a guide who keeps saying the quiet part out loud, until a voice offers "something more real."
- A working F.U. microsite laying out its hollow values.
- F.U. social and LinkedIn accounts posting in character, with a fictional president, Dr. Augustus Sinclair Featherstone III.
- CMU social answering with counter-lines: "No staged smiles." "No gimmicks."
- Transit digital out-of-home pairing F.U. and CMU panels.
- PR and earned media, with a limited paid budget focused on target DMAs in Colorado and nearby states.

**Results**

- 18M+ impressions across channels
- 2.6M+ engagements
- 2.5M+ earned media impressions
- National coverage in Forbes, The Chronicle of Higher Education, 9NEWS and KREX
- Applications up 9.4% since 2024, setting a new CMU record

Forbes called it "a satirical campaign creating a national stir."

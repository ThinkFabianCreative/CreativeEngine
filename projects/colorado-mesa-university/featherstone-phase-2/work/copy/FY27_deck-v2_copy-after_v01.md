## Notes

1. 08 (9:306–9:307, 9:308): "Featherstone proved it" rests on Phase 1 results we don't have yet. Until CMU confirms them, leadership reads it as a claim. Retitle or hold the column.
2. 04 (9:206): the deck's central belief is still a hypothesis. If the research isn't in before the client meeting, finance and legal will push on the whole case.
3. 05 close (11:402): "At CMU, you play on what you know" is the only line that says what CMU offers instead, and it's vague. A concrete Promise fact would land harder.
4. 23 (10:602–10:603): "Who pays" lists possible funders as if lined up. The NOTE covers it, but a finance reader may stop there. Consider "Possible funders."
5. 22 (10:558): the "we lose on camera" answer argues tone, not risk. Leadership may want what happens to the Promise message if the clip of CMU losing travels alone.

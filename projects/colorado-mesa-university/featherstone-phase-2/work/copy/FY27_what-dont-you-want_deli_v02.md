CLIENT: Colorado Mesa University. PROJECT: Featherstone Phase 2 / FY27 brand campaign. SOURCE: Andres's deli idea (2026-11-23). MODE: DEVELOP. Author: word-smither. Status: DRAFT for Andres.

What the Sullivan pass changed from v01 (Hey Whipple: one idea, cut everything that isn't it, let the audience finish the joke):
- One gag, played three times and then broken. Things you don't want come out of the sandwich. The anchor and the lectern are gone because they were props, not truths.
- The three objects that stayed are real deli things: a receipt, a name tag and a ticket number. The joke works because each one is already in a deli.
- President Marshall says less. He's the straight man, and the kids get the laughs.
- The menu board is gone. It was a second idea competing with the first.

## Notes

- **The break.** Three times he pulls something out. The fourth time he hands the sandwich over, and that turns "told what to think" from a line into an action. She chooses for herself, and the joke is that she's fussy about tomatoes.
- **President Marshall.** Seven short lines, all straight. If his delivery is stiff, the receipt, the name tag and the ticket carry the story without him.
- **Gary** is the runner and the button. Keep him in every cutdown.
- **Name tag.** "What you know" is crossed out and "who you know" written over it. That's the Phase 2 line, delivered as a prop in one second. Check it reads on a phone. If it doesn't, the tag just says HELLO, MY GRANDPA IS.
- **Claims.** None. No other school, no price, no better-than.
- **Cutdowns.** :15 = pickles, Gary, end card. :06s = one kid, one object.

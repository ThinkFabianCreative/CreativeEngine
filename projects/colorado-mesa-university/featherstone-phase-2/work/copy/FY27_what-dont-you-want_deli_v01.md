# "Hold the Pickles" :60, v01

CLIENT: Colorado Mesa University. PROJECT: Featherstone Phase 2 / FY27 brand campaign. SOURCE: Andres's deli idea (2026-11-23) on the client's "What don't you want?" brief. Replaces the v02 scripts. MODE: DEVELOP. Author: word-smither. Status: DRAFT for Andres.

The comic engine: every "don't want" gets pulled out of the sandwich as a physical object, and the objects get more absurd. President Marshall plays it completely straight. He's the calm one in the room and everyone else brings the jokes.

## Notes

- **Casting President Marshall.** Short lines, dead straight, no mugging. The deadpan carries the comedy and protects the president. If his read is stiff, give "Choosing a college…" and the end card to V.O. and keep him silent in the middle. The objects tell the story anyway.
- **Gary is the runner.** He wants the one thing everyone else throws away. That gives the spot a heart, a callback and a reason to watch again. Keep him in every cutdown.
- **The menu board is where the satire lives.** It mocks gatekeeping (waitlist, legacy club, hidden fees, "do you have a reference?"), never money. Freeze-frame bait for social. Every line on it must be invented, with no real school's name, colors or crest.
- **Claims.** "Bread's very affordable" is a joke, but it points at cost, so check the tone with CMU. Nothing here says CMU is better than any named school.
- **0:34.** The lectern gag still needs a better object for "told what to think." Options: a muzzle (too dark?), a script with someone else's name on it, or a pair of earplugs. Pick in the table read.
- **Cutdowns.** :15 = pickles + Gary + end card. :06s = each "don't want" plus its object, one per kid. The button stands alone as a :06.

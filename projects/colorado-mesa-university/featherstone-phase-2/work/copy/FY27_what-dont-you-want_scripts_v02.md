# "What Don't You Want?" :60 scripts, v02

CLIENT: Colorado Mesa University. PROJECT: Featherstone Phase 2 / FY27 brand campaign. SOURCE: client brief and scripts `raw/2026-11-22_client_what-dont-you-want_brief-and-scripts.docx`; v01 in this folder; director review `work/reviews/FY27_what-dont-you-want_scripts_director-review_v01.md`. MODE: DEVELOP. Author: word-smither, 2026-11-23. Status: DRAFT for Andres, not client-approved.

What changed from v01: the donor gags now hit the family name instead of a building. Each CMU half is a short scene, so CMU never scores points off anyone. Every spot ends on the question, and the Phase 2 line appears only in dialogue. Scripts are in presentation order. Claims marked [PROOF] need sign-off.

## 1. "The Honest Tour"

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Old ivy-covered quad, a composite that can't be read as any real school. A peppy TOUR GUIDE walks backwards in front of a group of families. | GUIDE: Okay! Questions? What do you want to know? |
| 0:04 | DESHAWN (17, hoodie, small town) raises his hand. | DESHAWN: What don't you want me to know? |
| 0:07 | The guide keeps smiling, but something behind the smile gives way. Long beat. | A distant bell. |
| 0:10 | The guide nods toward a student on the lawn. | GUIDE: …See the guy with the lacrosse stick? Fourth generation. Got in with a 2.1. |
| 0:15 | Families freeze. A dad lowers his phone. | GUIDE: He has a podcast. |
| 0:18 | They pass a seminar room window. | GUIDE: This class is great. You'll love it if you agree with the professor. |
| 0:23 | They pass the admissions office. | GUIDE: The waitlist is a vibe. It's mostly last names. |
| 0:27 | The guide stops walking backwards and faces forward for the first time. | GUIDE: It's not what you know here. (Long pause.) You know? |
| 0:32 | Deshawn takes off his visitor lanyard, hangs it gently on the guide's neck and walks out of frame. | |
| 0:36 | Hard cut: CMU, Grand Junction, daylight, the Monument in the distance. A CMU student guide walks forward with a small group. Deshawn is in it. | Music in, low. |
| 0:40 | Deshawn raises his hand. | DESHAWN: What don't you want me to know? |
| 0:43 | The CMU guide thinks about it, then shrugs. | CMU GUIDE: Parking's bad. |
| 0:46 | Deshawn waits for more. There isn't any. He almost smiles. The tour keeps walking. | |
| 0:54 | End card: **What don't you want?** CMU logo. coloradomesa.edu | V.O.: Colorado Mesa University. |

Notes: the joke is about inheritance, so no building, gift or donor appears anywhere. "Parking's bad" is the CMU answer: honest, small and dry, and it claims nothing against anyone. Legal: no real gate, crest, Gothic landmark, colors or mascot on the old campus. "Parking's bad" should be checked with CMU so it doesn't touch a sore point.

## 3. "The Custodian" (the client's script C, sharpened)

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A slick focus group room with a two-way mirror. Behind the glass, MARKETERS in quarter-zips mouth along to a script for a facilitator who hasn't shown up. One writes "WANTS" on a whiteboard and waits. Students and parents sit around the table. | Wall clock ticks. |
| 0:05 | CARLOS (60s, custodian, cart) backs in through the door, sees everyone, stops. | CARLOS: Oh. Sorry. Wrong night? |
| 0:08 | A PARENT waves at the glossy folders. | PARENT: It's about picking a college. They keep asking what our kids want. |
| 0:12 | Carlos looks at the kids. They look tired. He leans on his mop. | CARLOS: Huh. How's that going? |
| 0:15 | Silence. | |
| 0:17 | Carlos pulls out a chair, sits, takes off his gloves. | CARLOS: Try the other one. What don't you want? |
| 0:21 | Beat. A kid in a hoodie, almost to himself: | KID 1: I don't want my mom paying for me till she's seventy. |
| 0:25 | That opens the room up. Fast cuts: | GIRL: I don't want to keep my mouth shut for four years so I don't lose a grade. / FARM KID: I don't want to lose my spot to some guy because of his last name. / DAD: (quiet) I don't want her to do what I did. |
| 0:36 | Behind the glass, the marketer with the marker lowers it. "WANTS" is still the only word on the board. | |
| 0:38 | Carlos knocks twice on the mirror with his mop handle, without looking at it. | CARLOS: They heard you. |
| 0:41 | A GIRL, sincere: | GIRL: Is there anywhere that's not like that? |
| 0:43 | Carlos stands, gloves back on, and pushes the cart toward the door. His jacket hangs open: a faded CMU alumni sweatshirt underneath. | CARLOS: Went to one. Six years. |
| 0:48 | At the door, over his shoulder: | CARLOS: Nobody asked me my last name. |
| 0:52 | The door clicks shut. Behind the glass, someone crosses out "WANTS" and writes "DON'T WANT." | |
| 0:55 | End card: **What don't you want?** CMU logo. coloradomesa.edu | V.O.: Colorado Mesa University. |

Notes: the sushi and the grandpa's building are gone, so the gags now mock behavior. Carlos's last line pays off the family-name thread and agrees with the campaign. The dad's line stays as the emotional turn. [PROOF]: "Six years" implies part-time study while working, so CMU needs to confirm that path exists.

## 4. "Cross It Out" (cutdown and social system)

This :60 is the master for the cutdown system: each cross-out works as a :06, and any two plus the ending make a :15.

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | MAYA (17) on her bedroom floor, a fat black Sharpie in her hand. The wall is papered with mailers that use invented headlines and generic type, styled to look like no real school. | A phone keeps buzzing. |
| 0:04 | Grown-ups ask her, straight to camera: an aunt, a coach, a guy at the gas station, a guidance poster that talks. | AUNT: So what do you want to do? / COACH: What do you want, Maya? / GAS STATION GUY: Where you wanna go? / POSTER: Dream big! What's your passion? |
| 0:12 | Maya uncaps the Sharpie and crosses out "A LEGACY OF EXCELLENCE." | MAYA (V.O.): I don't know what I want. |
| 0:16 | "WHERE CONNECTIONS ARE MADE." She crosses it out. | MAYA (V.O.): I know I don't want someone's dad's contacts to matter more than what I know. |
| 0:22 | She flips a brochure to its price sheet, reads it and lays the brochure face down. | MAYA (V.O.): I don't want to pay for a name until I'm forty. |
| 0:28 | "JOIN A COMMUNITY THAT SHARES YOUR VALUES." She writes "MINE?" in the margin. | MAYA (V.O.): I don't want to be told what to think. I can do that myself. |
| 0:35 | Faster: cross, cross, cross, until there's no mailer left. The wall is all black marker. | MAYA (V.O.): I don't want a lecture hall where nobody learns my name. |
| 0:45 | Her MOM leans in the doorway and looks at the wall. | MOM: …So what do you want? |
| 0:48 | | MAYA: Wrong question. |
| 0:50 | Mom looks at the wall a long time. | MOM: …What don't you want? |
| 0:53 | Maya smiles and holds out the Sharpie. Mom takes it. | |
| 0:55 | End card over the black wall: **What don't you want?** CMU logo. coloradomesa.edu | V.O.: Colorado Mesa University. |

Notes: every mailer gets crossed out, so the spot never asks anyone to pick us. The question finally gets asked out loud in the story. The mom gets there herself, and the Sharpie handoff is the end beat. Legal: every headline and layout must be invented.

Phase 2 of "Welcome to Featherstone" will show who CMU is, not only who it isn't. We have read CMU's public website and would like to build on what CMU already says about itself. Before any of it appears in advertising, we need CMU to confirm it and to supply what the website does not cover.

## 1. Please confirm these published facts for advertising use

We found these on coloradomesa.edu. Please confirm each is current and approved, or correct it.

**Figures that disagree on the site. Which is right?**
- Academic programs: "255 programs and 51 minors" (homepage, future students) vs "200+ academic programs" (Why CMU).
- Financial aid: "$88 Million awarded in financial aid last year" (homepage) vs "$77 million total aid awarded last year" (financial aid page). Which year?
- Campus housing: "Nearly 2,300 students" in "14" halls (future students) vs "More than 3,200 students" across "more than 17 residence halls" (September 16, 2026 news release).
- Tuition ranking: "3rd lowest tuition in Colorado" (homepage) vs "3rd lowest tuition and fees among other four-year Colorado colleges" (financial aid page). Which wording is approved, and what is the source of the ranking?

**Facts we would like to use:**
- "More than 10,000 students", 15.4% from outside Colorado. Which year?
- "60% of first-time students received a scholarship last year." Which year?
- "Small classes with professors that know you by name." Is there a class-size or student-to-faculty figure we may cite?
- 2026-27 cost of attendance figures. Will 2027-28 rates be available before launch?
- Career outcomes: "74% of 2025 graduates have found success in their career path…". How is "success" defined, and may we cite it?
- CMU Tech: "We reduced tuition for career and technical programs by 40%." From what baseline, and when?
- Athletics, campus and founding facts (NCAA Division II, 28 teams, 141 acres, founded 1925, accredited by the Higher Learning Commission).
- Mission, values and "Human Scale University" language: may the campaign quote them?

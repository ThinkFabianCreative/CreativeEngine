# EWU brand standards (client knowledge)

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · SCOPE: CLIENT-LEVEL · Compiled 2026-11-09

## Typeface
- **Open Sans Variable** is the brand font, confirmed by Andres on 2026-11-09. EWU's PowerPoint templates name Arial as the system fallback "closest to the brand font".

## Color (EWU Logo Usage Guide)
| Name | Web/video | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Eagle Red | #B7142E, RGB 183/20/46 | Pantone 186, CMYK 0/100/80/05 |
| Black | #000000 | 100K |
| White | #FFFFFF | CMYK 0/0/0/0 |

- The university colors are red and white, with black as the tertiary color (EWU Athletics 2026 guide).
- The web red was adjusted for contrast. The guide says red and black don't contrast, so on black use the reverse (white) logo, never red type.

## Logo
- There are four logo options in vertical and horizontal versions: positive two-color, positive on light, reverse on dark, and black.
- Clear space around the logo is at least the cap height of the "E". Don't go below the minimum size.
- Don't skew, stretch, recolor, stroke, separate the eagle, or crowd the logo.
- Local files: `ewu-logo.svg` (white), `ewu-eagle.svg`, `ewu-eagle-black-red.png`.

## Positioning
- The official brand position is "the Region's Polytechnic", with a lowercase "the" in running text (2026 Editorial Style Guide). The brand identity was updated in September 2024.
- In print, web addresses are lowercase and bold: **ewu.edu**.

A Data-Driven System Built Around Slate

Our approach begins and ends with data. Slate remains EWU’s admissions system of record. We know that we will not be granted access to Slate, which is fine as our system connects to Slate via two FERPA-aware data flows: (1) inbound, real-time—Slate-hosted inquiry forms embedded directly on WordPress landing pages, capturing UTM and source data into Slate as it happens; and (2) outbound, scheduled—automated, PII-stripped Slate query exports configured by EWU’s Graduate Studies Office and delivered to TRAFFIK on a daily/weekly cadence. We have a proprietary attribution model that measures touch points 400 days+ from first interaction. You can be assured that every paid media, content, SEO, and nurture decision is connected to the prospect’s funnel-stage status reported through this asynchronous flow.

Creative Direction

Our recommended creative direction will position EWU graduate education as practical, regional, accessible, and career-moving.

The work will not rely on generic graduate school claims. It will be grounded in what makes EWU compelling: applied learning, workforce relevance, regional impact, faculty access, belonging, and upward mobility.

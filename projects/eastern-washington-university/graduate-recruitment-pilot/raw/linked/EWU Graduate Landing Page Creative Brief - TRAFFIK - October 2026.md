**Eastern Washington University**

**Graduate Landing Page Creative Brief**

October 2026

| | |
| :-: | :-: |
| **Client** | Eastern Washington University (EWU), Graduate Studies |
| **Project Title** | Graduate Enrollment Pilot, Program Landing Pages |
| **Task** | Write, design and test at least 12 landing pages for [EWU's Management](https://www.ewu.edu/cpp/business/management/management-ms/), [Creative Writing](https://www.ewu.edu/cahss/english-philosophy/creative-writing/mfa/) and [Occupational Therapy graduate programs](https://www.ewu.edu/chsph/occupational-therapy/). |

**Project Overview**

EWU wants to make it easier for prospective graduate students to understand each program and take the next step. The new pages will bring program information, downloadable guides and inquiry forms together, while helping admissions follow up and track applications.

- Answer four questions: Is this program right for me? Can I fit it into my life? What will it cost? Why choose EWU?

| | |
| :-: | :-: |
| **Campaign Objective** | Generate qualified interest and help EWU move prospective students from inquiry to application and enrollment. |
| **Deliverables** | - Desktop and mobile landing pages with reusable layouts<br>- Copy for each page, including headlines, program information, FAQs and confirmation messages.<br>- Form and tracking requirements, testing and editable files for EWU to maintain after launch. |
| **Proposed Pages** | - Proposed split: 5 MAS, 3 Creative Writing and 4 Occupational Therapy pages.<br>- **Master of Science (MS) in Management:** program overview and application, balancing study with other commitments, business and leadership learning, and application and funding guidance.<br>- **Master of Fine Arts (MFA) in Creative Writing:** program overview and application, writing community, and application and funding guidance.<br>- **Master of Occupational Therapy (OT):** program overview, eligibility, study and fieldwork, and application preparation. |
| **Audience** | Audience direction is based on EWU's inputs. **Management**: people looking to build business and leadership skills alongside work and other commitments. (Hybrid). **Creative Writing:** fiction and poetry writers looking for the right faculty, workshops, in-person writing community, alongside work and other commitments. (Hybrid). **OT:** applicants assessing eligibility and the commitment to full-time, in-person study and fieldwork. (In-Person) |
| **Core Message** | Get a clear picture of the program, the commitment and the cost, so you can confidently take your next step. |
| **Tone of Voice** | Warm, direct and credible. Use plain language and approved graduate program facts. Avoid guarantees about admission, funding or career outcomes. |
| **Key Messages** | - **Management:** explain the learning, schedule, format and cost.<br>- **Creative Writing:** highlight faculty, workshops, thesis and community.<br>- **OT:** explain eligibility, full-time study, and fieldwork. Distinguish earning the degree from certification and licensure.<br>- Use EWU-approved costs and claims. Keep eligibility, funding, and deadlines easy to find. |
| **Call to Action** | - **Request program information:** for visitors who want to learn more.<br>- **Start your application:** Add link to apply |
| **Forms and Tracking** | - Keep forms simple and send inquiries to admissions with the program the user is interested in and source of where they came from (Meta or Google). EWU must approve fields, consent and routing.<br>- Track inquiries and application progress without sending personal details to analytics. Confirm how OT applications will be reported. |
| **Creative Guidelines** | - Follow EWU's [brand and logo guidelines](https://www.ewu.edu/marcom/logos-standards/), using approved imagery and Eagle Red #B7142E.<br>- Design for desktop, mobile and accessibility. Test forms and downloads as part of the page experience. |

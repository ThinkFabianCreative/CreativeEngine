Based on the **Eastern Washington University SOW/contract**, the core TRAFFIK deliverables are below. The engagement is focused on building an inbound enrollment marketing and lead-generation engine for EWU's targeted graduate programs.

### EWU Deliverables

**Strategy & Planning**
- 12-month content, campaign, and communications calendar
- 4 program-specific candidate journey/persona maps
- Recruitment journey workflows for the target programs

**SEO & AEO**
- Keyword research
- Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) / AI search prompt analysis
- SEO/AEO optimization blueprints for at least 8 website pages
- Post-implementation QA after EWU implements the recommendations

**Content Development**
- 6 complete premium lead-generation assets, such as: program/career guides, interactive viewbooks, whitepapers
- Supporting ad copy
- Supporting social copy
- Supporting promotional email sequences

**Landing Pages / CRO**
- Strategic layout/design guidance
- Conversion copywriting
- QA/review for at least 12 program landing pages
- EWU hosts the landing pages

**Paid Media**
- Approximately 16–20 digital advertising campaigns
- Search, social, and display
- Campaign buildout; audience architecture; creative asset uploading; tracking pixel deployment; bid management; A/B testing; ongoing/daily optimization
- Campaigns run within EWU-owned ad accounts

**HubSpot / Lead Nurturing**
- Provision, configure, and manage HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise
- Build and manage automated lead nurture programs
- Distinct communications for Suspect, Prospect, and Inquiry stages
- 5 program-specific automated nurture workflows
- Lead capture/forms and campaign tracking
- Transition prospects to EWU's Slate communications once they reach Application Started

**Tracking, Attribution & Slate**
- Cross-domain tracking; UTM taxonomy; GA4/Slate event tracking
- Attribution for inquiries, application starts, and enrollments
- Map top-of-funnel engagement data into Slate
- Automated HubSpot/Slate data integration and synchronization
- Full-funnel attribution setup

**Reporting**
- Monthly Performance Report by the 5th business day of each month, including ad spend, lead volume, CPL, and HubSpot performance
- Quarterly Strategic Review covering enrollment funnel performance, SEO/AEO progress, and optimization recommendations
- Final Transition Documentation Package delivered 15 days before the end of the contract

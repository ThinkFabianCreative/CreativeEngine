**Eastern Washington University**

**Graduate Creative Assets Brief**

October 2026

| | |
| :-: | :-: |
| **Client** | Eastern Washington University (EWU), Graduate Studies |
| **Project Title** | Graduate Enrollment Pilot: Six Downloadable Guides |
| **Task** | Develop six downloadable guides, two for each pilot program: MS in Management, MFA in Creative Writing and Master of Occupational Therapy (OT). Include the ads, social posts and emails that promote them. |

**Project Overview**

EWU needs practical resources that help prospective graduate students understand each program and decide whether to apply. Give readers a clear picture of the experience, commitment and next step.

| | |
| :-: | :-: |
| **Campaign Objective** | Help the right students request information and move toward an application. |
| **Deliverables** | - Six accessible PDFs, plus editable source files.<br>- Supporting search, paid and organic social, display and email creative.<br>- Emails explain program fit and the next step. Once an application starts, communications pass to admissions. Confirm this handoff before launch. |
| **The Six Assets (proposed)** | - **M1 Management Skills Guide**: skills, coursework and practical projects. Next step: request program information.<br>- **M2 Management Study Planner**: schedule, workload, cost and entry requirements. Next step: review application requirements.<br>- **F1 MFA Writing Community Guide**: workshops, faculty and the residential experience. Next step: request MFA information.<br>- **F2 MFA Application and Funding Planner**: application materials, funding and deadlines. Next step: start an application when the cycle opens.<br>- **O1 OT Application Readiness Guide**: prerequisites, application materials and selection process. Next step: review application requirements.<br>- **O2 OT Study and Fieldwork Guide**: coursework, fieldwork, time commitment and costs. Next step: request OT information. |
| **Overarching Creative Direction** | - Create one cohesive EWU look with clear headings and easy-to-scan content. Show business in practice for Management, writing and workshops for MFA, and supervised practice for OT.<br>- Use approved imagery, with layouts that work without photography while permissions are pending. New shoots, video, interactive tools and extra channels require separate estimates. |
| **Brand Differentiators** | Show EWU's emphasis on access, practical learning and faculty connection through specific program examples. "The Region's Polytechnic" is background context, not approved campaign copy. |
| **Audience & Learnings** | Working audience assumptions, pending EWU validation: Management: graduates and working professionals building business and people-management skills. MFA: fiction and poetry writers seeking faculty guidance and a writing community. OT: prospective applicants assessing their readiness and the demands of professional study. |
| **Single-Minded Proposition** | Understand the program, the commitment and your next step. (Proposed.) |
| **Tone of Voice** | Warm, clear and helpful. Answer: Is this right for me? Can I make it work? What will it cost? |
| **Messaging Strategy** | Focus on practical student questions. EWU must verify: Management: delivery format, attendance, costs and entry requirements. Hybrid versus fully online remains unresolved; keep this program distinct from EWU's MBA. MFA: location, faculty, application requirements, funding and deadlines. Funding is competitive and must not be presented as guaranteed. OT: prerequisites, fieldwork, costs and application cycle. Clarify that graduation, certification and licensure are separate steps. Keep unconfirmed facts and unsupported salary, placement or admissions claims out of final copy. |
| **Call to Action** | Promotions lead to an approved download page. Each guide then directs readers to request information, review requirements or apply, as listed above. |
| **Creative Guidelines** | - Follow EWU's logo guide and use Eagle Red #B7142E for digital. Typography is pending confirmation.<br>- Create accessible, tagged PDFs with readable contrast, image descriptions and a logical reading order.<br>- EWU must confirm the required accessibility standard: WCAG 2.1 AA or 2.2 AA. |

1\. INTRODUCTION

1.1. PURPOSE AND BACKGROUND

Eastern Washington University (“EWU”) is requesting proposals from highly specialized marketing firms to provide comprehensive inbound enrollment marketing services for its graduate portfolio. The primary objective is to leverage data-driven digital strategy, high- value content development, and targeted paid media to systematically generate high-quality graduate student leads. The University seeks a partner who can move prospects through the entire recruitment journey—from initial awareness and inquiry to application submission and final enrollment.

The graduate program landscape has become increasingly competitive, and EWU is committed to establishing a sustainable, full-funnel marketing infrastructure to drive long- term enrollment growth. Because EWU’s graduate programs have not been subject to a centralized, strategic marketing effort in recent years, this contract represents a pivotal shift toward a modern, "pull-based" recruitment model.

A successful firm will demonstrate specific, quantifiable experience achieving graduate-level enrollment growth and yield at other accredited universities. While firms may utilize various industry-standard marketing automation platforms, the selected contractor must demonstrate a proven ability to integrate these tools seamlessly with the University’s existing CRM (Slate) to ensure full-funnel attribution and data integrity.

EWU is a regional, comprehensive public university located in Cheney and Spokane, Washington, with programs offered throughout the state and online with an enrollment of over 9,000 students. EWU’s 300-acre main campus is located in Cheney, a community of 11,000 residents, and is just 17 miles from Washington’s second largest city, Spokane.

1.2. OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE OF WORK

The Eastern Washington University (EWU) Graduate Programs office is seeking proposals from qualified firms to develop and implement a comprehensive inbound enrollment marketing program. The goal is to increase enrollment across a specific portfolio of graduate programs (initially targeting 3 priority programs: MS in Management, MOT, and MURP). The proposed services must be all-inclusive, encompassing Strategic Consulting, Organic Visibility (SEO/AEO), Paid Advertising, and Lead Nurturing.

The scope of work includes, but is not limited to, the following core services:

• Strategy & Planning: Develop a customized marketing and lead generation strategy, including a 12-month editorial calendar and persona-based journey mapping for the target programs.

• Search & Answer Engine Optimization (SEO/AEO): Conduct deep-dive research on critical keywords and AI-retrieval prompts to improve organic awareness for a minimum of 8 pages. The firm will provide detailed optimization blueprints and technical recommendations for EWU staff to implement.

• High-Value Content Development: Craft a minimum of 6 premium digital assets (e.g., career-focused program guides, interactive viewbooks, or whitepapers) designed specifically for lead capture. Develop the supporting ad copy and email sequences to promote these assets.

• Conversion Optimization & Consultation: Provide strategic design, conversion-centric copywriting, and Quality Assurance (QA) reviews for a minimum of 12 EWU-hosted landing pages. The firm will provide the "best practice" framework and performance audits, while EWU maintains internal control of the page builds.

• Multi-Channel Digital Advertising: Develop, launch, and manage a paid digital advertising strategy across approximately 16-20 targeted campaigns. This includes top-of- funnel awareness, event/deadline promotion, and direct "push-to-apply" tactics.

• Automated Lead Nurturing: Build and deploy a minimum of 5 program-specific communication flows within a Contractor-provided marketing automation platform. These flows must nurture prospects from initial inquiry through application submission using behavioral branching logic.

• Data Integration Expertise: Demonstrate high proficiency in integrating the proposed marketing automation platform with the Slate (Technolutions) CRM. The firm must demonstrate a proven methodology for bidirectional data sharing, ensuring lead engagement data is seamlessly and accurately reflected within Slate for admissions follow- up.

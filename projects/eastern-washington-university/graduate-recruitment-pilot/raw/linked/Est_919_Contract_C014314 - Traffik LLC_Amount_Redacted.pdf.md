PURPOSE The purpose of this Contract is to have Contractor provide EWU with comprehensive inbound enrollment marketing services for its graduate marketing portfolio by establishing a sustainable, data-driven inbound marketing and lead generation engine for EWU’s non-accelerated, traditional online, and on-campus graduate programs to generate increased high quality applicants.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the terms and conditions contained herein, or attached and incorporated and made a part thereof, EWU and Contractor mutually agree as follows:

1\. SCOPE OF WORK

A. Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated by reference, contains the General Terms and Conditions governing work to be performed under this Contract, the nature of the working relationship between EWU and Contractor, and specific obligations of both Parties. Contractor will provide services and staff, and otherwise do all things necessary for or incidental to the performance of work, as set forth below and as included in EWU’s Request for Proposals (RFP) \#26-CTR01 (Exhibit B), Contractor’s Proposal dated May 11, 2026 (Exhibit C), and in this Contract. The RFP and its attachments, as well as Contractor’s Proposal, are herein incorporated as terms of this Contract. Specifically, Contractor will:

1: PROJECT SCOPE & OBJECTIVES

1.A Targeted Program Matrix: Initial execution shall focus on a pilot portfolio of target graduate programs requiring distinct, program-specific tactical strategies:

• Master's in Management (MIM): Fully online (non-OPM); high-volume, speed-to-lead automated recruitment.

• MFA in Creative Writing: On-campus/national niche; high-value faculty storytelling and reputation-driven inbound content.

• Occupational Therapy (OT): On-campus/OTCASA; high-demand applicant filtering and prerequisite quality screening.

2: TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & HUBSPOT SANDBOX MANDATE

2.A HubSpot Front-End Operations Sandbox:

• To ensure rapid deployment and operational autonomy for the EWU Graduate Studies Office, Contractor shall configure, build, and execute all lead capture forms, landing pages, email nurture workflows, and campaign tracking within EWU’s HubSpot Marketing Automation Platform (MAP).

• EWU Graduate Studies retains sole administrative control over its HubSpot instance. Contractor shall build all assets directly within EWU's HubSpot environment so that EWU staff can maintain, edit, and duplicate campaigns independently.

2.B Downstream Slate CRM Data Synchronization:

• System of Record: Technolutions Slate remains EWU’s official institutional system of record for admissions and application tracking.

• Data Integration: Contractor shall establish and maintain an automated webhook/API integration between HubSpot and Slate. All prospective student leads, inquiry form submissions, and engagement metrics (email opens, content downloads, campaign sources) captured in HubSpot shall automatically sync downstream into Slate for admissions follow-up.

2.C Ad Account & Pixel Ownership:

• All digital advertising accounts (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, etc.), tracking pixels, conversion tags, and GA4 analytics properties shall reside within EWU-owned accounts.

• Contractor shall manage campaigns as an authorized agency user. EWU shall retain 100% ownership of all ad accounts, pixel history, remarketing audiences, and campaign data during and after the contract term.

2.D Intellectual Property (IP) Transfer: All whitepapers, program guides, ad copy, design files (raw, editable formats including Figma/InDesign), email scripts, and workflow logic created under this Contract are "work made for hire" and become the exclusive property of EWU immediately upon creation.

3: SPECIFIC TASKS & DELIVERABLES

3.A. Strategic Planning & Journey Mapping

3.A.1 Editorial Calendar: Deliver a comprehensive 12-month content, campaign, and communication execution calendar.

3.A.2. Persona Mapping: Deliver detailed, program-specific candidate persona maps and recruitment journey workflows for each target program listed in Section 1.B.1.A.

3.B. Organic Visibility (SEO & AEO)

3.B.1 Research: Conduct targeted keyword research and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) prompt analysis for AI-retrieval engines.

3.B.2 Optimization Blueprints: Deliver actionable SEO/AEO technical and content blueprints for a minimum of eight (8) program website pages. Implementation shall be executed by EWU web staff; Contractor shall perform post-implementation QA audits.

3.C. High-Value Content Asset Development

3.C.1 Lead Capture Assets: Concept, write, design, and produce a minimum of six (6) premium digital lead-gen assets (e.g., career-focused program guides, interactive viewbooks, or whitepapers).

3.C.2 Promotional Copywriting: Produce all supporting ad copy, social copy, and promotional email sequences required to drive traffic to these assets.

3.D. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) & Page Audits

3.D.1 Landing Page Guidance: Provide strategic layout design, conversion copywriting, and QA reviews for a minimum of twelve (12) program landing pages.

3.D.2 Hosting Control: All landing pages and inquiry forms shall be hosted within EWU’s web/Slate environment. Contractor shall not host landing pages on external subdomains or third-party page builders without prior written approval from EWU.

3.E. Multi-Channel Paid Digital Advertising

3.E.1 Campaign Deployment: Design, launch, and optimize approximately sixteen (16) to twenty (20) targeted digital advertising campaigns across search, social, and display channels.

3.E.2 Direct Media Cost Safeguard & Account Ownership: All active paid media campaigns shall be executed within native ad platform accounts created under and owned by EWU (via dedicated sub-accounts under EWU’s primary Business Manager/MCC structures), with full operational access granted to Agency. All media spend shall be billed directly to EWU’s institutional payment method, at cost. The Contractor shall not invoice EWU for media spend, and shall not apply any markup or additional fees for the placements.

3.E.3 Agency Hands-on-Keyboard Media Management & SLA: Agency acknowledges that EWU possesses no internal media buyers and that EWU’s Marketing and Communications (MarCom) department maintains a zero-operational-overhead policy for this pilot. Agency shall provide 100% turn-key, hands-on-keyboard media management, including but not limited to: campaign build-out, audience architecture, creative asset uploading, tracking pixel deployment (via Google Tag Manager), bid management, A/B testing, and daily performance optimization.

Agency shall require no ongoing administrative, operational, or creative deployment effort from EWU MarCom or Graduate Studies personnel following initial one-time credential/partner access delegation (not to exceed 30 minutes of EWU administrative setup time during onboarding).

3.F Automated Lead Nurturing & Communications

3.F.1 Marketing Automation Platform (MAP) Provisioning: The Contractor (TRAFFIK) shall provision, configure, and manage a HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise environment to serve as the primary marketing automation platform for the pilot programs.

3.F.2 Top-of-Funnel Lead Nurturing: The Contractor shall build, manage, and execute all automated lead nurture cadences within HubSpot for prospects in the Suspect, Prospect, and Inquiry stages. This includes deploying distinct multi-touch email communication flows that respond to prospect behavior and funnel stage.

3.F.3 Inbound Data Capture: To protect admissions data integrity and eliminate the need for external administrative access to Slate, the Contractor shall utilize inbound, real-time,

Slate-hosted inquiry forms embedded directly onto EWU's WordPress landing pages. These forms will capture all necessary UTM and source data into Slate as the inquiry occurs.

3.F.4 Slate Handoff and Transition: Once a prospect officially reaches the "Application Started" stage, the Contractor's HubSpot nurture campaigns will cease. The prospect will then transition to EWU's existing Slate drip campaigns for the remainder of the admissions process.

3.F.5 Outbound Data Export & Attribution: To facilitate performance tracking and optimization, EWU will configure an outbound, scheduled-automated, PII-stripped Slate query export. This file will be delivered to the Contractor via secure SFTP transfer on a regular cadence (daily recommended, weekly minimum). The Contractor will utilize this read-only export to map digital touchpoints to Slate conversions, ensuring clean attribution without requiring standing access to EWU's CRM.

3.G Advanced Tracking & ROI Attribution

3.G.1 Attribution Setup: Configure cross-domain tracking, UTM taxonomy, and GA4/Slate event tracking to attribute inquiries, application starts, and final enrollments to specific campaigns, ad sets, and content assets.

3.H Slate Data Integration & Handover

3.H.1 Field Mapping: Map all top-of-funnel prospect engagement data (ad source, asset downloads, email clicks) into Slate custom fields.

3.H.2 Closeout Transition: Upon contract completion or termination, Contractor shall deliver a complete documentation package and ensure EWU staff can independently operate and replicate all workflows inside Slate without ongoing Contractor assistance.

## 4. Core Creative Idea (REWORK)

**Recommended CCI:** *Stories where EWU hands graduate students the real work of their profession: the defense, the magazine, the patient.*

The engine is the handover and the proof object, not the doubter. The subject of every story is the work being handed over and the person doing it. Doubt can appear inside a story (a student's nerves before the defense), never as the idea's subject, so we never tell an audience it was "about to rule itself out". This also avoids implying MFA TA spots are easy to get: they are competitive.

**Seven tests**
1. Insightful: yes. Meets the primary barrier (§5): proof that it changes what you can do.
2. Global: yes, across all three programs and every EWU market.
3. Creative: yes. A handover is a dramatic moment with a physical object at its center.
4. Complete: functional (the work), emotional (being trusted with it), higher order (staying and leading in the region).
5. Fluid: film, stills, social, landing pages, nurture email; the object travels across all.
6. Simple: one verb, "hands", and three nouns.
7. Long-lived: every program, cohort and year produces new handovers and objects.

**Swap test, one line each** (CWU and WWU positioning not researched; those lines are our reasoning)
- CWU: could run "hands-on" stories, but cannot show EWU's defense, *Willow Springs* or EWU's clinic; with named proof objects the swap fails. Weakest margin; watch it.
- WWU: same structure risk; no equivalent named objects in our evidence. Fails with the proof rule, at risk without it.
- WGU: competency-based, self-paced; no one hands you anything. Feels false.
- Gonzaga: rigor and Jesuit leadership; would not frame the work as handed over by a public price. Feels off.
- Whitworth: leadership and AACSB; its OTD is doctoral and would not say "the patient" at master's level. Feels off.
- WSU: scale and online MBA; the handover story is not its register. Feels off.
- UW: research prestige; could tell handover stories but would lead with rank. Feels off.

**Rule the idea carries:** every execution shows a real EWU proof object (a defense, a page of *Willow Springs*, a supervised patient session) and a real EWU person. Without the object it collapses into "learn by doing", which anyone can say.

**Fit under The Region's Polytechnic:** "hands" is the graduate proof of "hands-on". The polytechnic makes the claim; the CCI shows it at graduate level, MFA included. EWU marketing must agree to this reading before it runs.

## 5. One cross-program Job to Be Done (REWORK)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Job** | PERSUADE working adults who are considering a master's TO start an EWU application (inquiry first). |
| **Target** | The broad target in §3; the pilot's paid reach is its active slice. (assumption) |
| **Behavior to change** | They delay, or default to the cheapest and fastest online option, because they doubt the degree will change what they can actually do at a cost they can justify. (assumption) |
| **Shared core insight (OUR SYNTHESIS, not a quote)** | Will this change what I can actually do, at a cost I can justify? |
| **Real voices behind it (category voices from public forums, not EWU prospects; small sample)** | "I'm having a hard time justifying the total investment." (MS/MiM prospect, r/ESCP) · "has the masters made getting promoted/a new job that much easier?" (r/managers) · "Only apply to funded programs." (MFA student, GradCafe) · "I would never advise someone to go to OT school for more than $50k debt." (r/OccupationalTherapy) |
| **New belief** | At EWU I'd be handed the real work of my profession before I graduate, at a price I can defend. (fits revised Promise A) |
| **New behavior** | Request information, then start the application this cycle instead of next year. |
| **Barrier type (HYPOTHESIS until EWU prospect voice exists)** | **Primary: practical/value.** Answered by the proof object and the plain cost fact. **Secondary: aspirational** ("not for me"), present but never the subject; carried inside stories. |

**Each program's own barrier**
- **MS in Management:** status and value against an MBA ("will an MS count as much?"). Answer: the defended portfolio as proof of what you can do.
- **MFA in Creative Writing:** funding ("only apply to funded"). Answer: *Willow Springs* work for all, and the chance to compete for a funded TA spot. **TA funding is selective, not universal.**
- **MOT:** debt, location, and master's versus doctorate (the "I just want to be an OT" voice belongs here, as a credential barrier: the MOT is an entry-level route to the profession). Answer: supervised patient work, Spokane classes, public price. No licensure claim; graduating does not confer it.

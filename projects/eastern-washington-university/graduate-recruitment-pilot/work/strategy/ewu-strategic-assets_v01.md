# EWU strategic assets, graduate recruitment (v01)

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · BRAND: EWU, graduate programs (MS in Management, MFA in Creative Writing, MOT) · SOURCE SCOPE: PROJECT-LEVEL, proposed by TRAFFIK, not approved by EWU · Author: brief-machine · 2026-10-26
Method: shared/frameworks/brand-strategy.md §§1–6. Andres's rule: invent where missing, but label it. Nothing here becomes client knowledge until EWU confirms it.

## 1. Existing assets we must respect

- **"The Region's Polytechnic"** is EWU's positioning and live campaign since September 2024 (idfive; anthem :30 on TV; Merit, 2026 Educational Advertising Awards). Before it: "The New Think" (2022). (from our brand scan)
- **"ALL IN"** creed: Grit, Grace, Gratitude, Greatness. (from EWU's site)
- **Red #B7142E** and white; Swoop the eagle; "Eags", #Eagle4Life; football alumni as proof (Kupp, Chism). (from our brand scan)
- **Mission, vision, values**: affordable academic excellence, applied learning in high-demand fields, upward social mobility, first-generation and low-income students named explicitly. (from EWU's RFP)

**How our idea sits under it, not against it.** The Region's Polytechnic is the brand's *claim* (hands-on learning that leads to work). EWU has no Core Creative Idea that turns that claim into a repeatable kind of story (scan: not found). Ours is the graduate episode of the polytechnic: it never replaces the line, never invents a rival tagline, and every piece carries EWU red and the master brand. It also answers the polytechnic's weak spot, the faculty charge that it is "in name only" and that it sidelines the humanities: we prove hands-on with real graduate jobs (a defended portfolio, a literary magazine, clinical fieldwork), the MFA included. (assumption: EWU marketing will accept a graduate-level story line under the master campaign; to confirm)

## 2. Brand promise

- **A.** EWU exists to put the real work of a profession within reach of people who thought it wasn't for them.
- **B.** EWU exists to turn what you learn into work the region needs.

**Recommend A.** B restates the polytechnic and the workforce mission; any regional public could say it. A keeps the polytechnic's "hands-on" (the real work) and adds the access half of EWU's own mission and values (first-gen, mobility, "affordable academic excellence"), which no Spokane competitor owns. (assumption, built from EWU's RFP and our research)

## 4. Core Creative Idea

| # | Candidate | Seven tests (one line) | Competitor swap (Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU, UW, a national online program) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stories where EWU hands the real work to people who were about to rule themselves out.** (T1, sharpened) | Insightful yes (the truth is its engine); Global yes for every EWU market; Creative: hands-over moments are dramatic and specific; Complete: functional (the work), emotional (being trusted), higher order (mobility); Fluid: film, social, landing pages, nurture email; Simple: one verb, "hands"; Long-lived: every program and year has a new handover. | Gonzaga and Whitworth sell selectivity and leadership, so "people about to rule themselves out" is off-brand for them; WSU and UW sell scale and rank; national online sells speed and format. The swap feels false for each. Passes. |
| 2 | Stories where EWU graduate students prove they're ready by doing the job first. | Insightful partly (no tension); Global yes; Creative risk of "learn by doing" cliché; Complete weak on emotion; Fluid yes; Simple yes; Long-lived yes. | Gonzaga, Whitworth OTD and any MOT with fieldwork can say it. Fails. |
| 3 | Stories where EWU people build the region's future with their own hands. | Insightful weak; Global regional only; Creative literal polytechnic; Complete strong higher order, thin for the MFA; Fluid yes; Simple yes; Long-lived yes. | WSU and the community colleges can say it; it also duplicates idfive's anthem. Fails as a separate CCI. |
| 4 | Stories where someone stops waiting for permission. | Insightful yes; Global yes; Creative open; Complete weak on functional proof; Fluid yes; Simple yes; Long-lived yes. | Any brand, any category (and a national online program's natural voice). Fails. |

**Recommend 1.** One story for all three programs: the Management student defends a real solution to faculty; the writer edits pages of *Willow Springs*; the OT student treats patients under supervision. Each is a handover to someone who nearly didn't apply. Under the polytechnic it reads as the proof episode: "hands-on," shown at graduate level. Rule it carries: every execution shows a real EWU job and one access fact; "learn by doing" alone fails.

## 5. One cross-program Job to Be Done

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Job** | PERSUADE working adults who are considering a master's but talking themselves out of it TO start an EWU application (inquiry first). |
| **Target** | The broad target in §3; the pilot's paid reach is the active slice of it. (assumption) |
| **Behavior to change** | They delay, or default to the cheapest and fastest online option, because they doubt the degree will change their actual work and doubt they belong. (assumption, consistent with voice-of-customer) |
| **Insight, in their words** | "I'm having a hard time justifying the total investment." (MS Management prospect) · "even considering a fully funded one feels kind of ridiculous to me." (MFA applicant) · "I just want to be an OT." (pre-OT) Combined: *I'm not sure a program like that is for someone like me, and I won't pay for one that doesn't change what I can actually do.* (category voices from public forums, not EWU prospects; small sample) |
| **New belief** | At EWU I'd be doing the real work of my field before I graduate, at a price I can defend. It is for people like me. (fits Promise A) |
| **New behavior** | Request information, then start the application this cycle instead of next year. |
| **Barrier type** | **Aspirational** ("not for me"), answered with the emotional benefit and backed by DNA proof so it never reads as a pep talk. Credential doubt is secondary, carried by the proof object in every piece. |

How the same job plays out:
- **MS in Management:** "not senior enough for an MBA, and is a master's worth it?" becomes "I'll defend a real business solution before I'm even a manager."
- **MFA in Creative Writing:** "funded programs aren't for someone like me" becomes "I'd be editing a real literary magazine, and could compete for a funded TA spot." (TA funding is selective; never imply universal)
- **MOT:** "OT school means huge debt and moving away" becomes "I'd treat patients under supervision, with classes in Spokane, at a public price." (no licensure claim; graduation alone does not confer it)

## 6. Creative ambition: the Bar

**Recommended Bar: FRESH (6)**, with OWNABLE (5) as the floor no piece may drop below. Graduate category ads sit at DEJA VU (4): "100% online", "in as little as", "take the next step", laptop smiles (competitor map). OWNABLE alone beats that. FRESH is reachable inside the pilot's calendar because the CCI gives a new spin (the handover, the proof object) without a long approval cycle; the program's rule of thumb puts work above FRESH at more than six months, which a pilot with leads due this cycle cannot carry. SHOWTIME is worth reserving for the anthem-style film if EWU funds it as incremental scope. (assumption on timing; launch date to confirm)

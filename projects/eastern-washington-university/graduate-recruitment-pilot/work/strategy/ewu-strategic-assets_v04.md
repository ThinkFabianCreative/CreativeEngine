# EWU strategic assets, graduate recruitment (v04)

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · BRAND: EWU graduate programs (MS in Management, MFA in Creative Writing, MOT) · SOURCE SCOPE: PROJECT-LEVEL, proposed by TRAFFIK, not approved by EWU · Author: creative-strategist · 2026-10-26 · Strategy loop, Round 2 (revise + red team). Supersedes v03 (kept).

Labels: "(from …)" is a sourced fact; "(our synthesis)" is our reading of evidence; "(assumption)" is a proposal to confirm. Nothing here is client knowledge until EWU approves it. These are strategic assets, not creative: no lines, names or layouts.

**What changed from v03 and why.** Round 1 found that "proof judged by people in the field" has no source except MOT fieldwork, and the CCI leaned on it. v04 rebuilds the idea on what is sourced: work that is shown, defended, published or practiced under supervision. It also replaces "title" and "standing" with per-program meaning, de-composites the target, simplifies the JTBD, corrects citations, and treats access as something to secure. Full list in the log, Round 2.

## 1. Existing assets to respect

- **"The Region's Polytechnic"**: EWU's live positioning and campaign since September 2024 (idfive; anthem :30 on TV; Merit, 2026 Educational Advertising Awards). Proof device: "hands-on". (from our brand scan)
- **"ALL IN"** creed: Grit, Grace, Gratitude, Greatness. Red #B7142E and white, Swoop, "Eags", #Eagle4Life. (from our brand scan)
- **Mission and vision**: affordable academic excellence, applied learning in high-demand fields, upward social mobility; first-generation and low-income students named. (from EWU's RFP)
- **Known tension**: a RANGE Media headline calls the polytechnic label "in name only"; faculty are skeptical that it misrepresents EWU or erodes the humanities. (from our brand scan)

**Fit.** Everything below sits under The Region's Polytechnic as its graduate proof; it never replaces the line. A graduate idea that holds for writers as well as therapists helps answer the humanities worry. (assumption: EWU MarCom accepts a graduate story line under the master campaign)

## 2. Brand promise

| | Promise | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | EWU exists to hand people the real work of a profession, within reach of those who thought it wasn't for them. | Concrete; carries access. | "Real work" is CWU and WWU territory. |
| **P2** | EWU exists to give capable people the proof that lets their field take them seriously, at a public price within reach. | Starts from capability; links to mobility in EWU's vision. | Needs per-program proof to land. |
| P3 | EWU exists so the region's work is done by people from the region. | Fits "Region's Polytechnic". | Institutional; weak for the national MFA. |

**Recommend P2.** The division of labor is explicit: **EWU makes the proof possible; the field or profession decides what it is worth.** No job, publication, promotion or licensure is implied. What "taken seriously" means, per program (internal shorthand, not copy):

- **MS in Management:** being trusted to lead and decide, on the strength of work you have defended.
- **MFA:** being read as a writer and editor, on the strength of work that has been published or edited in print.
- **MOT:** being trusted with practice, on the strength of supervised clinical work. Licensure is separate and comes after graduation through the profession's own exam.

## 4. Core Creative Idea

**Recommended, C2 (revised):** *Stories where people put what they can do in front of others, defended, in print, or practiced under supervision, before their field counts them as one of its own.*

- **The cross-program word is "counted as one of its own" by the field**, not "title". It holds for a manager, a writer and a therapist, and it leaves recognition with the field, not with EWU. (assumption: test with MFA and MOT prospects)
- **The difference from WGU is inside the sentence:** the proof is shown to people, as an act or object (a defense, a printed page, a supervised session). WGU's sourced language is competency-based and "success is measured by actual learning" (competitor map). How WGU assesses is **not sourced**, so we do not claim WGU uses tests. The idea holds against WGU only if WGU does not market a visible, shown proof. (assumption; live-ad check in §10)

**Proof moments per program** (examples, not a required set; the moment of being judged is the image, not a missing title):
- **MS:** the oral defense of the final portfolio, in front of faculty.
- **MFA:** the proof as an object: the page, the editor's mark-up, the printed *Willow Springs* issue or Willow Springs Books title, the Get Lit! program.
- **MOT:** a session during supervised fieldwork, the supervisor present.

**Seven tests (revised C2):** Insightful Y · Global Y (word now holds for all three; to be tested) · Creative Y · Complete Y · Fluid Y · Simple ~ (longer sentence; the three proofs can be dropped in the brief's short form) · Long-lived Y.

**Swap test** (positioning only; live ads not pulled; competitor map)

| Competitor | Evidence held | C2 revised |
|---|---|---|
| CWU | Institution-wide copy via third-party profiles, not CWU official or grad-specific: "real-world, professional settings" | Holds: setting, not shown proof or recognition by the field. |
| WWU | gradschool.wwu.edu: "hands-on research and practical experience"; MFA "teaching and editorial experience"; homepage "Best Value in the Pacific Northwest" | Holds on general claim; **closest on the MFA**. EWU's edge is named, paid editorial roles; WWU's is funding (see §6). |
| WGU | "pathways to opportunity", competency-based | Holds only if WGU does not market shown proof. Unverified. |
| Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU, UW | Format and duration only; no positioning lines captured | **Not tested.** Assumption that they hold. |

C1 (handover) stays the fallback, only with a named EWU object in every piece. C3 (region) is a targeting flavour, not an idea. C4 (access) is dropped as an idea; public price carries access as a fact.

**Rule the idea carries:** every story rests on a real, checkable EWU proof and implies no job, publication, funding or licensure. MOT students practice under supervision; graduating does not confer licensure.

## 5. Creative target

*Invented portrait, drawn from the broad target, to make the tension human. Not a real person and not a persona for media.* (assumption)

She is 33 and lives in Spokane Valley. For two years she has run the shift: she trains the new hires, fixes the schedule when it breaks, and is the one the floor manager calls. Her title still says "lead". She has priced master's programs twice and closed the tab both times, not because she doubts herself but because she will not spend two years and real money on letters after her name that change nothing on Monday *(do-not-lift: portrait language, not copy)*. Proof she could point to would change that.

**Same tension, other programs (one line each, invented):**
- **MFA:** a writer who already edits everyone's drafts, with nothing in print to show for it.
- **MOT:** a rehab aide who already knows what the patient needs next, and is not yet the one allowed to decide it.

## 6. The one cross-program Job to Be Done

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Job** | PERSUADE working adults considering a master's TO request information from an EWU program. |
| **Target** | The broad target (§3); the pilot's paid reach is its active slice. (assumption) |
| **Behavior to change** | They put it off, or pick the cheapest, fastest online option as a box to tick. (our synthesis) |
| **Insight (our synthesis, strategy not copy)** | Working adults already doing advanced work hold off on a master's because they doubt it will change how others see them enough to justify the cost. |
| **Voices behind it** (category voices, public forums, not EWU prospects; small sample) | "I'm having a hard time justifying the total investment." (r/ESCP, European MiM thread) · "has the masters made getting promoted/a new job that much easier?" (r/managers) · "Only apply to funded programs." (GradCafe) · "I would never advise someone to go to OT school for more than $50k debt." (r/OccupationalTherapy) |
| **New belief** | EWU would give me proof my field can see. |
| **New behavior** | Request information. (Application started is the later measure, not a second behavior.) |
| **Barrier type** | **Primary: credential-as-value**, doubt the degree changes how they are seen, for the cost. Cost is the most frequent theme across all three programs. **Secondary: aspirational** ("not for someone like me", 2–3 mentions, including MS leadership-experience and MS-vs-MBA status worries), carried inside stories, never the subject. **HYPOTHESIS** until EWU prospect voice exists. |

**Per-program barrier lines (price lives here)**
- **MS in Management:** will it count against an MBA, and against cheaper, faster online options such as WGU? Answer: the defended portfolio, public tuition.
- **MFA:** funding comes first. WWU reportedly funds over 95% of admitted MFA students through teaching assistantships (Poets & Writers, third-party, program-supplied, date uncertain; **unverified**). EWU's MFA funding is selective: 7–10 entering awards a year, and admission does not guarantee one. We say so plainly and never imply funding. EWU's answer is not funding but proof: named, paid editorial and publishing roles and work in print.
- **MOT:** debt, distance, master's versus doctorate. Answer: 24 weeks of supervised fieldwork, Spokane classes, public tuition. No licensure claim.

## 7. The Bar

**Commit: OWNABLE.** Graduate category ads sit at DEJA VU: "100% online", "in as little as", "take the next step", laptop smiles (competitor map, from ad thumbnails, not analyzed frame by frame). **Target: FRESH**, if access is secured in time. SHOWTIME is out of the pilot's reach.

**Both OWNABLE and FRESH depend on real proof.** Stock or generated stand-ins for a defense, a magazine or fieldwork drop the work to DEJA VU.

- **Lock access dates at kickoff, with an owner** (account team with EWU Graduate Studies).
- **Access to secure** (not a shot list): consenting students in each program; a defense or its room; printed *Willow Springs* issues and Willow Springs Books titles with their editors; the Catalyst Building; a fieldwork setting.
- **OWNABLE fallback if access slips:** real EWU objects and places plus consenting students only. **MOT:** a simulated or consenting session, no identifiable patients.

Benchmarks: three pieces at FRESH, ideally from outside education, chosen by DesignChamp and WordSmither at the brief stage.

## 8. Go-to-market outline

Across EWU's four journey stages (field guide): **Brand** rides inside program paid social and video (no institutional brand-awareness spend); its job is to make the tension and the proof recognizable to people not yet shopping. **Inquiry**: paid search and social send people to the three program hubs, where the six guides are gated to capture the inquiry. **Application**: email nurture moves inquirers to Application Started, where TRAFFIK's nurture ends (contract 3.F.4). **Yield**: paid retargeting only. Proof shifts from emotional (Brand) to practical (cost, deadlines, requirements). Creative is produced incrementally, starting with Inquiry. (our synthesis)

## 9. Measurement priorities

1. Enrollments and return on ad spend (RFP, proposal).
2. Applications started and submitted, per program (Slate; OTCAS for MOT, in-progress untracked).
3. Cost per qualified inquiry and hub-to-inquiry conversion.
4. Guide-to-application rate, reported only above a minimum volume agreed at kickoff.
5. Test-and-learn: proof-led versus cost-led messages on the hubs; the tension against category-standard ads. (assumption: design set with EWU)

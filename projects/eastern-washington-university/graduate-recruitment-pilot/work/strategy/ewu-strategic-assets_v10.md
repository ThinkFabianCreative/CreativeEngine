Also still barred: "doing", "hands-on" (Sullivan, WWU), "real world" (CWU), "are you in" (Fayetteville State).

**What agency must never become (rules for the whole document):**
1. Not validation: no audience, panel, field or boss is the payoff. Others may be present; they are never the point.
2. Tension with the culture of waiting, never bitterness at a person (Andres, 2026-10-26; replaces "no grievance"). The enemy is the waiting itself: "next cycle", wait your turn, the unwritten rule. Cultural tension is welcome (it is how work climbs the ladder). Never a named, pictured or mocked boss or employer; never revenge or walkout. Reference for the dial: *Hamilton* — Burr's "Wait for It" vs Hamilton's "My Shot": two ways of living, neither a villain (reference only; the show's songs, lyrics and imagery are not used).
3. Not an outcome: the move is enrolling and building the work. No promotion, job, publication, funding or licensure is implied.
4. Credible for a degree: **EWU gives the means; she makes the move.** A degree is not a shortcut around anyone; she can start it without anyone's sign-off.

## 4. Core Creative Idea (v08: Andres's key idea)

**CCI:** *Stories where people stop waiting to be chosen.* (7 words). **Campaign line, on purpose:** "Stop waiting to be chosen." Conditions at the top of this file.

**The gap (brief it next to the CCI every time):** they are already capable, and they are waiting to be chosen: for the tap, the nod, the promotion, waiting for an employer to make it their idea, the feeling of being ready. (Employer tuition support stays a welcome means, never framed as the wait.) The hinge is the moment she stops waiting and chooses herself. EWU is where that choice becomes a concrete step.

**Gloss (not the CCI):** waiting to be chosen sounds like "next cycle", "big shoes to fill", "more experience first", and her own "maybe next year" (Andres). Stopping looks like a dated act at EWU (ex-K2; "timeline" phrasing removed as copy-like): a start term, a first workshop, a defense date, a first fieldwork week. EWU supplies the dates and the work; she chooses to start.

- **Chosen by whom does not matter; no one is the villain.** The idea is about her stopping, not about the people who did not choose her.
- **Stopping is not rejecting.** She does not quit, rebel or prove anyone wrong. She stops making the wait her plan.
- **The degree is the means, not the chooser.** EWU does not "choose" her either: admission language must not turn EWU into the new gatekeeper ("we choose you"). Note the MFA teaching awards are selective; never imply EWU funding is her being chosen.
- **"Timeline" in the gloss means career and life, never study pace** (no "self-paced", "in as little as").
- **The told-to-wait phrases are setup, never rebutted.**

**Proof moments per program** (examples of her first dated act, not a shot list; others may be in frame, the camera is on her choice):
- **MS:** the defended portfolio on her own desk, a date on it she picked.
- **MFA:** her own draft in progress with a start date pinned above it; her editorial mark-up at *Willow Springs* as second beat only. No acceptance letter, no award, no publication of her own.
- **MOT:** her hands and tools on her first fieldwork day, the date in frame; supervisor in background as practice, no nod; no patient identifiable.

**Surviving devices (v09, from the v08 visual test and gate; strategic guardrails, not executions):**
- **The saved/taped chair she walks past.** The seat she has been waiting to be assigned. She never sits in it (being chosen), never tears the tape (aimed at a person); it is never shown who taped it; nobody watches her pass. The chair stays, she leaves frame. Never a seat EWU gives her.
- **"The title stays, she moves."** The title (badge, "lead") is a dignified object she leaves in place; no promotion implied. "Watch" is dropped (it made the title an audience). The 2026-10-26 print BET THE PITCH was given under the recognition strategy and is **withdrawn** until a compatible line passes WordSmither's re-test.
- **The Room: setting only.** Real EWU rooms (Catalyst, Get Lit!, the MS defense room) are where her dated act happens; nobody watching is the point.
- **Retired:** "Somebody's going to see this" (validation).

**Seven tests (exact wording "Stories where people stop waiting to be chosen."):**
- **Insightful ~.** It rings true (Andres; 2 thin voices on not having enough experience or not feeling eligible), but EWU-prospect evidence is missing (§6).
- **Global Y.** Manager, writer and therapist all wait to be chosen; no field term needed.
- **Creative Y.** The chair, the calendar, the tap that never comes, the line of people waiting all live here.
- **Complete ~ to Y.** On its own it does not say what replaces the waiting or where EWU fits. The gap and gloss carry that, so brief them with the CCI every time.
- **Fluid Y.** Paid social to hub to nurture.
- **Simple Y.** 7 words, one thought, plain.
- **Long-lived Y.** Waiting to be picked is lasting, not a fad.

## 5. Creative target (portrait kept; tension reframed)

*Invented portrait, drawn from the broad target, to make the tension human. Not a real person and not a persona for media.* (assumption)

She is 33 and lives in Spokane Valley. For two years she has run the shift: she trains the new hires, fixes the schedule when it breaks, and is the one the floor manager calls. Her title still says "lead". At her last review she heard "next cycle" again. She has priced master's programs twice and closed the tab both times, half waiting for someone at work to tap her, back her, tell her it's time *(do-not-lift: portrait language, not copy)*. **Her tension: waiting to be chosen versus moving herself.** The move is enrolling, on a date she picks, at a price she can carry. She does not need to quit, resent anyone, or prove anyone wrong.

**Same tension, other programs (one line each, invented):**
- **MFA:** a writer who already edits other people's work and keeps waiting to feel "real" enough to apply. Her object: the pile of other people's manuscripts on her desk, her own draft underneath.
- **MOT:** a rehab aide who already knows what the patient needs next, and keeps hearing from people around her that she should get a few more years in first. **Checked (v09):** EWU MOT admission asks for 40 hours of qualifying experience, 10 observing an OT, not years (field guide, MOT application path, reviewed 2026-10-20); "a few more years" is what people tell her, never a requirement. Her object: her own dated day-one schedule and her tools (gait belt, goniometer) laid out (sim lab; no patients; no sign-off).

## 8. Go-to-market outline

Across EWU's four journey stages (field guide): **Brand** rides inside program paid social and video (no institutional brand-awareness spend); its job is to make the tension and the proof recognizable to people not yet shopping. **Inquiry**: paid search and social send people to the three program hubs, where the six guides are gated to capture the inquiry. **Application**: email nurture moves inquirers to Application Started, where TRAFFIK's nurture ends (contract 3.F.4). **Yield**: paid retargeting only. Proof shifts from emotional (Brand) to practical (cost, deadlines, requirements). Creative is produced incrementally, starting with Inquiry. (our synthesis)

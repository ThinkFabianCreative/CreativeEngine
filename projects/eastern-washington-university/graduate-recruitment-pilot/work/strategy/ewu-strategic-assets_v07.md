# EWU strategic assets, graduate recruitment (v07)

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · BRAND: EWU graduate programs (MS in Management, MFA in Creative Writing, MOT) · SOURCE SCOPE: PROJECT-LEVEL, proposed by TRAFFIK, not approved by EWU · Author: creative-strategist · 2026-10-26 · Strategy loop, Round 3 (after Andres's redirect). Supersedes v06 (kept).

Labels: "(from …)" is a sourced fact; "(our synthesis)" is our reading of evidence; "(assumption)" is a proposal to confirm; "(Andres)" is the CD's practitioner insight, not external evidence. Nothing here is client knowledge until EWU approves it. These are strategic assets, not creative: no lines, names or layouts.

**What changed from v06 and why.** Andres, 2026-10-26: "It shouldn't be about validation from peers." He chose AGENCY: stop waiting to be chosen; move on your own terms; take control of your timeline (reviews/2026-10-25_clearances.md). v07 rewrites only what that touches: the human truth and gap, the promise, the CCI, the creative target's tension, the JTBD insight, belief and barrier, and the parts of the Bar and measurement that rested on "in front of others". Everything else is carried from v06 unchanged.

**One agency term.** Internal strategy term throughout: **"her own move"** (not copy). Retired: "counts them in", "in front of others", "proof my field can see". The recognition idea is gone; the work she builds stays, as hers, not as something shown to win approval.

**Do-not-lift (finished cadence; brief as tension, never as a line):** "stop waiting to be chosen" (Andres's direction words); "nobody hands you the promotion"; the CCI sentence; the new belief. Also still barred: "doing", "hands-on", "real world" (Sullivan, CWU, WWU), "are you in" (Fayetteville State).

**What agency must never become (rules for the whole document):**
1. Not validation: no audience, panel, field or boss is the payoff. Others may be present; they are never the point.
2. Not grievance: employers and bosses are not villains. "Told to wait" is a condition she lives in, not an enemy to beat.
3. Not an outcome: the move is enrolling and building the work. No promotion, job, publication, funding or licensure is implied.
4. Credible for a degree: **EWU gives the means; she makes the move.** A degree is not a shortcut around anyone; it is a step she can take without asking.

## 2. Brand promise (revised)

| | Promise | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | EWU exists so capable people stop waiting for permission to take their next step. | Short; pure agency. | Says nothing EWU gives; any school, or a job change, could claim it. |
| **A2** | EWU exists to give capable people the means to make their own next move, on their own timeline, at a public price within reach. | Splits the roles cleanly (EWU means, her move); carries access; ties to upward mobility in EWU's vision. | "On their own timeline" sits near online "self-paced" category language; must mean career timeline, never study pace. |
| A3 | EWU exists so the people the region keeps telling "not yet" can decide "now" for themselves. | Strongest tension; regional. | Leans on the told-to-wait phrases (thin evidence, §6) and edges toward grievance and WGU "They Said". |

**Recommend A2.** Division of labor: **EWU gives the means (the program, the work she builds, a public price); she makes the move; nobody, EWU included, promises what happens after.** What "her own move" means, per program (internal shorthand, not copy):

- **MS in Management:** she stops waiting to be put in charge and builds a defended portfolio of management work that is hers.
- **MFA:** she stops waiting to be picked as a writer or editor and spends two years writing, with editorial and publishing work done for others. No publication of her own is implied.
- **MOT:** she stops waiting to be the one who decides care and starts the path, through 24 weeks of supervised fieldwork. Licensure is separate and comes after graduation through the profession's own exam.

## 4. Core Creative Idea (revised)

**The gap (plain strategy, brief it next to the CCI every time):** they are already capable; they have been told to wait, by others and by themselves. The hinge is the moment the waiting stops being the plan. Without the gap the idea is a generic "take charge" message; with it, it has bite.

**Candidates**

| | CCI | Note |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | Stories where people stop waiting to be chosen. | Purest; Andres's own words, so do-not-lift; says nothing about what replaces the waiting. |
| **K2** | Stories where people stop living on someone else's timeline and start their own. | The told-to-wait phrases ("next cycle", "not yet") are someone else's clock; the move is hers. Concrete, dateable. |
| K3 | Stories where "not ready yet" gets answered by the person it was said to. | Strongest tension; reads as rebuttal, too close to WGU "They Said", risks grievance. |
| K4 | Stories where the next step is taken, not given. | Compact; "taken" can read as grabbing from someone (grievance), and "next step" is category filler ("take the next step"). |

**Recommend K2:** *Stories where people stop living on someone else's timeline and start their own.* (14 words)

**Gloss (not the CCI):** someone else's timeline is "next cycle", "big shoes to fill", "more experience first" and her own "maybe next year" (Andres). Her own timeline starts with a dated act at EWU: a start term, a first workshop, a defense date, a first fieldwork week. EWU supplies the dates and the work; she chooses to start.

- **The hinge is a date she sets, not a verdict she receives.** That is the difference from v06 and from every neighbour below.
- **"Timeline" means her career and life, never study pace.** Never "self-paced", "at your own speed", "in as little as": that is the online category (Whitworth "online self-paced", competitor map; "in as little as" in category ads, §7).
- **The told-to-wait phrases are setup, never the subject and never rebutted.** No "they were wrong", no proving anyone wrong.

**Proof moments per program** (examples of her first dated act, not a shot list; others may be in frame but the camera is on her choice, not their reaction):
- **MS:** the defended portfolio on her own desk, a date on it she picked; the defense is a milestone on her calendar, not an audience.
- **MFA:** her own pages in progress; her editorial mark-up on someone else's page at *Willow Springs*. Never her own publication.
- **MOT:** her first fieldwork week, supervisor present as a fact of practice, not a judge.

**Seven tests (K2):** Insightful ~ (rests on Andres's insight; external evidence thin, §6) · Global Y (holds for manager, writer, therapist; no field-recognition term needed) · Creative Y (the clock, the calendar, the chair saved "for later" all live here) · Complete Y with the gap · Fluid Y (paid social to hub to nurture) · Simple Y (14 words, one thought) · Long-lived Y (waiting to be chosen is not a fad).

## 5. Creative target (portrait kept; tension reframed)

*Invented portrait, drawn from the broad target, to make the tension human. Not a real person and not a persona for media.* (assumption)

She is 33 and lives in Spokane Valley. For two years she has run the shift: she trains the new hires, fixes the schedule when it breaks, and is the one the floor manager calls. Her title still says "lead". At her last review she heard "next cycle" again. She has priced master's programs twice and closed the tab both times, half waiting for someone at work to tap her, back her, tell her it's time *(do-not-lift: portrait language, not copy)*. **Her tension: waiting to be chosen versus moving herself.** Nobody is coming with the tap. The move is enrolling, on a date she picks, at a price she can carry. She does not need to quit, resent anyone, or prove anyone wrong.

**Same tension, other programs (one line each, invented):**
- **MFA:** a writer who already edits other people's work and keeps waiting to feel "real" enough to apply.
- **MOT:** a rehab aide who already knows what the patient needs next, and keeps being told to get a few more years in first.

## 7. The Bar (changed where it rested on "in front of others")

**Commit: OWNABLE.** Graduate category ads sit at DEJA VU: "100% online", "in as little as", "take the next step", laptop smiles (competitor map, from thumbnails). **Target: FRESH**, if access is secured in time. SHOWTIME is out of the pilot's reach.

**Both depend on real EWU material.** Stock or generated stand-ins drop the work to DEJA VU.

- **Lock access dates at kickoff, with an owner** (account team with EWU Graduate Studies). **Also ask for real program dates** (term starts, application deadlines, defense windows, fieldwork start): under K2 the date is part of the idea.
- **Access to secure** (not a shot list): consenting students in each program; the defended portfolio and its defense window; *Willow Springs* issues, Willow Springs Books titles and student editors' mark-up; the Catalyst Building; a fieldwork setting.
- **Clinical partner site permission (MOT)**, its own item, unchanged.
- **OWNABLE fallback if access slips:** real EWU objects and places plus consenting students; the objects must carry **her act and her date** (her portfolio, her mark-up, her fieldwork tools, a real calendar date), not the act of being judged. A building alone is generic. MOT: simulated or consenting session, no identifiable patients.
- **Agency check on every piece:** if the audience's reaction is the payoff, it has slid back to validation; if a boss looks foolish, it is grievance.

Benchmarks: unchanged (Monash "A Future Without Change", USMC "Battles Won", per Andres 2026-10-26; DesignChamp and WordSmither may add).

## 8. Go-to-market outline

Across EWU's four journey stages (field guide): **Brand** rides inside program paid social and video (no institutional brand-awareness spend); its job is to make the tension and the proof recognizable to people not yet shopping. **Inquiry**: paid search and social send people to the three program hubs, where the six guides are gated to capture the inquiry. **Application**: email nurture moves inquirers to Application Started, where TRAFFIK's nurture ends (contract 3.F.4). **Yield**: paid retargeting only. Proof shifts from emotional (Brand) to practical (cost, deadlines, requirements). Creative is produced incrementally, starting with Inquiry. (our synthesis)

## 9. Measurement priorities (item 5 and 6 reworded)

1. Enrollments and return on ad spend (RFP, proposal).
2. Applications started and submitted, per program (Slate; OTCAS for MOT, in-progress untracked).
3. Cost per qualified inquiry and hub-to-inquiry conversion.
4. Guide-to-application rate, reported only above a minimum volume agreed at kickoff.
5. Test-and-learn: agency-led versus cost-led messages on the hubs; the tension against category-standard ads. (assumption: design set with EWU)
6. First paid-social test (assumption: design set with EWU; owner: TRAFFIK media + CreativeStrategist): three cells on Meta, same audience, budget and offer. (a) single image: her, with her own work and a real date in frame, attention on the work, not on an audience; (b) static carousel: the move built across cards, from waiting to a dated start (card one must work alone); (c) category-standard control: smiling student, campus backdrop. Primary metric: cost per inquiry; secondary: click-through and swipe-through. Engagement alone does not decide. Run to the minimum volume before reading.

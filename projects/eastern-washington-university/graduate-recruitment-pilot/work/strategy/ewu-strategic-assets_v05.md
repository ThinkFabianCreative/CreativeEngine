## 2. Brand promise

| | Promise | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | EWU exists to hand people the real work of a profession, within reach of those who thought it wasn't for them. | Concrete; carries access. | "Real work" is CWU and WWU territory. |
| **P2** | EWU exists to give capable people the proof that gets their field to count them in, at a public price within reach. | Starts from capability; links to mobility in EWU's vision. | Needs per-program proof to land. |
| P3 | EWU exists so the region's work is done by people from the region. | Fits "Region's Polytechnic". | Institutional; weak for the national MFA. |

**Recommend P2.** The division of labor is explicit: **EWU makes the proof possible; the field or profession decides what it is worth.** No job, publication, promotion or licensure is implied. What "counts them in" means, per program (internal shorthand, not copy):

- **MS in Management:** being trusted to lead and decide, on the strength of work you have defended.
- **MFA:** being trusted as an editor and literary professional, on the strength of editorial and publishing work done for others. No publication of her own is implied.
- **MOT:** being trusted with practice, on the strength of supervised clinical work. Licensure is separate and comes after graduation through the profession's own exam.

## 4. Core Creative Idea

**Recommended, C2:** *Stories where people show what they can do, in front of others, before their field counts them in.*

**The gap (plain strategy, brief it next to the CCI every time):** they are already capable; their field has not counted them in yet. The proof moment is the hinge between the two. Without the gap the idea reads as a process story (students present work); with it, it has bite.

**Gloss (long proof form, not the CCI):** shown in front of others means defended aloud (MS), in editorial and publishing work (MFA), or practiced under supervision (MOT).

- **The cross-program term is "counts them in"**, not "title". It holds for a manager, a writer and a therapist, and it leaves recognition with the field, not with EWU. (assumption: test with MFA and MOT prospects)
- **"In front of others" is load-bearing.** It is the WGU edge; do not cut it in any short form.
- **The difference from WGU is inside the sentence:** the proof is shown to people, as an act or object (a defense, a printed page, a supervised session). WGU's sourced language is competency-based and "success is measured by actual learning" (competitor map). How WGU assesses is **not sourced**, so we do not claim WGU uses tests. The idea holds against WGU only if WGU does not market a visible, shown proof. (assumption; live-ad check in §10)

**Proof moments per program** (examples, not a required set; the moment of being judged is the image, not a missing title):
- **MS:** the oral defense of the final portfolio. Strategy note, not a shot: the defended portfolio is an object in frame, with the same status as the MFA page; a defense room alone reads as any seminar.
- **MFA:** the proof as an object of her editorial work: the editor's mark-up on someone else's page, the *Willow Springs* issue or Willow Springs Books title she helped edit or produce, the Get Lit! program she helped run. Never implies her own publication.
- **MOT:** a session during supervised fieldwork, the supervisor present.

**Seven tests (C2, v05 wording):** Insightful Y · Global ~ (to test: "counts them in" with MFA and MOT prospects) · Creative Y · Complete Y (with the gap beside it) · Fluid Y · Simple Y (17 words, one breath; proofs live in the gloss) · Long-lived Y.

**Rule the idea carries:** every story rests on a real, checkable EWU proof and implies no job, publication, funding or licensure. MOT students practice under supervision; graduating does not confer licensure.

## 5. Creative target

*Invented portrait, drawn from the broad target, to make the tension human. Not a real person and not a persona for media.* (assumption)

She is 33 and lives in Spokane Valley. For two years she has run the shift: she trains the new hires, fixes the schedule when it breaks, and is the one the floor manager calls. Her title still says "lead". She has priced master's programs twice and closed the tab both times, not because she doubts herself but because she will not spend two years and real money on letters after her name that change nothing on Monday *(do-not-lift: portrait language, not copy)*. Proof she could point to would change that.

**Same tension, other programs (one line each, invented):**
- **MFA:** a writer who already edits other people's work, with no editorial credit of her own.
- **MOT:** a rehab aide who already knows what the patient needs next, and is not yet the one allowed to decide it.

## 6. The one cross-program Job to Be Done

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Job** | PERSUADE working adults considering a master's TO request information from an EWU program. |
| **Target** | The broad target (§3); the pilot's paid reach is its active slice. (assumption) |
| **Behavior to change** | They put it off, or pick the cheapest, fastest online option as a box to tick. (our synthesis) |

| **New belief** | At EWU I would build proof that gets my field to count me in. *(do-not-lift)* |
| **New behavior** | Request information. (Application started is the later measure, not a second behavior.) |
| **Barrier type** | **Primary: credential-as-value**, doubt the degree changes how they are seen, for the cost. Cost is the most frequent theme across all three programs. **Secondary: aspirational** ("not for someone like me", 2–3 mentions, including MS leadership-experience and MS-vs-MBA status worries), carried inside stories, never the subject. **HYPOTHESIS** until EWU prospect voice exists. |

**Per-program barrier lines (price lives here)**
- **MS in Management:** will it count against an MBA, and against cheaper, faster online options such as WGU? Answer: the defended portfolio, public tuition.
- **MFA:** funding comes first. WWU's own pages describe TA positions "if awarded", "a limited number" of assistantships, a stipend and a **partial** tuition waiver (gradschool.wwu.edu, fetched 2026-10-26). A third-party figure of over 95% of admitted MFA students funded (Poets & Writers, program-supplied, date uncertain) stays **unverified**; never call WWU's offer full funding. EWU's MFA funding is selective: 7–10 entering awards a year, and admission does not guarantee one. We say so plainly and never imply funding. EWU's answer is proof: four named, paid second-year editorial and publishing roles, and the editorial work they produce.
- **MOT:** debt, distance, master's versus doctorate. Answer: 24 weeks of supervised fieldwork, Spokane classes, public tuition. No licensure claim.

## 7. The Bar

**Commit: OWNABLE.** Graduate category ads sit at DEJA VU: "100% online", "in as little as", "take the next step", laptop smiles (competitor map, from ad thumbnails, not analyzed frame by frame). **Target: FRESH**, if access is secured in time. SHOWTIME is out of the pilot's reach.

**Both OWNABLE and FRESH depend on real proof.** Stock or generated stand-ins for a defense, a magazine or fieldwork drop the work to DEJA VU.

- **Lock access dates at kickoff, with an owner** (account team with EWU Graduate Studies).
- **Access to secure** (not a shot list): consenting students in each program; a defense, its room and the defended portfolio; printed *Willow Springs* issues and Willow Springs Books titles with their student editors; the Catalyst Building; a fieldwork setting.
- **Clinical partner site permission (MOT), its own item:** Level II fieldwork happens at external clinical sites EWU does not control; student and EWU consent do not cover filming there.
- **OWNABLE fallback if access slips:** real EWU objects and places plus consenting students only, and the objects must carry the act of judging (the mark-up, the defended portfolio, fieldwork tools in use), not just the place. A building alone is generic. **MOT:** a simulated or consenting session, no identifiable patients.

Benchmarks: three pieces at FRESH, ideally from outside education, chosen by DesignChamp and WordSmither at the brief stage.

## 8. Go-to-market outline

Across EWU's four journey stages (field guide): **Brand** rides inside program paid social and video (no institutional brand-awareness spend); its job is to make the tension and the proof recognizable to people not yet shopping. **Inquiry**: paid search and social send people to the three program hubs, where the six guides are gated to capture the inquiry. **Application**: email nurture moves inquirers to Application Started, where TRAFFIK's nurture ends (contract 3.F.4). **Yield**: paid retargeting only. Proof shifts from emotional (Brand) to practical (cost, deadlines, requirements). Creative is produced incrementally, starting with Inquiry. (our synthesis)

## 9. Measurement priorities

1. Enrollments and return on ad spend (RFP, proposal).
2. Applications started and submitted, per program (Slate; OTCAS for MOT, in-progress untracked).
3. Cost per qualified inquiry and hub-to-inquiry conversion.
4. Guide-to-application rate, reported only above a minimum volume agreed at kickoff.
5. Test-and-learn: proof-led versus cost-led messages on the hubs; the tension against category-standard ads. (assumption: design set with EWU)

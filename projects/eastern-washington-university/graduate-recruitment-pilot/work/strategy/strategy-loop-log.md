**Questions for creative-researcher** (could change the strategy; proceeding meanwhile on labeled assumptions)
1. Do CWU, WWU or WGU run live graduate ads (Meta, Google) that frame the student by capability ahead of title or credential, or that show a named proof artifact? Assumption meanwhile: no one owns "proof before the title".

# On Ice, MS in Management 30s animatic v12: DesignChamp review

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · MODE: JUDGE (art direction, visual craft) · Reviewed: 2026-11-08 from frames every 0.5s of `EWU_OnIce_MS_30s_animatic_v12.mp4`. Animatic only; final is shot practically. The locked direction is not reopened here.

## What works
- 6–9 hand grip: the frost climbing over the knuckles while the rejection plays is the best shot in the film. The politeness reads as cold.
- 17.5–20 hammerhead orbit: a red body with a steel face and ice spray, centered and heroic. It is the clearest "red tool = university" frame.
- 20–23.5 hips break: khaki trousers fracturing out of a clear block. She reads INSIDE the ice here, which is the principle working.
- Captions on the memories (0–15) stay in one lower-third position at one size, and the rejection lines read as kind.
- End card is restrained: black field, one line, wordmark, offer.

## Top problems, ranked
1. **Red tool continuity breaks at 9–12.** The impact sledge has a chrome/steel head on a red handle, while 17.5–20 shows a red head. The 12–15 crack shot shows only a red shaft. Three different hammers in eight seconds.
2. **Inside vs behind the ice fails at the reveal (15–17.5).** The wide shows her standing in a waist-high block, swinging the hammer from above. She reads as a person behind or in a tub of ice, not as a Self Made Man figure carved out of it. The climax image of the idea is missing, and she's shown wielding the tool before we've seen her trapped.
3. **Reading order: the ice-sculptor misdirect never sets up.** 0–3 is abstract grey texture with dark verticals (legs?), and 3–6 is a chisel on a plain cube. Nothing says "sculpture of a person" before the reveal, so the reveal has nothing to overturn.
4. **The walk-away is missing (23.5–25.9).** Chunks on a wet floor, then a splash. We never see her step out and leave, which is the payoff.
5. **Pacing.** The 9–15 impact and crack beats repeat each other (two "hit and spray" shots), and they eat the time the reveal and walk need. The narrator line runs over two shots and a cut to the orbit, so the climax line lands on a product shot, not on her.
6. **Caption placement drifts.** Lower third (0–12), italic lower right (12–15, her inner voice "Maybe next year / When I'm ready"), top (15–20), lower center ("Not anymore."). Three positions and two styles inside 25 seconds. Italic for her own voice is a good idea, but it should hold one position.
7. **End card craft.** About 0.5s of pure black at 25.9 before the line. "EASTERN WASHINGTON UNIVERSITY" is tiny tracked red caps under a larger white line, so the brand is the weakest element on the card. The offer line and the legal line are too small for mobile. Red in the wordmark is defensible (red = the university) but should be the only red, and it should be legible.

## Verdict
**REWORK.** The hand, orbit and hips shots are worth keeping, and the idea holds. But the climax image (her carved inside the ice) and the walk-away aren't on screen yet, and the red tool changes between shots. Fix shots 1, 2, 4, 6 and 9 and the 15–17.5 reveal, then re-cut. It should advance after that.

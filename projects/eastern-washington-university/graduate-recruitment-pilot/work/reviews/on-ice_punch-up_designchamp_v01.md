CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · SOURCE SCOPE: PROJECT-LEVEL, internal · Author: design-champ (Marketing design) · 2026-11-08 · Mode: DEVELOP · Frameworks: barry-concept-art-direction (rough test, who carries the idea, reading order, premise vs template), visual-craft (composition, color jobs, real-condition checks). Reviewed: MS, MFA, MOT v2 16:9, cast_contact, both hand-mouse top-downs. Amber and MOT v3 skipped as instructed.

## 1. What's working
- The concept sentence reads cold: a working adult breaks herself out of the block, mid-swing. Nobody frees her. The rough test passes.
- Single top spotlight with dark negative space on the right gives the headline a stage.
- Program props carry the specifics without any copy: MS lanyard/badge, MFA manuscript pages frozen in the ice, MOT gait belt. Keep all three.
- Red sits only on her tool. Color job is right.
- Hand v2 (real hand out of a cracked slab, crimson hair tie, mouse) is the right support shot. Ice hand v1 reads as "frozen while clicking", the opposite of the idea.

## 2. Punch-ups, ranked
1. **She is standing in a glass tube, not carved from ice.** This is the biggest gap from Self Made Man. In Carlyle the figure and the block are one material; here the ice is a vitrine around trousers. The legs should be ice turning into her at mid-thigh, with the break line on her body. *Artlist edit:* "Her legs below mid-thigh are solid ice, the same block, rough-chiselled, merging seamlessly into her clothes at the thigh; no visible legs or fabric inside the ice." Production: practical block + composite, retouch the transition seam.
2. **Ice is too clean and glassy.** The brief says bubbly, slightly cloudy, real. Clear ice reads as glass/CGI and as "showcase", not "kept for later". *Edit:* "Ice is dense, cloudy white at the core, full of trapped air bubbles and frost, clear only at the cracked edges." Production: real block ice, not clear sculpture ice.
3. **Scale and crop: she is too small for a feed.** On a phone the figure is ~25% of the frame and the debris field eats the bottom third. Push in. *Edit:* "Tighter framing: figure fills two thirds of the frame height, block cropped at the bottom edge, less floor debris." Build 4:5 and 9:16 first; 16:9 is the stretch, not the master.
4. **The moment is the windup, not the break.** Mallet raised plus a pre-made spray is ambiguous. Pick the frame a hundredth after impact: one big slab peeling off her thigh, shards in the air, mallet at the end of the follow-through. *Edit:* "Moment just after impact: a large slab of ice breaking away from her thigh, mid-air, chisel buried in the crack." Production: high-speed strobe on the practical shoot.
5. **Series reads as one template.** Same plate, same splash, same debris in every program and every cast. Barry: a campaign premise, not a template. Keep the spotlight and column; vary the stage of the break per program (MFA: pages bursting out with the slab; MOT: break at the knee, step forward; MS: almost free). Retouch/production job.
6. **Type lockup.** Headline lives in the dark right third, top-aligned with her eyeline so the read is face, line, mallet, chisel, CTA. The crimson in the line or date is the only other red; tie it to the tool. Put the year ("since 2022") in crimson, nothing else. Test 9:16 with the headline above her head, not beside.
7. **Motion for Meta.** The idea is a cut: 0–1s still, ice, silence; 1s crack sound and first strike; 2–4s slab falls, headline lands. Loopable 6s. Shoot it practical; AI animatic only for the pitch.
Smaller: mallet head is pink/silver in MS/MFA, make it crimson #B7142E; lose the generic sparkle spray.

## 3. Bolder alternate frame
**Pull back: a row of blocks.** Same studio, same light, eight frozen working adults in identical blocks in a line, still, waiting. One block, hers, mid-break and lit. She is the only one moving. It dramatizes "waiting to be chosen" (the line-up) and her refusal in one frame, without blaming anyone. Risk: the others must read as on hold, not as losers; keep their faces neutral and turned away.

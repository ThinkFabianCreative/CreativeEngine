# DEVELOP copy v02: The Room (with Before the Title as hub and guide system)

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · BRAND: EWU graduate programs (MS in Management, MFA in Creative Writing, Master of Occupational Therapy) · SOURCE SCOPE: project-level · AUTHOR: word-smither · DATE: 2026-10-26 · MODE: DEVELOP · STATUS: for creative-engine-director critique; nothing here is EWU-approved.

Built from: copy/develop_A-red-mark_C-before-title_copy_v01.md (unchanged), reviews/develop_room_review_v01.md (required fixes), concepts/develop_A-red-mark_B-room_visual_v01.md (card order), brief/creative-brief_v19.md (claims). Placeholder names ([Name]) are slots for consenting students.

## 0. Rules (v01 rules stand, plus these)

- Campaign line **"Somebody's going to see this."** runs only over a room image. Never in search, text-only or subject-line placements without the room.
- "Pick the room you want to impress" lives on the MFA hub and in email only.
- "Do it in front of people" appears on end cards only.
- No "thirty-one classmates" anywhere. MOT says "your cohort"; a real headcount appears only in a photo caption, taken from the session photographed.
- Peers, not judges: no "a panel" as the centre of a line, no admissions committee as a room, no "What the panel will ask".
- MS shows panel and cohort only until EWU confirms who may attend defenses. No colleagues, guests or public.
- MOT: no "patient". Sim lab, classmate scenarios.
- Card one: 6 words or fewer.
- Off the copy: "count(s) in", "are you in", "show your work".

## 2. Carousels (DesignChamp's order)

1 watchers (hook) · 2 you in the seat, watching someone else · 3 the reverse: now you stand there · 4 the work, credited by role · 5 fact + request information (red end card).

3. Now it's your slide on the screen.

5. MS in Management at EWU. No management experience required. A public university. Do it in front of people. **Request information.**

**MFA in Creative Writing**

5. MFA in Creative Writing at EWU. No GRE; your writing sample decides. Selective teaching assistantships include a tuition waiver and stipend. **Request information.**

(Card 4 caption "Student editor, Willow Springs" only for a student who holds the role. Card 3 shows her reading another writer's work she championed, never her own publication.)

**Master of Occupational Therapy**

2. Your classmate guides the transfer. You watch.

([n] is the real number from the photographed session. Card 4 "Level I skills session" in EWU wording once confirmed.)

## Who approves what
- **Graduate Studies** approves the strategy and the work.
- **Marketing and Communications** approves anything touching the brand.
- **Program directors** confirm that every fact about their program is accurate.

## If EWU doesn't approve the core idea
The core idea runs only after EWU approves it at the Nov 9 presentation. If EWU rejects it, the program guides and program pages still answer the four practical questions every prospect asks: is this for me, can I afford it, can I get in, and why EWU. Nothing else in the plan depends on the idea.

## Why this idea
- **The pattern we found:** good candidates rule themselves out before EWU sees them. They ask themselves one question and assume the answer is no.
- **MS in Management:** "I don't have enough leadership experience to be in the MBA program." They feel too junior for the MBA and suspect the alternative is second-best.
- **MFA in Creative Writing:** "Only apply to funded programs." Some strong writers say even applying "feels kind of ridiculous." What they want is "a writing community to help me improve my craft."
- **Master of Occupational Therapy:** "You do not need a doctorate to be an occupational therapist," but "all the OT programs are so expensive," and many want to stay near their support system.
- **How EWU's brand becomes the creative:** EWU positions itself as the Region's Polytechnic, where students turn classroom knowledge into real-world impact through hands-on learning rooted in the liberal arts. Our core idea is a creative expression of that position; we keep the phrase itself out of campaign copy.
- **How EWU's mission becomes the creative:** EWU's own words (affordable academic excellence, applied learning in high-demand fields, upward mobility, first-generation students) are usually stated. We propose showing them. Our core idea: stories where the real work is handed to people who didn't think it was meant for them. Each program has real work to show: defending a business solution to faculty, editing a literary magazine, treating patients under supervision. The story is the person who almost ruled themselves out, and the access EWU gave them is the proof.

## The decisions we need from EWU
### Brand promise
- **Our recommendation:** "EWU exists to put affordable academic excellence and applied work in high-demand fields within reach of people who'd ruled themselves out." Built from EWU's mission and vision wording.
- **If EWU prefers its own:** we adopt it and check the core idea against it. If the core idea is not approved, program guides and hubs still work on their own.
### MS in Management format and name
- **Our recommendation:** fully online, 37 quarter credits, named "MS in Management," as the contract, public page and catalog currently agree.
- **If it is hybrid or renamed:** the media mix, ad copy and landing page change. We need this before MS creative starts. We also need one real project and defense EWU is comfortable showing.
### MFA format and deadline
- **Our recommendation:** describe the MFA as taught almost entirely at EWU Spokane, which is what the program's own page says. Teaching assistants teach their composition course in Cheney. Applications close early March 2027; we assume this from the pattern of past years, since the program page still shows the 2025 date. Describe funding as "seven to ten teaching assistantships a year" with a full tuition waiver and a stipend of about $9,000 to $10,000.
- **If any of these differ:** the calendar and the funding language change. Funding must stay accurate, so we need the 2027 deadline and the award figures confirmed.
### MOT fieldwork wording
- **Our recommendation:** "two full-time Level II placements at clinical sites across the Inland Northwest; may require travel or temporary housing." Never "in Spokane."
- **If EWU wants "without leaving Spokane" or "the only public master's route in Spokane":** we need written sign-off, because both are claims prospects will test.
### Success measures (KPIs)
- **Our recommendation, directional:** measure enrollments first, then applications, then cost per inquiry. Starting points: MOT, 96 or more complete, prerequisite-met applications for 32 seats; MFA, 120 or more applications for 24 seats; MS, inquiries at twice the current baseline. Final targets set after the Slate baseline. EWU publishes only a university-wide goal (new-student enrollment up half a percent a year), so we need graduate targets from EWU.
- **If EWU prefers volume targets only:** we will hit volume, but lead quality, which the RFP asks for, becomes harder to prove.
### Journey maps
- **Our recommendation:** four maps, one per persona (MS early-career, MS career changer, MFA, MOT), each staged by Slate stage.
- **If EWU prefers one per program or per stage:** workable, but the two MS audiences have different barriers and would blur.
### Landing pages
- **Our recommendation:** one hub per program (three), with sub-sections and tailored variants that count toward the contract's "at least 12 program landing pages."
- **If EWU wants 12 separate pages:** we build them. EWU staff will have more to maintain, and each page will be thinner.
### Permission to show the real work
- **Our recommendation:** MFA (Willow Springs pages, Get Lit! materials, cleared alumni names) is cleared to proceed. Using an alumna such as Lauren Hohle, now Managing Editor of The Gettysburg Review, still needs her own consent. We ask permission to show an MS defense record and an MOT placement record, without naming clinical sites.
- **If not granted:** we use clearly labeled illustrative versions, which are weaker proof.
### Alumni and faculty participation
- **Our recommendation:** invite named MFA alumni and faculty to appear, with each person's consent per production; one faculty member per program for MS and MOT.
- **If they cannot take part:** the work leans on the objects. It still works, with less warmth.

## What we still need from EWU
- **Typography:** EWU names no brand font publicly and suggests Arial as the closest system font. We need the brand typeface from Marketing and Communications.
- **Accessibility level:** we plan for WCAG 2.2 AA. Please confirm that is EWU's standard.
- **Ad approval turnaround:** an agreed number of days for written consent, so the MOT last call can run.
- **MFA 2027 deadline:** we assume early March, by pattern. Please confirm.
- **MOT fieldwork sites:** where students are placed, or confirmation we should describe them only as regional and often rural.
- **MS format and start terms:** online or hybrid, and which terms students can start.
- **A real MS defense story** we can show, with the student's consent.
- **Graduate success targets** for enrollments, applications and inquiries.
- **Social insight:** we could only see part of EWU's social pages without logging in. A logged-in review by EWU staff, or EWU's own analytics, would tell us what already works.

## Creative considerations
- **The proof-object approach:** each program is shown through a real thing a student makes or holds before graduating, with people shown doing the job, not being selected.
- **What it could look like (illustrative only, not final copy):** MFA, a Willow Springs masthead or page with a first-year student's name on it. MS, the record of a defense in front of faculty. MOT, a Level II placement record with the Catalyst Building in view as the place students study, never as the fieldwork site.
- **Fitting beside EWU's own voice:** EWU's social posts are warm and proud, call students "Eags," and celebrate named students' achievements. Our work should feel at home next to them, while being more specific about the work students do.
- **Sign-off direction:** "Before you graduate, you'll have…" followed by a fact, never a promise.
- **Lesson films (optional add-on):** short, premium "lesson" films taught by EWU people inside the real work ("Lesson 1: read 400 submissions"). This is incremental to the contracted scope and estimated separately.
- **Where to start:** the MFA. Its proof objects are cleared, its people are known, and its strength (working inside a magazine, a press and a festival) belongs to EWU.
- **Clichés we will avoid:** "100% online," "flexible," "take the next step," "find your voice," "make a difference," "hands-on"; ranking badges; smiling professional at a laptop; notebook and coffee; scrubs and therapy balls. Any ad Gonzaga, Whitworth, UW or a national online program could run unchanged gets cut.

## Strategic considerations
- **The MS insight is a working assumption.** We found few online voices on this degree, mostly European. We will validate it in the 45 to 60 planned interviews before developing MS creative.
- **"Only" and Spokane claims** run only with EWU's written sign-off.
- **MFA funding** reads "selective teaching awards," never "funded"; prospects hear "funded" as universal.
- **MOT cost:** we describe the cost worry prospects voice without quoting any figure, and we do not state a total program cost in the work.
- **Timing:** proposal presentation Nov 9; MOT applications close Nov 15, 2 PM PT (a last call only; the main MOT push is the 2027–28 cycle); MS winter start Dec 15; MFA applications close early March 2027 (to confirm).

## The problem
Prospects for all three degrees shortlist on one filter (cost, funding, or whether the credential "counts"), and EWU looks like the regional default on it, so they inquire elsewhere or stall (VoC; CM). EWU's graduate programs have had no centralized marketing "in recent years"; it wants a "pull-based" model with "high-quality" leads (RFP 1.1) that its staff can run alone (CT 2.A). Term Sep 2026–Aug 2027 (CT §2).

## Platform: one school, three chapters
- **Brand promise:** MISSING. ⚑ Ask EWU for brand guidelines.
- **Core Creative Idea (PROPOSED ⚑):** *Stories where the real work is handed to people who didn't think it was meant for them.*
- **Not chosen:** B "Stories where the good option stops being for someone else": no job, fails Complete. C "Stories where people told to wait start the real work now, at public cost": three thoughts, reads as a tagline; its "now, at public cost" survives as an access-fact pattern inside chapters.
- **Universal Human Truth:** People rule themselves out of the good option (too junior, not good enough, can't afford it) long before anyone else does.
- **Access inside the idea:** built from EWU's own words: "affordable academic excellence" (mission); "applied learning in high-demand fields" and "upward social mobility" (vision); "first-generation, low-income, and other structurally marginalized students" (values; RFP 1.1). The work is the proof; the person who thought they weren't the candidate is the story. Not a brand campaign: PR excludes institutional awareness spend and "Why EWU" brochures, so the platform rides inside program work.
- **Chapters (job + access, one thought):**
  - **MS:** Defend a real business solution to faculty before anyone has called you a manager (MNGT 591 oral defense; no management experience required).
  - **MFA:** Edit a literary magazine on the strength of your pages, not your test scores (Willow Springs; writing sample weighs most, no GRE).
  - **MOT:** 24+ weeks treating patients under supervision, at a public university's price (FG s.29, s.32). No cost comparisons ⚑.
- **Starting direction (Andres, CD) — not a prescribed execution:** MasterClass-style lessons, each taught by an EWU person inside the real job; sign-off "Before you graduate, you'll have…" as a candidate device, completed with a fact, never a promise.
- **Content standard:** every piece shows a real EWU job and an access fact. No access fact, no piece.
- **Visual unit (DesignChamp), lead candidate: the proof object.** MFA: a Willow Springs page, press proof or Get Lit! program. MS: the defense record, faculty sign-off on a defended solution, from someone with no management title. MOT: a Level II placement record with a Spokane/Catalyst site cue ⚑ (sites unconfirmed). People stay in play, shown doing the job, never being chosen.
- **Fit with AB/LB (APPROVED, Andres):** the CCI frames ad headlines, guide covers and LP heroes. AB's SMP is a content standard, not copy: every guide/LP answers LB's four questions. Guides M1–O2 and 12 LPs (5/3/4) stand if EWU rejects the CCI.
- **Cautions:** the CCI is a proposal at Nov 9; it is applied across deliverables only after EWU signs. Every ad naming EWU needs EWU's prior written consent (CT Exh. A §22): build review time into the MOT last call.

## The job to be done
### MS in Management
- **Persuade:** early-career professionals, career changers, new managers to inquire and apply; "high-volume, speed-to-lead" (CT 1.A), so low-friction inquiry matters.
- **Behavior to change:** they default to an MBA/MBL (Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU, EWU's own MBA, kept distinct per AB) or a fast online MS, or wait until "experienced" (CM §1; VoC).
- **Insight (PROXY; gate before DEVELOP):** *They feel too junior for the prestige degree and suspect the alternative is second-best* (MSG §3; no PNW voice). PR's 45–60 prospect interviews (PR 3.4) are the validation route.
- **New belief:** the MS is built for where I am now, and it proves itself with work I defend.
- **USP: PRE-EMPTIVE, UNVERIFIED ⚑.** The MNGT 591 oral defense. Never "only"; never "in person".
- **Say this:** Earn the master's you can defend, before you're a manager.
- **Proof:** MNGT 589 applied project; no management experience required; 3.0 GPA, $75 fee; no assistantships (FG s.21–23; MSG §2). $427.47/credit resident, $940.92 non-resident; never a total. PR's "$11,628 vs $27,018" is unsourced: do not use.
- **⚑ BLOCKING:** format (CT "fully online (non-OPM)" vs LB "Hybrid" vs 10/21 call hybrid); catalog (37 cr quarterly vs 44 cr 12-month cohort); name (MS vs "MIM", CT); one cleared student project and defense.
- **Barrier:** Credential.

### MFA in Creative Writing (fiction, poetry)
- **Persuade:** fiction writers and poets, nationally ("national niche", CT 1.A) and regionally, *including strong writers who think they won't get in*, to apply for fall 2027.
- **Behavior to change:** they apply only to fully funded programs (UW, Idaho) or heavily advertised low-residency ones (CM §2).
- **Insight:** "Only apply to funded programs" (VoC), yet they want "a writing community to help me improve my craft".
- **New belief:** at EWU I'd work inside a magazine, a press and a festival, not just a workshop.
- **USP: UNIQUE.** Practica in EWU's own literary institutions (FG s.25), taught by faculty (director Gregory Spatz; Laura Read). Doesn't beat the funding barrier.
- **Say this:** Edit a literary magazine on the strength of your pages, not your test scores.
- **Proof:** practica also at Willow Springs Books and Get Lit! (FG s.25); 7–10 selective teaching awards a year, waiver plus stipend (FG s.26) ⚑ "selective", never "funded" (AB); two years, 72 quarter credits, evening classes in Cheney, workshops of 10–15, ~20–24 admitted, book-length thesis (FG s.20, s.25). Alumni Shann Ray, Leyna Krow, Kate Peterson (Jess Walter is BA '87, never MFA); names cleared (Andres, 2026-10-25), personal consent per production.
- **⚑ OPEN:** format (CT "on-campus" vs LB "Hybrid" vs AB "residential"); 2027 deadline.
- **Barrier:** Credential plus Aspirational.

### Master of Occupational Therapy
- **Job:** the 2027–28 OTCAS cycle; this cycle gets a last call only (Nov 9–15, 2 PM PT). On-campus, applicant filtering and prerequisite screening (CT 1.A).
- **Persuade:** pre-OT graduates and career changers in eastern Washington and the Inland Northwest who meet prerequisites, to apply through OTCAS.
- **Behavior to change:** they assume they need an OTD, see programs at $141k, and stall, pick Whitworth's OTD or move away (VoC; CM §3).
- **Insight:** "You do not need a doctorate to be an occupational therapist," yet "all the OT programs are so expensive" and "I want to avoid moving far away from my support system" (VoC).
- **New belief:** I can become an OT here, with a master's, in 2.5 years, at public tuition.
- **USP: UNIQUE (place-based) ⚑.** The public master's route to OT in Spokane (CM §3; FG s.35); "only" needs sign-off.
- **Say this:** 24+ weeks treating patients under supervision, at a public university's price. Never "practice as an OT" (students aren't licensed); no cost comparisons ⚑. "Without leaving Spokane" ⚑ (sites unpublished, may add travel, FG s.29).
- **Fallback (Nov 9–15 only):** "Earn your Master of Occupational Therapy in Spokane. 2.5 years, at EWU's Catalyst campus."
- **Proof:** Catalyst Building; ≥24 weeks Level II fieldwork; cohort 32 (FG s.29); $6,412 WA resident per semester plus $975 surcharge (FG s.32); graduation 96%, NBCOT 95% (FG s.33). Graduation, certification and licensure are separate steps (AB, LB). Prerequisites visible.
- **Barrier:** Credential.

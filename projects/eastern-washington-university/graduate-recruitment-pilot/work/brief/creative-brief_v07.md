## The problem
Prospects for all three degrees shortlist on one filter (cost, funding, or whether the credential "counts"), and EWU looks like the regional default on that filter, so they inquire elsewhere or stall (VoC; CM). EWU's graduate programs have had no centralized marketing "in recent years" and it wants a "pull-based" model that yields "high-quality" leads (RFP 1.1), run by Graduate Studies staff who can "maintain, edit, and duplicate campaigns independently" (CT 2.A). Now: MOT closes Nov 15, the MFA window is open, the Nov 9 presentation sets the year (FG s.4, s.6). Pilot term Sep 1, 2026 to Aug 31, 2027 (CT §2).

- **Persuade:** early-career professionals, career changers and new managers to inquire and apply. CT frames it "high-volume, speed-to-lead" (CT 1.A): low-friction inquiry matters as much as the idea.
- **Behavior to change:** they default to an MBA/MBL (Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU, EWU's own MBA) or a fast national online MS, or wait until "experienced" (CM §1; VoC). Keep it distinct from EWU's MBA (AB).
- **Insight (PROXY; gate before DEVELOP):** *They feel too junior for the prestige degree and suspect the alternative is second-best* (MSG §3; no PNW voice). PR planned 45–60 prospect interviews (PR 3.4): the natural validation route.
- **New belief:** the MS is built for where I am now, and it proves itself with work I defend.
- **USP: PRE-EMPTIVE, UNVERIFIED ⚑.** The MNGT 591 oral defense. Never "only"; never "in person".
- **Say this:** Earn the master's you can defend, before you're a manager.
- **Proof:** MNGT 589 applied project; no management experience required; 3.0 GPA, $75 fee; no assistantships (FG s.21–23; MSG §2). $427.47/credit resident, $940.92 non-resident; **never a total**. PR's "$11,628 vs $27,018" in/out-of-state figure is unsourced: do not use.

## Tone
- **We are:** warm, plain-spoken, concrete, credible (AB "warm, clear and helpful"; LB "warm, direct and credible"), *a little Inland Northwest*.

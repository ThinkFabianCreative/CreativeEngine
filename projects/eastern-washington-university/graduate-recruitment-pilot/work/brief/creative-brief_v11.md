## The job to be done
### MS in Management
- **Persuade:** early-career professionals, career changers, new managers to inquire and apply; (CT 1.A).
- **Behavior to change:** they default to an MBA/MBL (Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU, EWU's MBA), a fast online MS, or wait until "experienced" (CM §1).
- **Insight (PROXY; gate before DEVELOP):** *They feel too junior for the prestige degree and suspect the alternative is second-best* (MSG §3). Validate in PR's 45–60 interviews.
- **New belief:** the MS is built for where I am now, and it proves itself with work I defend.
- **USP: PRE-EMPTIVE, UNVERIFIED ⚑.** The MNGT 591 oral defense. Never "only"; never "in person".
- **Say this:** Earn the master's you can defend, before you're a manager.
- **Proof:** MNGT 589 applied project; 3.0 GPA, $75 fee; no assistantships (FG s.21–23). $427.47/credit resident, $940.92 non-resident; never a total, never PR's "$11,628 vs $27,018".
- **⚑ BLOCKING:** format, catalog, name, one cleared project and defense.
  - **Format/catalog/name:** **PROPOSED (invented, EWU to confirm):** fully online, 37 quarter credits, "MS in Management": CT (governing), public page and catalog agree; hybrid rests on one call.
  - **Defense example:** **PROPOSED (invented, EWU to confirm):** a shift supervisor with no management title defends a scheduling fix that cut overtime on her team to faculty. Never shown as a real student.
- **Barrier:** Credential.

- **Insight:** "Only apply to funded programs" (VoC), yet they want "a writing community to help me improve my craft".
- **New belief:** at EWU I'd work inside a magazine, a press and a festival, not just a workshop.
- **USP: UNIQUE.** Practica in EWU's own literary institutions (FG s.25), with faculty (director Gregory Spatz; Laura Read). Doesn't beat the funding barrier.
- **Proof:** practica also at Willow Springs Books and Get Lit! - **⚑ OPEN:** format; 2027 deadline. **PROPOSED (invented, EWU to confirm):** on-campus evening classes in Cheney (CT, FG); applications close early March 2027 (FG calendar).
- **Barrier:** Credential plus Aspirational.

### Master of Occupational Therapy
- **Job:** the 2027–28 OTCAS cycle; this cycle gets a last call only (closes Nov 15, 2 PM PT). Prerequisite screening (CT 1.A).
- **Persuade:** pre-OT graduates and career changers in the Inland Northwest who meet prerequisites, to apply through OTCAS.
- **Behavior to change:** they assume they need an OTD, see $141k programs, and stall, pick Whitworth's OTD or move away.
- **Insight:** "You do not need a doctorate to be an occupational therapist," yet "all the OT programs are so expensive" and they want to stay near their support system (VoC).
- **New belief:** I can become an OT here, with a master's, in 2.5 years, with classes in Spokane.
- **USP: UNIQUE (place-based) ⚑.** The public master's route to OT in Spokane (CM §3; FG s.35); "only" needs sign-off.
- **Say this:** the chapter line. Never "practice as an OT" (students aren't licensed); no cost comparisons. "Without leaving Spokane" ⚑.
- **Fieldwork:** **PROPOSED (invented, EWU to confirm):** two full-time Level II placements at clinical sites across the Inland Northwest; may need travel or temporary housing (FG). Never "in Spokane".
- **Proof:** Catalyst Building classes; ≥24 weeks Level II; cohort 32 (FG s.29); $6,412 WA resident per semester plus $975 surcharge (FG s.32); graduation 96%, NBCOT 95% (FG s.33), never as licensure. Prerequisites visible.
- **Barrier:** Credential.

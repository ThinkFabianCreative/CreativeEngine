CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · BRAND: EWU graduate programs (MS in Management, MFA in Creative Writing, Master of Occupational Therapy) · SOURCE SCOPE: project-level. The promise and the core creative idea are approved inside TRAFFIK and still proposed to EWU.

## 1. Strategic assets

**Where this sits.** EWU's live positioning is the Region's Polytechnic: hands-on learning, rooted in the liberal arts, in high-demand fields. This work is its graduate proof. It is background for us, never campaign copy.

**Brand promise (proposed to EWU; do not lift: strategy, never a line).** EWU exists to give capable people the proof that gets their field to count them in, at a public price within reach. EWU makes the proof possible; the field decides what it is worth. No job, publication, promotion or licensure is implied.

**Core Creative Idea (proposed to EWU, carried here as a strategic asset).** *Stories where people show what they can do, in front of others, before their field counts them in.* (Do not lift: this sentence is strategy, never a line.)

**The gap.** They are already capable; their field has not counted them in yet.

**Proposition (do not lift: plain strategy, not a line).** EWU gives people a way to show their field what they can already do.

**Why only EWU can say it.** It holds against Central Washington University (CWU) and Western Washington University (WWU) on their own published copy; against Western Governors University (WGU) it is pre-emptive until the live-ad check; Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU and UW are untested.

The work expresses the promise and is inspired by the idea. Whatever the execution, the proof is shown to other people.

## 2. Objectives

**Business challenge.** EWU wants more enrollments in three graduate programs, with applications and cost per inquiry as supporting measures. Good candidates rule themselves out before EWU hears from them: they shortlist on cost, funding or whether the degree counts, see EWU as the regional default, and inquire elsewhere or wait a year. Each program runs on its own calendar, and the first deadlines are weeks away.

**The job, across all three programs.**
- **Persuade** working adults considering a master's **to** request information from an EWU program.
- **Behavior to change:** they put it off, or pick the cheapest, fastest online option as a box to tick.
- **Insight (our synthesis, not quotes):** working adults already doing advanced work hold off on a master's because they doubt it will change how others see them enough to justify the cost.
- **What people say (public forums, not EWU prospects):** "I'm having a hard time justifying the total investment." (a Reddit forum) · "has the masters made getting promoted/a new job that much easier?" (a Reddit forum for managers).
- **New belief (do not lift: a belief, not a line):** at EWU they would build proof their field can see.
- **New behavior:** request information. Application started is the later measure.
- **Barrier type:** *hypothesis until we hear from EWU prospects: mainly doubt that the degree is worth it for the cost; secondly, a sense that it is not for someone like them, carried inside stories, never the subject.*

**Per program.**
- **MS in Management:** someone already leading without the title, who wonders if this counts against an MBA or a cheaper online option. Reasons to believe: no management experience required; an applied business project; a final portfolio with an oral defense.
- **MFA in Creative Writing:** a writer who needs funding before anything else. Reasons to believe: seven to ten teaching assistantships a year, each with a tuition waiver and stipend, selective, admission does not guarantee one (EWU's MFA pages); four second-year students hold paid roles at Willow Springs, Willow Springs Books, Get Lit! and Writers in the Community (EWU's MFA funding page); no GRE, admission rests on the writing sample; class location needs EWU sign-off: a December 2025 relocation notice says evening MFA classes move to Cheney from fall 2026, while the MFA apply page still says almost all classes are at EWU Spokane.
- **Master of Occupational Therapy:** a career changer weighing debt, distance and master's versus doctorate. Reasons to believe: two and a half years, full-time, in Spokane's Catalyst Building; a cohort of 32, 96% graduation and 95% pass on NBCOT (the national OT certification exam) for new graduates, 2025 (EWU's OT program page; exact wording needs EWU sign-off, since the same page reports 82% on first attempt); 24 weeks of full-time Level II fieldwork (supervised clinical placements) with "a focus on placement in rural communities" (EWU's fieldwork page); a public university. MOT's numbers are proof the copy carries, not the scene.

## 3. Creative target

*An invented portrait, not a real person or a media persona. Do not lift its wording.*

She is 33 and lives in Spokane Valley. For two years she has run the shift: she trains the new hires, fixes the schedule when it breaks, and is the one the floor manager calls. Her title still says "lead". She has priced master's programs twice and closed the tab both times, not because she doubts herself but because she won't spend two years and real money on letters that change nothing on Monday. Proof she could point to would change that.

- **MFA, same tension:** a writer who already edits other people's work, with no editorial credit of her own.
- **MOT, same tension:** a rehab aide who knows what the patient needs next but isn't allowed to decide it. MOT is the thinnest of the three: the work must find MOT's moment in front of others. One doubt per phrase.

## 4. Executional requirements

**Round one.** Two or three campaign ideas, each working for all three programs under one look, shown in real placements: a paid social static and carousel, search and display, a program landing hub, a guide cover and an email.

**Then, per contract.** Six premium program guides; three program hubs with sub-sections and dynamic content; ad, social and email copy; paid campaigns. Video is incremental and scoped separately.

**Timing.** Nov 9 strategy presentation · Nov 15 MOT applications close (2 PM Pacific) · Dec 15 MS winter deadline · early March 2027 MFA applications close (to confirm).

**Budget.** Media budget set by EWU (amount to confirm). Creative production is incremental to the retainer.

**Mandatories and claim rules.**
- MFA funding is selective. Never say "funded".
- Never say "only", or place fieldwork in Spokane, without EWU sign-off. Never call the MS defense "only" or "in person".
- No cost comparisons or totals.
- OT students are not licensed; keep graduation, certification and licensure apart.
- MFA proof shows her editorial work for others, never a publication of her own.
- Eagle Red (#B7142E) and white; follow the logo guide; typeface to confirm with Marketing and Communications.
- WCAG 2.2 AA (the web accessibility standard) and accessible PDFs. EWU's written consent before any ad naming EWU runs.
- Tone sits beside EWU's warm, proud social voice, but more specific.
- Real EWU people, places and work; no stock or generated stand-ins in final work.

**Access to secure, dates locked at kickoff with an owner.** Top MOT ask: a clinical partner's permission (MOT fieldwork is at outside clinical sites; student and EWU consent do not cover it) and access to the Catalyst simulation labs. Then: consenting students in each program; an MS defense, its room and the defended portfolio; printed Willow Springs issues and Willow Springs Books titles with their student editors; the Catalyst Building.

**If access slips.** Real EWU people, places and work with consenting students only; for MOT, a simulated or consenting session with no identifiable patients.

## 5. Creative ambition

**The Bar.** Committed: OWNABLE. Target: FRESH, if real access is secured in time. For a first-time reader: OWNABLE means no other school could run the work unchanged; FRESH means it also feels new to the category.

**Benchmarks.**
- **US Marine Corps, "Battles Won" (JWT, 2017):** the mechanism match. Character shown in action, in front of strangers, before anyone hands out a title.
- **NHS England, "We Are the NHS" (MullenLowe, 2018–22):** register, not mechanism. Real people doing the actual work, unvarnished; its people already hold the title. Its reported 35% lift in nursing applications is unverified.
- **Monash University, "A Future Without Change" (VML, 2020):** the ceiling, a higher bar than this pilot's. A reversal that shows the world without the university's work.

**Category clichés to beat.** Smiling student portraits on a billboard; campus or lecture-hall backdrops; skyline and flying aspiration; "change", "impact", "dream", "potential"; "100% online", "in as little as", "take the next step"; job-title outcome proof. If Gonzaga, Whitworth, UW or a national online program could run it unchanged, it fails.

## 6. Assessment and measurement

**Priorities.** Enrollments and return on ad spend; applications started and submitted per program; cost per qualified inquiry and hub-to-inquiry conversion; guide-to-application rate above a minimum volume agreed at kickoff.

**First paid-social test (design to set with EWU).** Three message cells on Meta, same audience, budget and offer: proof-led (what you would show, and to whom); cost-led (a public price within reach, no totals or comparisons); and a category-standard control. Formats are set after round one. Cost per inquiry decides; click-through is secondary; engagement alone does not decide.

**How the work will be judged.** Does it show the gap and the proof in front of others? Does it rest on a real, checkable EWU proof with no implied job, publication, funding or licensure? Does it work for all three programs under one look? Is it OWNABLE, and could it reach FRESH with the access we get?

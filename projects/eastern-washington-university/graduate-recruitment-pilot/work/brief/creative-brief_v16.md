## Objective
Grow enrollments in three graduate programs: the MS in Management, the MFA in Creative Writing and the Master of Occupational Therapy. Applications and cost per inquiry follow as supporting measures. Targets to agree with EWU.

## Background
Eastern Washington University is a public university in Cheney and Spokane. This pilot covers three of its graduate programs.

The problem is that good candidates rule themselves out before EWU ever hears from them. They shortlist on cost, funding or whether the degree counts, see EWU as the regional default, and inquire elsewhere or wait a year.

EWU wants leads that arrive on their own, are worth the staff's time, and that staff can handle without us. Each program runs on its own admissions calendar, and the first deadlines are weeks away.

## Comms approach
EWU calls itself the Region's Polytechnic: a place where students turn classroom knowledge into real-world impact, through hands-on learning rooted in the liberal arts. This work expresses that position rather than replacing it. It is background for us, not campaign copy.

### Key takeout
Prospects who doubt they are ready should come away believing that EWU's graduate programs are within their reach and are built on real, professional work.

The three programs are chapters of one story, not three campaigns. Whatever idea wins must work for all three under one look.

## Audience target
- **MS in Management:** early-career professionals, career changers and new managers, mostly in the Inland Northwest, fitting study around work (online or hybrid, to confirm).
- **MFA in Creative Writing:** fiction writers and poets, from the region and nationally, looking for a community that improves their work.
- **Master of Occupational Therapy:** pre-OT graduates and career changers in the Inland Northwest who meet the prerequisites, often with family ties that keep them close to home.

## Creative target
### The person who already said no
They could do the work, and somewhere they suspect it. But they've already done the sums and decided: too junior for a master's, not good enough to get in, can't afford it, not now. Nobody turned them down. They turned themselves down, quietly, before filling in a form. What changes their mind is evidence: that the work is real, and that it is open to someone like them. People rule themselves out of the good option before anyone else does.

## Messaging priorities + reasons to believe
### MS in Management
**Message:** The MS in Management is open to people without management experience, and students prove their ability through applied work they defend to faculty.
- No management experience required to apply.
- An applied project built on your own work.
- A final oral defense of a real business solution to faculty.

### MFA in Creative Writing
**Message:** Admission is decided mainly by the writing, students work inside real literary institutions (four second-year students hold paid roles running them), and a limited number of assistantships cover tuition.
- Seven to ten teaching assistantships a year, each with a tuition waiver and a stipend.
- Four paid second-year roles: at Willow Springs, Willow Springs Books, the Get Lit! festival and Writers in the Community.
- Admission rests on your writing sample, not the GRE.
- Almost all classes are at EWU Spokane.

### Master of Occupational Therapy
**Message:** EWU offers a public master's degree route into occupational therapy, taught in Spokane, with extensive supervised work with patients.
- Two and a half years, full-time and in person, in Spokane's Catalyst Building.
- A cohort of 32, with a 96% graduation rate and a 95% board-exam pass rate for new graduates (2025).
- At least 24 weeks of supervised fieldwork, with a focus on rural communities.
- A public university.

## Deliverables
**First round:** two or three campaign ideas that deliver the key takeout across all three programs, each shown as proof of concept in real placements: paid social, search and display, a program landing hub, a guide cover and an email.

**Then, per contract:** six premium program guides; landing hubs, one per program, with sub-sections and dynamic content; ad, social and email copy; and paid campaigns. Video and film are incremental and scoped separately.

## Creative considerations
- Funding is "selective." Never say "funded."
- Never say "only," and never place fieldwork in Spokane, without EWU sign-off.
- Never call the MS defense "only" or "in person."
- No cost comparisons or cost totals.
- OT students aren't licensed. Keep graduation, certification and licensure distinct.
- The work should answer the human truth with evidence specific to EWU. How it does that is open.
- Avoid category clichés. If Gonzaga, Whitworth, UW or a national online program could run it unchanged, it fails.
- EWU Eagle Red (#B7142E) and white; follow the logo guide; typeface to confirm with Marketing and Communications.
- Accessible: WCAG 2.2 AA and accessible PDFs.
- EWU's written consent before any ad naming EWU runs.
- Tone sits beside EWU's warm, proud social voice, but more specific.

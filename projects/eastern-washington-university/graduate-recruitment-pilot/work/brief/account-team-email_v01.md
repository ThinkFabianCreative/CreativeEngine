Subject: EWU creative: brief v20, what we need from you, production questions

Hi team,

Thanks for the production brief and the GTM deck. The deliverables, quantities and media plan work for us. Attached is our creative brief (v20). It replaces any earlier version.

**The idea.** Everything follows "Stop waiting to be chosen." It's for capable people waiting for a promotion, a boss's backing or "next year." EWU is where they stop waiting and take a dated step: a start term, a first workshop, a defense date, a first fieldwork week. The guides are the offer; the idea carries every piece.

**Roles.** Please tell us what to say and to whom. Creative decides how it looks and reads, so take the visual direction and carousel sequences out of the production brief. We present territories on Nov 13 and you review them against your inputs.

**Journey.** Meta ad → program micro-site (3 sites, 4 sections each) with program content and guide downloads. Ads and pages use the same images and video, so plan one asset set for both.

**Images.** AI is for scamps and mockups only. Key image development and photo retouching belong in concept development and production. Please scope them there; the production brief currently says no new photography, and that needs to change.

**Per guide, we need:**
- Approved facts to show, with the source and who signs off
- Real dates: term starts, deadlines, defense windows, fieldwork start
- Anything that must appear word for word (legal, funding, accreditation)
- The CTA, the Slate form fields and the micro-site section each guide sits in

**Messaging, within the idea:**
- What the wait looks like for each program and guide, in priority order (for MOT: holding out for a doctorate)
- Who each message is for, and where they are in the journey
- Objections to answer: cost, time, value, eligibility
- How messaging shifts around deadlines
- What we're testing, and which result picks the winner

**Rules for any messaging you draft:**
- Nobody chooses her. No "you're in"; the MFA awards are never her being picked.
- No boss or employer as the villain.
- No pace or outcome promises ("in as little as", job, promotion, funding, licensure).
- No "only public MOT in the region" until EWU confirms it.

**Production questions:**
- Can we schedule a shoot, and what's the budget and earliest date? If not, can we use EWU's photo library?
- Consenting students per program, Catalyst sim lab access and clinical partner permission for MOT. Who collects consent?
- MS format: the GTM deck says one campus day a week, Monique said online. Which is it?
- Who signs off on facts per program?
- Micro-site platform and hosting, tracking and form fields (Ginny and Will on the web team?)
- Who writes guide content, how long is each guide, and are downloads gated?
- Typeface and logo files from Marketing and Communications; who gives written consent before ads run?
- Delivery order for the 66 files and 6 PDFs, review rounds and approvers
- Who uploads to EWU's ad accounts, and the new Meta ad account setup

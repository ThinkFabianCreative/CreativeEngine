This note goes with the creative brief. It explains the direction we propose and lists the decisions we need from EWU before creative work starts. Graduate Studies approves the strategy and the work. Marketing and Communications approves anything that touches the brand. Program directors confirm every fact about their program. Anything marked as our recommendation is a proposal for EWU to confirm.

## 1. Why this direction

### What we found
- Graduate advertising looks alike. Smiling student portraits, campus backdrops, skylines, and words like "change", "impact", "potential", "100% online", "in as little as" and "take the next step" appear across almost every school we reviewed.
- "Doing", "hands-on" and "real world" are already claimed. Sullivan University runs "The School of Doing". Central Washington University promises "immersive, hands-on learning". Western Washington University says its graduate programs offer "hands-on research and practical experience".
- Western Governors University competes on price and speed: competency-based, flat-rate, fully online, with a one-year MS in Management and Leadership.
- None of the work we found shows students presenting their work to other people before their field recognizes them, so that ground is still open to EWU.

### How it sits under The Region's Polytechnic
EWU's live positioning is the Region's Polytechnic: hands-on learning in high-demand fields, rooted in the liberal arts. The graduate work would be its proof at the graduate level, showing what "hands-on" looks like for a manager, a writer and an occupational therapist. An idea that works as well for writers as for therapists may also help with the concern some faculty have raised that the polytechnic label leaves out the humanities. The polytechnic phrase stays out of the graduate campaign copy.

### The human truth and the gap
People often grow into work before anyone recognizes them for it. They hesitate to pay for a degree unless it gives them something they can show. Our prospects are already capable, and their field hasn't counted them in yet. The campaign is set in that gap.

### Proposed promise and core idea
- **Brand promise (proposed for EWU review):** EWU exists to give capable people the proof that gets their field to count them in, at a public price within reach. EWU makes the proof possible, and the field decides what it is worth. No job, publication, promotion or licensure is implied.
- **Core creative idea (proposed for EWU review):** stories where people show what they can do, in front of others, before their field counts them in.
- This sentence is a strategic statement for the creative team. It is not campaign copy and won't appear in ads.
- Each program has its own proof moment: the oral defense of the MS portfolio, editorial and publishing work an MFA student does for others, and supervised clinical fieldwork in the MOT.

## 2. The decisions we need from EWU

### Promise and core idea
- **Our recommendation:** approve both as proposed, under The Region's Polytechnic. We ask Marketing and Communications to confirm the fit with the master campaign.
- **Why:** they give all three programs one story without competing with EWU's main line.
- **If not approved:** we adopt EWU's preferred wording and check the work against it. The program guides and hubs still answer the practical questions prospects ask.

### One job across all three programs
- **Our recommendation:** every piece of work asks prospects to request information from an EWU program. Applications started are measured later.
- **Why:** one action keeps the campaign comparable across programs and gives the media test a clear measure.
- **If EWU prefers a different action:** the calls to action, the hubs and the measurement plan change.

### MS in Management format and catalog version
- **Our recommendation:** confirm whether the program is fully online or hybrid, and which catalog version applies to winter and spring 2027 starts. The current catalog shows 37 credits. A catalog PDF from April 2026 describes a 12-month cohort with 44 credits, an internship and a fall start.
- **Why:** format, start terms and length shape the media mix, the hub and every MS ad.
- **If unresolved:** MS creative can't start, and the Dec 15 deadline is at risk.

### MS defense panel
- **Our recommendation:** tell us who sits on the MS oral defense panel, and whether people from the field ever take part.
- **Why:** the defense is the MS proof moment. Practitioners on the panel would strengthen it, though the idea doesn't depend on that.
- **If unknown:** we show the defended portfolio and the act of defending, without naming who judges it.

### MFA class location
- **Our recommendation:** confirm where MFA classes meet. A December 2025 relocation notice moves evening MFA classes to Cheney from fall 2026, while the MFA apply page still says almost all classes are at EWU Spokane.
- **Why:** prospects will check, and the two sources disagree.
- **If unresolved:** we leave location out of MFA work.

### MOT board-pass wording
- **Our recommendation:** confirm which figure we may use. EWU's OT page reports 95% passing the national certification exam among 2025 new graduates, and 82% on first attempt.
- **Why:** both figures are on the same page, and the difference matters to prospects.
- **If unresolved:** we leave the pass rate out of MOT work.

### Permissions and access
- **Already cleared:** Willow Springs and Get Lit! imagery, and alumni names.
- **Our recommendation:** grant access to an MS defense and its record, an MOT fieldwork placement record, a clinical partner's permission to film at a fieldwork site, the Catalyst simulation labs, and consenting students in each program. Each needs a date and an owner, locked at kickoff with Graduate Studies.
- **Why:** real proof is what makes the work EWU's own. Clinical sites are outside EWU's control, so student and EWU consent don't cover filming there.
- **If access slips:** we use real EWU people, places and work with consenting students only. For MOT, that means a simulated or consenting session with no identifiable patients.

### Landing pages
- **Our recommendation:** three program hubs, each with sub-sections and dynamic content that count toward the contracted landing pages. We ask EWU to confirm it accepts this.
- **Why:** each hub stays rich and easier for EWU staff to maintain, and prospects get one clear destination per program.
- **If EWU wants separate pages:** we build them. There will be more for staff to maintain, and each page will be thinner.

### Targets, budget and standards
- **KPI targets:** we need graduate targets for enrollments, applications and inquiries. Our proposed starting points will be set after the Slate baseline.
- **Media budget:** EWU sets the amount, and we need it confirmed.
- **Accessibility:** we plan for WCAG 2.2 AA and accessible PDFs. Please confirm this is EWU's standard.
- **Ad consent:** we need an agreed turnaround for EWU's written consent before any ad naming EWU runs.
- **Brand guidelines and typeface:** we follow the Eagle Red and white palette and the logo guide. We need the brand typeface from Marketing and Communications.

## 3. Creative considerations

### The Bar
We commit to work that is ownable: no other school could run it unchanged. We aim for fresh, work that also feels new to graduate advertising, if access to real people and work comes through in time. Both depend on real proof. Stock or generated stand-ins would put the work back among everything else in the category.

### Benchmarks
- **Monash University, "A Future Without Change":** the ceiling, set higher than this pilot's reach.
- **US Marine Corps, "Battles Won":** the closest match in how it works. Character is shown in action, in front of strangers, before anyone hands out a title.
- **NHS England, "We Are the NHS":** the register. Real people doing the actual work, unvarnished.

### What round one will show
Two or three campaign ideas, each working for all three programs under one look and shown in real placements. We won't present headlines or finished executions in this document. Video is optional and would be scoped and estimated separately.

### Clichés we will avoid
Smiling portraits, campus and lecture-hall backdrops, skylines, "change", "impact", "dream", "potential", "100% online", "in as little as", "take the next step", and job titles as proof of outcomes. If Gonzaga, Whitworth, UW or a national online program could run a piece unchanged, we cut it.

## 4. Strategic considerations
- **Western Governors University:** our difference rests on proof shown to other people. We haven't yet checked WGU's live ads, so the position is pre-emptive until that check is done.
- **MS insight:** few prospect voices exist online for this degree. We treat the insight as a working assumption until the 45 to 60 planned prospect interviews confirm it.
- **MOT depends on access:** it is the thinnest of the three without a clinical partner's permission and the simulation labs.
- **MFA funding:** it must read as selective. Seven to ten teaching assistantships a year, and admission doesn't guarantee one. We never say "funded".
- **Cost:** we make no cost comparisons and state no program totals.
- **Licensure:** OT students practice under supervision. Graduation, certification and licensure stay separate, and nothing implies a license.
- **Ad consent timing:** the MOT last call needs EWU's consent turnaround to fit before applications close on Nov 15.

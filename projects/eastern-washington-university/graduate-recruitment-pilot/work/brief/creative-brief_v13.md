## The problem
Good candidates for EWU's graduate programs rule themselves out before EWU ever hears from them. They shortlist on one question: what it costs, whether there's funding, or whether the degree counts. On that question EWU looks like the regional default, so they inquire somewhere else or wait a year.

EWU wants leads that come to it on their own, are worth the staff's time, and can be handled by that staff without us.

## Who we're talking to
People who could do the work and have decided they probably shouldn't apply. They're early in their careers, changing careers, or finishing an undergraduate degree, and most live in the Inland Northwest. The MFA also draws writers from across the country.

## The human truth
People rule themselves out of the good option before anyone else does. They tell themselves they're too junior, not good enough, or can't afford it.

## The core idea
Stories where the real work is handed to people who didn't think it was meant for them.

**Brand promise (proposed):** EWU exists to put affordable academic excellence and applied work in high-demand fields within reach of people who'd ruled themselves out.

EWU talks about upward mobility and first-generation students. We show that through the work instead of saying it. Every piece shows a real job a student does at EWU and one fact about access. If it doesn't, it doesn't run.

The visual lead is the proof object: something a student makes or holds before graduating. People are shown doing the job, never being chosen for it.

## The programs

### MS in Management
**Who and their doubt.** Early-career professionals, career changers and new managers. They feel too junior for an MBA and suspect anything else is second-best, so they pick an MBA, a fast online MS, or wait until they feel experienced.

**The line:** "Earn the master's you can defend, before you're a manager."

**Why only EWU.** Students defend a real business solution to faculty in a final oral defense, before anyone has called them a manager. *Needs EWU sign-off.*

**Proof.** No management experience required. An applied project built on your own work. Fully online, 37 quarter credits (proposed).

### MFA in Creative Writing (fiction, poetry)
**Who and their doubt.** Writers and poets, including strong ones who think they won't get in. They apply only to funded programs, yet what they want is a writing community that makes their work better.

**The line:** "Edit a literary magazine on the strength of your pages, not your test scores."

**Why only EWU.** Students work inside EWU's own literary institutions: the magazine Willow Springs, Willow Springs Books and the Get Lit! festival.

**Proof.** Admission rests on your writing sample, with no GRE. Workshops of 10 to 15 and a book-length thesis. Seven to ten selective teaching awards a year with a tuition waiver and stipend. *Needs EWU sign-off.*

### Master of Occupational Therapy
**Who and their doubt.** Inland Northwest pre-OT graduates and career changers who meet the prerequisites. They assume they need a doctorate, see $141k programs, and either stall or move away. They want to stay near family.

**The line:** "24+ weeks treating patients under supervision, with classes in Spokane."

**Why only EWU.** The public master's route to OT in Spokane. *Needs EWU sign-off.*

**Proof.** Classes in Spokane's Catalyst Building. At least 24 weeks treating patients under supervision. A cohort of 32. 96% graduation and 95% national board pass rate.

## By stage
- **Inquiry:** "Is this for me, can I afford it, and why EWU?" Guide download, then request for information.
- **Application:** "Can I get in, and what do I need?" Requirements and the application.
- **Yield:** "Is it worth it?" Paid media through to deposit.

## Tone
Warm, plain-spoken, concrete and credible, with a little Inland Northwest in it. No guarantees and no clichés. If Gonzaga, Whitworth, UW or a national online program could run the ad unchanged, it fails.

## Mandatories

- Accessible PDFs and WCAG 2.2 AA
- No guarantees or unapproved claims
- No personal data sent to analytics
- EWU's written consent before any ad naming EWU runs

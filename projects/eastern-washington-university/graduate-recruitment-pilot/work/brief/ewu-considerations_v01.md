## Why this idea
- **The pattern we found:** good candidates rule themselves out before EWU ever sees them. They filter on one question and assume the answer is no.
- **MS in Management:** "I don't have enough leadership experience to be in the MBA program." They feel too junior for the prestige degree and suspect the alternative is second-best.
- **MFA in Creative Writing:** "Only apply to funded programs." Some strong writers say even applying "feels kind of ridiculous." Yet what they want is "a writing community to help me improve my craft."
- **Master of Occupational Therapy:** "You do not need a doctorate to be an occupational therapist," but "all the OT programs are so expensive," and many want to stay near their support system.
- **How EWU's mission becomes the creative:** EWU's own words (affordable academic excellence, applied learning in high-demand fields, upward mobility, first-generation students) are usually stated. We propose to show them instead. Our core idea: stories where the real work is handed to people who didn't think it was meant for them. Each program has real work to show: defending a business solution to faculty, editing a literary magazine, treating patients under supervision. The person who almost ruled themselves out is the story; access is the proof, not a slogan.

## The decisions we need from EWU
### Brand promise
- **Our recommendation:** "EWU exists to put affordable academic excellence and applied work in high-demand fields within reach of people who'd ruled themselves out." It is built from EWU's mission and vision wording.
- **If EWU prefers its own:** we adopt it and check the core idea against it. Program guides and hubs stand on their own if the core idea is not approved.
### MS in Management format and name
- **Our recommendation:** fully online, 37 quarter credits, named "MS in Management," as the contract, public page and catalog currently agree.
- **If it is hybrid or renamed:** the media mix, ad copy and landing page change. We need this before MS creative starts. We also need one real project and defense EWU is comfortable showing.
### MFA format and deadline
- **Our recommendation:** on-campus evening classes in Cheney; applications close early March 2027; "7 to 10 selective teaching awards a year."
- **If any of these differ:** the calendar and the funding language change. Funding must stay accurate, so we need the award figure confirmed.
### MOT fieldwork wording
- **Our recommendation:** "two full-time Level II placements at clinical sites across the Inland Northwest; may require travel or temporary housing." Never "in Spokane."
- **If EWU wants "without leaving Spokane" or "the only public master's route in Spokane":** we need written sign-off, because both are claims prospects will test.
### Success measures (KPIs)
- **Our recommendation, directional:** MOT, 96 or more complete, prerequisite-met applications for 32 seats; MFA, 120 or more applications for 24 seats; MS, inquiries at twice the current baseline. Final targets set after the Slate baseline.
- **If EWU prefers volume targets only:** we will hit volume, but lead quality, which the RFP asks for, becomes harder to prove.
### Journey maps
- **Our recommendation:** four maps, one per persona (MS early-career, MS career changer, MFA, MOT), each staged by Slate stage.
- **If EWU prefers one per program or per stage:** workable, but the two MS audiences have different barriers and would blur.
### Landing pages
- **Our recommendation:** one hub per program (three), with sub-sections and tailored variants that count toward the contract's "at least 12 program landing pages."
- **If EWU wants 12 separate pages:** we build them, at the cost of more maintenance for EWU staff and thinner pages each.
### Permission to show the real work
- **Our recommendation:** MFA (Willow Springs pages, Get Lit! materials, cleared alumni names) is cleared to proceed. We ask permission to show an MS defense record and an MOT placement record, without naming clinical sites.
- **If not granted:** we use clearly labeled illustrative versions, which are weaker proof.
### Alumni and faculty participation
- **Our recommendation:** invite named MFA alumni and faculty to appear, with each person's consent per production; one faculty member per program for MS and MOT.
- **If they cannot take part:** the work leans on the objects themselves rather than people, which still works but loses warmth.

## Creative considerations
- **The proof-object approach:** each program is shown through a real thing a student makes or holds before graduating, with people shown doing the job, never being chosen.
- **What it could look like (illustrative only, not final copy):** MFA, a Willow Springs masthead or page with a first-year student's name on it. MS, the record of a defense in front of faculty. MOT, a Level II placement record with the Catalyst Building in view.
- **Sign-off direction:** "Before you graduate, you'll have…" followed by a fact, never a promise.
- **Lesson films (optional add-on):** short, premium "lesson" films taught by EWU people inside the real work ("Lesson 1: read 400 submissions"). This is incremental to the contracted scope and estimated separately.
- **Where to start:** the MFA. Its proof objects are cleared, its people are known, and its strength (working inside a magazine, a press and a festival) is genuinely EWU's own.
- **Clichés we will avoid:** "100% online," "flexible," "take the next step," "find your voice," "make a difference," "hands-on"; ranking badges; smiling professional at a laptop; notebook and coffee; scrubs and therapy balls. If Gonzaga, Whitworth, UW or a national online program could run the ad unchanged, we don't run it.

## Strategic considerations
- **The MS insight is a working assumption.** Online voices on this degree were thin and mostly European. We will validate it in the 45 to 60 planned interviews before developing MS creative.
- **"Only" and Spokane claims** run only with EWU's written sign-off.
- **MFA funding** reads "selective teaching awards," never "funded"; prospects hear "funded" as universal.
- **Ad approval time:** the contract requires EWU's written consent before ads naming EWU run. We need an agreed turnaround, especially for the MOT last call.
- **Timing:** proposal presentation Nov 9; MOT applications close Nov 15, 2 PM PT (a last call only; the main MOT push is the 2027–28 cycle); MS winter start Dec 15; MFA applications close early March 2027 (to confirm).

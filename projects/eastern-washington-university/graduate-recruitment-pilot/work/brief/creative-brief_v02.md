## The job to be done
### MS in Management
- **Persuade:** early-career professionals, career changers and new managers in the Inland Northwest *and online* to inquire and apply for winter or spring 2027 start.
- **Behavior to change:** they default to an MBA or MBL (Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU, EWU's own MBA) or a fast national online MS, or wait until they "have enough experience" (CM §1; VoC).
- **Insight (PROXY, to validate with EWU prospects):** *They feel too junior for the prestige degree and suspect the alternative is second-best.* Basis: one non-US Reddit voice ("I don't have enough leadership experience to be in the MBA program"; VoC) and one undated US forum voice drawn to the MS because it "seems like the only MS that doesn't require 3 years work experience" (Beat the GMAT; BM Q1). No Inland Northwest or EWU prospect voice yet. ⚑ Validate via EWU admissions or a short prospect check before DEVELOP.
- **New belief:** the MS is the degree built for where I am now, and it proves itself with work I can show.
- **USP: PRE-EMPTIVE, UNVERIFIED ⚑.** You finish with work you can show: a final portfolio you defend in person, plus an applied business project (FG s.21), open without management experience. Uniqueness only partly checked: none of the pages retrieved (Gonzaga MBA, WSU policy, Whitworth aggregator, TESU, Excelsior, Fordham online) mention a portfolio with oral defense, but capstones are common and the sample is small (BM Q2). Claim it first; never claim it as "only".
- **Say this:** Earn the master's you can show, before you're a manager.
- **Proof:** 37 quarter credits, flexible pace, ten required courses (FG s.19, s.21); no management experience required (FG s.23); year-round applications, quarterly starts, Dec 15 winter deadline (FG s.6, s.22). ⚑ Need from EWU: one real, cleared student portfolio or applied project (topic, artifact, defense moment) to show; none is in sources. ⚑ Delivery format (online vs hybrid one day/week) is unresolved and cannot appear in work until EWU confirms (FG s.14, s.23). ⚑ No tuition rate or outcomes data for the MS.
- **Barrier:** Credential. They doubt it counts, so the portfolio and defense are the proof, not a supporting detail.

### MFA in Creative Writing (fiction, poetry)
- **Persuade:** fiction writers and poets nationally and regionally, *including strong writers who think they won't get in*, to apply by the 2027 deadline (*early March, to confirm*).
- **Behavior to change:** they apply only to fully funded programs (UW, Idaho) or to unfunded low-residency programs that advertise heavily (CM §2), and skip EWU.
- **Insight:** "Only apply to funded programs" (VoC). Funding is the first filter, yet what they actually want is "a writing community to help me improve my craft" and a way into literary life that doesn't depend on writing income.
- **New belief:** at EWU I'd be working inside a magazine, a press and a festival from year one, not just in a workshop.
- **USP: UNIQUE.** Practica in EWU's own literary institutions: Willow Springs magazine, Willow Springs Books, Get Lit! festival and Writers in the Community (FG s.25). Competitor swap fails: UW, Idaho and Montana don't run these. Defensible: institutions, not features. Weakness: it doesn't beat the funding barrier; EWU's teaching awards are selective (7–10 a year), and the community's "funded" vocabulary is binary (FG s.26; VoC).
- **Say this:** Here you don't only write the book; you learn to edit, publish and put it on a stage.
- **Funding stance: meet it head-on.** The work does not dodge the first filter; it states plainly that teaching awards exist and are selective, then lets the practica carry the reason to apply.
- **Proof:** funding: selective teaching awards, 7–10 a year (FG s.26), stated plainly, never as "funded"; two years, 72 quarter credits, evening classes in Cheney from fall 2026; workshops of 10–15; book-length thesis with an adviser (FG s.25); Willow Springs, Get Lit! (founded 1998) (FG s.15); alum Leyna Krow, *Fire Season* (FG s.12). ⚑ Any funding language must say "selective" and never imply all are funded. ⚑ Use of alumni names. ⚑ 2027 deadline unpublished.
- **Barrier:** Credential (funding and value) plus Aspirational ("am I good enough"). Lead with proof of the practica; answer funding plainly in proof.

### Master of Occupational Therapy
- **Job: the 2027–28 OTCAS cycle.** This cycle gets a last call only: at most six days (Nov 9 to Nov 15, 2026, 2 PM PT), using fallback wording.
- **Persuade:** pre-OT graduates and career changers in eastern Washington and the Inland Northwest who meet prerequisites, to apply through OTCAS in the 2027–28 cycle.
- **Behavior to change:** they assume they need an OTD, see programs at $141k, and either stall, pick a doctorate (Whitworth OTD locally) or move away (VoC; CM §3).
- **Insight:** "You do not need a doctorate to be an occupational therapist," yet "all the OT programs are so expensive," and "I want to avoid moving far away from my support system" (VoC). They just want to be an OT, without the debt or the move.
- **New belief:** I can become an OT here, with a master's, in 2.5 years, at public tuition.
- **USP: UNIQUE (place-based), ⚑.** The public master's route to OT in Spokane: Whitworth in the same city offers the OTD; the regional set's other master's programs are in Tacoma and southern Idaho (CM §3; FG s.35). Ad pattern: every entry-level OT ad found is for an OTD (CM). ⚑ "Only" claims need EWU sign-off and a complete check (UW not retrieved; UW admissions paused as of Oct 20).
- **Say this (one thought, stay near home):** You can become an OT without leaving Spokane. *Chosen over the myth reversal, which any master's program can say; staying near home is what the place-based USP owns. The master's route sits in proof.*
- **⚑-safe fallback (usable now, before "only" and cost claims clear):** "Earn your Master of Occupational Therapy in Spokane. 2.5 years, at EWU's Catalyst campus." No "only", no cost or debt comparison, no total cost.
- **Proof:** 2.5 years full time, Catalyst Building Spokane; 24 weeks of supervised fieldwork; cohort of 32 (FG s.29, s.35); $6,412 WA resident tuition per full-time semester plus $975 surcharge (FG s.32); graduation 96% (89/93, 2023–25); NBCOT 95% new grads, 82% first attempt, 2025 (FG s.33). ⚑ Debt or cost comparisons with OTD programs. ⚑ No total degree cost may be inferred from semester rates (FG s.32). Certification and licensure remain separate. ⚑ Need from EWU: named Spokane fieldwork sites and Catalyst OT lab access/photography (none published, BM Q4); a published total-cost figure if cost is ever to be used.
- **Barrier:** Credential (do I need the doctorate, will it pay back). Lead with proof; EWU's stated goal is applicant quality, so prerequisites stay visible (FG s.4).

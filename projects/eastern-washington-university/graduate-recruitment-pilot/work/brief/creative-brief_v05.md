## The problem
Prospects for all three degrees shortlist on one filter (cost, funding, or whether the credential "counts") and EWU's programs look like the regional default on that filter, so they inquire elsewhere or stall (VoC; CM). EWU weights the three programs equally but wants one recruitment process its staff can run alone (FG s.4), so three separate campaigns are the wrong shape. Now: MOT closes Nov 15, the MFA window is open (Oct to early March), Management has the only pre-fall-2027 intake, and the Nov 9 presentation sets the year (FG s.4, s.6).

## Platform: one school, three chapters
- **Brand promise:** MISSING. No approved EWU brand house. ⚑ Ask EWU for brand guidelines.
- **Core Creative Idea (PROPOSED, for EWU review):** *Stories where EWU students do the job before they graduate.* Anchor: EWU's polytechnic "learn by doing"; FG s.13 itself names Management portfolios, publishing roles and OT fieldwork as the applied work.
- **Vessel:** a MasterClass-style frame stretched to the school. Each "lesson" is taught by an EWU person (student, alum, faculty) and is something you only learn by doing the real job. EWU is promoted once; the programs run as chapters with their own lessons, audiences and calls to action.
- **Sign-off device (candidate):** "Before you graduate, you'll have…" completed per chapter with a fact, never a promise.
- **Chapters:**
  - **MFA:** before you graduate, you'll have edited a magazine, worked on a press and helped run a festival (Willow Springs, Willow Springs Books, Get Lit!; FG s.25).
  - **MS:** before you graduate, you'll have solved a real business problem and defended your work to faculty (MNGT 589 applied project; MNGT 591 portfolio, "mandatory final oral defense"; MSG §1).
  - **MOT:** before you graduate, you'll have spent at least 24 weeks in two full-time supervised placements (FG s.29). ⚑ Placement sites are not published and may involve travel (FG s.29); do not say "in Spokane" for fieldwork until EWU confirms.
- **Seven tests (brief):** Insightful, partly (truth is per chapter: credential doubt). Global, yes for EWU's markets. Creative, depends on lesson specificity. Complete, hosts all three jobs and the stage CTAs. Fluid, yes (film lessons, social cards, landing chapters, email). Simple, yes. Long-lived, yes; new chapters can be added beyond the pilot.
- **Swap test:** "learn by doing" alone FAILS; Gonzaga, Whitworth or WSU could say it. Ownability lives only in the named jobs: Willow Springs and Get Lit!, the oral defense, the 24-week placements. A lesson that could be taught at another school is cut.
- **Mandatory for the vessel:** no lesson without a real EWU person and a real EWU job behind it.

## The job to be done
### MS in Management
- **Persuade:** early-career professionals, career changers and new managers in the Inland Northwest *and online* to inquire and apply for the next start.
- **Behavior to change:** they default to an MBA or MBL (Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU, EWU's own MBA) or a fast national online MS, or wait until they "have enough experience" (CM §1; VoC).
- **Insight (PROXY; validation gate before DEVELOP):** *They feel too junior for the prestige degree and suspect the alternative is second-best.* Basis: seven forum voices, five new (MSG §3): the MS framed as the no-experience route, with doubt about its value against the MBA in 3 of 5. Mostly non-located or non-US; no Pacific Northwest or EWU voice. **Gate stays:** no DEVELOP until EWU admissions or a short prospect check confirms or replaces it.
- **New belief:** the MS is the degree built for where I am now, and it proves itself with work I defend.
- **USP: PRE-EMPTIVE, UNVERIFIED ⚑.** The oral defense: MNGT 591 ends in a "mandatory final oral defense" (catalog, MSG §1). The portfolio alone is not ownable: WSU's MBA also has students curate a portfolio (MSG §4). No oral defense found at Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU or UW. Never "only"; never "in person" (not stated in any source).
- **Say this:** Earn the master's you can defend, before you're a manager.
- **Proof:** applied project MNGT 589 (consulting, applied research or case analysis); ten required courses; no management experience required (FG s.21, s.23; MSG §2). Normally 3.0 GPA, $75 fee (FG s.22). No assistantships or tuition waivers (FG s.22). Tuition: university-wide graduate rate $427.47/credit resident, $940.92 non-resident (MSG §2); no program rate exists, so **never state a total in copy**.
- **⚑ OPEN, BLOCKING (catalog divergence):** live catalog page: 37 credits, quarterly starts (FG s.6, s.19). Catalog PDF dated 2026-04-19: 12-month fall cohort, 44 credits with 12 paid Spokane internship credits (MSG §2). Credits, starts, internships and the Dec 15 winter deadline are all unusable until EWU says which version applies to 2027. Format also open (public page online; 10/21 call hybrid one day/week; FG s.23).
- **⚑ BLOCKING EWU ask:** one real, cleared student project or portfolio and its defense moment; none found publicly (MSG §1). Visual show test stays THIN without it.
- **Barrier:** Credential. They doubt it counts; the defense is the proof.

### MFA in Creative Writing (fiction, poetry)
- **Persuade:** fiction writers and poets nationally and regionally, *including strong writers who think they won't get in*, to apply for fall 2027 (*deadline early March, to confirm*).
- **Behavior to change:** they apply only to fully funded programs (UW, Idaho) or to unfunded low-residency programs that advertise heavily (CM §2), and skip EWU.
- **Insight:** "Only apply to funded programs" (VoC). Funding is the first filter, yet what they want is "a writing community to help me improve my craft" and a way into literary life.
- **New belief:** at EWU I'd be working inside a magazine, a press and a festival, not just a workshop.
- **USP: UNIQUE.** Practica in EWU's own literary institutions: Willow Springs magazine, Willow Springs Books, Get Lit! and Writers in the Community (FG s.25). UW, Idaho and Montana don't run these. It doesn't beat the funding barrier.
- **Say this:** Here you don't only write the book; you learn to edit, publish and put it on a stage.
- **Funding stance: meet it head-on.** Teaching awards exist and are selective; the practica carry the reason to apply.
- **Proof:** selective teaching awards, 7–10 entering a year: teach one composition class per term for a tuition waiver and monthly stipend; extra assistant roles exist (FG s.26). Writing sample carries the greatest weight; no GRE (FG s.26), which answers "am I good enough". Two years, 72 quarter credits, evening classes in Cheney from fall 2026; workshops of 10–15; about 20–24 admitted a year; book-length thesis with an adviser (FG s.20, s.25). Faculty storytelling is a stated EWU emphasis (FG s.4): program director Gregory Spatz; faculty Laura Read (MA). Alumni: Shann Ray, Leyna Krow, Kate Peterson (MA); Jess Walter is BA '87, never an MFA grad (FG s.12). Names and Willow Springs / Get Lit! images CLEARED (Andres Fabian, 2026-10-25); each person's consent is a production step. ⚑ Funding copy says "selective", never "funded". ⚑ 2027 deadline unpublished.
- **Barrier:** Credential (funding, value) plus Aspirational ("am I good enough").

### Master of Occupational Therapy
- **Job: the 2027–28 OTCAS cycle.** This cycle gets a last call only: Nov 9 to Nov 15, 2026, 2 PM PT, using fallback wording.
- **Persuade:** pre-OT graduates and career changers in eastern Washington and the Inland Northwest who meet prerequisites, to apply through OTCAS.
- **Behavior to change:** they assume they need an OTD, see programs at $141k, and stall, pick a doctorate (Whitworth OTD locally) or move away (VoC; CM §3).
- **Insight:** "You do not need a doctorate to be an occupational therapist," yet "all the OT programs are so expensive," and "I want to avoid moving far away from my support system" (VoC).
- **New belief:** I can become an OT here, with a master's, in 2.5 years, at public tuition.
- **USP: UNIQUE (place-based), ⚑.** The public master's route to OT in Spokane: Whitworth offers the OTD; the other regional master's programs are in Tacoma and southern Idaho (CM §3; FG s.35). ⚑ "Only" needs EWU sign-off and a complete check (UW admissions paused as of Oct 20).
- **Say this:** You can become an OT without leaving Spokane. ⚑ Classes are in Spokane, but fieldwork sites are unpublished and "can add transportation and temporary living expenses" (FG s.29): EWU must confirm the line holds, or it narrows to "study in Spokane".
- **⚑-safe fallback (Nov 9–15 only):** "Earn your Master of Occupational Therapy in Spokane. 2.5 years, at EWU's Catalyst campus." No "only", no cost comparison, no total cost.
- **Proof:** 2.5 years full time, Catalyst Building; at least 24 weeks of Level II fieldwork in two full-time placements; cohort capacity 32 (FG s.29); $6,412 WA resident tuition per full-time semester plus $975 surcharge (FG s.32); graduation 96% (89/93, 2023–25); NBCOT 95% new grads, 82% first attempt, 2025 (FG s.33). ⚑ No debt comparison with OTDs; no total cost from semester rates. Certification and licensure remain separate. Prerequisites stay visible: EWU's goal is applicant quality (FG s.4, s.30).
- **Barrier:** Credential (do I need the doctorate, will it pay back).

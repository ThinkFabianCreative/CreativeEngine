## The problem
Prospects shortlist on one question: cost, funding, or whether the degree "counts". There EWU looks like the regional default, so they inquire elsewhere or wait.

EWU wants "pull-based", "high-quality" leads its staff can run alone.

## Platform: one school, three chapters
**Brand promise.** Proposed — EWU to confirm: "EWU exists to put affordable academic excellence and applied work in high-demand fields within reach of people who'd ruled themselves out."

**Core idea.** Proposed — EWU to confirm: stories where the real work is handed to people who didn't think it was meant for them.

**Human truth.** People rule themselves out of the good option (too junior, not good enough, can't afford it) before anyone else does.

**Access sits inside the idea.** EWU talks about "upward social mobility" and "first-generation, low-income" students. We show that through the work, not by announcing it.

**The chapters**, each a real job plus an access fact:
- **MS in Management:** defend a real business solution to faculty (MNGT 591) before anyone has called you a manager. No management experience required.
- **MFA in Creative Writing:** edit the literary magazine Willow Springs on the strength of your pages. The sample counts most; no GRE.
- **Master of Occupational Therapy (MOT):** 24+ weeks treating patients under supervision, with classes in Spokane's Catalyst Building.

**Starting direction (Andres), optional:** MasterClass-style lessons by EWU people inside the real job, ending "Before you graduate, you'll have…" plus a fact.

**Rule:** every piece shows a real EWU job and an access fact, or it doesn't run.

**Visual lead (DesignChamp): the proof object.** A Willow Springs page or Get Lit! program; the MS defense record; the MOT placement record with a Catalyst cue, no sites named until EWU signs off. People doing the job, never being picked.

**Fit with EWU's briefs (approved by Andres).** The core idea shapes headlines, guide covers and page heroes. Guides and hubs still answer the landing-page brief's four questions, so they survive if EWU rejects the idea.

**Landing pages (Andres, Oct 26).** One hub per program. The landing-page brief's topics become sections or dynamic-content variants, which should count toward the contract's "at least 12 program landing pages". Needs EWU sign-off.

**Cautions.** The core idea runs only after EWU approves it Nov 9. Ads naming EWU need its written consent, so the MOT last call must allow for review.

## The job to be done
### MS in Management
Early-career professionals, career changers and new managers pick an MBA or MBL (Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU, EWU's own), a fast online MS, or waiting until they feel "experienced". *They feel too junior for the prestige degree and suspect the alternative is second-best.* Unconfirmed; we test it in 45 to 60 interviews before development.

We want them to inquire and apply, believing the MS fits where they are now.

**The line:** "Earn the master's you can defend, before you're a manager."

**Difference:** the MNGT 591 oral defense, claimed first but not verified unique; needs EWU sign-off. Never "only", never "in person".

**Proof:** MNGT 589 applied project; 3.0 GPA; $75 fee; no assistantships. $427.47 per credit resident, $940.92 non-resident. Never quote a total or the proposal's "$11,628 vs $27,018".

**Blocking:**
- Format and name. Proposed — EWU to confirm: fully online, 37 quarter credits, "MS in Management". Contract, public page and catalog agree; one call suggested hybrid.
- Defense example. Proposed — EWU to confirm: a shift supervisor with no management title defends a scheduling fix that cut her team's overtime. Never a real student.

**Barrier:** doubt the credential counts.

### MFA in Creative Writing (fiction, poetry)
Writers and poets, national and regional, *including strong ones who think they won't get in*, apply only to funded programs (UW, Idaho) or big low-residency ones. They say "only apply to funded programs", yet want "a writing community to help me improve my craft".

We want them to apply for fall 2027, believing EWU puts them inside a magazine, a press and a festival.

**Difference:** practica in EWU's own literary institutions, with faculty such as director Gregory Spatz and Laura Read. Unique, but it doesn't answer funding.

**Proof:** practica at Willow Springs Books and Get Lit!; 7 to 10 selective teaching awards a year with waiver and stipend ("selective", never "funded"; needs EWU sign-off); two years, 72 quarter credits, workshops of 10 to 15, a book-length thesis. Alumni Shann Ray, Leyna Krow, Kate Peterson (not Jess Walter, a BA), with consent per production.

**Open:** Proposed — EWU to confirm: on-campus evening classes in Cheney; applications close early March 2027.

**Barriers:** doubt the credential counts, and doubt they're good enough.

### Master of Occupational Therapy
The campaign targets the 2027–28 cycle on OTCAS, the shared OT application service. This cycle gets a last call only, closing Nov 15 at 2 PM Pacific.

Inland Northwest pre-OT graduates and career changers who meet prerequisites assume they need a doctorate, see $141k programs and stall, pick Whitworth's doctorate, or move away. They want to stay near family and find "all the OT programs are so expensive".

We want them to apply, believing they can become an OT here with a master's in 2.5 years.

**Difference:** the public master's route to OT in Spokane. "Only" needs EWU sign-off.

**The line:** the chapter line. Never "practice as an OT" (students aren't licensed); no cost comparisons. "Without leaving Spokane" needs EWU sign-off.

**Fieldwork.** Proposed — EWU to confirm: two full-time Level II placements across the Inland Northwest; may need travel or temporary housing. Never "in Spokane".

**Proof:** Catalyst Building classes; 24+ weeks of Level II; cohort of 32; $6,412 per semester for Washington residents plus a $975 surcharge; 96% graduation and 95% national board (NBCOT) pass rate, never framed as licensure. Show prerequisites.

**Barrier:** doubt the credential counts.

## By stage

@ CREATIVE BRIEF | EASTERN WASHINGTON UNIVERSITY · GRADUATE RECRUITMENT PILOT | TRAFFIK · v20 · 2026-11-02 · Draft

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · BRAND: EWU graduate programs (MS in Management, MFA in Creative Writing, Master of Occupational Therapy) · SOURCE SCOPE: project-level. The promise and the core creative idea are approved inside TRAFFIK and still proposed to EWU.

## 1. Strategic assets

**Where this sits.** EWU's live positioning is the Region's Polytechnic: hands-on learning, rooted in the liberal arts, in high-demand fields. This work is its graduate proof. It is background for us, never campaign copy.

**Brand promise (proposed to EWU; do not lift: strategy, never a line).** EWU exists so capable people can stop waiting to be chosen and make their own move, with the means at a public price within reach. EWU gives the means: the program, the work she builds, a public price. She makes the move. Nobody, EWU included, promises what happens after.

**Core Creative Idea (proposed to EWU).** *Stories where people stop waiting to be chosen.* The campaign line is "Stop waiting to be chosen." on purpose (Andres's decision), on three conditions: it always sits beside a dated EWU act (a start term, a first workshop, a defense date, a first fieldwork week); it never ends with anyone choosing her, not EWU, admissions, the MFA awards or a boss; and a live-ad check for "stop waiting" runs before lock.

**The gap.** They are already capable, and they are waiting to be chosen: for the tap, the nod, the promotion, for their employer to make it their idea, for the feeling of being ready. The hinge is the moment she stops waiting and chooses herself. EWU is where that choice becomes a concrete step. Employer tuition support is a welcome means, never the wait.

**Proposition (do not lift).** At EWU, capable people stop waiting and start, on a date they pick.

**Why only EWU can say it.** No university or adult-learner advertiser was found using "stop waiting to be chosen". Western Governors University (WGU) is closest on setup ("They Said", "On Your Time") but resolves to study pace; ours resolves to her dated act. Central Washington and Western Washington University own "real world" and "hands-on"; the idea holds against both. Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU and UW are untested. EWU's own values back it: Accessibility ("a more inclusive, obtainable, and equitable college education") and Student Success, which names first-generation, low-income and Pell-recipient students, the people most often told to wait. Background, never copy; EWU never becomes the one who finally "sees" her.

## 2. Objectives

**Business challenge.** EWU wants more enrollments in three graduate programs, with applications and cost per inquiry as supporting measures. Good candidates put it off: they wait for a promotion, an employer's backing or "next year", then shortlist on cost and pick the cheapest, fastest online option or nothing.

**The job, across all three programs.**
- **Persuade** working adults considering a master's **to** request information from an EWU program.
- **Behavior to change:** they put it off, or pick the cheapest, fastest online option as a box to tick.
- **Insight (hypothesis, our synthesis):** capable working adults treat a master's as something they must be chosen for, by a boss, an employer's budget or a feeling of being ready, so doubt about cost becomes one more way of waiting.
- **What people say (public forums, not EWU prospects):** "I'm having a hard time justifying the total investment." · "i don't have enough leadership experience to be in the MBA program" · "even considering a fully funded one feels kind of ridiculous to me lol."
- **New belief (do not lift):** I don't need anyone's go-ahead to start; EWU is a step I can take and afford.
- **New behavior:** request information. Application started is the later measure.
- **Barrier type:** primary, doubt the degree is worth the cost. Second, being told to wait or not feeling ready, carried in stories, never the subject. Agency does the reframe at the brand stage; price and real dates are the proof at inquiry and application.

**Per program: what her own move is.**
- **MS in Management:** she stops waiting to be put in charge and builds a defended portfolio of management work that is hers. Reasons to believe: no management experience required; an applied business project; a final portfolio with an oral defense.
- **MFA in Creative Writing:** she stops waiting to be picked as a writer and spends two years writing. Reasons to believe: seven to ten teaching assistantships a year, each with a tuition waiver and stipend, selective; four paid second-year roles at Willow Springs, Willow Springs Books, Get Lit! and Writers in the Community; no GRE.
- **Master of Occupational Therapy:** she starts the path toward practicing. Reasons to believe: two and a half years, full-time, in Spokane's Catalyst Building; 24 weeks of supervised fieldwork; a cohort of 32; 95% of new graduates passed the national certification exam in 2025 (wording needs EWU sign-off).

## 3. Creative target

*An invented portrait, not a real person or a media persona. Do not lift its wording.*

She is 33 and lives in Spokane Valley. For two years she has run the shift: she trains the new hires, fixes the schedule when it breaks, and is the one the floor manager calls. Her title still says "lead". At her last review she heard "next cycle" again. She has priced master's programs twice and closed the tab both times, half waiting for someone at work to tap her. **Her tension: waiting to be chosen versus moving herself.** The move is enrolling, on a date she picks, at a price she can carry. She does not quit, resent anyone or prove anyone wrong.

- **MFA, same tension:** a writer who already edits other people's work and keeps waiting to feel "real" enough to apply. Her object: other people's manuscripts on her desk, her own draft underneath.
- **MOT, same tension:** a rehab aide who knows what the patient needs next and keeps hearing she should get a few more years in first. EWU asks for 40 hours of experience, not years. Her object: her own day-one schedule and her tools laid out.

## 4. Executional requirements

**Round one.** Two or three campaign directions, each working for all three programs under one look, shown in real placements: a Meta static and carousel, a Performance Max image, a guide cover and an email.

**Production (account team's production brief, Oct 30).** Six guides, two per program, as accessible PDFs; 18 Meta statics, 12 carousel cards, 30 Performance Max images, 6 email heroes; Meta, Performance Max, Search and email copy. Every piece carries the idea; the guides are the offer, not the idea. Web: three program micro-sites, each with four sub-pages or sections (the contract's 12+ pages), where the guides are gated; outside the Oct 30 brief, briefed separately.

**Journey and asset consistency.** Meta ad → program landing page (micro-site) with program content and guide downloads. Images and video are one set: the same people, frames and look run in the ads and on the pages, so the click lands on what the ad showed. Plan every shoot as one set, cropped for 4:5, 1:1, 9:16 and page heroes. AI is used for scamps and mockups only; key image development and photo retouching are part of concept development and production, and are scoped there.

**Timing.** Nov 9 strategy presentation · Nov 15 MOT applications close (2 PM Pacific) · Dec 15 MS winter deadline · early March 2027 MFA applications close (to confirm).

**Budget.** Media budget set by EWU (amount to confirm). Creative production is incremental to the retainer.

**Mandatories and claim rules.**
- Nobody chooses her. EWU, admissions and the MFA awards never become the chooser ("we choose you", "you're in").
- Tension with the culture of waiting, never bitterness at a person: no boss or employer named, pictured or mocked; no quitting or proving anyone wrong.
- No outcome implied: no job, promotion, publication, funding or licensure. MFA funding is selective; never say "funded".
- No pace language: "self-paced", "in as little as", "100% online", "take the next step". No claims about Management's delivery format.
- OT students are not licensed; keep graduation, certification and licensure apart. No identifiable patients.
- No cost comparisons or totals. Never say "only" without EWU sign-off.
- Eagle Red (#B7142E) and white; follow the logo guide; typeface to confirm with Marketing and Communications. WCAG 2.2 AA and accessible PDFs.
- Real EWU people, places, dates and work; no stock or generated stand-ins in final work. EWU's written consent before any ad naming EWU runs.

**Access to secure, dates locked at kickoff with an owner.** Real program dates (term starts, deadlines, defense windows, fieldwork start); consenting students in each program; the defended portfolio; Willow Springs issues and student mark-up; the Catalyst simulation labs; a clinical partner's permission for MOT.

**If access slips.** Real EWU objects and places with consenting students, each carrying her act and her date. A building alone is generic.

## 5. Creative ambition

**The Bar.** Committed: OWNABLE (no other school could run the work unchanged). Target: FRESH (it also feels new to the category), if real access is secured in time.

**Benchmarks.**
- **US Marine Corps, "Battles Won" (JWT, 2017):** character shown in action, before anyone hands out a title.
- **Monash University, "A Future Without Change" (VML, 2020):** the ceiling, a higher bar than this pilot's.

**Category clichés to beat.** Smiling student portraits; campus backdrops; skyline and flying aspiration; "change", "impact", "dream", "potential"; pace words; job-title outcome proof. If Gonzaga, Whitworth, UW or a national online program could run it unchanged, it fails.

## 6. Assessment and measurement

**Priorities.** Enrollments and return on ad spend; applications started and submitted per program; cost per qualified inquiry; guide-to-application rate above a minimum volume agreed at kickoff.

**First paid-social test (design to set with EWU).** Three cells on Meta, same audience, budget and offer: her own work with a real date in frame; a carousel moving from waiting to a dated start; a category-standard control. Cost per inquiry decides; click-through is secondary.

**How the work will be judged.** Does she stop waiting, and does she make the move herself? Is a dated EWU act in it? Does nobody choose her, and nobody look like the villain? Does it rest on a real, checkable EWU fact with no implied outcome? Does it work for all three programs under one look?

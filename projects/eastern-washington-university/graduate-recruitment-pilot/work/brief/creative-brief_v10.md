## The job to be done
### MS in Management
- **Persuade:** early-career professionals, career changers, new managers to inquire and apply; "high-volume, speed-to-lead" (CT 1.A).
- **Behavior to change:** they default to an MBA/MBL (Gonzaga, Whitworth, WSU, EWU's MBA, kept distinct per AB), a fast online MS, or wait until "experienced" (CM §1; VoC).
- **Insight (PROXY; gate before DEVELOP):** *They feel too junior for the prestige degree and suspect the alternative is second-best* (MSG §3; no PNW voice). Validate via PR's 45–60 prospect interviews (PR 3.4).
- **New belief:** the MS is built for where I am now, and it proves itself with work I defend.
- **USP: PRE-EMPTIVE, UNVERIFIED ⚑.** The MNGT 591 oral defense. Never "only"; never "in person".
- **Say this:** Earn the master's you can defend, before you're a manager.
- **Proof:** MNGT 589 applied project; 3.0 GPA, $75 fee; no assistantships (FG s.21–23). $427.47/credit resident, $940.92 non-resident; never a total. Not PR's unsourced "$11,628 vs $27,018".
- **⚑ BLOCKING:** format (CT "fully online (non-OPM)" vs LB/10-21 call hybrid); catalog (37 cr quarterly vs 44 cr 12-month cohort); name (MS vs "MIM", CT); one cleared project and defense.
- **Barrier:** Credential.

- **Persuade:** fiction writers and poets, national ("national niche", CT 1.A) and regional, *including strong writers who think they won't get in*, to apply for fall 2027.
- **Behavior to change:** they apply only to fully funded (UW, Idaho) or heavily advertised low-residency programs (CM §2).
- **Insight:** "Only apply to funded programs" (VoC), yet they want "a writing community to help me improve my craft".
- **New belief:** at EWU I'd work inside a magazine, a press and a festival, not just a workshop.
- **USP: UNIQUE.** Practica in EWU's own literary institutions (FG s.25), with faculty (director Gregory Spatz; Laura Read). Doesn't beat the funding barrier.
- **Say this:** the MFA chapter line.
- **Proof:** practica also at Willow Springs Books and Get Lit! (FG s.25); 7–10 selective teaching awards a year, waiver plus stipend (FG s.26) ⚑ "selective", never "funded" (AB); two years, 72 quarter credits, evening classes in Cheney, workshops of 10–15, book-length thesis (FG s.20, s.25). Alumni Shann Ray, Leyna Krow, Kate Peterson (Jess Walter is BA '87, not MFA); names cleared (Andres, 2026-10-25), consent per production.
- **⚑ OPEN:** format (CT "on-campus" vs LB "Hybrid" vs AB "residential"); 2027 deadline.
- **Barrier:** Credential plus Aspirational.

### Master of Occupational Therapy
- **Job:** the 2027–28 OTCAS cycle; this cycle gets a last call only (closes Nov 15, 2 PM PT). Prerequisite screening (CT 1.A).
- **Persuade:** pre-OT graduates and career changers in the Inland Northwest who meet prerequisites, to apply through OTCAS.
- **Behavior to change:** they assume they need an OTD, see $141k programs, and stall, pick Whitworth's OTD or move away (VoC; CM §3).
- **Insight:** "You do not need a doctorate to be an occupational therapist," yet "all the OT programs are so expensive" and they want to stay near their support system (VoC).
- **New belief:** I can become an OT here, with a master's, in 2.5 years, with classes in Spokane.
- **USP: UNIQUE (place-based) ⚑.** The public master's route to OT in Spokane (CM §3; FG s.35); "only" needs sign-off.
- **Say this:** the MOT chapter line. Never "practice as an OT" (students aren't licensed); no cost comparisons. "Without leaving Spokane" ⚑ (fieldwork sites unpublished, may add travel, FG s.29).
- **Proof:** classes at the Catalyst Building, Spokane (FG); ≥24 weeks Level II fieldwork; cohort 32 (FG s.29); $6,412 WA resident per semester plus $975 surcharge (FG s.32); graduation 96%, NBCOT 95% (FG s.33). Graduation ≠ certification ≠ licensure. Prerequisites visible.
- **Barrier:** Credential.

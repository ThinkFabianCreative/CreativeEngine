# Territory T1 (lead): The real work, handed over

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · STATUS: EXPLORE, lead territory for round 1 (one of 2–3 campaign ideas). Moved out of the brief 2026-10-26 at Andres's direction: the idea is creative output, not brief content.

**Idea:** stories where the real work is handed to people who didn't think it was meant for them.
**Human truth it plays on:** people rule themselves out of the good option before anyone else does.
**Program chapters (draft lines, not approved):**
- MS in Management: "Earn the master's you can defend, before you're a manager."
- MFA in Creative Writing: "Edit a literary magazine on the strength of your pages, not your test scores."
- MOT: "24+ weeks treating patients under supervision, with classes in Spokane."
**Vessel (Andres's starting direction):** MasterClass-style lessons taught by EWU people inside the real job; candidate sign-off "Before you graduate, you'll have…" + a fact. Film is incremental scope.
**Visual lead (DesignChamp):** the proof object: an artifact of the job (Willow Springs page or press proof, faculty sign-off on a defended solution, fieldwork placement record). People shown doing the job, never being chosen.
**Rules it carries:** every piece shows a real EWU job and an access fact; "learn by doing" alone fails the competitor swap.

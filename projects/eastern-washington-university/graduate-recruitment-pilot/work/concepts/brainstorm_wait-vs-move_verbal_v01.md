# Brainstorm: wait vs move (verbal v01)

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · BRAND: EWU graduate programs (MS in Management, MFA in Creative Writing, MOT) · SOURCE SCOPE: project-level · AUTHOR: word-smither · DATE: 2026-10-26 · MODE: EXPLORE (no verdicts, rough) · STATUS: brainstorm with Andres; nothing EWU-approved.

Built from: strategy/ewu-strategic-assets_v10.md (line "Stop waiting to be chosen."; rule 2: tension with the culture of waiting, never bitterness at a person), concepts/brainstorm_promotion-phrases_verbal_v01.md.
Andres's references: (1) the resignation letter, reframed as "I resign from waiting"; (2) the structure of a musical's two-ways-of-living duet (patience vs seizing the moment, neither a villain). Structure only: no lyrics, song titles, character names or paraphrase in copy.

Guardrails held: no boss named or blamed; no promotion or job outcome; MOT no licensure or patients; MFA no publication or award; MS keeps her job; nothing ends with anyone choosing her, EWU included. Dates marked [confirm] are placeholders for real EWU term dates.

Duet rule: the wait voice is never stupid or cruel. It is careful, loving, reasonable. It has kept her safe. The move voice does not win an argument; it just acts. Both voices are hers.

## Ideas

### 1. Two Voices, One Woman
- **Idea:** Split-screen or dual VO. Same woman, same kitchen, two takes on the same evening. Left voice: "Next year makes more sense." Right voice opens the application. The edit lets both be right; only one of them has a date.
- **Lines:** "One of you is careful. One of you has a start date." · "Both voices are yours. Pick which one signs."
- **Fits:** all three; MFA strongest (writers already live with two voices).

### 2. I Resign From Waiting
- **Idea:** The letter as the campaign artifact. Formal, dated, signed; addressed to Waiting, not a person. MS and MFA keep their jobs; MOT's letter can double as a real, warm resignation. Full letters below.
- **Lines:** "Effective today, I resign from waiting." · "Two weeks' notice, given to myself."
- **Fits:** all three. Formats: static letter, letter being typed (social), letter-in-hand OOH.

### 3. The Exit Interview
- **Idea:** HR exit-interview format, but the departing employee is her own patience. Questions on a clipboard ("Reason for leaving?" "Would you return?"), answered by the wait voice, gently. It leaves on good terms.
- **Lines:** "Reason for leaving: she started." · "Would you rehire Waiting? Sometimes. Not this time."
- **Fits:** MS (corporate language native). Light humor, no grievance.

### 4. Counterpoint
- **Idea:** A 30s spot scored as a duet: two melodic lines, one slow and sustained (wait), one quick and rising (move), sung or hummed, no lyrics. They overlap, then the slow line resolves into the quick one on a dated frame. Sound carries the idea; copy is only the end card.
- **Lines:** end card: "Spring workshop starts [date, confirm]. Stop waiting to be chosen."
- **Fits:** MFA (rhythm, voice), MOT (movement). Original score only, no reference melody.

### 5. Out of Office
- **Idea:** The auto-reply as her dated act. Her inner wait voice is set to Out of Office. "I'm away from waiting from [date]. For hesitation, please contact Next Year."
- **Lines:** "Out of office: Waiting. Back: never scheduled." · "Auto-reply on. Second-guessing off."
- **Fits:** MS and MFA (keep their jobs, so the email client is literal). Lightweight social, email subject lines.

### 6. Minutes of the Meeting
- **Idea:** She holds a meeting with herself. Two attendees, both her. Agenda item 1: "Next year?" Discussion noted fairly. Motion: apply. Carried, 1 to 1, chair's casting vote. She is the chair (ties to the chair device without anyone giving her a seat).
- **Lines:** "Motion carried. Chair's vote." · "Minutes: Waiting raised valid concerns. Noted. Overruled."
- **Fits:** MS strongest; works as carousel (agenda, discussion, vote, date).

### 7. Notice Period
- **Idea:** Every resignation has a notice period. Hers is the gap between today and the first dated EWU act. Countdown format: "Notice period: 68 days." Each day the wait voice gets a little quieter in the copy, shrinking type.
- **Lines:** "Notice given. Last day of waiting: [date, confirm]." · "Waiting is working its notice."
- **Fits:** all three; built for retargeting near deadlines (urgency without a discount).

### 8. Both Were Right
- **Idea:** The generous version of the duet. Long copy that thanks the wait voice: it kept her solvent, kept her steady, got her here. Then it says the timing changed. No villain, just a turn.
- **Lines:** "Waiting got you this far. Thank it. Then go." · "Patience did its job. Now do yours." (watch: "do" is fine as verb, avoid "doing")
- **Fits:** MOT (career changer, the gratitude is real), MS.

## Resignation letters (rough, one per program)

### MS in Management (she keeps her job)

October 26, 2026

To: Waiting
Re: Notice of resignation

Please accept this letter as formal notice that I am resigning from my position as someone who waits. This is effective immediately.

I am grateful for our years together. You kept me patient, polite and ready for a conversation that always seemed to belong to next cycle. I learned a great deal about standing still with good posture.

To be clear, I am not leaving my job. I am leaving you. On [start date, confirm], I begin the MS in Management at Eastern Washington University, building a portfolio of management work I will defend in my own voice.

Stop waiting to be chosen.

Sincerely,
Dana Ruiz

### MFA in Creative Writing (she keeps her job)

October 26, 2026

To: Maybe Next Year
Re: Resignation, effective today

I am writing to formally resign from the role of writer-in-waiting, a position I have held, without pay or pages, for six years.

Thank you for your concern. You were right that the timing was never perfect, and that my day job matters. I am keeping it.

What I am giving up is you. On [first workshop date, confirm], I sit down in my first workshop of the MFA in Creative Writing at Eastern Washington University with a draft I started myself, and two years to keep writing it.

Stop waiting to be chosen.

Respectfully,
Marisol Vega

### Master of Occupational Therapy (a real, warm resignation)

October 26, 2026

To the team I have loved working with,
Re: Letter of resignation

Please accept this as formal notice of my resignation, effective [date, confirm]. I am leaving with gratitude, not complaint. You taught me patience, and I will carry it.

I am also resigning from something else: waiting until it felt like the right time to change careers. It never did, so I am choosing the time.

On [program start, confirm], I begin the Master of Occupational Therapy at Eastern Washington University in Spokane, starting the path toward practice, full-time, in person.

Stop waiting to be chosen.

With thanks,
Renee Okafor

# On Ice: campus brand act (idea, v01)

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · 2026-11-08 · Status: IDEA for the Nov 13 deck ("bolder" slide). Not approved, not budgeted.
Graphic language only from Zug Zug (Minneapolis) and the fake Criterion "Encino Man" cover; their stories are not used.

## The act
**Act 1, the frozen block (2–3 days).** A tall block of cloudy, frosted ice on the Cheney campus mall or PUB (MS, MFA) and the Catalyst Building plaza, Spokane (MOT). Inside, half-visible: a dressed mannequin (abstract head, no realistic face) in her real clothes: cardigan, polo, lanyard with a blank badge (MOT: scrubs, fleece, gait belt). A crimson-handled sledgehammer and chisel lean against the block. Small plaque, fine type, no logo: *"On ice since 2019."*

**Act 2, the break (one night).** Broken open from the inside: hollow shell, slabs on the ground, wet footprints leading toward the Graduate Studies door (MOT: the Catalyst door), sledgehammer left behind. The mannequin never reappears. New plaque: *"She stopped waiting to be chosen. Graduate applications are open."* + QR to the program micro-site.

**Act 3, the content.** Overnight time-lapse/hidden camera of the break, cut like the film (excuses, slams, reveal). The block (day 1) and the empty shell are shot as the practical key image and aftermath plates.

## Build
- Ice-sculpture vendor stacks and fuses ~300 lb blocks around the mannequin, carving cavities; cloudy/bubbly ice is the normal result or a frosting pass (to confirm with vendor).
- Insulated base/pallet, drainage, barrier, tip-proof; shaded, north-facing site.
- Overnight: vendor cuts the front, removes the mannequin, breaks the shell from the inside out, scatters slabs, lays footprints.

## Weather (from memory; check NOAA normals)
Spokane/Cheney Dec–Feb highs ~33–40°F, lows ~22–26°F. Run in January (start of winter quarter), keep dates flexible by a week, refresh plan from the vendor, shoot the key image on day 1. Never indoors.

## Rules
- Nobody else breaks it: no crew seen, no ceremony, no EWU official.
- Stylized mannequin, never a lifelike frozen body.
- No boss, excuse or person mocked on the plaques.
- Safety: campus facilities and police in the loop; cordon, drainage, melt plan.
- MOT block in January points to the next application cycle (current cycle closes Nov 15, 2026).

## Needs
Campus approval; ice vendor quote (build, delivery, overnight break, refresh); mannequin and wardrobe; camera crew or time-lapse rig; budget as incremental grassroots, outside the $140K media plan.

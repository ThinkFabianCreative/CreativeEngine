CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · BRAND: EWU graduate programs (MS in Management, MFA in Creative Writing, Master of Occupational Therapy) · SOURCE SCOPE: project-level · AUTHOR: word-smither · DATE: 2026-10-26 · MODE: EXPLORE (no verdicts, no scores) · STATUS: brainstorm with Andres; nothing EWU-approved.

Built from: brief/creative-brief_v19.md, copy/the-room_copy_v02.md (lead idea The Room, line "Somebody's going to see this."). Provocation (Andres): "We're constantly told at work the barriers to get a promotion. 'You got big shoes to fill' — self-doubt and anxiety."

Guardrail from round one: naming barriers is not an idea. Every flip below ends with the person in front of others, carrying the moment. The phrase is the setup; the room is the answer.

## 2. Five rough ideas

### 1. Bring Your Own Shoes
- **Idea:** Nobody fills big shoes in a room; people watch you walk in your own.
- **Lines:** "Big shoes to fill? Wear yours to the front." · "Forget filling them. Somebody's going to watch you walk." · Footwear-only shots: a row of seated feet, one pair standing.
- **Tie to The Room:** card 1 is the room's feet (the watchers), card 3 is one pair stepping up. The shoes are the person, not the predecessor.
- **Fits:** MS (strongest: leading without the title, inherited role). MOT works visually (clinic shoes on sim lab floor).

### 2. This Cycle
- **Idea:** "Let's revisit next cycle" answered by a dated moment on the calendar where you stand up anyway.
- **Lines:** "Let's revisit next cycle." / "Applied project defense: this spring." · "Your review says next cycle. Your cohort says Thursday." · "Pinned for later. Presented on Tuesday."
- **Tie to The Room:** the calendar invite is the room. Somebody's going to see this, and it already has a date.
- **Fits:** MS (portfolio, oral defense). Works with program deadlines, which are weeks away: urgency without a discount.

### 3. Ready Is Watched
- **Idea:** Nobody hands you "ready." It shows up while people are watching, then you know.
- **Lines:** "Not ready yet? Neither was anyone at the plinth." · "Ready looked like this: hands on the gait belt, cohort watching." · "Ready isn't a feeling. It's a Tuesday in the sim lab."
- **Tie to The Room:** card 2 (you watching a classmate) is where "not ready" lives; card 3 (your turn) is where it ends.
- **Fits:** MOT (career changer, master's vs doctorate doubt). No licensure, no outcomes promised: the claim is only that the cohort watches you practice.

### 4. Under Your Belt
- **Idea:** "Get a few more years under your belt" swapped for the belt you actually wear in the room.
- **Lines:** "A few more years under your belt? Try a gait belt." · "Experience is something your cohort watches you get." · MS variant: "More years under your belt, or one defense in front of a panel and cohort." (keep panel off-centre per rules)
- **Tie to The Room:** literal object in frame; the room watches the belt get buckled.
- **Fits:** MOT (pun lands only here; flag it as a pun, may be too cute). MS variant is weaker.

### 5. Who Do You Think You Are?
- **Idea:** Take the head-voice question literally and answer it with a role, said in front of others.
- **Lines:** MFA: "Who are you to edit someone else's story? Their editor." · "Who do you think you are? The one who made the case for this page." · MS: "Who do you think you are? Ask the room after your slide."
- **Tie to The Room:** the answer is given by the room, not by the person. MFA card 4 caption "Notes by [Name]" is the answer.
- **Fits:** MFA (strongest: editing and championing others' work at Willow Springs, Get Lit!; never her own publication). Also MS.

## 3. Recurring device candidates

- **The struck phrase.** The overheard line set small in quotes, struck through in EWU red (links to the Red Mark direction), the room image answering. One phrase per execution, rotated across the 16. Risk: drifts back into naming barriers if the image is weak; the room must be the hero, not the strikethrough.
- **"Said in a review. Answered in a room."** A sign-off frame pairing a phrase with a date and place. Lets the campaign run many phrases without new lines.
- **"Next cycle" as a calendar device.** Dated cards (MS defense, MFA reading at Get Lit!, MOT sim lab session) as a series; needs real dates from EWU.
- **Shoes.** Visual-verbal motif that travels across all three programs if DesignChamp wants it.

## 4. Checks
- Banned list scanned: no "count(s) in", "are you in", "show your work", "doing" (except the quoted phrase 8, flagged), "hands-on", "real world".
- MOT: no licensure, no NBCOT, no "patient". MFA: editing others' work only. No cost comparison.
- "Somebody's going to see this." used only over room images.

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · STATUS: EXPLORE input for WordSmither and DesignChamp (2026-10-26, Andres direction). Mechanisms to take and remix, not executions to copy.

Anchored to the proposed core idea (strategic assets v06, not yet approved): **people show what they can do, in front of others, before their field counts them in.** The gap to dramatize: already capable, not yet counted.

## 5. Craft and format

- **Andres's MasterClass idea, flipped:** the student teaches what they just proved, rather than a celebrity teaching (see T1).
- **Monash, "A Future Without Change"** (D&AD, Cannes) [R]: the ambition benchmark for a university campaign.
- **Editors' mark-up** (*The New Yorker*, *Paris Review*) [K]: red-pen marks as a visual system, MFA first, possibly all three.
- **The defense room as a stage** (*Shark Tank*, TED) [K]: the MS chapter, lit like a stage.

## 6. Format note: the static carousel

A carousel settles person-or-object: each card does one job, and the swipe builds the scene (shown, then judged, then counted in). One possible shape, illustrative only:

1. The person, mid-work, gaze on the work (the hook; a face helps engagement here)
2. The proof object, close up
3. The room: who is watching
4. The moment they're counted in
5. The fact and the access: plain line, public price, request information

- Card one must work alone; many people never swipe.
- Same structure for all three programs, different proof.
- Static, so it runs on real stills and consenting students: keeps the OWNABLE fallback alive without video budget.
- Tested against a single image and a category-standard control (strategic assets v06, measurement item 6).

## Don't steal

- Sullivan University, "The School of Doing" [R]: owns "doing".
- Fayetteville State, "Are You In?" [R]: owns "are you in"; keep "counts them in" lines away from it.
- The category look: smiling graduate, regalia, campus backdrop (11 of 29 in our scan) [R].
- The military structure where the institution judges you [R].

**Suggested starting combination:** the chalkboard story shape (work first, reveal second) + "the work is the ad" as the media idea + the handshake as the moment the field counts them in.

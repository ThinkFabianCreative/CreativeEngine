# The Room: ChatGPT prompts, The Reverse Angle (v01)

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · For Andres to run in his own ChatGPT account. Pitch comps only; the campaign will be shot in EWU's rooms. Source: `room_photo-directions_v01.md` (DesignChamp), Direction 2.

## How to run them

1. Paste one prompt per chat (fresh chat each time, so styles don't bleed).
2. If ChatGPT offers sizes, choose **portrait**. If it returns 2:3, that's fine; we crop to 4:5 keeping the floor edge.
3. If the result looks glossy or the faces look generic, reply: *"Less polished. Keep the film grain, natural skin texture and the dark background. Nobody looks into the lens."* Don't accept any rewrite that adds "vibrant", "stunning" or "cinematic lighting".
4. **Leave the bottom strip empty.** The red floor band (#B7142E) is added afterwards in Figma; don't ask ChatGPT for red.
5. Make 2–3 versions of each and send me the best one or two per program.
6. **Reference images (your call):** uploading public photographer work for style is fine. EWU's own photos (for example the Creative Writing page's student-on-the-lawn hero) are EWU material and may show identifiable students: only upload them with EWU's permission. They're also the look we're trying to beat, so better used in the deck as the "before".

Label every result: *pitch comp, generated, stand-ins; to be shot in EWU's rooms.* Credit Bradford Young, Shabier Kirchner and Harris Savides in the deck.

## 1. MS in Management (the presentation room)

Cinematic film still, the reverse shot in the style of Bradford Young's cinematography in Selma: the view from where a graduate student stands to present her business project, at standing eye height, locked off and level. A small seminar room, mixed chairs. Six cohort classmates and two faculty readers seated among them, readers to one side, not at a table and not in front. Front row, sharp: a plus-size Mexican-American woman, late 30s, burgundy blouse, leaning forward with interest; a Black man in his 40s, heavyset, in a quarter-zip, hand at his mouth, about to ask a question; a white woman in her late 20s, average build, small smile of recognition. Second row soft: a South Asian man in his 30s, a white professor in her 60s with silver hair and reading glasses. All eyes converge just past the camera, to the left. A single cool key light from the camera side, the glow of a projected slide, carves their faces out of a dark room; the background falls two stops. 40mm lens at f/2.8, shallow focus on the front row. A grey carpet floor edge runs cleanly across the bottom tenth of the frame, empty. Kodak Vision3 500T look, cool shadows, warm skin, fine film grain, soft highlights, natural pores and uneven skin tone, unretouched, no beauty lighting, nobody looking into the lens, no logos or text. 4:5 vertical.

## 2. MFA in Creative Writing (hosting the public reading)

Cinematic film still in the style of Shabier Kirchner's 16mm cinematography in Small Axe and Bradford Young: the reverse shot from a small stage at a public literary reading, from the position of the graduate student hosting the event at the microphone, camera about a meter above the front row, looking gently down. Public audience in the dark, front two rows caught in warm amber stage spill from the camera side. Sharp in front: a Black woman in her 60s with close-cropped grey hair, eyes bright, hand at her mouth; an Afro-Latino man in his 20s, heavyset, leaning forward with elbows on knees; a white woman in her 40s, average build, in a wool coat, head slightly tilted. Softer behind: an East Asian woman in her early 20s, an older white man in a flannel shirt, silhouettes beyond. Every gaze lands just past the camera to the right, on the host. Deep shadow behind. 40mm lens at f/2.8. The front edge of the wooden stage runs horizontally across the bottom tenth of the frame, empty, a little worn. No windows, no exterior, no signage. Kodak Vision3 500T look, warm skin, deep blacks, medium 16mm-like grain, natural skin texture, unretouched, no glossy finish, no logos or text. 4:5 vertical.

*Change from DesignChamp's draft: she is hosting the reading (a documented EWU Get Lit! internship role), not reading her own work.*

## 3. Master of Occupational Therapy (the simulation lab)

Cinematic film still in the style of Harris Savides' cinematography in Elephant: the reverse shot from beside a hospital-style bed in a university occupational therapy simulation lab, at the student's standing eye height, locked off and level. Bottom left foreground, out of focus: the back of the head and shoulder of a classmate in scrubs playing the client, seated on the bed edge with a gait belt at the waist. Facing camera, looking just past it at the student: a loose arc of five classmates in navy and grey scrubs, a plus-size white woman in her mid-20s holding a clipboard, a Mexican-American man in his late 20s leaning in, a Black woman in her early 30s, average build, nodding, an Indigenous woman in her 20s with a braid; at the far right, an instructor in her 50s with grey hair, lanyard, arms folded, observing from the side. Bed rail, IV pole and wall equipment soft behind. Flat even overhead fluorescent light, slight green neutrality, faces still the brightest elements. 40mm lens at f/2.8. The pale vinyl floor line runs cleanly across the bottom tenth, empty. Kodak Vision3 500T look balanced for fluorescent, fine grain, real skin, unretouched, no patients, no logos or text. 4:5 vertical.

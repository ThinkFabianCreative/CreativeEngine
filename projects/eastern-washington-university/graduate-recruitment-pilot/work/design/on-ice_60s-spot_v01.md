## What the 60s adds
More people. The 30s is one woman. The 60s cuts between four "sculptors" in four lit blocks in the same black studio (MS woman, Native American man, silver-haired MFA writer, Black OT student), each answering a different excuse with a slam. The audience reads a studio of artists. The reveal turns it: every one of them is carving themself out. The end is all four walking out of the light in different directions.

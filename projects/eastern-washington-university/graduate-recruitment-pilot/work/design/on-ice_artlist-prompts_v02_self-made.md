# On Ice: Artlist prompts v02, Self Made Man composition

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · design-champ · 2026-11-08
SCAMPS / MOCKUPS ONLY; key image is developed in concept development and production. Label: *scamp, generated, stand-ins.*
Decision (Andres, 2026-11-08): copy Bobbie Carlyle's *Self Made Man* composition, translated into ice. Supersedes v01's "not Self Made" rule for composition. Credit Bobbie Carlyle in the deck.

## The composition we take
- **One block, one figure, frontal, slightly low camera.** The figure rises out of the top of a rough rectangular block; the block is the base of the picture.
- **Free from the waist up, locked from the hips down.** The legs are still raw, unbroken block.
- **Hammer raised overhead in one hand, chisel set against the block in the other**, striking down at her own waist line. This is the action that makes the picture.
- **Head turned slightly down toward the strike**, focused.
- **Rough to finished:** the block is raw and faceted at the bottom; her body is fully formed at the top.

## The translation into On Ice
- Bronze/stone becomes **cloudy, wet, real ice**; chips become ice shards in the air and meltwater at the base.
- She is a real working woman in her own clothes, not a bronze nude.
- **The crimson object (#B7142E) is the hammer handle.** Only red in frame.
- Setting: the plinth sits **in her workplace** (the career on ice), so the picture still reads as work, not sculpture. A studio version is included for OOH and the guide covers.

## Setup in Artlist
- Nano Banana Pro. Generate the 4:5 hero; reuse it as the reference for 9:16, 1:1, 16:9 and the 3:4 cover ("same woman, same pose, same block").
- Upload a photo of *Self Made Man* as a **composition reference only** (pose and framing), with: "match the pose and framing of the reference; the material is ice, the figure is the woman described."
- Fix-ups: *"Ice, not stone or bronze: translucent, wet, cracked."* · *"Hammer raised higher, mid-swing."* · *"Only the hammer handle is red."*

## 1. MS in Management

**Hero 4:5**
> Documentary-style photograph, vertical 4:5, composed exactly like Bobbie Carlyle's sculpture "Self Made Man": a single figure rising out of the top of a rough rectangular block, seen frontally from a slightly low camera. The block is cloudy, wet, real ice, about waist high, standing on the grey carpet of an open-plan office after hours: cubicle walls, an empty whiteboard, one monitor still on. A Mexican American woman, about 36, average build, dark hair tied back, navy cardigan over a work polo, lanyard with a blank badge, is free from the waist up; her hips and legs are still unbroken ice. Her right arm raises a hammer high overhead, its handle deep crimson (#B7142E); her left hand sets a steel chisel against the ice at her own waist. Head tilted down to the strike, jaw set. Ice chips flying from the chisel point, meltwater running down the block. Light: office fluorescents neutralised to grey, one hard key from high left like a sculpture spotlight, ice backlit so its cracks glow grey-white. 35mm, f/8, sharp. Grade: cool neutral greys, true warm skin with real pores, Portra 800 grain, no retouching. The hammer handle is the only red. Bottom 25% is the plain base of the ice block and wet floor, for text. No text, no logos, no stone, no bronze, no statue finish, no blue glow, no sparkles.

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · Author: design-champ · 2026-11-08
SCAMPS / MOCKUPS ONLY. AI frames sell the idea for the Nov 13 territories. The key image is developed in concept development and production (practical ice, consenting EWU people, retouching). Label every frame: *scamp, generated, stand-ins.*
Source: `work/concepts/on-ice_visual_v01.md` (Route C, documentary in situ). Earlier ChatGPT versions: `on-ice_chatgpt-prompts_v01.md`.

## Setup in Artlist

- **Model:** Nano Banana Pro for the hero frames: best skin, hands and ice physics, and it holds a character across edits. Seedream as a second opinion if the ice turns glassy.
- **One woman per program, held across every frame.** Generate the 4:5 hero first. Then upload it as the reference for every other frame (9:16, 1:1, landing hero, guide cover) with "same woman, same clothes, same location". That's the ad-to-landing-page consistency rule.
- **Ratios per frame set:** 4:5 (Meta feed, the master), 9:16 (Stories/Reels), 1:1, 16:9 (micro-site hero), and 3:4 for guide covers.
- **No text in the image.** Headline and line go on in Figma.
- **Fix-up lines** (send as an edit, don't regenerate): *"Less polished: cloudy real ice, grey not blue, no sparkles, real skin pores."* · *"She's determined, not scared."* · *"Remove every red except the [object]."* · *"Ice, not stone: translucent, wet, cracked."*

## Prompt anatomy (every prompt follows it)
Format and style → who she is (age, body, clothes from her life today) → the ice and how far out she is (30–70%) → her action and the one red object → the place she works now → light → lens → grade and texture → space for type → negatives.

## 1. MS in Management · "after hours, still the lead"

**Hero 4:5**
> Documentary photograph, vertical 4:5, staged like a Jeff Wall picture with the cool patience of Nadav Kander. A Mexican American woman, about 36, average build, dark hair tied back, navy cardigan over a work polo, a lanyard with a blank badge, is breaking herself out of a block of cloudy, wet, real ice that holds her from the hips down, in an open-plan office after hours: grey cubicle walls, an empty whiteboard, one monitor still on. Her arms and shoulders are free. She drives her shoulder into a slab of ice and it splits away mid-fall, shards in the air. Her right hand grips a steel ice pick with a deep crimson handle (#B7142E). Jaw set, eyes on the ice, not the camera. Meltwater spreading over grey carpet tiles. Light: overhead fluorescents neutralised to grey, one soft window key from the left, the ice backlit so internal cracks glow grey-white. 35mm, eye level, f/8, everything sharp. Grade: cool neutral greys, true warm skin with real pores and flyaway hairs, Kodak Portra 800 grain, no retouching. The crimson handle is the only saturated color. Bottom 30% of the frame is plain wet floor with ice shards, for text. No text, no logos, no blue glow, no sparkles, no stone, no statue, not fantasy.

**Detail 1:1 (carousel card / micro-site section):**
> Same woman, same office, same light. Close-up of her hands: one gripping the crimson-handled ice pick, the other pushing a cracked slab away, water running over her knuckles, a blank lanyard badge dangling half-frozen in the ice. Shallow focus on the hands, 85mm, f/4. No text.

**Landing hero 16:9:**
> Same woman, same office, wide horizontal 16:9. She stands at the left third, one leg already out of the waist-high ice block, the crimson ice pick at her side, looking toward the dark right side of the frame, which is an empty row of desks, plain and out of focus, for headline space. Same light and grade.

## 2. MFA in Creative Writing · "the draft under the pile"

**Hero 4:5**
> Documentary photograph, vertical 4:5, in the spirit of Dawoud Bey and Philip-Lorca diCorcia, quiet and cool. An Afro-Latina woman, about 31, plus-size, natural hair in a loose puff, oversized grey sweater, ink on the side of her hand, sits at a cluttered kitchen table at night, and the table, her chair and her lower body are locked in a block of cloudy, wet ice. Stacks of other people's manuscripts with red-pen edits sit frozen inside the ice; her own handwritten draft is breaking free on top, pages cracking loose. Her writing arm is free and she grips a pen with a crimson barrel (#B7142E) like a tool, chipping at the ice. Concentrated, almost a smile at herself, not looking at the camera. Light: one warm-neutral pendant lamp corrected to cool white, ice backlit with grey fractures. 35mm, seated eye level, f/5.6. Grade: slate grey and cool blue-grey, true warm skin with real texture, fine film grain, no retouching. The crimson pen is the only red (the edits on the manuscripts are grey pencil, not red). Bottom 30% plain dark floor with meltwater, for text. No legible text, no book covers, no logos, no sparkles, no stone.

**Detail 1:1:**
> Same woman, same table. Top-down close-up: her hand with the crimson pen breaking a frozen corner off her own draft page, ice crumbs on the paper, other manuscripts blurred under the ice. No legible words.

**Landing hero 16:9:**
> Same woman and table, wide 16:9. She is at the right third, half free, pages lifting off the ice; the left side is the dark kitchen and a window at night, empty for the headline. Same light and grade.

## 3. Master of Occupational Therapy · "a few more years"

**Hero 4:5**
> Documentary photograph, vertical 4:5, with Rineke Dijkstra's honest portraiture and Alec Soth's plain dignity. A Filipina American woman, about 28, athletic build, hair in a practical braid, navy scrubs top from her current aide job with a fleece vest, stands in an empty hospital rehab gym after shift: parallel bars, a parked wheelchair, a blue therapy mat. A block of cloudy, wet ice holds her from the thighs down and locks her left forearm. One leg is already over the edge. Her free right hand wears a crimson work glove (#B7142E) and wrenches her left arm free; slabs crack and drop. A grey gait belt hangs frozen at her waist. Effortful, mid-breath, eyes on her arm. No patients anywhere. Light: fluorescents neutralised to grey, daylight key from tall windows at right, ice backlit with grey-white cracks. 35mm, eye level, f/8. Grade: cool desaturated greys, true skin with pores and flush, Portra 800 grain, no retouching. The crimson glove is the only red. Bottom 30% plain floor with meltwater and shards, for text. No text, no logos, no patients, no blue glow, no sparkles, no stone.

**Detail 1:1:**
> Same woman, same gym. Close-up of the crimson-gloved hand pulling a goniometer and her own day-one schedule card out of the cracking ice; the card is blank except for a date in pencil, slightly blurred. 85mm, f/4.

**Landing hero 16:9:**
> Same woman, same gym, wide 16:9. She steps fully out of the block at the left third, ice shattering behind her, crimson glove at her side; the right two-thirds is the empty gym with parallel bars soft in the background, for headline space. Same light and grade.

## Guide cover (3:4, one per program, same woman)
> Same woman, same place, vertical 3:4. A calm after-moment: the broken ice block behind her, puddles and shards at her feet, she is sitting on the edge of the block, catching her breath, holding her one crimson object. Top 40% of the frame is plain, cool grey background for the guide title. Same light and grade. No text.

## Quality check before a frame goes in the deck
- She is 30–70% free. Not fully out, not fully trapped.
- She looks determined, not afraid. Nobody helps her, and nobody watches.
- Ice reads as wet and cloudy, not glass, stone or carbonite.
- Exactly one red object, and no other red in frame.
- Hands, fingers and the pick or glove are anatomically right (zoom in).
- Bottom-30% type zone is clean.
- The same woman holds across her 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 and cover.

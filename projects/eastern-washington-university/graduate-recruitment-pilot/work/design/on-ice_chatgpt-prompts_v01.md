# On Ice: ChatGPT prompts (v01)

CLIENT: Eastern Washington University · PROJECT: graduate-recruitment-pilot · For Andres to run in his own ChatGPT account. Pitch comps only; the campaign will be shot practically with real ice and consenting EWU students. Source: `work/concepts/on-ice_visual_v01.md` (DesignChamp, Route C: documentary in situ).

## How to run them

1. One prompt per fresh chat. Ask for **portrait**; if you get 2:3, we crop to 4:5.
2. If it comes back glossy, sparkly or fantasy-blue, reply: *"Less polished. Real cloudy ice, grey not blue, no sparkles, real skin texture, documentary photograph. The red object is the only red."*
3. **She must look in control, not in danger** (the Frankfurt fire "trapped under ice" ad is the wrong reading). If she looks scared, reply: *"She's breaking out with effort and determination, not fear."*
4. **Never stone.** If it drifts toward carving or a statue, regenerate.
5. Keep the bottom 30% empty for type. No text in the image; the headline and line go on in Figma.
6. Make 2–3 per program and send me the best one or two.

Label every result: *pitch comp, generated, stand-ins; to be shot practically.* Credit LaToya Ruby Frazier, Jeff Wall, Nadav Kander, Dawoud Bey, Philip-Lorca diCorcia, Rineke Dijkstra and Alec Soth in the deck.

Headlines these sit under (WordSmither, `work/copy/on-ice_copy_v01.md`):
- MS: *Your career's been on ice since 2022.*
- MFA: *Your novel's been on ice since 2019.*
- MOT: *"A few more years." You've heard it for a few years.*
- Line on all: **Stop waiting to be chosen.**

## 1. MS in Management (after-hours office)

Documentary photograph, vertical 4:5, in the style of LaToya Ruby Frazier and Jeff Wall's staged-documentary pictures, with the cool patience of Nadav Kander. A Mexican American woman, about 38, average build, shoulder-length dark hair tied back, reading glasses pushed up on her head, plain navy cardigan over a work polo with a lanyard, is breaking herself out of a solid block of clear, cloudy-white ice that encases her from the knees to the chest, standing in an open-plan office after hours: grey cubicle walls, a whiteboard with someone else's org chart, an empty office chair. She has already freed both arms and shoulders; with her shoulder she is shoving a large slab of ice away and it is mid-fall, shards in the air. Her right hand grips a steel ice pick with a deep crimson red handle (#B7142E); her left hand holds an open laptop pressed against her chest, frost still on its lid. Jaw set, eyes on the ice, not the camera, breath visible. Real meltwater pooling on grey carpet tiles. Light: overhead fluorescent neutralised to grey plus one soft daylight key from a window at left, backlight making the ice glow grey-white with internal fractures. 35mm lens at eye level, f/8, everything sharp. Grade: cool neutral greys and grey-blue shadows, true slightly warm skin with real pores, freckles, under-eye texture and flyaway hairs, no retouching, Kodak Portra 800 grain. The crimson handle is the only saturated color; no other red anywhere. Keep the bottom 30% of the frame as plain wet floor with scattered ice shards and no subject, for text. No text, no logos, no glossy skin, no blue glow, no sparkles, not a fantasy look.

## 2. MFA in Creative Writing (library carrel at night)

Documentary photograph, vertical 4:5, in the style of Dawoud Bey and Philip-Lorca diCorcia, cool and quiet like Nadav Kander. An Afro-Latina woman, about 32, plus-size, natural hair in a loose puff, oversized grey sweater, ink on the side of her hand, is breaking out of a block of clear, cloudy-white ice that holds her lower body and the old desk chair she sits on, in a library carrel at night: stacked returned books, a corkboard, a sticky note reading nothing legible. Her head, shoulders and writing arm are free; she has just snapped her arm loose and chunks of ice are dropping; handwritten manuscript pages, her own draft with crossings-out, are frozen mid-slide at the edge of the ice and beginning to fall free. She grips a pen with a crimson red barrel (#B7142E) in her fist like a tool. Concentrated, half a laugh at herself, breath visible, not looking at camera. Light: a single desk lamp neutralised to cool white plus soft overhead fluorescent, ice backlit so its fractures glow grey. 35mm lens at seated eye level, f/5.6. Grade: cool grey and slate blue, true warm skin with real texture and pores, no retouching, fine film grain. The crimson pen is the only red. Bottom 30% of the frame stays as plain dark floor with meltwater and shards, no subject, for text. No text, no book covers, no logos, no glossy skin, no blue glow, no sparkles.

## 3. Master of Occupational Therapy (the rehab gym where she works now)

Documentary photograph, vertical 4:5, in the style of Rineke Dijkstra's honest portraits and Jeff Wall's staged-documentary images, with Alec Soth's plain dignity. A white woman, about 29, average-to-athletic build, freckles, hair in a practical braid, fleece vest over a long-sleeve tee and gym shoes, is stepping out of a block of clear, cloudy-white ice in a hospital rehab gym: empty parallel bars, a parked wheelchair, a blue therapy mat, hand-sanitiser station. One leg is already over the edge of the block, her other leg and left forearm still locked in ice; a grey gait belt is frozen in a loop at her waist. Her free right hand wears a crimson red work glove (#B7142E) and holds a plastic goniometer; she is wrenching her left arm free, slabs cracking. Effortful, mid-grunt, eyes on her arm, not at camera. No patients present. Light: cool fluorescent neutralised to grey, plus a daylight key from tall windows at right, ice backlit with grey-white fractures. 35mm at eye level, f/8. Grade: cool desaturated greys and grey-blue shadows, true skin with pores and redness, no retouching, Portra 800 grain. The crimson glove is the only red. Keep the bottom 30% of the frame as plain floor with meltwater and ice shards for text. No text, no logos, no glossy skin, no blue glow, no sparkles, not fantasy.

*Note: the rehab gym is her current workplace as an aide (before EWU), so it's allowed; the EWU program scenes stay in the Catalyst sim lab. No patients in either.*

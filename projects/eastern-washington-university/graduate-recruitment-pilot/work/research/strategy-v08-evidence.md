## 2. WGU "They Said" (full free read)

- WGU brand site, https://www.brand.wgu.edu/they-said-campaign (seen 2026-10-26): campaign guide. Device is a strikethrough: "the industry" definition struck through, WGU's written in. Example: industry "defines flexible as changing the environment"; WGU defines it "as changing the way the system works". Video titled "They Said TV Spot – A University that Listens to You". Channels: digital, print, video, flyer template; testimonial photography. Agency and year not stated. No "wait", "chosen" or "permission" language.
- iSpot, WGU "On Your Time", 2022-08-30, https://www.ispot.tv/ad/b9F3/western-governors-university-on-your-time: people (nurse, construction worker, mother) "share remarks others told them about college"; payoff "earn your degree on your own time". This is the doubters-remarks device, tied to pace/flexibility.
- Agency case study: NOT FOUND (one search). Trade press: NOT FOUND. Ads archive: iSpot only; Meta/Google live read not done (paid MCP excluded).
- **Observation on closeness:** both WGU executions start from what other people said; both resolve into flexibility/pace ("on your own time", "the system"). Neither is about waiting to be chosen or promotion. Overlap with v08 is the "others' judgment" setup and "own timeline" (gloss); the "chosen" wording is not present.

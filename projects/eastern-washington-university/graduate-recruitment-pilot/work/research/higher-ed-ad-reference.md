## 4. Done-before check: "showing work in front of others before being counted in"

Closest precedents found:
- **SVA Subway Series** — school's people publish work in public as the ad. But these are established artists (faculty/alumni), not people *before* the field counts them in. Partial overlap: public display of work. **Not the same tension.**
- **Lindenwood (2026)** — students' own campaign concept runs as the university's billboards. Real work, in public, by people not yet in the field. Undergraduate, local, documented as experiential-learning PR, not as a platform. **Closest structural overlap.**
- **Smith Effect (2011)** — part-time MBA student pitched to industry figures after a competition: a story of showing work to the field, but told as outcome testimonial.
- **Monash Future Without Change** — faculties built artefacts the public touched; work shown, but by the institution, not by students proving themselves.
- **Art-school degree shows** — a category convention (graduates exhibit to industry). Not documented here as advertising; searched, not found as a campaign.

Observation: no piece found frames graduate students showing work to an audience *before* recognition as the recurring story engine. Pieces exist on each component (public work, real students, outcomes). Sample is web-search-limited; not a clean bill.

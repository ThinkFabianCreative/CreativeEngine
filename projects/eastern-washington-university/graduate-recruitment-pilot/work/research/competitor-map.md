### Live ads (Meta Ad Library, US, keyword "master's in management online", active, pulled 2026-10-25)
Of 15 returned, the relevant ones: Robert Kennedy College "British Master's 100% Online!" / "in 1 Year" (IDs 1349133837361696, 1342095961257520); Bellevue "Business Degrees Online or On Campus" (843962385175476); Purdue Online "Top 8 Public University in the U.S. (QS…)" and "Your Next Giant Leap" (1598167838564757, 1699033001176388); Texas Tech Online "100% Online Master's" (1669872324056594); OU Online "Learn to Lead and Innovate" (1725581892031545).
Dominant mechanisms in headlines: format ("100% online", 5 of 7), speed ("in 1 Year"), rank/brand authority (Purdue), generic aspiration ("Next Giant Leap", "Lead and Innovate"). Formats: mostly DCO carousels with "Learn more".
No Gonzaga, Whitworth or WSU MSM-type ad surfaced in this keyword pull (not searched by company; gap).

### Category clichés (observed across ads and program pages)
"100% online", "flexible", "in as little as X months", "lead / leader", "take the next step / giant leap", rankings and accreditation badges, smiling-professional-at-laptop imagery (from carousel thumbnails; not analyzed frame by frame).

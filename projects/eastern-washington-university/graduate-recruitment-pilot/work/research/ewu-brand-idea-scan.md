## 2. Story EWU tells vs what outsiders say

EWU's repeated themes (FOUND across 1.1–1.7 and prior file): hands-on/applied learning to in-demand careers; workforce for the region; "75% of our graduates find jobs related to their major" (Reser blog, 2024-09); affordability/access via scholarships; alumni pride (NFL players, Swoop).

Outsiders:
- Board/strategy rationale: crowded market (34 community colleges, 18 privates, 6 publics; WA "48th in terms of going to college"); idfive "blue ocean" framing. RANGE Media, 2024-05-23, https://www.rangemedia.co/eastern-washington-university-polytechnic — FOUND.
- Tension: RANGE headline "polytechnic metamorphosis – but in name only"; faculty skepticism that the label misrepresents a regional comprehensive or erodes humanities; context of budget cuts and 150+ programs recommended for disinvestment; "EWU has struggled with its identity for decades." Same URL — FOUND (journalist + faculty view).
- Reddit r/Spokane: "more of a commuter school, but the price is right" and free STA buses (https://www.reddit.com/r/Spokane/comments/1pe8yow/thoughts_on_ewu); "a ridiculously good deal cost wise… spend more on your masters" (https://www.reddit.com/r/Spokane/comments/1mjbe7s/eastern_washington_university); employers "didn't care that I went to Eastern"/"plenty fine" (z69oij). FOUND, anonymous, thread dates not captured.
- Grad-relevant risk: Reddit related-post title "EWU's MPH program is on probation from CEPH". FOUND as title only; not verified.
- Niche grad reviews 4.31/5 (161); a working-full-time MSW reviewer: cost the only dislike. https://www.niche.com/graduate-schools/eastern-washington-university/reviews — FOUND.
- US News 2026: #52 Regional Universities West; 92% acceptance (meetyourclass). FOUND.
- Pattern (INFERRED, 4 sources): outsiders frame EWU as value/safe-regional choice; the "polytechnic" claim is contested internally, unremarked by students.

"Burgers 99 Goes Ghost"

Business Evolution:
March 2, 2020: Burgers 99 opened to a warm welcome from the community, with a strong market and product.
April 30, 2026: The original restaurant location closed and Burgers 99 transitioned into a ghost kitchen, moving just a few doors down (101 to 103).
Third-party delivery platforms (3PD), including DoorDash, Grubhub, Uber Eats, and Postmates, have generated approximately $200K–$250K in sales.

Current Challenges:
Location confusion: Yelp and TripAdvisor have flagged the business as closed following the move.
Delivery driver cancellations: Drivers have been cancelling orders due to confusion between the old and new addresses. The removal of the large neon sign has also made the new location more difficult to identify.
Address visibility: The old location is under construction. A sign in the window and an easel on the walkway have helped direct drivers, but confusion remains despite the new address being updated in the apps.
AT recommendation: Add "*NEW ADDRESS" directly within the address details across 3PD platforms.
Organic social: Current social presence is inconsistent.
Promotional strategy: The client considered a BOGO offer on Postmates to help move Burgers 99 higher in the listings. AT recommended holding off for now, as pricing is not currently the primary concern.
Price point: There is flexibility to increase the sale price, even if it results in lower order volume.

Strategic Ideas:
Reposition the Brand - Shift the communication away from simply telling customers "the restaurant is still open." Instead, introduce the evolution of the business: "We moved locations and are now a ghost kitchen."
Explore Burgers 99 by Badmaash to leverage the established quality and uniqueness of Badmaash.
Position Burgers 99 as "your next night-in meal."
The client raised the idea of developing an icon or mascot so the brand does not have to rely on the brothers as the primary faces of Burgers 99.
Own the Hyperlocal Market - Hyperlocal coverage is key, particularly while Burgers 99 operates within a defined delivery footprint (5–6 mile radius)
Activation ideas: Geo-fenced digital advertising, neon/retro-inspired posters and flyers, Sticker takeover (Obey-style), Magnets
Long-term client goal: Expand coverage across all of LA next year.
Leverage the Badmaash Ecosystem - Use the existing Badmaash customer base and brand equity to introduce customers to Burgers 99.
Include flyers inside other Badmaash delivery orders: "Craving a burger next time? Try Burgers 99 by Badmaash…". Include a QR code that links directly to ordering.
Explore the Pacific Electric venue space in Chinatown, which Badmaash eventually plans to operate Thursday–Sunday.
Build an Influencer & Creator Strategy - Use creators to turn menu moments into culturally relevant activations.
Invite cooking influencers into the kitchen for executions "@XYZ is cooking your burger tonight."
Partner with influencer chefs to create a menu item.
Treat creator partnerships as a long-term strategy, rather than one-off influencer posts. Reference what Chillies did to increase the delivery sales for their cheese pulls.
The brothers already have access to photographers and videographers who are willing to shoot content at no cost.
Connect Burgers 99 to LA Culture - Create organic/partnership opportunities for Burgers 99 to show up within music, film, nightlife, and entertainment.
Supply burgers to musicians, film productions, green rooms, etc.
Capture film-style photography from these moments & have participating artists collaborate on social content around messaging such as "Best ghost kitchen in LA."
Once the brand is more established, explore bar and venue takeovers.
Extend operating hours on weekends to capture late-night/bar traffic.

Campaign Stages:
Stage 1 | Fix the Foundation - Ensure customers and delivery drivers can consistently find and order from Burgers 99.
Focus: 3PD platforms, address visibility, Yelp, Google, TripAdvisor and organic social consistency.
Stage 2 | Build Hyperlocal Awareness - Establish Burgers 99 within the neighborhoods it can currently service and convert.
Focus: Geo-fenced digital activation, stickers, flyers, magnets, multi-housing, Badmaash cross-promotion and QR-driven ordering.
Stage 3 | Build Cultural Relevance -  Expand awareness through people, places and experiences that give Burgers 99 a stronger presence within LA culture.

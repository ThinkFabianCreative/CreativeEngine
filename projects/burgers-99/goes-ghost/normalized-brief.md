**Read this first.** Both sources are agency-side. Nothing has come directly from the client yet: no brief, no data, no brand files. Everything the client "said" reaches us through the agency's notes, so client statements are SOURCE-STATED at best.

## 1. Project identity

- **Client:** Burgers 99, a local LA burger business (S2). Opened March 2, 2020 (S1).
- **Operating model:** a ghost kitchen since April 30, 2026. The original restaurant closed and the kitchen moved "a few doors down (101 to 103)" (S1). The street isn't given.
- **People:** the owners are brothers. S2 names one as "NAkul". They also own "Baad MAArsh Venice", which S1 spells "Badmaash". S1's email mentions "Anu", whose role isn't stated. See Q5 and Q6.
- **Market:** delivery footprint of "5–6 mile radius" (S1). Long-term goal is "all of LA next year" (S1).
- **Timing and budget:** not supplied.

## 2. Assignment & scope

- Client ask, as the agency relays it: "an awareness push due to dip in their sales" and "take a holistic look at their business and help them get back on track" (S2).
- Scope proposed in S1 is three stages: **1 Fix the Foundation**, **2 Build Hyperlocal Awareness**, **3 Build Cultural Relevance**.
- Scope Andres added in S2: "full brand refresh, developing strong branding guidelines, and a brand book, direction on leanin towards unbrella brand with badmaash or not, a new website, facilitate online ordering, strong social media precense, leveraging food content creator, micro influencers, develop partnership with Fashion, Music, Art, street art personalities".
- Proposal scope set by Andres (S5 `raw/2026-09-29_andres-scope-direction.md`, user direction): "new website, new brand system, digital experience, better product photography, bring in live events and collabs". Budget is not a constraint for the proposal ("dont worry about budget").
- **The client hasn't agreed to any of this scope.** Out of scope: nothing stated. See Q1.

## 3. Objectives

### 3.1 Business / marketing objective
- Recover sales that dipped after the move (S2). No baseline, target or timeframe was supplied (Q3).
- Price: "flexibility to increase the sale price, even if it results in lower order volume" (S1, client-relayed). So the goal may be revenue or margin rather than order count (Q3).
- Long-term: "Expand coverage across all of LA next year" (S1).

### 3.2 Creative / communication objective
- "Awareness push" (S2). S1 frames the stages as findability first (drivers and customers can "consistently find and order"), then hyperlocal awareness, then cultural relevance.
- Andres's ambition (S2, user direction): "become a street cool … this is a digitl product that can have a real life experiential feel … simple yet significant."

## 4. Offering architecture

One offering, Burgers 99 delivery. No menu, hero product, price or order channel details were supplied. Badmaash is a related business, not an offering in this assignment. How the two brands relate is OPEN (Q2).

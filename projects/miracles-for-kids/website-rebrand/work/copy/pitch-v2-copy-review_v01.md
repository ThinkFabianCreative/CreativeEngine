## Claims that need a source

1. Slide 08, Google research: any figures (studies, participants, "users found it easier") need Google's published source and link, or come out.
2. Slide 09, "proven": replaced with "widely used". If "proven" is wanted, it needs evidence for what it's proven at.
3. Slide 08/09, "accessible": M3 provides accessible defaults; final accessibility depends on our color and type choices. Fine as written ("stay clear and accessible" is a commitment, not a Google claim).
4. Slide 03, all three stats: source line must cite the April Brand Review (R5). "6 min / 4x" is source-stated there, not independently checked.
5. Slide 03, "every stakeholder": six interviews. Wording now says "we spoke to".

## Condescension check (Autumn)

- Slide 04 "doesn't yet fix how MFK looks": highest risk. Rewritten.
- Slide 03 "3 versions of the mission": reads as a gotcha in front of the person who wrote the mission. Rewritten.
- Slide 09 "Fix the logo": rewritten to "tune".
- Slide 08 "never feels like a toy": implies her brand might; cut.
- Slide 01-02 is safe because slide 07 shows "Agreed in April" first: present those together in the room.

## Humanizer pass

Support copy: removed "at the heart of it" (stock phrase), "shape morphing" (jargon), "proven" (inflation), "so the site never feels like a toy" (arguing with no one). Headlines diagnostic only: the 01/02 reversal and 08 "Joy where... Familiar where..." parallel are earned by the idea and stay. No dashes added; typeset quotes/dashes per DesignChamp.

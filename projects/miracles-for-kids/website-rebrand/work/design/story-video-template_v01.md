One template, filled once per family. The template fixes structure, look and rules so an editor can cut 36+ stories consistently. Each story's own spec fills only the per-story fields at the end.

## The handoff spec (template level)

**IDEA:** Illness interrupts everything; MFK keeps the rest of life going. Each video shows the ordinary life the family kept, not the hospital.

**WHAT MUST SURVIVE:**
- The child's name and age before any diagnosis (person-first)
- Real words only: the parent's or child's own voice, never lines written for them
- One concrete thing MFK did (the paid bill, the kept home), named plainly
- No pity framing; joy and normal life carry the frame

**LENGTH:**
- Page cut: 60–90s. Long enough for one turn, short enough to finish.
- Vertical cut: 20–30s for social and mobile. Rewritten, not trimmed.

**HOOK (0–3s):** The child doing something ordinary and theirs (laughing, playing, a favorite thing), with name and age on screen. Frame one works as the CMS poster still.

**STRUCTURE (page cut):**
| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Who | 0–10s | The child, name, age, what they love |
| What happened | 10–25s | Diagnosis in one plain line, in a parent's words |
| What it cost the family | 25–45s | The ordinary life at risk: rent, lights, a parent's job |
| The turn | 45–65s | What MFK did, named plainly. The miracle is the paid bill, never a cure |
| Now | 65–80s | Where the family is today, back to the child |
| Ask | last 5–10s | "Be the miracle for a kid like [Name]." MFK logo. Donate |

**STORYBOARD / REFS:** None yet. Any AI comps are REFERENCE ONLY, never footage.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** Name + age (0–3s), one-line diagnosis super, one line for what MFK did, end card ask. Outfit for supers; Caveat only for a child's or parent's own quoted words. Lower third, inside safe zones.

**SOUND-OFF CHECK:** Supers alone must tell who, what happened, what MFK did and the ask.

**CAPTIONS:** Full speech captions, separate from designed supers. Burned into the vertical cut; .vtt for the page cut.

**AUDIO:** Parent interview as spine (phone or simple lav is fine). Licensed warm, light instrumental; never sad piano. No VO actor; if narration is needed, MFK staff, not talent.

**SOUND-ON VALUE:** The parent's voice. Never the only carrier of the message.

**HUMAN PRESENCE:** The family, as themselves. No talent, no recreations.

**LOOK:** Candid, child's eye level, natural light, real homes. Casual over polished: authenticity is the trust signal. Grade warm and clean; no heavy filters.

**EDITING STYLE:** Unhurried; hold on faces. Simple cuts and gentle Ken Burns on stills. Avoid flash transitions, slow-motion tears, hospital B-roll as decoration.

**BRAND:** Look A Saturated: white canvas, royal #1B5FBF, orange #F99400 for the action, sun #FFD23F accents. MFK mark on the end card only. Shape crops from the brand system may frame stills.

**FORMATS:** 16:9 page cut (lightbox). 9:16 vertical cut. 1:1 optional for feed. Re-frame to keep the child's face, not the center.

**SAFE ZONES:** Vertical cut follows the platform module for Reels and TikTok. Page cut: keep supers out of the lightbox close-button corner.

**CUTDOWNS:** 20–30s vertical (hook → turn → ask). 6s optional bumper: child + name + "Be the miracle."

**VARIANTS:** Photo-led cut (stills + parent audio) for stories with gallery photos and no footage. Footage cut for new shoots. Same structure both ways.

**AUTOMATIC CHANGES:** Switch off platform auto-music, auto-captions styling, and AI enhancements when boosted on Meta.

**CLAIMS / FLAGS:** Video release from parent or guardian before the edit starts (Consent on file in CMS). Any medical or dollar figure checked with MFK. Music license on file.

**DELIVERY:** Page cut 1920×1080 MP4 H.264; vertical 1080×1920. Poster still exported from frame one. Naming: `MFK_story_[slug]_[16x9|9x16]_v01.mp4`. Two review rounds: DesignChamp + WordSmither, then MFK.

**QUESTIONS FOR PRODUCTION:** Ask before choosing between footage and photo-led cut, cutting any parent line, or adding anything to the diagnosis line.

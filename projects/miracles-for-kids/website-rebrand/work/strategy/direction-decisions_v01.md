## Decided

0. **What the project name means (Andres, 2026-10-16):** "Rebranding" is Jordan's first attempt, the rebrand landing page design. "Pivot" is the new approach we're building now. MFK itself isn't changing what it does or who it serves.

1. **Scope:** a small MFK brand system plus the homepage. Not just a homepage.
2. **Color:** adopt Gillette's *philosophy*, not its colors. That means a short palette (about 6) with fixed proportions, white as the dominant neutral, at most 3 brand colors per layout, an approved-combinations chart, and type-color rules (body copy in navy or white). MFK's own hues are derived from its logo and current site.
3. **Shapes:** a set of graphic shapes **derived from MFK's own mark**, used as color floods and image crops to make the page whimsical and dynamic. They come with rules, following the Gillette model: bleed off the page, similar scale, at most 3 per screen.
4. **Type:** the primary face is sans serif, geometric, rounded or friendly, interesting yet legible. Gillette's own faces (Baloo 2, Mokoko) are excluded. The secondary role still needs a decision: the "Miracles" script for a child's or parent's own voice only, or a slab or serif for labels and quotes.

## Carried from earlier (Andres agreed)

- The pushback is right. Round 1 felt like a bank or a health plan, vibecoded, with no images and no emotion. The root cause is that there was no copywriter.
- The work is copy-led: voice traits with "but nots" and example lines, and person-first language.
- Photography: real families, candid, at the child's eye level. No stock heroes.

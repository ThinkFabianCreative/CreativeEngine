MFK landing v4: the personality is right, but the springs bounce in the wrong places

Miracles for Kids charity landing prototype. Stories first, Donate and Volunteer as the calls to action, and components built on M3 Expressive. Audited in code with one browser pass, and nothing in the prototype was changed.

Static HTML/CSS/JS · CSS transitions, Web Animations API, scroll-linked JS parallax · work/prototype/v4/index.html · weighting: Jakub primary, Jhey secondary, Emil selective

4Critical 4Important 6Opportunities

Overall

The motion vocabulary fits the brand. Shapes that morph, a parallax that makes the blob and heart feel physical, and springy controls all suit a warm, kid-centred charity. Two things undercut it. First, the overshoot spring is used everywhere, including the donation controls people tap repeatedly, where bounce feels like wobble rather than joy. Second, the most important content changes snap with no transition: filtering stories, opening a video and expanding the donation form. One animation also ignores reduced motion. The fix is not less motion. Put the bounce where delight belongs, and smooth the transitions that are missing.

What's off. The four hollow dots at zero are the most important state changes on the page, and none of them has a transition. Meanwhile, the high-frequency controls (dots 7, 8 and 10) sit in the deliberate band with overshoot, so a single tap on an amount takes about half a second to settle. The section reveal (12) is long and applied to 26 elements, including headings and paragraphs. The count-up (13) is long, but it runs once per visit and is fine.

Jakub Krehel — Production Polish

Primary

Concern: the polish is there, but uneven.

What's working well

  • The pop-out uses an effects curve for opacity and a spatial curve for position (--m3-spring-effects / --m3-spring-spatial). Splitting them like this is the right instinct.
  • The mosaic page turn has an asymmetric exit and enter (180ms ease-in out, 260ms decelerate in), with a direction-aware 24px shift.
  • The parallax uses translate on a rAF-throttled scroll and skips offscreen layers. It only moves compositor-friendly properties, and the background photos are oversized (inset:-12% 0) so no edges show.
  • Hover states all have transitions, with no instant colour flips.

Issues to address

Critical · motion gap

Story filters snap tiles in and out

The primary content area changes with no transition. Choosing a filter sets display:none on tiles, so the grid reflows in one frame and the visitor loses track of which stories stayed.

index.html · .tile.hide{display:none} · render()

Wrap the change in a View Transition so the remaining tiles glide to their new positions, and fade the leaving tiles out with a slight scale.
const swap=()=>render();
document.startViewTransition ? document.startViewTransition(swap) : swap();
/* CSS */ .tile{view-transition-name:var(--vt)}  /* set --vt per tile: t1…t7 */
::view-transition-old(*){animation:180ms ease-in both fadeOut}
::view-transition-group(*){animation-duration:320ms;animation-timing-function:cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)}

Important · exits

The pop-out leaves as loudly as it arrives

Closing it replays the same 600ms spring in reverse, a full 100% + 48px drop. The visitor has decided to dismiss it, so the exit should get out of the way faster and travel less.

.give and .give.open transitions

Keep the spring on enter. Use a short eased exit with a small drop.
.give{transform:translateY(12px) scale(.98);opacity:0;visibility:hidden;

.give.open{transform:none;opacity:1;visibility:visible;
 transition:transform .6s var(--m3-spring-spatial),opacity .25s var(--m3-spring-effects)}

Important · motion gap

The video lightbox appears and disappears instantly

showModal() and close() have no transition, so the backdrop and player pop into place. It's the one moment where the page asks people to watch a story, and it arrives with a jolt.

.vbox · vbox.showModal()

Use @starting-style for a 220ms scale-and-fade in, with a 150ms exit.
.vbox{opacity:0;transform:scale(.96);transition:opacity .15s ease-in,transform .15s ease-in,display .15s allow-discrete,overlay .15s allow-discrete}
.vbox[open]{opacity:1;transform:none;transition-duration:.22s;transition-timing-function:cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)}
@starting-style{.vbox[open]{opacity:0;transform:scale(.96)}}

Important · motion gap

The donation bar jumps to full size

Tapping "Choose an amount" swaps display:none on the switch, amounts and button, so the card grows about 110px in one frame. It is the conversion moment, and it should open like a drawer.

Put the three rows in one wrapper and animate grid-template-rows from 0fr to 1fr. That transition is height-safe, can be interrupted, and needs no measuring.

Important · uniformity

Every block fades up the same way, headings and paragraphs included

26 elements share one 700ms opacity + translateY(24px) reveal: the hero text, every h2, lead paragraphs, stat cards, the logo wall and every card. When everything moves, nothing is emphasised, and the text arrives later than it should.

.reveal × 26

Remove the reveal from headings, lead paragraphs, the hero text and the filters. Keep it on media and cards, shortened to 500ms with a 12px rise, and stagger the four stat cards by 60ms as the one deliberate moment.

Opportunities

  • The one-time heading swap changes the text instantly. Crossfade only the words that change (120ms out, 180ms in), and let the script word miracle. stay put. · give-h innerHTML
  • The empty-state panel for filters appears in one frame. Reusing the tile enter (fade plus 0.96 scale) would fix it. · .empty

Through Jakub's lens: the craft is good where it exists. Fill the four zero-duration gaps, quieten the exits, and stop animating text, and the page will feel considered rather than decorated.

Jhey Tompkins — Experimentation & Delight

Secondary

Strong: the personality is earned.

What's working well

  • Parallax on the hero blob, heart and close-section shapes, with layers moving at opposite speeds, makes the Figma shapes feel like objects rather than flat decoration.
  • The shape morph on press (pill to rounded square) and the play button turning into a squircle on hover are small, ownable signatures.
  • The M3 spring is written as a pure CSS linear() curve, with no JS physics library. That's exactly the right tool.
  • The asymmetric tile corners and two-tone shapes are left untouched by any motion, so the brand silhouette holds still while controls move.

Opportunities

  • Celebrate the gift. Submitting a donation is the rarest, most meaningful action on the page, and right now it only swaps text. A heart could pop out of the Give button and arc up with the M3 spring, with three small sun-coloured shapes scattering. Run it once, keep it under 900ms, and skip it under reduced motion. · #give onsubmit
  • Newsletter success is another rare moment. The orange close circle could drift slightly and the heart could beat once. · .news form
  • Move the parallax to the compositor. Use animation-timeline: view() with @supports, and keep the JS as a fallback. It's smoother on low-end phones and uses less code. · [data-speed]
  • Morph the program crops on hover. Each crop could ease its clip-path towards its neighbour's shape (oval to pebble, arch to heart). This uses M3's shape morph on content rather than controls, and it would be the most distinctive moment on the page. · .prog .crop

Through Jhey's lens: the page has a voice. Now give the two rare, emotional moments, giving and signing up, a moment of joy. That's where bounce belongs, not on the amount buttons.

Emil Kowalski — Restraint & Speed

Selective: forms, nav, frequent controls

Problem, in a narrow scope.

What's working well

  • The global prefers-reduced-motion rule removes CSS animations and transitions, the parallax checks the media query itself, and the count-up is guarded.
  • Esc and the close button dismiss the pop-out, menu and lightbox instantly, without waiting for an animation.
  • All the motion uses transform, opacity, border-radius or clip-path, except one case (below).

Issues to address

Critical · accessibility

The story page turn ignores reduced motion

turn() calls mosaic.animate() directly. The Web Animations API isn't covered by the CSS kill-switch, so visitors who asked for reduced motion still see the grid slide sideways on every Prev/Next.

index.html · function turn(d)

Gate it like the count-up is gated, and change the content immediately.
const still=matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)');
function turn(d){page+=d;if(still.matches){rotate(d);render();return;}
 mosaic.animate(…)…}

Critical · high-frequency + layout

The donation amounts bounce and animate flex-grow

Every tap on an amount animates flex-grow (a layout property that reflows all six buttons) on the overshooting spatial spring, so the row wobbles for about 400ms. People tap several amounts while deciding, so the wobble repeats. The frequency switch thumb overshoots too.

.give .amounts button · .give .toggle::before

Drop flex-grow. Show the selection with fill, shape morph and a small scale, using a no-bounce curve at about 200ms. Keep the overshoot spring for rare moments.
--m3-spring-fast:cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1);
.give .amounts button{transition:border-radius .2s var(--m3-spring-fast),transform .2s var(--m3-spring-fast),background .15s,color .15s}
.give .amounts button[aria-pressed=true]{transform:scale(1.06);border-radius:999px}
.give .toggle::before{transition:transform .24s var(--m3-spring-fast)}

Critical · keyboard-initiated

Keyboard focus lifts tiles and cards

The focus states added in the last pass mirror the hover lift (translateY(-4px/-6px) on :focus-visible). Tabbing through seven tiles and eight cards makes the page jump on every keystroke. Keyboard navigation should never animate.

.tile:focus-visible, .icard:focus-visible, .ev:focus-visible, .prog:focus-visible .crop

On focus, show the static outline with no transform, and keep the lift for the pointer only.
.tile:focus-visible,.icard:focus-visible,.ev:focus-visible{transform:none;transition:none;box-shadow:none}
@media (hover:hover){.tile:hover{transform:translateY(-4px)}}

Opportunities

  • Use one spring vocabulary. The legacy --spring (a 1.56 overshoot cubic-bezier) still drives the nav underline, chips, play buttons and card lifts, beside the M3 spatial and effects curves. Define three tokens (spatial-playful, spatial-fast and effects) and map every transition to one of them. · :root
  • Rapid Prev/Next clicks stack Web Animations and rotations. Cancel the in-flight animation before starting a new one so the turn can be interrupted. · turn()

Through Emil's lens: on a landing page, most motion can be generous. The donation controls and keyboard paths are the exceptions. Make them fast, flat and honest to reduced motion, and spend the bounce somewhere it won't be repeated.

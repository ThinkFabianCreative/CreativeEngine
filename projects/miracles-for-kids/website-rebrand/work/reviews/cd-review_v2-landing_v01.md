# CD review: v2 landing page, four looks (v01)

CLIENT: Miracles for Kids Charity · PROJECT: website-rebrand · SOURCE SCOPE: PROJECT-LEVEL
Reviewer: creative-engine-director · Mode: JUDGE, with DEVELOP notes · Date: 2026-10-16
Scope: aesthetic pass only (design and copy). Reviewed from the v2-screens slices and the four-look comparisons. Mobile is not built, so not reviewed.

## 1. Does v2 answer the pushback?

Pushback (R1): no images, no emotion, "vibecoded", no vibrancy. Andres: it feels like a bank or health plan, not childlike, whimsical or inspirational. No copywriter.

| Section | Verdict on the pushback | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | **Mostly yes** | Real family photo, a heart, a warm organic shape, a donation card. Emotion and imagery fixed. Headline is the weak link (see §3). Four CTAs compete: Give monthly, Watch William's story, the video pill, the card button. |
| Truth band ("Rent's due. Miracles too.") | **Yes in copy, under-built in design** | This is the idea of the page and the best line on it. It gets a short band with one small link. In A the dark photo overlay makes it heavy and somber, which pulls toward the old tone. Flat color (B) carries it better. |
| Family stories | **Not yet** | The structure is right (early on the page, video first, Starlight-style grid). But the video tiles show abstract shapes, not children. The format people come for is currently the least emotional thing on the page, and empty shape tiles are exactly what "vibecoded" looks like. Known placeholder, but it decides the presentation. |
| Programs ("Five kinds of miracle") | **Yes** | Strongest design-plus-copy section. "The lights-on miracle", "full-fridge", "what's-next" are childlike and specific without being cute. The organic photo crops do the whimsy job. Stat lines are too small to read. |
| Trust / stats | **No, this is the bank section** | Four outlined boxes of numbers plus a line of tiny hospital names. Every stat is in brackets. It is the most like a health plan on the page, in every look. |
| Waitlist band | **Partly** | Good urgency move, but see risk on "[6+]". |
| More ways / events | **Partly** | Cards are fine. The events list is a plain table (gala dates, ruled lines), which reads admin. Miracle Manor cutout artifact visible. |
| Final CTA + newsletter | **Yes** | "It's easier than it sounds." is a good human line. Big circle shape adds play. A's dark photo again muddies it. |
| Copy overall | **Copywriter is now present** | Voice is warm, plain, specific. Main problem: "miracle" appears in nearly every headline, and the script-highlight word is applied to every headline, so both become a tic. |

**Childlike and whimsical:** improved, but the whimsy comes only from shapes. Nothing on the page is made by or in the voice of a child (handwriting, a drawing, a kid's own words as a caption, as Make-A-Wish and Starlight do). The script face is decorating headlines, not speaking for anyone. That is the gap between "friendlier template" and "a children's charity".

## 2. Verdicts per look

| Look | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **A Saturated** (Outfit + Caveat; royal blue, orange, sun) | **ADVANCE** | Cleanest type and the strongest hero; closest to Make-A-Wish. But the dark photo bands and the light-blue trust section drift back toward sober. |
| **B Blocks** (Fredoka/Nunito + Caveat Brush; sun, magenta, sky on cream) | **ADVANCE (lead color)** | Most vibrant and most childlike; flat color bands answer "no vibrancy" head-on; magenta is MFK's own brand hue; closest to the Starlight board Andres liked. Fredoka across everything risks reading young and soft for donors. |
| **C Pastel** (Nunito + Dancing Script; lavender, sky, magenta) | **KILL** | Pastel answers the opposite of the pushback (less vibrancy), and Dancing Script reads wedding invitation, not child. |
| **D System** (Lexend + Kalam; navy, sky, orange on white) | **REWORK, not for this presentation** | Navy-led and orderly: the most "bank" of the four. Useful later as the brand system's restrained application, not as the answer to this client. |

**Take forward: B's color mode with A's type mode (Outfit + Caveat).** B's flat sun/magenta/sky blocks give the vibrancy and MFK ownership; Outfit keeps it "interesting yet legible" and adult enough for a donor deciding on a monthly gift; Caveat is more like real handwriting than Caveat Brush. Replace any photo-overlay band with a flat color band. No BET THE PITCH yet: the stories section has to carry real faces first.

## 3. Ranked changes for the Autumn/David presentation

1. **Put children in the stories grid.** Replace every abstract video poster with a real still from that family's video (the brief says child at eye level). Make William's tile the large one. Without this, the section people come for reads empty and the "no images, no emotion" note comes back.
2. **Make the truth band the second hero.** "Rent's due. Miracles too." should be big (hero-scale), on a flat sun or magenta block, with a small concrete proof under it ("Up to $500 a month toward rent, power, insurance", C2, kept as "up to") and a stronger link than "How we help". Remove the dark photo overlay in this band and the final CTA.
3. **Sharpen the hero headline.** "Small miracles. Big difference." is generic charity language and the only line on the page that any charity could run. Bring the T5 idea up: lead with the ordinary-life twist (for example, route the "Rent's due. Miracles too." thinking or a "lights-on" line into the hero, and let "Small miracles" go). WordSmither to give 3 options against the proposition. Also fix the line break in A ("Small / miracles. Big / difference.").
4. **Add one child-made element.** A handwritten kid quote or caption (Starlight), or a child's drawing used once as a texture, with consent placeholders. Use the script face only where a person is speaking, not on every headline. Cut the script highlight to about 3 headlines (hero, truth band, final CTA).
5. **Rebuild the trust section so it is not a dashboard.** Two stats as colored blocks inside sentences (Make-A-Wish), not four outlined boxes; hospital names as a proper logo wall placeholder (Q7) at readable size; Charity Navigator/Candid as badges. Drop "[88¢]" and "[2,100+]" from the presented version, or label them clearly as pending: both are in open conflicts (C9/C10/C11, C16).
6. **Reduce "miracle".** Count it and cut to roughly half. Candidates: "Meet the miracles" → "Meet the families", "More ways to make miracles" → plain "More ways to help", and keep "miracle" for the five program names, where it earns it.
7. **Clean the craft bugs.** The gap in "More ways to make    miracles." (all four looks); Miracle Manor white cutout; tiny stat lines under programs; trim the 9 filter pills to 4 (All, Video, Read, Where Are They Now?) since programs have their own section; one hero video entry point, not two.
8. **Fix dated or thin content.** The events list starts Oct 17 (tomorrow); show it as event cards for the next two events, not a table. Verify footer legal identity matches §11 (EIN 91-2160616, 17848 Sky Park Circle, Suite C, Irvine, CA 92614, (714) 730-3040).

## 4. What would make Autumn and David reject it

- **Numbers.** The page is full of bracketed stats. If one is shown as fact and is wrong (or comes from Jordan's unsupported set), the whole page loses credibility with the CEO. Present stats as labeled placeholders and say so before they ask.
- **"[6+] families are waiting."** If the real waitlist is single digits, the urgency band undercuts itself ("only six?"). Confirm the number or reframe the band before showing it.
- **"Miracle" fatigue or cure confusion.** A CEO may hear twenty "miracles" as cute, or worry it implies medical outcomes. Defend with the tension in the brief: MFK's miracle is a paid bill. The truth band has to be prominent for that defense to work.
- **Empty story tiles.** If the stories are abstract shapes, David will say it still looks vibecoded and has no families. This is the single most likely rejection.
- **Four looks shown as equals.** Showing C or D invites a soft, safe pick. Show B-color/A-type as the recommendation, A as the alternative, and keep C/D out of the main flow.
- **Consent.** Real children's names, faces and diagnoses on a pitch page will raise consent questions (Q2). Say up front they are placeholders pending consent.
- **Logo and brand change worries.** Make clear the logo is untouched (Q5) and the colors are MFK's own magenta, navy and light blue, extended.
- **Load-bearing elements to defend, not trade:** the truth band, real children in the stories grid, flat saturated color. Fine to concede: filter pills, events format, the exact stat set.

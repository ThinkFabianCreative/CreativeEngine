## Verdict: REWORK (close to ADVANCE)

The spine is right and minimal: you told us this in April, Phase 1 fixes the words but not the look, here's a glimpse, extend Phase 1. It builds on work MFK already accepted, so it reads as finishing their plan, not selling a new one. Three things stop it going out as is: the deck contradicts our own chosen direction (magenta and "navy, sky and magenta stay" against Look A's royal, orange and sun); the trust risk is answered with a Google name-drop rather than shown; and the ask has no number or cost. Fix those and it's ADVANCE.

## Does it land?

- **Autumn:** mostly. Slide 03 ("1 founder") and the families-first cover speak to her. But the deck talks about her ("carries Autumn's story everywhere she can't be") more than it gives her something to feel. One real family story moment, or Autumn's own words from April, would land harder than the stat row.
- **David:** partly. He owns marketing, so he'll measure this on time, cost, workload and whether his team can keep it up. The deck gives him "many different styles" (slide 04) as the problem, but no proof of that pain (no side-by-side of current pages, emails, event pieces) and no answer on cost or timeline. He's the one who'll quietly kill it with "let's do this in Phase 2."

## Objections and whether the deck answers them

| Objection (likely voice) | Answered? | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "We already did a brand review in April." (Autumn, David) | Partly (03, 07) | Say it outright on 04 or 07: "April gave you the words. This gives them a look." Frame it as completing April, not redoing it. Credit the April work. |
| "How much longer and how much more?" (David) | No | Number on 10, cost on 07. Show the trade: one guide now saves redesigning every email and event piece later. Pre-compromise option: a lighter "visual starter kit" (color, type, shapes, photo rules) if the full guide is too much. |
| "Won't playful look childish, or less trustworthy to donors and hospitals?" (David, board, major donors) | Weakly (08) | Show, don't claim. Pair the playful hero with a calm donate flow. Cite their own trust signals (Charity Navigator 4-star, 20+ years) living inside the new look. Name the rule: whimsy never touches money, forms or medical content. |
| "Is the logo changing?" (Autumn, emotionally; David, operationally) | Ambiguous (07 "Logo for every size", 09 "Fix the logo for small sizes") | One explicit line: "Your logo stays. We make it work at small sizes." Show a before/after favicon or social avatar. |
| "Are our colors changing?" | Contradicted | Deck says magenta stays (09) but our direction drops it as the action color. Resolve internally first. If magenta goes, say why and show it; don't let them discover it on the site. |
| "Is this really Google's research-backed system?" | Risky (08) | M3 Expressive is Google's published design system and Google has said it was informed by extensive user research. Keep the claim to that, sourced, with no numbers unless we cite Google's own. Don't imply Google endorses MFK or that M3 makes a site "trusted". Better: lead with the benefit (familiar buttons, forms and navigation, accessible by default) and footnote Google. Check that Instapage, the production path, can actually express M3 Expressive shapes and controls natively before we promise it. |
| "Who keeps this consistent after launch?" (David, Janina) | No | One line on 07: templates and a guide the team can use without us. |
| "What about 360 and Pillars?" | Only on 09 | Fine as a mention. Don't add slides. |

## Recommended order

01 (+02 merged) → 03 April evidence → 04 the gap, with the current-materials strip → 07 Phase 1 extended (moved up: the ask in shape before the dream) → 05 voice line → 06 glimpse → 08 playful and trusted → 09 recommendation → 10 the ask.

Reasoning: put the logic (gap, scope) before the emotion (voice, glimpse), then close the trust objection right before the recommendation, so the last thing they see before the ask is the fear removed. Alternative if Autumn leads the room: keep 05 and 06 before 07, since she buys with the heart first. Andres's call.

## Top 5 changes, ranked

1. **Make the deck agree with Look A.** Orange Donate on 06; rewrite "navy, sky and magenta stay" on 09 to whatever is true; fix "09" to "01". A pitch about one consistent brand can't contradict itself.
2. **Put a real number and cost on the ask** (10, and the "Added" column on 07), plus a lighter fallback option for David.
3. **Show the trust answer on 08** with one screen: warm shapes and photo, plain familiar donate form. Trim the M3 claim to what Google says, footnoted, and confirm Instapage can build it.
4. **Prove the problem on 04** with a strip of MFK's current, inconsistent materials.
5. **Say the logo stays, in one sentence**, and frame the whole pitch as finishing April's Phase 1, crediting that work.

Smaller: merge 01 and 02; make the 06 hero full-bleed and finished; label 05 as the voice; fix "Joy where" spacing; date the workshop.

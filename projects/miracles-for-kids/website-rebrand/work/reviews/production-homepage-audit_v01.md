## Page order as built

1. Nav: How We Help, Take Action, Why We Help, Contact Us, with **Volunteer Now** and **Donate** buttons
2. Hero: "Be our miracle" over a smiling stock family, with Donate to Miracles and Get Involved
3. Positioning line: "one of the only organizations on the West Coast…"
4. "20 Years of Miracles" YouTube embed
5. Four cards: Our Story / Programs / Financials / Events
6. "We Do Better Together" with a How We Help link
7. Newsletter signup ("Join Us" script watermark)
8. Hospitals Serviced logo wall (9)
9. Family stories carousel: Michelle, Kiara, Oziel
10. "Make miracles today": Donate Monthly / Events / Ways to Give
11. Volunteer With Us
12. Footer

## What's working (keep it)

- **The truth is already on the page.** "Fight bankruptcy, homelessness, hunger and depression, so they can fight for their kids' lives" is a real insight. MFK carries the household so the parents can carry the child. It's the strongest line on the site and it's buried in hero body copy.
- **The positioning line is ownable.** Few organizations do monthly aid, housing and counseling together. That's a reason to give here and not to a hospital foundation.
- **Real families.** Michelle's photo is real, specific and warm, and it outperforms the stock hero emotionally.
- **The hospital wall** is strong borrowed credibility (CHLA, City of Hope, UCSF, Rady and others).
- **Transparency** (audited financials, Charity Navigator) is a real trust asset for donors.

## Problems, ranked

1. **Stock hero over a real-family story.** The opening image is a generic model family in a park. It reads as a family-photography ad, not a family in crisis, and it contradicts the specificity the copy promises. (Barry: show, don't tell. A truth needs a real image.)
2. **The hero is fragile.** At narrower widths the image collapses into a narrow strip and the headline clips ("…our mira"). On desktop the headline doesn't read on the first screen in the capture. Treat both as must-fix and confirm on real devices.
3. **No hierarchy of asks.** Donate, Donate Monthly, Volunteer Now, Get Involved, Ways to Give, Events, newsletter, Partner: around eight competing asks with equal weight. Monthly giving, which is probably the most valuable ask for a recurring-bills charity, sits two-thirds of the way down.
4. **The proof arrives late and incomplete.** The real impact numbers (2,100+ families, 21 counties, 167+ illnesses) aren't on the homepage at all; they're on How We Help. There's no "what $X does" either, even though the programs (bills, rent, groceries) are unusually concrete and ready for it.
5. **The programs are invisible.** Lifeline / Safe Haven / Balance / Boost / Pillars, the real product, are collapsed into one "Our Programs" card with a small icon.
6. **Generic nonprofit language.** "We Do Better Together," "Make miracles today," "thousands of families": interchangeable with any charity. The specific lines exist but get diluted.
7. **The "miracle" metaphor fights the offer.** MFK's work is unglamorous and recurring (light bills, rent, groceries). "Miracle" suggests a cure. There's a tension here worth using: the miracle is a paid electricity bill.
8. **Visual system.** Script accents in three places (wordmark, "only organizations," "Join Us," "meet") plus orange buttons, teal, navy and light blue make for no clear system. Washed-out, low-contrast card section (5). Mixed photo styles: stock color, black-and-white video, real families.
9. **Information architecture leaks.** Footer links go to "our-partners-old," and duplicate or inconsistent URLs exist (/how-we-help-2/, /our-story/ vs /why-we-help/our-story/). The founding story conflicts between pages (1996 vs 2002/2004).
10. **The video is a dead block.** A 20-year anniversary film with a play button gives no reason to press play.

## Initial direction for discussion (not a decision)

- Lead with the household truth and a real family.
- Make one primary ask (likely monthly), with the others tiered below it.
- Translate dollars into bills paid, nights housed and groceries delivered.
- Give each program its own space.
- Move the proof (hospitals, numbers, financials) up and group it.

Andres's view comes next, before any of this goes further.

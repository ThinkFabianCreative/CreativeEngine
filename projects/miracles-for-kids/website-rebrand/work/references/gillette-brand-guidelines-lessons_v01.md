# Gillette Children's brand guidelines: lessons for MFK, v01

```
CLIENT:   Miracles for Kids Charity
PROJECT:  website-rebrand
SOURCE:   "Redefining Our Future", Gillette Children's Brand Guidelines 2022, 47 pp
          (website-rebrand/raw/MiraclesForKids/Gilette-childrens-web.pdf, supplied by Andres; read in full 2026-10-16)
SCOPE:    Outside reference, a different organization. Its method is useful; its facts, colors, fonts,
          shapes and lines are Gillette's and are not copied.
```

## Why it's the right model

Gillette solved our exact problem: a medical-adjacent children's organization that had to be **playful, warm and youthful without looking like a hospital or a health plan, and without being childish**. It did that with a **system**, not a style. Jordan's rounds failed in two opposite directions: round 1 had restraint but no warmth, round 2 had warmth but no rules. Gillette shows both can coexist when every element has a job and a limit.

## What to take (method, not look)

| Gillette does | The principle | MFK application |
|---|---|---|
| **A brand house** first (vision, purpose, positioning, target, personality, values, differentiators), then voice, then visuals | Strategy decides the look, not the reverse | Our brief has to produce a one-page MFK brand house before design. This answers the "rebrand or evolution" question with something concrete |
| **Voice as four traits, each with "Including / But not" lists**, plus 2–3 example lines (Pioneering, Honest, Warm, Undeterred). "Honest: grit, realistic… but not grim, cold, despairing" | Gives a copywriter guardrails and gives the client words for what felt wrong. "Feels like a bank" becomes "cold, distant", which are named "but nots" | Write MFK's traits the same way. Candidates: **Honest about money** (but not grim), **Warm** (but not saccharine or pitying), **Plucky / kid-hearted** (but not childish), **Steady** (but not corporate). Each gets example lines |
| **"Warm" means writing about the details of daily life** (a joke, a crayon drawing, a meal together) | Emotion comes from specific, ordinary moments, not adjectives | This *is* MFK's idea: the ordinary family life that MFK protects. The paid bill buys the crayon drawing |
| **Person-first language** ("a child who has CP," not "a CP patient") and "not defined by a diagnosis" | Dignity is a writing rule, not a vibe | MFK style rule: the child first, the illness second and in the caption. "Dailani, 2, who is fighting cancer," never "cancer patient Dailani." This fixes the "never condescending" note |
| **Palette: 6 colors, with ratios** (white 30%, navy 15%, teal 20%, then 10/10/10/5) and **at most 3 brand colors per layout**, with an approved-combinations chart and type-color rules | Vibrancy with discipline. It solves Jordan's round 2 (about 25 swatches, every section a new color) | Cut MFK's palette to about 5 or 6 drawn from the logo (light blue, navy, magenta) plus one warm accent. Define ratios, allow 3 colors per screen, and use a combinations chart |
| **Two typefaces with roles**: a playful rounded sans (Baloo 2, also used in the logo) for everything, a rounded slab serif (Mokoko) only for labels and pull quotes. A defined hierarchy | Personality comes from the type itself, not from added decoration | One rounded, friendly primary face plus one accent with a single job. For MFK the accent is the **"Miracles" script, used only for a child's or parent's own voice or quotes**, never on random words |
| **Graphic shapes derived from the logo symbol**, with 5 shapes and hard rules: bleed off the page, similar scale, no more than 3 per page, used as image crops or color floods | Whimsy that is **owned**, not stock confetti | Derive MFK's playful device from its own mark (the script stroke, the "FOR KIDS" block, or the heart). Replaces Jordan's generic stars, thumbs and apples. Give it rules |
| **Photography rules**: warm, candid, honest. Lighting matters. Lifestyle photos outside the hospital. Meet children at their level. Equipment never overpowers the child. Never flip or alter a patient | The child is a person, not a patient. Matches the board's finding | MFK photography brief: kids at home, at Miracle Manor, at events, at eye level. The illness is visible only when it's honest. Real families only, no stock heroes. Also needs a consent policy |
| **Icons and emoji on a grid** with defined stroke and color | Even small UI pieces belong to the system | MFK's program icons (Lifeline, Safe Haven, and so on) get redrawn on one grid in one style |
| **4x4 grid** and real layout examples. Headline pattern "He can be whoever he wants to be." | The headline states the child's possibility, not the institution's service | MFK headlines are about what the family gets back, not what MFK "provides" |
| **Boilerplate, vision and mission as fixed copy** | One approved way to say who you are | MFK needs one approved boilerplate. That also fixes the 1996 vs 2002 origin conflict and the inconsistent numbers |

## What not to take

- Gillette's look: teal/purple/navy, the splash-symbol shapes, Baloo 2 + Mokoko. Those are theirs, and Gillette is a hospital; MFK is a family-support charity.
- Its institutional voice traits (Pioneering, global beacon) belong to a clinical leader. MFK's authority comes from closeness and practicality, not research.
- The photography lighting motif ("beacon") is tied to Gillette's positioning. MFK needs its own photographic idea, for example **home** and ordinary daily life.

## What this means for the work

The output of this project should be **a small MFK brand system plus the homepage**, not just a homepage:
1. A one-page brand house
2. Voice traits with "but nots" and example lines
3. Palette with ratios and allowed combinations
4. Type roles
5. One owned graphic device, with rules
6. A photography brief
7. The homepage built from that system

The client's pushback (no images, no emotion, vibecoded, no vibrancy, "a bank or a health plan") maps directly onto items 2, 3, 5 and 6.

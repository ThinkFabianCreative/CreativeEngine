## Derived layer
### Derived communication hypothesis
Turn "miracle" from a cure promise into ordinary life kept intact: the paid bill buys the childhood. Two registers: the adult world (plain, blunt, like a bill) and the child's world (joyful, in the child's own voice). Basis: 16.2, C2, C5, C7.
### Creative stimulus
- What does $500 a month actually let a kid keep?
- What's the most ordinary moment a sick child's family would miss most?
- How would a 7-year-old explain what MFK does?
- What does "PAID" feel like to a parent at 2am?
### Do not assume
That donors know families can't apply directly; that "miracle" must stay in the headline.

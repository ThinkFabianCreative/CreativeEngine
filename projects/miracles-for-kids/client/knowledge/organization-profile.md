## Identity

- **Name:** Miracles for Kids (VolunteerHub lists "Miracles For Kids Inc."). 501(c)(3), EIN 91-2160616.
- **HQ:** 17848 Sky Park Circle, Suite C, Irvine, CA. (714) 730-3040, info@miraclesforkids.org.
- **Mission (footer, verbatim):** "to improve the lives of critically-ill children and their families in need."
- **Positioning claim:** "one of the only organizations on the West Coast that provides monthly financial aid, subsidized housing, and counseling to families fighting for their child's life."
- **Core idea in their words:** help families "fight bankruptcy, homelessness, hunger and depression, so they can fight for their kids' lives." MFK doesn't treat the child's illness; it carries the household so the parents can carry the child.

## Program detail (program pages, read 2026-10-16)

- **How families arrive:** every family receiving financial help is **pre-screened and referred by top children's hospitals**. Families don't apply through the website. So the homepage's main audience is supporters (donors, volunteers, partners), not families looking for help.
- **Who they serve:** low-income families. The average annual income of families served is **$31,065** (Miracle Manor page).
- **Lifeline (bill pay):** grants of **up to $500 a month** toward rent, utilities, insurance and more. **230+ families** currently receive monthly help; **6+ are on the waitlist**. Quote from Maribel, an MFK mom, about food on the table. Michelle's family's story: Lifeline covers the car payment for trips to treatment.
- **Safe Haven (housing):** **Miracle Manor** has long-term housing for **12 families** around the corner from CHOC. **Miracle Manor II** has temporary housing for **10 families**, about 10 minutes from CHOC. **223 people housed, 41,437 nights of sleep.** "More than a home: a community of families." Betsy's quote (son Jayden, leukemia).
- **Balance (wellness):** in-house therapy (**400+ individuals** this year); **80% of families reported counseling decreased their depression**. Movie nights, and Surf & Paddle Camp ("critically-ill children and their siblings… experience the joys of childhood").
- **Boost (basic needs):** Basket of Miracles (several times a year: food, household supplies, toys) and the monthly Box of Miracles. Ingardia Bros. Produce donates vegetables. Host-a-drive model. A Madrid family quote mentions a snack at home and a smile.
- **Pillars (stability):** referrals to Merge Opportunities (employment readiness) and Futuro Health (entry-level medical training), plus financial literacy courses. **78% of family members interviewed were hired**; 100% of Futuro referrals enrolled.

## Giving (Ways to Give, Donate pages)

- Donate page headline: "Be their Miracle." Options are Give Once, Give Monthly and Give with Crypto (70+ currencies).
- **360° Miracle:** a women's giving circle, $1,000 a year or $83.33 a month. Its site claims **3,156 essential bills paid, 512 families served, wellness program expanded 36%** (period not stated). Contact: Laura Collins.
- Other routes: event sponsorship ($1,000 to $100,000, contact Jake Tirabassi), in-kind donations and auction items (Sapphira Roberson), birthday fundraisers ($25+), matching gifts, stock.
- **Volunteer roles:** social media, speakers bureau, Miracle Movers, donation procurement, donor stewards, gala and major events, host a drive, distribute goods, stuffing parties, moving day, manor maintenance. Businesses and individuals. "If you have time and heart, we want you on Team Miracles."
- Events archive: a long history of Night of Miracles galas (themed, for example "The Miracle of Magic"), a golf invitational (20th in 2025), Rockers Against Cancer, a Coto de Caza Tennis Pro-Am, restaurant give-backs.

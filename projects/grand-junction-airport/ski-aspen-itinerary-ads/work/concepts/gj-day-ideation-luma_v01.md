## Build: Side Quests × Roblox (Andres Fabian, 2026-10-08) [LATER]
**Idea:** "Junction Quest", a Roblox experience of the Western Slope. Side quests in Grand Junction (dig up the Brach on Riggs Hill, climb Rim Rock, find the Art on the Corner sculptures, soak at Glenwood); the main quest is Aspen powder. Kids play it; parents see the real trip in the ads and book it. The digital passport links to the physical one at the airport.
**Fit:** strongest for the **multigen family** audience; weak for boomers and the millennial girls' trip.
**Status:** outside 10/14 scope; a partnership needing GJRASA, Roblox and possibly destination approvals (R1 C10).
**Checks before pitching (all UNVERIFIED):** current Roblox audience demographics; Roblox's rules on advertising and links to off-platform purchases for younger users; brand-experience build cost and timeline (studio-built, typically months); how play would be measured against R1's booking-intent KPI; destination and NPS permissions for in-game likenesses.
**For 10/14:** the side-quest language can run in Meta ads **without** Roblox. Use our own game-UI styling only, no Roblox branding or look-alike trade dress.

## Build: "A Grand Night in Grand Junction" + activation (Andres Fabian, 2026-10-08)
**Idea:** the GJ night as an epic, story-worthy night out (tone reference: The Hangover), activated with free perks for travelers whose trip continues to Aspen.
**What's strong:** the name (Grand / Grand Junction) is ownable; it gives the GJ night a reason to exist; high energy for the girls' trip and the millennial couple.
**Flags:**
- **Conflicts with R1's payoff "Ski Aspen rested."** A hangover the night before a 2 h mountain drive also invites a drowsy or impaired driving reading, a real risk for a public air-service alliance. Keep the *epic* and drop the *hangover*: "the night you'll tell stories about".
- **The Hangover is Warner Bros. IP:** no title, characters, poster parody or look-alike scenes.
- **Alcohol:** Meta alcohol ad rules (21+ targeting, regional limits) and Colorado liquor rules on free or discounted drinks: UNVERIFIED, check before any drink perk.
- **"Free passes":** lift tickets and pass offers are prohibited by R1 unless a partner approves. Perks must be partner-funded and client-approved (R1: no offer claims unless approved). Giveaways may need sweepstakes rules (no purchase necessary): check.
- Fit: family and boomer versions need a softer "grand evening".
**Activation mechanic [LATER]:** "Boarding Pass Perks": show a GJT boarding pass at participating Downtown GJ / Palisade / Horizon Drive partners for a perk; an "Aspen-bound" badge via proof of onward trip is complex, so the boarding pass alone is simpler. Brokered by GJRASA with Downtown GJ and Visit GJ.
**For 10/14 (no offer):** "A Grand Night in Grand Junction" can run as the GJ-night beat in carousels and video for the girls' trip and millennial couple, using R1 destinations only, ending on R1's line.

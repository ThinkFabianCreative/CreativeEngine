# Creative platforms: EXPLORE round (v01, 2026-10-08)

CLIENT: Grand Junction Airport / GJRASA · PROJECT: ski-aspen-itinerary-ads
MODE: **EXPLORE.** Rough, divergent, no verdicts. Lines are placeholders that show the thinking, not final copy.
INPUTS: normalized-brief_v02 · target-markets_v02 (Coloradans) · colorado-ski-culture-research_v01
GUARDRAILS (brief §9): no faster / save time / shortcut · no "no tunnel" / "no closures" / road promises · resorts as destinations only · no fares or reliability.
⚠ **Accuracy trap for every platform:** the GJT route still uses I-70 (about 85 mi, GJT → Glenwood). Never "skip I-70" or "no I-70". The enemy is **the Front Range side**: the mountain corridor, Floyd Hill, the tunnel, Vail Pass.

---

## P1 · SAME STATE. DIFFERENT DRIVE.
**Idea:** Coloradans have done the Denver–Aspen drive a hundred times. Turn the map around and show them a Colorado they've driven past: red rock, wine, hot springs on the way in.
**Truth:** I1 + locals' I-70 fatigue.
**Flex:** girls' trip = wine + soak · family = gondola + pool · millennial couple = Monument hike + nightcap · boomers = vapor caves + Carbondale coffee.
**Visual:** split frame: Front Range brake lights vs. snow on red rock. Route line in rust.
**Range:** smart, broad, safe. Easy to run for years.

## P2 · LET I-70 BE SOMEONE ELSE'S STORY
**Idea:** Every Colorado ski weekend comes with an I-70 story: "three hours at the tunnel", "Floyd Hill, again". This weekend the story is the hot springs. Local wink, shared enemy.
**Truth:** research §Pain points 1–4; I-70 folklore.
**Flex:** each audience swaps the bad story for its stop ("We were supposed to be stuck at the tunnel. Instead: Palisade.").
**Visual:** group-chat / text-message device ("where r u" → photo of steam at Glenwood); UGC-style but licensed stock.
**Range:** the most culturally sharp. Risk: leans on negativity. Must stay clever, not whiny. Watch accuracy (P-wide trap).

## P3 · DAY ZERO
**Idea:** Every ski trip has a day nobody counts: the drive. GJT turns it into Day Zero, the day before the skiing that's already holiday.
**Truth:** I3 (arrival day is the day nobody skis).
**Flex:** Day Zero itineraries per audience; carousel = Day Zero → Day One. Works with weekend pass holders (Flex Pass, 2–7 days).
**Visual:** itinerary/countdown typography; "DAY 0" as a graphic mark in the rust band.
**Range:** ownable device, very carousel-native. Must not imply an extra ski day or time saved (C3).

## P4 · RED BEFORE WHITE
**Idea:** On this trip the red rock comes before the powder. A visual platform: every execution opens on red and ends on white.
**Truth:** I4 (snow on red rock is visual property), brand palette is literally rust + sky.
**Flex:** red = audience's stop (Monument, Palisade, Lunch Loops); white = Aspen. The cut from red to white is the story.
**Visual:** strongest art-direction idea. Colour-blocked transitions; rust band → white field.
**Range:** beautiful and ownable. Risk: stock dependency (winter red-rock shots).

## P5 · FLY OVER THE PASS
**Idea:** Coloradans know every curve of the pass. This time, look down on it. One short flight over the Divide, then the scenic side.
**Truth:** the DEN → GJT flight literally crosses the mountain corridor.
**Flex:** the window-seat moment per audience (girls toasting, kids pointing, couples holding hands) → the stop.
**Visual:** aerial of snowy peaks / a ribbon of highway far below; window-seat POV.
**Range:** simple and visual. Check: "over the pass" is geography, not a speed claim. Avoid implying it's quicker.

## P6 · APRÈS BEFORE SKI
**Idea:** Flip the ritual: the tasting, the soak, the toast happen on the way in. You arrive in Aspen already in après mode.
**Truth:** I2 (people mind dead time, not travel time) + R1 stop menu.
**Flex:** girls = Peach Street + hot springs · couples = Palisade tasting · boomers = vapor caves · family = s'mores and pool (après, kid edition).
**Visual:** warm, golden, steamy. Drinks and soaks with snowy peaks behind.
**Range:** fun, social-native. Risk: alcohol-forward for some placements; family version must lean pool, not drinks.

## Framework grounding (added 2026-10-08)

Object type: **02 · Campaign platform** (`creative-object-taxonomy.md`): a generative rule that produces a family of executions, judged on tension, generativity, coherence and brand ownership. Its failure modes ("tagline pretending to be platform", "broad theme with no creative rule", "one execution pretending to be platform") are listed below as DEVELOP flags, not verdicts (EXPLORE mode).

Shared truths (`hey-whipple-copycraft.md` §7): **product truth:** a short DEN→GJT flight, then a 130-mi drive with stops (R1) · **human truth:** people mind dead time, not travel time (I2) · **cultural truth:** I-70 folklore, and the Winter Park Express as proof locals pay to avoid the drive (research). **Enemy** (Whipple §8): the Front Range I-70 corridor ritual, a behavior and inconvenience, not a brand.

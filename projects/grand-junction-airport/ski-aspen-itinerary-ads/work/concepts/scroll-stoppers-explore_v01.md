CLIENT: Grand Junction Airport / GJRASA · PROJECT: ski-aspen-itinerary-ads · BRIEF: normalized-brief_v03
ASK (Andres Fabian): the ads must stop the scroll and be more provocative.
MODE: EXPLORE. These are card-1 / 0–3s hooks; cards 2–5 stay R1 structure (stops → Aspen → "Start sooner. Ski Aspen rested. Fly GJT." + Book Now).
GUARDRAILS checked per line: no faster / time saved; never "skip I-70" (C28); no road, weather or closure promises; no excess drinking (UD5); no CDOT bashing or crash imagery; resorts as destinations only; no skiing the day you land (UD3).

## Provocation principles (why these stop thumbs)
- **Wrong-looking frame** (Pricken context shift): ski gear where it doesn't belong.
- **Picking a fight** with a shared enemy (Whipple §8): the Denver-side drive, not people.
- **Saying the unsayable** (Pricken reversal): an airport telling you not to start in Denver.
- **Text-only bluntness** in a feed full of pretty mountains.

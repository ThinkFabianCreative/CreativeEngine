## Most useful

**1. Icelandair · "Easy to stop, hard to leave"** · Film · Iceland · Apr 2023 · PRO · DESCRIPTION ONLY
LINK: https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/easy-to-stop-hard-to-leave
WHAT IT DOES: sells the connection city as a destination, a free multi-day stopover on the way to somewhere else.
MECHANISM: reversal (the layover as the highlight) · IDEA TYPE: product-truth
BORROW: the logic of our **Grand Junction Night**: the place you pass through becomes the place you don't want to leave. "Hard to leave" maps to "Can we stay one more night?"
DON'T BORROW: the word "stopover" (reads as a delay for a domestic trip) or the "easy to stop, hard to leave" construction.

**2. Etihad · "The Abu Dhabi Stopover Challenge"** · Content · UK · Apr 2017 · PRO · DESCRIPTION ONLY
LINK: https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/the-abu-dhabi-stopover-challenge
WHAT IT DOES: a musician tries to cram 24 activities into a two-day layover to prove how much the stop city holds.
MECHANISM: demonstration / exaggeration
BORROW: a **challenge format** for the GJ night (how much can a girls' trip or a couple fit into one Grand Junction evening + morning?), and native social content.
DON'T BORROW: celebrity dependence; checklist cramming that contradicts "Ski Aspen rested".

**3. Austria Tourism · "NoSki Instructor"** · Film / social · Austria · Nov 2024 · PRO · KEY VISUAL VIEWED 2026-10-08 (via roastbrief.us image supplied by Andres; film not watched)
LINK: https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/noski-instructor
WHAT IT DOES: a comedian "instructor" teaches everything a ski holiday offers *off* the slopes (snow hikes, ice bathing, food, sleep) in TikTok/Instagram snippets.
MECHANISM: reversal (an instructor for not skiing) · IDEA TYPE: category-cliché flipped
LOOK: live-action photography; a comedian in a red towel and headband lounges deadpan in a wooden sauna, mountains through the window. Character-led, warm wood palette.
BORROW: **positive, funny reframing of the non-ski parts** as the point; serial short social lessons fit "side quests" (wine, soak, red rock) for our audiences.
DON'T BORROW: the instructor character or "NoSki" naming; we still sell skiing Aspen.

**4. Arapahoe Basin · "Leave No Days Behind"** · Film · US (Colorado) · Aug 2024 · PRO · DESCRIPTION ONLY
LINK: https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/leave-no-days-behind
WHAT IT DOES: a Colorado ski area tells pass holders not to waste the season: an office escape cut to skiing.
MECHANISM: enemy (lost days) · tone: urgent, local
USE: **a Done-before marker in our market**: Front Range skiers have seen "don't waste days" ski urgency. Our angle (the way there is part of the trip) is distinct; avoid office-escape openers.

**5. Visit Dublin, Ohio · "Don't Plan a Trip to Dublin, Ohio. Just Go."** · Digital/Film · US · Jun 2025 · PRO · DESCRIPTION ONLY
LINK: https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/don-t-plan-a-trip-to-dublin-ohio-bff4bdfe-e19c-4b16-9bcb-b45621f71a20
WHAT IT DOES: a small destination sells spontaneity: skip the planning, just show up.
MECHANISM: reversal (advise against planning) · IDEA TYPE: permission
BORROW: **the confident small-city voice**: a modest place being cheeky about itself, useful for Grand Junction's tone.
DON'T BORROW: the "Don't plan… just go" line structure.

## What this tells our work
- **Grand Night / stopover logic has strong precedent** (Icelandair, Etihad). Make it domestic and positive, and don't call it a stopover.
- **The positive "not just skiing" angle is proven in tourism** (Austria NoSki). It supports the "stay positive" direction and Side Quests.
- **Colorado ski urgency ads exist** (A-Basin); keep our angle about the journey.
- **No precedent found** for skis-on-a-cactus / desert-to-powder in one trip, in these searches. Run a proper Done-before check before presenting.

Limit: one archive, four searches, films not watched. "Not found" ≠ "never done".

## Done-before: CLOSE, deliberate remix
**DECISION (Andres Fabian, 2026-10-08):** remixing Austria's NoSki Instructor is intentional; we make it our own. The borrowed part is the off-slope instructor device; ours differs on route, pro-ski stance and ski-jargon comedy (below). State that distance if the client asks.

Austria Tourism Board · "NoSki Instructor" (Wien Nord Serviceplan, Nov 2024, social film; `tourism-references_v01.md` #3) is a comedian instructor teaching the off-slope winter (snow hikes, ice baths, food, sleep). "AntiSki Instructor" + off-slope lessons is the **same mechanism and the same key idea** (ad-reference distance: CLOSE, bordering SAME on name). Present only with a clear, stated difference.

## The difference that makes it ours
1. **The instructor teaches the way there, not the resort.** Every lesson happens on the GJT → Aspen route (R1 stops): that's the USP; Austria's is about the destination.
2. **Pro-ski, not anti-ski.** Our instructor is a ski instructor who insists you warm up properly first: the lessons are "pre-ski", ending at Aspen's first chair. Fits brief (we sell skiing Aspen) and "stay positive".
3. **Ski-lesson language applied to the journey:** the comedy is technical ski-instruction terms for non-ski things (that's the device Austria doesn't use).

# Target markets: origin cities × destinations (v01, 2026-10-08)

CLIENT: Grand Junction Airport / GJRASA · PROJECT: ski-aspen-itinerary-ads
STATUS: Recommendation. GJT routes are UNVERIFIED (brief C9 / Q3). Drive times are internal estimates (resort-drive-times_v01.md). Vail is not in R1's scope: adding it is a proposal for the client to approve.

## Destinations

| Destination | From GJT | From DEN | Why it works / doesn't | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Aspen Snowmass** | ~2–2.5 h [R1] | ~3.5–4.5 h [R1] | Clearest case. No Vail Pass, no Eisenhower Tunnel. | **Lead** |
| **Vail / Beaver Creek** | ~2–2.5 h / ~2 h | ~2 h | No time win. The win is skipping Vail Pass and the Eisenhower Tunnel, the worst stretch of the Denver drive. Eagle County airport (EGE) is closer than GJT, so GJT must win on routes, availability or the itinerary. | **Secondary**, client approval needed; "skip the pass" framing only (C25 wording) |

## Origin cities

**Tier 1: GJT nonstop markets** (seen in the old Figma ads / web; CONFIRM Q3). One flight, no Denver connection, so the story is strongest here.

| City | Airport | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Dallas–Fort Worth | DFW | Texas is a big Colorado ski market; American service reported |
| Phoenix | PHX | Large drive-to-ski market with no local skiing to speak of |
| Las Vegas | LAS | Reported nonstop |
| Los Angeles / Orange County | SNA (LAX?) | Reported Santa Ana service |
| Salt Lake City | SLC | Delta hub, so it works as a connection point too; local skiers less likely |

**Tier 2: big ski-origin markets connecting through DEN** (story = "connect to GJT instead of driving from DEN")

| City | Why |
|---|---|
| Houston | Texas ski volume; DEN connection |
| Chicago | Top-tier Colorado ski origin |
| New York / New Jersey | Affluent, Aspen-skewed |
| Atlanta | Strong winter fly-to-ski market |
| Minneapolis | Ski-culture market |
| Boston | Ski-culture, affluent |
| San Francisco Bay Area | Affluent; Aspen-skewed |

**Exclude:** the Colorado Front Range (Denver, Boulder, Colorado Springs). Locals drive; this hook doesn't fit them.

## Audience × market lean (starting hypothesis, test in media)
- Millennial girls' trip / millennial couple: DFW, PHX, LAS, Chicago, NY
- Multigen family: DFW, Houston, Atlanta, Chicago
- Boomer couple: SNA/LA, NY, Boston, SF, PHX

All CONFIRM with client media data. Never put route names in ads until Q3 confirms them.

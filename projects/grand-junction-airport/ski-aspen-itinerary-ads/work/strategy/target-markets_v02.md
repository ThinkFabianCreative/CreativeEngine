# Target markets v02: in-state Colorado (2026-10-08)

CLIENT: Grand Junction Airport / GJRASA · PROJECT: ski-aspen-itinerary-ads
DECISION: Andres Fabian, 2026-10-08: the campaign targets **Coloradans**. This supersedes v01 (out-of-state). Brief Q1 is answered by user instruction; I5 in brief v02 is reversed. Route DEN→GJT is the R1 route story; United DEN–GJT nonstop ~1h20 is [RESEARCH] UNVERIFIED.

## What changes
- v01 logic said "Denver locals won't fly then drive." For Coloradans the reason to fly *is* the I-70 drive they already know: Friday traffic, Vail Pass, the tunnel queue, chain laws. They don't need it explained; they've lived it.
- Still no "faster" (C3). The promise to a local is **skip the I-70 grind and start the weekend relaxed**, not save time.
- New realities to resolve: a car at GJT (rental needed; R1 has no offer), the cost of a short flight vs gas, and the weekend-trip format (locals ski in short trips, often Fri–Sun).

## Destinations for Coloradans
- **Aspen Snowmass: lead.** It's the longest, most dreaded drive from the Front Range.
- **Vail / Beaver Creek: weak for locals.** Front Range skiers reach Vail in ~2h by car, so a flight + 2h drive makes no sense. Drop it, unless it's framed only as "skip Vail Pass" for Beaver Creek. Not recommended.

## Audience fit (in-state)
- Millennial girls' trip: Denver, Boulder — the weekend escape; Palisade + hot springs is a real draw for locals who've never stopped
- Millennial couple: Denver, Boulder, Fort Collins
- Multigen family: Denver suburbs, Colorado Springs — the hardest car to be stuck in on I-70
- Boomer couple: Denver, Boulder, Colorado Springs — "done the I-70 grind for 30 years"

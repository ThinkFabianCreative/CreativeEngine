System in one line

The photograph is the mountain; the ski manual is drawn on top of it. Rust lesson band (inherited from the old Meta band) = the class tag; white 4px instruction line, dashed paths, arrowheads and sky-blue numbered steps = the instructor's chalk. The numbers run 1 to 5 across the carousel, so the itinerary reads as a syllabus. Navy band only on graduation.

Craft checks that matter most here

  • Diagram restraint (visual-craft): max 1 path + 1 callout + 1 number per card; the line must point at something real in the photo, or it is decoration. Final comps draw the diagram to each licensed photo, not a template.
  • Contrast of white line on snow (visual-craft, real-condition): on bright snow cards (Aspen) the line flips to navy. Test at phone size.
  • Headline face (typography): proposed Azo Sans Uber (same family/kit as site, heavy wide caps close to the old F1 band). Alt: Monument Extended (not Adobe Fonts, licensing needed). Confirm F1 face with designer before production.
  • Reading order: band headline → diagram → logo. Logo visible on every card (brief: immediately visible).
  • Safe zones: 9:16 nothing in top 270px or bottom 340px (Stories); if also run in Reels, lift CTA above ~1250px.

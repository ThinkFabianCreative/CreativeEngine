## 1. Lesson lines (24). ★ = top 6

Format: LESSON NAME / instructor cue. Names work as on-image titles; cues as captions or primary text.

**Landing at GJT (Day 0)**
1. ★ **Lesson 1: Unclipping.** "Step out of the car mindset. Literally, you flew."
2. **The bunny slope.** "Jet bridge, baggage claim, done. Everyone graduates the bunny slope." *(check: no small-airport-ease claim, C27; see §6. Alt-safe version: "Everyone starts on the bunny slope. Ours has a baggage claim.")*
3. **Proper stance on arrival.** "Feet shoulder-width. Sunglasses up. Group text: 'landed.'"

**A Grand Night in Grand Junction (Main Street dinner)**
4. ★ **Dinner on the fall line.** "Pick your line down Main Street. Dessert is not a detour."
5. **Linking turns.** "Shop. Coffee. Shop. Dinner. Smooth transitions, ladies."
6. **Reading the terrain.** "Scan the menu like a trail map. Black diamond: the special."
7. **Group lesson.** "Stay together. Nobody goes off-piste without the group chat."
8. **The traverse.** "Cross Main at a gentle angle. Toward the patio heater."

**Peach Street nightcap (tasting/nightcap only)**
9. ★ **Après, correctly sequenced.** "Rule one: après comes after skiing. Tonight we practice."
10. **Pole plant.** "One glass. Plant it. Pivot to bed. Aspen tomorrow."
11. **Controlled descent.** "Peach brandy, small pour, slow finish. Form over speed."

**Palisade tasting (alt card)**
12. **The tasting stance.** "Weight forward. Swirl. Commit to the downhill sip."
13. **Edge control.** "Hold the glass by the stem. Same as holding an edge: lightly."
14. **Wine country, first tracks.** "Red rock at the window. Be first to the tasting room."  *(drop if tasting hours unconfirmed, Q17)*

**Glenwood soak (Day 1, on the way)**
15. ★ **Proper soak form.** "Shoulders down. Knees soft. Hold that steam."
16. **Snowplow, reversed.** "Toes out, heels out, fully out. You're floating now."
17. **The tuck.** "Chin in. Ears under. Snowflakes land on your forehead only."
18. **Ski-lift posture, rehearsed.** "Sit back. Let it carry you. Same at the chair tomorrow."
19. **Vapor cave breathing.** "In through the nose. Out through the week you had." *(Yampah, C18)*

**Carbondale / Mt Sopris (last easy stop)**
20. **Spotting your line.** "Look at Sopris, not your phone. Look where you want to go."
21. **The final warm-up.** "Coffee. Stretch. Photo. In that order."

**Aspen graduation (first chair = Day 2, never Day 0)**
22. ★ **Graduation: first chair.** "You warmed up properly. Now ski."
23. ★ **Lesson complete.** "Rested legs. Loud group. Four mountains. Class dismissed."
24. **Certified.** "Pre-Ski School: passed with flying colors. Literally, you flew."

Top 6 rationale: 1 and 22 bookend the class; 4 (Grand Night), 9 (nightcap with built-in restraint), 15 (cleanest cue-to-subject fit in the set) and 23 (group energy for the end card).

## 5. Alternative hooks for card 1

1. **"Class starts at GJT."** (lead, in the carousel above) Clean, sets the device.
2. **"Your ski lesson starts at baggage claim."** Bigger surprise, more literal; risk: reads as small-airport ease (C27). Safe only if the image is the crew walking out, not a speed cue.
3. **"Before first chair: four lessons."** Syllabus curiosity, promises a payoff, carousel-native ("swipe for the syllabus").
4. (bonus) **"Pre-Ski School. No skiing required. Yet."** Positive anti-ski wink without becoming "NoSki"; the "Yet" keeps us pro-ski.

## 6. Self-critique

**Headline stress test (end line + top hook)**
- "Class starts at GJT": short, ownable to this platform, puts the brand in the hook. Weakness: without the "Pre-Ski School" tag it is ambiguous (flight school?). Keep the tag on the same card.
- Lessons rated honestly: 15 (soak form) and 4 (fall line) are the only cues that are funny on a cold read with no setup. 9 needs the word "après" to be understood by the audience; Front Range millennials will get it. 2, 6, 8, 13 are weak (puns that explain themselves); 2 is also a claim risk. Cut 2, 6, 8, 13 before client.
- Repetition risk: "Literally, you flew" appears in 1 and 24; use once (keep in 1).

**Smile in the mind**
- Strongest: the reader finishes the joke by recognizing the coaching cue ("knees soft") on a non-ski body. That is the gap. When the line names the joke ("Same at the chair tomorrow", 18) the smile dies. Prefer cues that the image completes.
- The graduation card turns the joke into the payoff: lessons = warm-up, Aspen = the real exam. This is what separates us from NoSki: our instructor wants you on the mountain.

**Claim check**
- No faster / time saved / shortcut: pass. "Start sooner" only in R1's own end line. "Unclipping... you flew" is mode, not speed.
- Never "skip I-70": pass. No I-70, Denver, traffic or road words anywhere.
- No road/weather/closure: pass. "Bluebird" is image direction, not a promise; keep it off on-image copy.
- Resorts destinations only, no passes: pass. "Four mountains" from C21, no logos.
- No fares / reliability: pass. Hook 2 and lesson 2 flirt with small-airport ease (C27): flagged, lesson 2 cut.
- No skiing the day you land: pass. Sequence is Day 0 arrival + dinner + nightcap, Day 1 drive + soak, graduation shown as morning first chair. Design must not put skis on the crew in card 1.
- R1 stops only, no invented venues: pass. Restaurants, shops, coffee are generic (C8). Peach Street, Glenwood Hot Springs / Iron Mountain, Yampah named as destinations only.
- End line + Book Now on every piece: pass (carousel card 5, Stories A/E, video 13–15).
- **OPEN / risk, Peach Street logistics:** Peach Street is in Palisade (~20 min from GJT per C15), not on Main Street. A nightcap there after dinner implies someone drives after drinking, which UD5 forbids in spirit. Options: (a) make it a Day 1 morning/midday tasting on the way east (still drink-then-drive; weaker), (b) keep card 3 visually a tasting with no car anywhere and a "designated-driver" or rideshare assumption off-ad, (c) swap card 3 for Palisade alt or a Main Street nightcap (generic, no venue). **Recommend (c) for the client draft, or keep Peach Street only if account confirms a sensible framing.** Alcohol ad rules (C30, Q19) still unverified; 21+ targeting required.
- Venue hours for tastings (Q17) unconfirmed: no "open late" or timing language used.

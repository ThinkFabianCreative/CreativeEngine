### 16.2 Strategy improvement (requested)

**I1. The drive isn't the problem; the Denver drive is.** Basis: R1 contrasts "a long mountain drive from Denver" with the GJT route; geography. The Denver route (I-70 west) crosses the Eisenhower–Johnson Tunnel and Vail Pass; the GJT route runs I-70 east along the Colorado River, then Hwy 82 south from Glenwood Springs, and crosses neither. **Correction to v01:** v01 said the GJT route "runs through Glenwood Canyon" and has "no pass, no tunnel". Both are wrong as written: Glenwood Canyon lies east of Glenwood Springs, on the *Denver* side; the GJT route instead runs through De Beque Canyon, and I-70 there passes through the Beavertail Mountain Tunnel. Safe derived wording, pending a map check (C25): "no Vail Pass, no Eisenhower Tunnel" or "skip the mountain passes"-type framing. Never "no tunnel", never "no closures", never road-condition promises (C4). Confidence: medium-high on geography, unverified.

## Type

- **Headline:** heavy, extended/wide geometric sans, all caps, tight leading, white on rust. Face NOT identified: the Figma API needs a layer selected in the desktop app, or the designer can name it. Close candidates: Monument Extended / Clash Grotesk Bold / Azo Sans Black-style wide caps. Do not guess in production.
- **Sentence-case headline** (card variant): bold geometric sans, white.
- **Subhead/body:** regular geometric sans, white, sentence case. Possibly Azo Sans (the website face). Confirm.
- **Label pill and CTA:** bold caps, letterspaced, small.

## Headline band (layout)

- Story 9:16: rust band across the top with rounded bottom corners, holding a 3-line caps headline + a 1-line subhead. Full-bleed photo below. Keeps clear of the Stories UI.
- Card variant: rust rounded card floating over the photo. White pill label → bold sentence-case headline → 2-line body → full-width black pill CTA with arrow.

## Photo style

Full-bleed, saturated, warm daylight; red-rock and high-desert landscapes, people active in the frame (biking, rafting, climbing, wine with friends), golden-hour skies. Subject in the lower two-thirds, sky or calm area behind the band.

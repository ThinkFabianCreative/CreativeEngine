## Decisions (Andres Fabian, 2026-10-21)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Invitation or attitude? | **Both.** It's an invitation to misbehave: eat the fried food you're not supposed to, ride the rides that may or may not make you sick, play the carnival games nobody wins, get your hands on produce and the land in ways your family normally can't, and make memories across every generation |
| 2005 overlap ("It's Callin' You Out to Play") | **Pay homage.** 25 years later it's vintage, retro, cool. The pitch names the echo on purpose |
| Reference the Offspring song? | **Yes.** Local, relevant, irreverent |
| Will "extra" hold up in 2030? | **Yes.** Proceed |

### What the decisions change
- **Platform idea:** *an invitation to misbehave.* "Come Out and Play" is the invitation; "Extra is Normal" is the permission.
- **The homage becomes a story for the pitch:** "In 2005 the Fair called you out to play. In 2030, we're answering." It shows we know the archive and turns the overlap into a strength.
- **The Offspring:** OC's own anthem title, referenced openly. Title use and pitch-room reference are fine; using the recording, the band's name in ads or the lyrics needs licensing and band approval, and the song's lyric is about street violence, so we use the title and the attitude, never the lyrics.
- **New fifth pillar, Extra dirt:** produce, livestock and the land, the misbehavior that's actually good for you.

### Watch-outs that come with "misbehave" (to handle, not to block)
- The carnival operator and vendors are District partners: the "games nobody wins" and "rides that might make you sick" jokes must read as affectionate, never as a claim about rigged games or unsafe rides.
- A state agency inviting families to "misbehave" needs the wink to be obvious: misbehaving = indulgence and play, never rule-breaking or alcohol.

## Campaign platform

### Insight
Orange County has a reputation for being polished and controlled. Its real culture has always been a little extra: surf, skate, punk, ska, car culture, lowriders, theme parks built on orange groves. Every family has a part of themselves that only comes out at the Fair.

### Tension
**Everyday OC tells you to tone it down. The Fair is the one place that tells you to turn it up.**

### Platform line (multi-year)
## Come Out and Play.
### ¡Sal a jugar!

An invitation, in the Fair's own tradition of calls to action ("Come & Get It," "Free Your Inner Farmer," "It's Callin' You Out to Play"). It can run beyond 2030, and every property can use it.

### Attitude (2030 theme)
## Extra is Normal.
### Aquí lo exagerado es normal.

The rule of the Fair. Every execution proves it.

### Lockup
```
COME OUT AND PLAY.
Extra is normal at the OC Fair.
¡Sal a jugar! · OC Fair · Since 1890
```
*Open question for the team: which line leads, the invitation or the attitude. Explore both.*

### The device: the NORMAL stamp
A loud, saturated, mid-moment photo, with one small calm word in the corner: **NORMAL.** Set like an official label. The gap between the photo and the word is the joke, and the stamp carries the theme even where no line is written.

### Visual world: OC flyer culture
- '90s OC show-flyer energy: photocopied textures, cut-and-paste type, sticker bombs, ska checkerboard.
- Evolved for 2030, not a costume party. Clean hierarchy, real Fair photography, the OC Fair orange-and-leaf mark on every piece.
- Range in casting and music history: OC's scene was punk, ska, surf, Chicano rock, lowrider culture, and today's K-pop and corridos too.

### Pillars (what the work talks about)

| Pillar | Proves "extra is normal" through | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Extra food** | The over-the-top things only the Fair makes | *Deep-fried [placeholder]. Normal.* |
| **Extra devotion** | Ag, 4-H and competitions: people who care too much | *Six hours on a steer's hair. Normal.* |
| **Extra family** | Three generations doing too much together | *Three coolers, two wagons, one Grandma. Normal.* |
| **Extra nights** | Concerts, the midway after dark | *A goat show and a headliner. Normal.* |
| **Extra dirt** | Produce, livestock, the land: the misbehavior that's good for you | *Pulled a carrot. Met a goat. Normal.* |
| **Extra OC** | The county's own loud culture and soundtrack | *Checkered Vans since '95. Normal.* |

### Engines (what keeps it producing)
1. **The NORMAL stamp** on every moment, from TV to onsite signage.
2. **The Extra Awards.** The Fair crowns the most extra creation: vendor food first, then 4-H costumes, concert outfits, family wagons. Public vote, judged with the Fair's own stamps. Winners become in-flight ads. *(Inspired by the principle behind Sydney Royal Easter Show's "The Winning Bite": crown what's already outrageous. We don't copy its name or format.)*
3. **Come Out and Play trails:** five-stop routes of the most extra things at the Fair, with a stamp card.
4. **The OC soundtrack:** every generation's car-ride-to-the-Fair song. A guest playlist, not necessarily licensed tracks.

### Channels (one line each)
- **TV/CTV :30, "Normal."** Rapid, deadpan shots of OC behaving at work, in traffic, at a quiet dinner. Then hard cut at the Fair gates: the same people screaming, frying, judging, dancing. NORMAL stamp. *Come out and play.*
- **Radio:** a calm, polite voice reads absurd Fair facts like a public-service notice. "This is normal." Spanish recorded natively, not translated.
- **OOH:** one extra photo, one NORMAL stamp. Freeway-readable.
- **Social:** "Is this normal?" posts from guests and creators; the Fair stamps them NORMAL. Live Extra Awards voting.
- **Web/planner:** "How extra are you today?" picks your day, your trail and your tickets.
- **Onsite:** NORMAL stamp stations, the Extra Awards stage, trails, an "Extra since 1890" exhibit.
- **Properties:** Pacific Amphitheatre gets "Come out and play" for the season. Imaginology: "Extra curious is normal." Centennial Farm: "Extra muddy is normal." Heroes Hall stays out of the jokes.

### Phasing

| When | Phase | What runs |
|---|---|---|
| Jan–Mar | Reveal & recruit | The "Normal" film reveal; Extra Awards entries open for vendors and competitors |
| Apr–Jun | Plan & buy | "Come out and play" presale, trails planner, concert season |
| Jul–Aug | Live | Daily NORMAL stamps, live Extra Awards, midweek "Extra Wednesday" lines |
| After | Thank you | "Extra, Normal, 2030" recap; 2031 tease |

### What we'd measure
Extra Awards entries and votes · UGC with the NORMAL stamp · planner-to-purchase · midweek mix · 18–34 share of purchasers · brand-lift recall.

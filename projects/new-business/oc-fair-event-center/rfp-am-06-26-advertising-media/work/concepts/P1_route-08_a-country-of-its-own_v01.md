## The idea

For 23 days a year, 150 acres in Costa Mesa become a country of its own: its own flag, motto, passport, currency, dish, sport, anthem, guard, court and regions. Everyone in Orange County is a citizen. Nobody needs a visa.

## Tone: warm and touristy, never screening

**In:** passports, flags, banners, mottos, sigils, postcards, stamps, a court, kingdoms and regions, ceremonies, honorary titles, a tourism board.
**Never:** customs, checkpoints, inspections, "papers, please," border walls, deportation or citizenship-test jokes. In Orange County that lands on real families. Every border moment is a welcome.

## Visual direction: Wonka × Seuss × Alice in Wonderland, dressing a nation

What each influence gives the country (mood only; our own world, our own characters):

| Influence | What it gives the nation |
|---|---|
| **Willy Wonka** | Showmanship and impossible treats. Tailcoats, a guide who performs the tour, a golden-ticket feeling for the Passport |
| **Dr. Seuss** | Shape and rhyme. Tall curving hats, leaning signposts, stacked arrows, nothing perfectly straight. The Rules of Extra written in rhyme |
| **Alice in Wonderland** | Court and ceremony with a wink. Card-soldier guards, a court that hands out honors, "this way / that way" signs, a storybook map, rabbit-hole wonder |

**Signature move: the Ticket Guard.** Alice's card soldiers become the nation's guard, but their tabards are giant OC Fair ride tickets, the national currency. You see a guard and you see the currency and the Fair at once.

### Wayfinding (a map you can walk)

- **The national map:** an illustrated storybook map of the 150 acres, every region with its sigil, little Seuss hills, a rabbit-hole "you are here." Printed on the Passport's inside cover and on giant boards.
- **Leaning signposts:** Seuss-curved posts with stacked arrows, each in its region color: *"Pig Races, this way. Funnel cake, that way. Normal, nowhere."*
- **Region gateways:** an arch at each region with its banner and sigil: *"You are now entering the Barns. Population: 400 goats."*
- **Distances that make no sense:** *"Corn dogs: 47 steps. Your diet: 2,000 miles."*
- **Bilingual by design:** every sign in English and Spanish, equal size.
- **Accessibility:** clear plain-language layer under the whimsy (big type, high contrast, pictograms, accessible routes marked). The joke never costs anyone their way.

## How it plays (carried from the original frame)

- **Tourism-board films** (VisitOSLO premise): a glossy travel film for a 150-acre nation, hosted by the Grand Guide. *"Experience the ancient tradition of the pig race."*
- **Passport stamps:** each region is a stamp, and collecting them is the trail (the scavenger-hunt idea with a reason to exist). Collect them all to become an honorary citizen.
- **Onsite:** the welcome arch at the gate ("You are now entering a country of its own"), Citizen of the Day, the sunset anthem led by the Grand Guide, the Court of Extra at the competitions, the Ticket Guard on parade.
- **Every property is a region,** year-round: the Amphitheatre as the nation's concert hall; Centennial Farm and The Ranch as the countryside; Imaginology as the academy; Heroes Hall honored quietly.
- **Engines carried from Route 07:** "Extra is Normal" as the motto and the NORMAL stamp; the Is-This-Normal Line; the Extra Awards, presented by the Court.
- **National calendar:** Independence Day (reveal), Passport Season (presale), The Nation Is Open (Fair), See You Next Year (recap).

## Relationship to the other routes

- **Route 07 (Extra is Normal / Come out and play)** stays its own route. Route 08 borrows its motto, stamp and engines.
- **Deck row 3 ("The Extra-ordinary Works")**: the steampunk machine is demoted to one region, the Midway, if it's kept at all.

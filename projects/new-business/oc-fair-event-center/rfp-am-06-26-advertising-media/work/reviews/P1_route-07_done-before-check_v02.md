## What this means

1. **"Come Out and Play" can't lead.** Six Flags used the exact line in Oct 2025. Keep it inside the manifesto as the Offspring nod and the 2005 homage.
2. **The :30 needs a new spine.** "Office behaving → hard cut at the gates" is the theme-park default (Universal did it). Ours has to start from something only OC and only the Fair have. Options: start inside the Fair and never leave it; or make the "behaving" half specific OC clichés (the HOA letter, the Whole Foods parking lot, the 405) so the contrast is about Orange County, not about work.
3. **The NORMAL stamp's tone is close to Six Flags' current platform.** Deadpan official seriousness is their 2025 voice. Our difference has to be visible: NORMAL is a *permission label* on real guest moments, not a spokes-character acting serious. Don't add a spokes-character to Route 07 (That's Fair's Judge would also sit close to the Funsultant).
4. **"Extra" is clear,** with Las Vegas as the nearest shared territory. Keep the family, ag and OC-specific content so ours can't read as a destination-tourism spot.
5. **Events, festivals and award shows** turned up no line or device overlap.

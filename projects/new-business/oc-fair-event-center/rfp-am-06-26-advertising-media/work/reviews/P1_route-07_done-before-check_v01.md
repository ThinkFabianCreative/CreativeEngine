## Conclusion

**"Extra is Normal" is clear here. "Come Out and Play" is not:** Six Flags used it as a campaign title in October 2025. The idea is different (horror vs. family misbehavior), but it's the same line in the same category, from a SoCal leisure competitor, one year before our pitch. A committee member may have seen it.

Options, for the team to decide:
1. **Lead with "Extra is Normal,"** and use "Come out and play" only as the OC-song nod inside the manifesto and the 2005 homage.
2. **Keep "Come Out and Play" as the lead** and own the distance openly: the Offspring and the Fair's own 2005 theme came first. Risky in a written proposal where we can't explain it in person.
3. **Twist it** so it's ours: e.g. *"Orange County, come out and play."*

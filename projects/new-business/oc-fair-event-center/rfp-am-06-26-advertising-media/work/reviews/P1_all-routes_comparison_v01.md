## What the comparison tells us

1. **The new work is better, and not by a little.** The originals were five well-produced versions of the same move: a common phrase made ownable by a device (seal, ticket, stamp, chat bubble, marquee). Every one has "medium" ownability on the words. Route 08 is the first idea where the ownability is in the idea, not the sticker on it.
2. **The originals' best thinking already lives in 08.** Made Here's people, Unexpected's two-beat discoveries, That's Fair's date-mix wit, Meet Me's address, Best Night's sunset ritual, Everybody's button, Extra is Normal. 08 is the frame that finally holds them. That's a strong pitch story in itself: we explored widely and converged.
3. **The originals still win on finish.** 01–05 have full channel sets, Spanish radio and phasing. 08 has two load-bearing gaps (Ticket Guard frame, film frames; CD review v03). The Section 4 build should borrow the originals' channel architecture, phasing table and measurement slide structure rather than re-invent them.
4. **The deck's recommendation (Made Here) no longer holds.** Rescored with the done-before evidence, the whole original set drops 0.3–0.8. Nothing in it beats 08 on any of the scoring items except Spanish (04) and properties (01, 04), and those are one point.

## Where 08 is weakest, honestly

- **Spanish:** "Un país aparte" vs "La Feria es otro país" is still open; needs the linguist and Latino creative input before submission (scored 4, not 5).
- **Year-round stretch:** a 23-day nation and year-round venues pull in opposite directions. The "every property is a region" answer works; it should be stated so a reviewer doesn't find the tension first.
- **Shown vs described:** per CD review v03, the Ticket Guard and film frames.

## Recommendation

Submit **Route 08**. Don't present the other routes in Section 4. If the team wants proof of exploration, one slide in the full concept book: "Seven roads to one country," showing what each earlier route contributed. Due Tue Oct 27, 11:00 a.m. PT.

## Addendum: gen-AI signatures in the original five (deck, Oct 19)

Read: full deck text; rendered pages 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 75. Photography is real (District media gallery); no AI faces found in the pages checked. The tells are structural and verbal.

**Structural (strongest tell)**
- Five concepts, each given identical treatment: same chapter order (idea → headline system → identity → key art → TV → radio EN/ES → OOH → social → … → phasing), the same page headers, and the same "why it scores / watch-outs" boxes. Symmetry like this signals generation, not authorship.
- Identity slides (15, 30, 45, 60, 75) are one template: logo on top, a lockup centred, an English panel left and a Spanish panel right, and four bullets for device, palette, type and photography. Only the color changes.
- Every concept gets one "ownable device" (seal, ticket, stamp, chat bubble, marquee) plus a "sonic logo." It reads like a checklist, not like ideas.
- Self-scoring that grades its own work against the rubric and recommends itself, while including the generator's hedges ("A close call," "This one is a team call").

**Verbal**
- One headline formula per concept, run on repeat: "___ made here." / "Came for X. Found Y." / "X? That's fair." / "Meet me at X." / "Best night of summer, with…". The fill-in-the-blank engine is the most common shape for an AI campaign.
- Stock pairings: "Come for the sunset. Stay for the lights." "Came for the headliner. Stayed for the sunset." "Came for one thing. Found everything."
- Forced triads and stacked middots: "Proud, warm, a little wry," "Curious, warm and quick-witted," "Cinematic, euphoric, a little nostalgic, never rowdy." Every tone line uses the same three-adjectives-plus-a-qualifier shape.
- Self-praise in the rationale: "Strongest storytelling engine of the five," "Best year-round umbrella," "Clearest payoff."
- Disclaimer boilerplate repeated on every slide: "Spec comps for internal review… placeholders until confirmed by the District."
- "Spanish written natively, not translated" is said about a dozen times. Saying it that often reads as a claim, not as proof.
- Insight wording that is close to generic: "Everyone arrives with a reason and leaves with a story."

**Visual**
- Default AI-deck palettes: cream + navy + orange (01), cream + teal + red-orange (02), purple-to-orange sunset gradient with glowing marquee type (05), and a light-blue sky gradient with chat bubbles (04).
- Glow text on a dark gradient (05) and the rubber-stamp-with-texture graphic (03) are stock template moves.
- Rounded friendly sans in bubbles (04) and heavy grotesque with one colored word (02): the default "friendly brand" type.

**What survives the audit:** the research pages (theme archive, attendance, the 300k gap), the real photography, the Spanish lines (several are well written: "Baja el calor. Sube la fiesta."), and the specific lines with a real observation in them ("Entering your abuela's recipe without telling her?").

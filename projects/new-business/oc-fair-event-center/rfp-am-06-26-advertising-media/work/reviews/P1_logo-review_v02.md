## Two systems are now on the table

**1. GPT's responsive swirl system** (full ≥200 px · compact 100–199 · OC stamp <100)
- Full: your swirl lockup outline, unchanged. Strong.
- Compact: re-set in a plain tracked serif. Legible, but it loses the lettering's character; it no longer looks like the same brand.
- Stamp: a circle with "OC". Clean at 32 px, but generic: no leaf, no Fairlandia, nothing only the Fair owns.

**2. The endorsed lockup: OC FAIR + "A Country of Its Own"** (your .ai and the 3D renders)
- The real OC Fair mark (orange, leaf, OCFAIR with the swash R) carries the theme line in a wavy swash script underneath.
- This solves the masterbrand question: the Fair's logo is the hero, the theme is the year. The committee sees its own brand first.
- Versions: original-color flat (orange/white/black + gold theme line), one-color flat (black, "flatAndWavy"), 3D gold (Berkshire hero, FlatWavy v1, GPT ornate 02_38), 3D copper-orange (OCFair_ACounttry-3D), dark-extrusion gold (Asset 2).

## Issues to resolve

1. **Two theme wordmarks now exist:** the swirl lockup and the wavy swash line. Pick one theme lettering so the system has one voice. Recommendation: the wavy line inside the OC FAIR lockup for client-facing; the swirl lockup stays as the standalone hero for the nation (cover, zeppelin).
2. **"of Its" stacked small** in the wavy line closes up below ~250 px wide. Same fix as before: enlarge it or set it on the baseline.
3. **Small sizes:** replace GPT's generic "OC" stamp with the Fair's own orange-and-leaf icon (it already exists and is ownable), and keep the passport stamp for in-world uses at ≥80 px.
4. **Compact:** derive it from the chosen theme lettering, not a new serif.
5. **Recoloring the District's mark** (gold, copper): keep at least one version in the Fair's official colors as the primary. Gold/3D versions are celebratory treatments to show as options. (Noted once.)

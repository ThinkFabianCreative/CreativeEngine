## The big tropes (deck-wide)

1. **Same slide, 20 times.** Top-left serif headline, small body copy, content in a box or grid to the right, empty bottom third. No full-bleed moments except the satin slides. No scale contrast between slides.
2. **Headline-as-sentence.** Every slide headline is a full-sentence Fraunces Black line ("Every country needs the paperwork of joy.", "A country with a closing date sells itself by the day.", "The delegation is coming to your farmers market."). It's the slogan-on-every-slide pattern.
3. **Boxes for everything.** Tickets (2), thumbnails (7), postcards (10), stamps (13), passport cells (14), people tiles (15): rounded rectangles in rows with small text inside. Even after "objects", they are still evenly spaced tiles of equal weight.
4. **Text too small to read in the room.** Postcard populations, stamp text, the Top 10 descriptions, people captions, storyboard VO, calendar copy: 14–20 px on a 1080 slide. The deck reads as a document, not a presentation.
5. **Empty placeholders presented as content.** Storyboard (7) is 8 grey [PHOTO] boxes; calendar (19) has four tiny stamps floating on a line; "[dates]" brackets everywhere.
6. **Gradient-satin wallpaper.** Same purple satin + gold rope on cover, truth, Spanish, close. Strong once; repeated, it becomes a template background.
7. **Gold-glow rendering.** 3D gold lockup, glowing sparkle trails, gold rules everywhere: the "AI luxury" finish when used on every slide.
8. **Colored-dot legends and pills.** Map (16) uses colored bullet dots for regions; people (15) uses empty gold circles as icons; troupes (11) uses empty circles. Classic AI-UI filler.
9. **Duplicate and collisions.** Cover shows OC FAIR twice (flat logo above + on the zeppelin). Slide 3's right half is an empty panel with one tiny stamp.
10. **AI-image sameness.** Character sheets as tiny thumbnails (15), the same zeppelin reused on 1, 11, 14; the aerial and map are generic "fantasy kingdom" renders with no OC specificity visible at this size.

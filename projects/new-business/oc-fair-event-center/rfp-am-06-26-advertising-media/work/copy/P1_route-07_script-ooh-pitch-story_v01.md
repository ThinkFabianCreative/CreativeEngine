## 3. Pitch story (one page for Section 4 and the presentation)

> ### In 2005, the Fair called you out to play.
>
> The theme was "It's Callin' You Out to Play." Twenty-five years later, that invitation is vintage. The kids who answered it are now bringing their own kids, and their parents too.
>
> ### Orange County has always been a little extra.
>
> This is a county that turned orange groves into theme parks, backyards into skate ramps and garages into bands the world sang along to. It has a reputation for being polished. Its real culture has always been loud.
>
> But all year, OC behaves. The Fair is the one place that tells it not to.
>
> ### So for 2030, we answer the invitation.
>
> **Come Out and Play** is an invitation to misbehave: to eat the thing you're not supposed to, ride the ride you might regret, chase the prize you'll never win, get your hands in the dirt and meet the animals, and do all of it with three generations at once.
>
> **Extra is Normal** is the permission. It's the rule of the Fair: everything that would be too much anywhere else is exactly right here.
>
> The line borrows from OC's own soundtrack. The Offspring's "Come Out and Play" is a local anthem, and the bands that made this county loud are the ones today's parents grew up with. It's local, it's a little irreverent, and only the OC Fair can say it.
>
> ### How it works
>
> - **One device.** A loud photo and one calm stamp: **NORMAL.** It works in English and Spanish, from a freeway board to a hand stamp at the gate.
> - **An engine that runs itself.** The Extra Awards crown the Fair's most over-the-top creations every year, from food to 4-H to concert outfits. Winners become the advertising.
> - **A reason to come back.** Stamp-card trails of the most extra things at the Fair.
> - **Every property.** Pacific Amphitheatre: come out and play all season. Imaginology: extra curious is normal. Centennial Farm: extra muddy is normal.
> - **In Spanish, natively.** *¡Sal a jugar! Aquí lo exagerado es normal.*
>
> 140 years after the first Fair, Orange County is still doing too much.
>
> **Come out and play.**

## 1. Spanish platform line

Andres asked: *"La Feria de OC es otro mundo"* or *"un mundo aparte"*?

| Option | Read |
|---|---|
| **La Feria de OC. Un país aparte.** | Keeps the nation. "Aparte" carries both "separate" and "special," and it agrees with the pasaporte, the bandera and *Bienvenidos a OC Fairlandia*. Fits a 14×48 |
| La Feria de OC. Un mundo aparte. | More idiomatic and warmer, but it's a stock phrase in Spanish ads (resorts, spas, malls). The country turns into a "world," and the passport and flag lose their logic |
| La Feria de OC es otro mundo. | The most natural speech ("¡es otro mundo!" = it's amazing). Reads as praise, not as a place |

**Recommendation:** lead with **Un país aparte**, and use *"Es otro mundo"* as a headline inside the system, where its spoken warmth works without breaking the frame:
- *¿Ya fuiste a OC Fairlandia? Es otro mundo. Bueno, otro país.*

The linguist and a Latino creative make the final call. If they prefer "mundo," the system still holds, with "país" kept on the passport, the flag and the welcome.

## 4. Year-round: the nation never really closes

The tension: a 23-day country against District venues that run all year. Put it on the table before a reviewer finds it.

> **The country is open 23 days. Its embassy is open all year.**
> Between Fairs, every District property acts as OC Fairlandia's embassy in Orange County. Centennial Farm is the countryside, the Amphitheatre is the national concert hall, Heroes Hall is the national archive, and Imaginology is the academy. They don't run the nation. They keep its passport office open:
> - Winter: competition entries open. *"The Court is accepting applications."*
> - Spring: Passport presale. *"Plan your trip before the borders open."*
> - Concert season: *"The national concert hall is open."*
> - All year: postcards from the countryside, Farm events stamped as "embassy visits."

This keeps the scarcity (the country itself only opens for 23 days) and gives the venues a role. Confirm the venue names and their 2030 programming with the District.

## 10. AI-tell guard (from the audit of Routes 01–05)

So the 08 build doesn't repeat what made the originals read as generated:
- No single fill-in formula. Lines rotate across the two hinges in different shapes: statement, fact sheet, travel tip, advisory, postcard.
- No three-adjective tone lines in the deck. Show the tone in a line instead of describing it.
- No "written natively, not translated" refrain. One note on the Spanish slide is enough.
- No self-scoring slide in the client deck. The rubric map goes in the Section 4 narrative as plain headings (a, b, c), with the evidence, not marks.
- One disclaimer per image section, not per slide.

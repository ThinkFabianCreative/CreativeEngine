## Style lock (paste first)

> I'm art-directing an internal concept board for a fictional storybook nation: the OC Fair imagined as "a country of its own," a warm, welcoming 150-acre country that exists 23 days a year. Hold this style for every image: whimsical storybook world-building with the showmanship of Willy Wonka, the curving shapes and leaning, nothing-straight architecture of Dr. Seuss, and the court, ceremony and card-soldier wit of Alice in Wonderland. Think Wes Anderson symmetry and palette discipline, hand-crafted costume and set design, sunny Southern California light. Palette: OC Fair orange (#F46B17), warm cream, leaf green, with accents of card red and sky blue. Tone: welcoming and touristy, never official or intimidating; nothing that looks like customs, checkpoints, police or military. No purple-and-gold candy branding, no top hats, no recognizable characters from any film or book, no logos, no text unless I ask. Diverse, multigenerational Orange County people. 16:9 unless I say otherwise.

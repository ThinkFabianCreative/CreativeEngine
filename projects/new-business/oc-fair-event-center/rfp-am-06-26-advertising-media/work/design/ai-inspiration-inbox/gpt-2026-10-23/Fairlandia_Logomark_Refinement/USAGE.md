# A Country of Its Own — responsive logomark

Source for the full lockup: `Acountry_logomark-01.svg` supplied by the art director. The full lockup keeps that outline unchanged. The compact and stamp are flat, one-color companion marks for smaller placements.

| Rendered width | Use | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 200 px and wider | Full lockup, including the founding line | `Fairlandia-full-positive.svg` / `Fairlandia-full-negative.svg` |
| 100–199 px | Compact wordmark, “A Country of Its Own” only | `Fairlandia-compact-positive.svg` / `Fairlandia-compact-negative.svg` |
| Below 100 px | OC passport stamp; especially under 80 px | `Fairlandia-stamp-positive.svg` / `Fairlandia-stamp-negative.svg` |

The compact wordmark removes the ornamental tails and founding line. “of Its” is enlarged relative to the source lockup; its stout, tracked lettering keeps the C, O, y, ry, and wn forms open at small sizes. It uses a single flat fill and transparent background. Positive is black; negative is white and is intended for a dark field such as royal purple `#4B1A6E`.

The founding line never appears inside a mark under 200 px. When needed in a smaller composition, set `THE OC FAIR · FOUNDED MDCCCXC` as a separate text line in Playfair Display SC. Editable text templates are supplied as `Fairlandia-tagline-separate-positive.svg` and `Fairlandia-tagline-separate-negative.svg`. They require Playfair Display SC installed for an exact rendering; otherwise the SVG falls back to Bodoni small caps. Keep the tagline outside the logo's clear space; do not scale the source line down with the mark.

Proof renders include the full mark at 200 px, compact at 100 px, stamp at 80 px and 32 px, a 150 px half-inch/300 dpi stamp simulation, and a 128 px social avatar simulation. Physical print and stamp reproduction still require production proofing.

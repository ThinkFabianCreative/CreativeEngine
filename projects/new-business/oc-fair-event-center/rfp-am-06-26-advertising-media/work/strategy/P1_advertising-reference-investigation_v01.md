# OC Fair 2030: three campaign investigations

Research date: October 21, 2026. Project-level research for P1. Working reference analysis, not a concept selection or an originality clearance. No automation has been installed.

## The finding

The useful common principle is to make the destination's promise observable: a conversation with a resident, an attraction that contradicts a skeptical narrator, or a tourism experience embedded in entertainment. Each supplies something more specific than a theme attached to attractive photographs.

For OC Fair, investigate what guests and participants actually make, what a familiar visitor could unexpectedly discover, and how that discovery leads to choosing a visit. These are research-derived development questions, not established audience insights.

## 1. The Swedish Number — local people become the experience

**Identity:** Swedish Tourist Association; INGO Stockholm; launched 2016. Interactive telephone service, website, PR and supporting creative. The One Show credits Grey PR&Activation New York and Cohn&Wolfe Stockholm alongside INGO.

**Recognition verified:** 2017 One Show, Direct Marketing, Digital / Websites, Mobile, & Banners, Gold Pencil; annual ID OS_DI17019G. [Official record](https://www.oneclub.org/awards/theoneshow/-award/45242/the-swedish-number/). D&AD's consolidated 2017 record also displays Black and other Pencils, but its extracted page does not map each level to a category; this report does not manufacture that mapping. [D&AD record](https://www.dandad.org/work/d-ad-awards-archive/the-swedish-number).

**Material inspected:** original campaign site's explanatory text and FAQ; a supporting execution image linked by The One Show, visually inspected in the browser; the extracted campaign account on PDF p.13 / printed p.205 of the Gunn Report. The latter includes a board, but a reliable readable visual rendering was not obtained. Its small board text is therefore not certified as visually checked. No full case-film review or test call was performed; the original site says the service is closed.

### What the material supports

**Source-stated:** the anniversary of Sweden's abolition of censorship supplied the occasion for international callers to be connected to volunteer residents. The FAQ explains the routing and privacy arrangements. It makes the campaign's openness tangible as an interaction. [Original site](https://www.theswedishnumber.com/).

**Observed:** the inspected execution combines a wintry river landscape with a short invitation to talk to a random resident. Its image establishes place; its instruction introduces a person and an action. This is not a board. [Inspected execution](https://d2z00kf51ll94q.cloudfront.net/archive/2017/large/OS_DI17019G_11.jpg).

**Interpretation:** the tension is between a destination speaking about its people and allowing those people to speak for themselves. The mechanism is a demonstration: an institutional claim about openness becomes something a caller can experience. Residents supply changing content; the organization supplies the connection. Its identity depends on the relationship between the anniversary, the country's reputation and the service, not on a scenic treatment alone.

### Results and evidentiary limit

The Gunn Report text states 188,625 calls, 32,069 ambassadors and 186 calling countries. These are campaign-reported participation totals, not an independently audited tourism lift. Other source versions contain different totals; their measurement cutoffs are not reconciled here. The original site's extracted counters display zero and must not be read as historical totals. [Gunn Report, PDF p.13](https://www.phdmedia.com/london/wp-content/uploads/sites/25/2017/02/TGR2016_Report-For-Media_v2.pdf#page=13).

### Transfer to OC Fair

**Reusable principle:** let the people who constitute a place provide evidence of its character.

**Proposal:** for Made Here, investigate one real maker's process across time: preparing an entry, the judging moment and the encounter with visitors. The organizing question is what was made and who made it. A verified story would give the words a physical subject and could become film, short-form and onsite interpretation.

**Conditions:** access to participants, permission and production time; native language storytelling; a demonstrable connection to the Fair. A general resident testimonial is weaker than a specific process the Fair enables. Do not reproduce the national phone-number conceit, random-call mechanic or original invitation.

**Assessment:** strong reference for a participatory platform; insufficient evidence here to promise attendance growth.

## 2. Is it even a city? — the narrator doubts what the viewer wants

**Identity:** VisitOSLO; NewsLab; campaign launched June 24, 2024. Film and connected destination content. Director August Jorfald is named in VisitOSLO's submission.

**Recognition verified:** Cannes Lions 2025, Film, Silver, category displayed as Travel, Leisure, Retail, Restaurants & Fast-Food Chains. Verified on the official results listing, rather than an agency award tally. [Official listing](https://www.lovethework.com/en/awards/winners-shortlists/cannes-lions/film?tag=publication+dates%40%40year%40%402025). [Entry link](https://www.lovethework.com/en/work/entries/is-it-even-a-city-738902).

**Material inspected:** selected sequences from the original 1:45 film on VisitOSLO's YouTube channel; its full available auto-generated transcript; the first two complete pages of the original three-page ICCA submission, visually rendered and read, with all three pages available as extracted text; and the destination's connected attraction page. The submission is an original written case, not a designed case board. Auto-captions contain name errors, so uncertain words were not treated as accurate quotations. This was selected-frame inspection with transcript review, not continuous audiovisual observation.

### What the material supports

**Source-stated:** VisitOSLO wanted awareness for leisure and business visitation. The submission describes deliberate understatement of the city's strengths and a fictional resident who is too cool for tourism. Its narrative and the film share that central device. [Original submission, pp.1–2](https://bma.iccaworld.org/gallery/finalists/pdfs/VisitOslo%20Submission.pdf).

**Observed:** around 0:31, Halfdan questions swimming in the city while the waterfront supplies the attractive context. Around 0:52, the walkability complaint accompanies a small-scale street scene. The transcript's roughly 1:08–1:25 passage turns short queues and easy restaurant access into supposed shortcomings. These are benefits misjudged by the narrator. [Original film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8vhD59ac7nw).

**Interpretation:** the joke rests on a mismatch between two standards: the narrator expects a city to be difficult and exclusive; visitors may want access and ease. This is more precise than generic reverse psychology. The audience completes the favorable judgment, while the destination provides evidence. The character's skepticism earns attention without requiring an invented attraction.

The connected official page identifies attractions from the campaign and provides practical information. That supplies a useful bridge from irony to planning. [Campaign destination page](https://www.visitoslo.com/articles/campaigns/isitevenacity).

### Results and evidentiary limit

The submission reports 5.7m owned-channel views and 7.4m flight searches in the five weeks after launch, a 17% increase against the preceding period, attributed to ForwardKeys. These are source-stated figures. The underlying flight-search dataset and a seasonal or controlled counterfactual were not inspected. Searches are not bookings; a before/after change alone does not establish causation. The submission supports a reported attention and interest outcome, not a certified incremental visitation figure.

### Transfer to OC Fair

**Reusable principle:** make a viewpoint reveal the attraction instead of explaining the attraction in a list.

**Proposal:** for That's Fair, investigate the specific moment an adult's expected behavior changes at the Fair. The existing deck's parent-screaming-louder-than-child idea is a hypothesis that could supply observable contradiction. The Judge should have a point of view that generates a scene, rather than simply stamping approval over a photograph. Unexpected can use the same discipline: show the actual surprise.

**Conditions:** the visual must prove the joke quickly, and the benefit must survive in Spanish without the English pun. Avoid adopting Halfdan's persona, anti-city premise, monologue or complaint sequence. A multilingual cold read should determine whether the joke communicates welcome and desire.

**Assessment:** the strongest of the three for studying economical scene construction and visual/verbal tension.

## 3. Dundee: A Tourism Campaign in Disguise — surprise with a next step

**Identity:** Tourism Australia; Droga5 New York, with UM and Kovert Creative; campaign 2018. Teasers, entertainment-style promotion, big-game reveal and tourism follow-through.

**Recognition verified:** Effie US 2019, Gold in Travel and Tourism and Branded Content; Finalist in Carpe Diem–Services. The finalist status is preserved separately from wins. [Effie/Ipsos report, printed p.39](https://www.effie.org/system/downloadable_files/2/original/2019-EffieUS-Report.pdf). The official winners PDF corroborates the Travel and Tourism Gold. [Official results, printed p.8](https://s3.amazonaws.com/effie_assets/downloads/2019_EffieUS_Winners.pdf#page=8).

**Material inspected:** Droga5's original campaign presentation in a rendered browser, selected frames of its embedded Big Game Trailer, the official Effie summary as text, and credited creative director Kevin Weir's portfolio text. No standalone readable case board was obtained. The full Effie entry requires subscription access. Direct Vimeo playback was restricted, but the film loaded in the agency's authorized embed. Full-film audiovisual review was not completed; scene-by-scene claims about the final reveal are therefore source-stated.

### What the material supports

**Source-stated:** promotion imitated a Hollywood franchise reboot before the reveal recast it as tourism advertising. Droga5 describes the website, accounts and promotional artifacts changing to direct people to travel planning. **Observed:** inspected frames use an adventure-film world, with recognizable performers, costumes and landscape; they support the entertainment packaging, but cannot alone establish the complete audience journey. [Agency presentation](https://droga5.com/work/tourism-australia-webby/).

**Interpretation:** the useful mechanism is a change in how the viewer interprets what they have already seen. Destination scenery first functions as entertainment and then becomes something to visit. The practical destination pages give that reinterpretation somewhere to go. The people and cultural property make the entertainment specific to Australia; a generic fake trailer would lose that justification.

### Results and evidentiary limit

Effie recognition verifies judged effectiveness, while the accessible short summary does not expose the full research design or numerical results. The creative director's portfolio refers to a projected economic contribution; a projection must not become realized revenue. This investigation does not assert a numeric attributable sales lift. [Credited creator's account](https://www.kevinjweir.com/tourismaustralia).

### Transfer to OC Fair

**Reusable principle:** design what the surprise changes, and what the viewer can do immediately afterward.

**Proposal:** for Unexpected, test one true pair of experiences from the deck's came-for/found structure, using two genuinely different moments. The inspected p.36 social comps use the same selfie across formats: that adapts a placement, but does not itself show the discovery. A reveal could lead directly to a confirmed program detail and a visit-planning action.

**Conditions:** verified programming, clear dates and availability, and an affordable execution that works without celebrities or a national event. The reference offers no evidence about OC Fair midweek demand. Avoid copying the fake-sequel setup, franchise references, casting pattern or campaign chronology.

**Assessment:** strong for joining a creative reveal to a journey; its production scale is not the recommendation.

## Comparison and implications

| Reference | What carries the idea | Closest OC Fair use | Question to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swedish Number | A real participant interaction | Made Here; participant-led onsite storytelling | Can we identify a person and process that make the theme tangible? |
| VisitOSLO | Narrator's judgment against observable benefits | That's Fair; surprise scenes | Does the audience understand the benefit without an explanation? |
| Dundee | Entertainment reinterpreted as destination desire | Unexpected; reveal-to-planning journey | Does the reveal show a new reason to visit and lead somewhere useful? |

These references do not select the winning route. They expose different development needs in the supplied work: Made Here needs specific human proof; That's Fair needs a repeatable dramatic viewpoint; Unexpected needs two distinct observable beats. No claim is made that these mechanisms will reproduce another campaign's results.

For an eventual OC Fair concept test, evidence would come from viewers: unaided understanding, correct brand attribution, recalled attraction, the same meaning in English and Spanish, and the ability to choose a confirmed visit option. Those tests have not been run. Attendance or revenue effects would require an appropriate live measurement design and operational context.

## Verification and access record

- All external links above were discovered and checked on October 21, 2026; some material was accessible only as extracted text or via an embedded browser player.
- Official award recognition, creative inspection and effectiveness evidence are independent fields. An award does not automatically mark the creative as viewed or all results as audited.
- D&AD's Swedish Number video required login. Cannes' public listing supplied Oslo's exact recognition; its detailed entry did not supply the full creative in text extraction. Effie's complete Dundee case was subscription-gated.
- Swedish Number's supporting execution was visually inspected. The Gunn Report case text was read, but its board did not receive reliable high-resolution visual QA. Consequently this is not a completed three-board visual study.
- Oslo's original submission was visually inspected and provides the strongest accessible written case in this set. It should retain the label 'submission', rather than being presented as a board.
- Dundee's selected embedded film frames and agency page were inspected; the full audiovisual sequence and a readable board remain access/inspection gaps. Conclusions are narrowed accordingly.
- No exhaustive pagination, global originality search, or every-award cross-check was performed. Search scope was mechanism-led destination work with recognizable primary-source trails, spanning campaign years 2016, 2018 and 2024.

## What this establishes for automation

The reusable research unit is a campaign with a linked evidence trail: original execution, board or case document, exact official recognition, results source, inspection extent and an explicit analytical inference. Source availability must determine the evidence label, rather than the automation treating every discovered campaign as fully validated.

This pilot establishes that structure and three useful readings. It also exposes a practical limit: discovery can be automated more easily than reliable board and film inspection. A future workflow should flag incomplete primary material for review and retain useful partial findings without claiming complete verification.

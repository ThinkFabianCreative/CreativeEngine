## 1. What is being bought

A full-service or integrated agency for strategy, creative, media planning and buying, digital performance, research and analytics across all District properties. **[RFP p.13]**

- Term: Jan 1, 2027 – Dec 31, 2028, plus three optional 1-year renewals (through 2031). No guaranteed volume. **[RFP p.4, p.14]**
- Media budget: about **$1.2M/year** (evaluation assumption, not a commitment). Radio production about **$30K/yr**, TV production about **$100K/yr**. Both are outside agency compensation. **[RFP p.33]**
- Must be in-market in Costa Mesa, with event-critical coverage during the Fair and major Amphitheatre on-sales. **[RFP p.14]**

### District objectives **[RFP p.14]**
1. Grow ticket sales, earned revenue and attendance, inside the attendance model, dates and dayparts.
2. Acquire new customers; retain and reactivate past ones; widen geographic and audience reach.
3. Strengthen and protect each District brand separately.
4. Reach OC's diverse communities with culturally informed, inclusive, accessible work.
5. Transparent media, first-party data, disciplined testing, measurement tied to business outcomes.
6. Fast, event-capable response to pacing, weather, artist changes, misinformation and safety issues.

## 5. Section 4: the 2030 theme

The District's arc **[RFP p.29]**: 2027 "Forecasting Fun"; 2028 "It's a Blue Ribbon Summer"; 2029 "Screaming Good Fun" (2028–29 focus on community participation, togetherness and discovery, with LA28 Olympic/Paralympic moments).

Ask: **one** 2030 theme and campaign concept that is (a) distinctive, ownable, strategically relevant; (b) a concept that can anchor the annual campaign; (c) shown across multiple channels and formats. Hard copy or electronic. Not obligated to be used. **[RFP p.29]**

## 8. Requirements that shape the creative and the approach

- **AI**: disclose anticipated generative/predictive AI use in the proposal; written approval before publishing materially AI-generated content or synthetic people, voices or testimonials; no District data in public AI tools; STD 1000 GenAI reporting. **[RFP p.13, p.19–20]** This is scored (3 pts) and must be answered honestly about how Traffik works.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 A/AA on digital deliverables, captions, transcripts, alt text, accessible PDFs and presentation files. **[RFP p.19]** Applies to our proposal PDF in spirit.
- **Multicultural** as strategy, not translation, with qualified human language review. **[RFP p.18]**
- **Media transparency**: agent vs. principal, all margins and fees disclosed; accounts held in the District's name. **[RFP p.17, p.20]**
- **Evidence**: separate facts, estimates, platform-reported results, assumptions and judgment. **[RFP p.14]**
- **Conflicts**: disclose clients that are competing fairs, festivals, attractions or venues. **[RFP p.24]**

## The job

Give the OC Fair a 2030 theme it can run for a whole year, and show the committee in one look that Traffik is the agency that can make it. **[RFP]** asks for one theme and campaign concept, scored on fit, distinctiveness and ownability (3), strength of the concept (4) and how it travels across channels (3).

The concept is the job interview. It shows the committee what working with us would be like.

## The real problem

**Every theme sounds the same.** "Fun" has appeared in five themes since 2017, counting the planned 2027 and 2029 lines, and "happy" in two more. Themes have become a summer mood, not an idea. The years people remember were the specific ones: "Free Your Inner Farmer," "Acres of Fun," and the "Year of the Tomato." **[AGENCY, from the ocfair.com archive, unverified]**

**More demand isn't the whole answer.** Daily caps mean Saturdays sell out. The theme has to be able to say "come Wednesday," "come early" and "plan your day" without losing its charm. **[AGENCY]**

**2030 is the first year after the District's three-year theme run.** 2027–29 are about anticipation, competition and thrills, with LA28 in the middle. **[RFP]** 2030 needs a reason that belongs to the Fair, not to the Olympics.

## Who we're talking to

**The person:** the Orange County parent who organizes the family's summer. They have taken their kids to the Fair the way their parents took them. They know where the corn dog stand is. They think they have seen it all, and they have to pick a day when there's room. Very often they plan in Spanish, or in both languages.

Around them: grandparents, 18–34s who need a reason to share it, concertgoers, ag and 4-H competitors (who enter in winter), Asian American households, and first-timers who need to know what to do and when. **[RFP names these groups; the person is AGENCY]**

**The real judge:** the District's evaluation committee. They know every past theme by heart. They are looking for an idea they could actually run, and an agency that understands their Fair better than the other bidders.

## What we know (the truths to build on) [AGENCY]

1. **Traditions are made here.** Families have Fair rules: the same stand, the same photo spot, a first ride repeated with the next generation.
2. **Everyone comes with a reason and leaves with a story.** The campaign lives in the gap between the two.
3. **A third of the county is Hispanic, and close to half speak another language at home.** A line that only works as an English pun leaves too much on the table.
4. **2030 is 140 years since the first Fair** (Santa Ana, 1890). It's proof, not the message. Say "140 years" or "since 1890"; never number the Fair, because some years were canceled.

## The one thing to say

**The OC Fair is where Orange County's summers become family stories.**

Support: 140 years of it; the same county, every culture, in one place; the things you can only do here (ag, competitions, the midway, the Amphitheatre).

## Tone

Warm, proud, quick, a little wry. Plain-spoken and welcoming. Never nostalgic for its own sake: history is the proof, and today's guests are in every piece.

## What the idea must do (the test for every route)

1. **Be ownable:** only the OC Fair could say it. Owned through a device or system, not just a common phrase.
2. **Not be another "fun" or "happy" line.**
3. **Be a campaign engine:** a format that keeps producing headlines (fill-in lines, two-beat reveals, rulings) across food, rides, animals, music and competitions.
4. **Work natively in Spanish:** written in Spanish, not translated.
5. **Sell dates:** flex into midweek, early entry and plan-your-day.
6. **Stretch across properties:** Pacific Amphitheatre, Centennial Farm, The Ranch, Heroes Hall and Imaginology, without breaking their separate brands. **[RFP]**
7. **Be something guests do at the Fair**, not only see in ads.
8. **Read in six seconds** on a freeway bulletin.

## What the committee must see **[RFP Section 4 + SOW §3.2]**

Theme name with a Spanish line · visual direction · key messages · audience relevance · extensibility · use across the guest experience. Then show it working in: TV/CTV · radio (English and Spanish) · OOH · paid social and short-form · web, search and display · email and partner materials · creators · onsite. The current deck already covers this per route; the job now is to make one route outstanding, not to cover five.

## Mandatories and guardrails

- The OC Fair orange-and-leaf mark on every piece. The theme device never replaces it.
- Evolve the Fair's own look; don't copy the 2026 art, and don't abandon it.
- Real OC Fair photography. No AI-generated faces or synthetic people in anything we show. **[RFP requires approval for those, AGENCY rule for the pitch]**
- No invented attractions, dates, prices or offers; mark placeholders. **[RFP]**
- Spanish lines reviewed by a qualified linguist before submission.
- Accessible files: captions, transcripts, alt text, readable contrast. **[RFP]**
- Run a trademark search on the chosen line before Oct 26.

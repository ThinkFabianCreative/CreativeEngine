**Win pitches.** The site is a credibility tool prospects check before or after a pitch. **[SOURCE]**

**Measurable goal: at least 2% conversion within 6 months of launch** (2025: 1.10%, 423 thank-you visits from 38,447 users; Jan–Sep 2026: 1.03%; B2B benchmark 2–5%). Target 2%+ on project scoping, measured per form. **[SOURCE: discovery deck]**

## 3a. Brand level (WORKING / ASSUMED)

Source: `work/strategy/2026-11-18_round3b_strategic-assets.md` and Andres's decision in `work/strategy/strategy-loop-log.md` (2026-11-18): "Lets assume validation will be there, keep moving." Adopted for EXPLORE only. Not promoted to client knowledge. Per `shared/frameworks/brand-strategy.md` §7.

**Brand Promise. [WORKING]** Traffik exists to get brave work approved in categories built to say no.

**Core Creative Idea. [WORKING]** Stories where the rule everyone works around becomes the idea. The work is an expression of the promise and is inspired by the CCI; neither is a line to use.

Three rules travel with the CCI: **[WORKING]**
1. **Remove-the-rule test.** In each episode one named rule (a regulation, an approval step, a category habit) is the material of the idea. Remove the rule and the idea disappears; if it would not, it is a good case but not an episode. Never present the CCI as a general claim that constraints breed creativity.
2. **Candor.** Cases are told straight: the brave version, what review changed, what shipped, what it delivered, weak numbers included. Only where the client allows it (A8).
3. **Episode anchor guardrail.** Each episode anchors in that case's real rule and real material (the actual disclosure, form, plan-choice question, operator audio). No traffic-signal images or words as the house way of showing rules or approval: no lights, stops, barriers, tape, warning signs; no "green light", "red light", "go", "stop", "yield", "cleared". Describe what the reviewer actually did. No red-pen redlines or APPROVED / REJECTED stamps as a house motif; they appear only as a case's actual markup. Black, red and yellow carry the work's evidence, not a signal. "Stop traffic" stays a separate territory (§10), never the CCI's default.

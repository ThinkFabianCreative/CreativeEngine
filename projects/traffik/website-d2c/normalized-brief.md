## 5. Content realities

These shape the work more than any page design. All **[RESEARCH]** unless tagged.

- **15 of 18 current clients appear nowhere** on the three sites. Only 3 of the 39 published cases are for current clients. Only 4 case pages were updated in 2024 or later.
- **Most recent work lives outside CreativeEngine.** Eight current clients have no CreativeEngine workspace (most of the health and MedTech work). The 15 projects that are in CreativeEngine are work in progress with no delivery, approval or results records.
- **Permissions are pending.** A team request for 2024 to 2026 work and per-client publication permissions is drafted, not sent. **[SOURCE: draft file]**
- **The case template matters more than the launch list.** The newest strong cases are 3 to 9 months from having results, so the site has to launch with the best existing cases and take in new ones as they land.
- **Regulated clients may allow only a still and a quote.** Life sciences work (Edwards, Starling) will arrive as film and animation and needs regulatory review. The case format has to work with a still, a quote and a line, without full results.
- The current case template is one frame for every case, and its Process section, where the thinking should show, is usually one generic sentence.
- The inventory recommends 6 hero, 16 supporting and 17 off the work section. These are recommendations for Andres and leadership, not decisions.

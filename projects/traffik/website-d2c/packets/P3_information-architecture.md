## Guardrails (from brief §3a; apply to every route)
- No traffic-signal images: lights, stops, barriers, tape, warning signs as the house image for rules or approval.
- No traffic-signal words as the house way of saying approval: green light, red light, go, stop, yield, cleared. Describe what the reviewer actually did.
- No red-pen redlines or APPROVED / REJECTED stamps as a house motif; only as a case's actual markup.
- Black, red and yellow carry the work's evidence, not a signal.
- No creative answer is given here. Stereotypes to avoid: smiling-senior and diverse-student stock, partner-logo walls, award tallies as the lead, "AI-enabled" badges.

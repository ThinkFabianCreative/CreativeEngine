## Problems found on the current pages

These matter whichever cases survive:

1. **Copy pasted between cases.** Uber Freight contains IEHP's website-discovery paragraph. Harvest and IEHP share near-identical process language. A prospect reading two cases will notice.
2. **Duplicate cases drifting apart.** BCBSVT, FIDM, Apria, SDSU, eternalHealth and CMU each exist on two domains, and some versions differ (BCBSVT's CPL figure only appears on the Health version).
3. **Results that render as "0".** FIDM, Redlands and SCIT use animated counters that show 0 until JavaScript runs, which is what search engines and screen readers may see.
4. **Contradictory numbers.** HCSC is "9 Million Members" in the headline and "more than 16 million members" in the body.
5. **Future-tense outcomes.** Memphis and Harvest describe what the work "will" do.
6. **One template, one voice.** Every case uses the same Overview / Agency Focus / Audience / Goal / Process / Outcomes frame, and Process is usually one generic sentence. The thinking, which is where leadership shows, is the thinnest part of nearly every page.

## A pattern for CreativeStrategist to test

This is an observation from the inventory, not a positioning. Most of the strongest cases share one move: **making a complicated, institutional decision clear and human for people the category usually overlooks.**

- Care1st, WellCare and VHP reached diverse, often in-language Medicare audiences
- PIH gave seniors the questions to ask
- HCSC made documents people weren't reading understandable
- CMU attacked elitism and argued for access and affordability
- SDSU spoke to adults who are still reaching

If it holds up with the team, this could be the thread that lets one consolidated site speak for Education, Health and Brands at once.

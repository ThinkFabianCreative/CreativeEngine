## Method

For each current client with a CreativeEngine workspace, I listed its projects and read only each project README's title and status line. I also checked whether `outputs/delivered/`, `outputs/approved/` or `results/` exist.

## Finding

CreativeEngine holds **work in progress, not finished cases.** Across 15 projects there are no delivery records, no approval records and no `results/` folders. That doesn't mean nothing shipped, because deliveries may happen outside CreativeEngine. It does mean none of this is a case study yet. It's the pipeline the next site has to be built to receive.

## What this means for the site

1. The newest strong cases are 3–9 months from having results. The site has to launch with the best of the existing 22 and take in new cases as they land, so **the case template and CMS matter more than the launch list.**
2. **Life sciences will arrive as film and animation** (Starling, Edwards). That work is regulated and approval-heavy. The site needs a case format that works when a client allows only a still, a quote and a line, not full results.
3. **Featherstone Phase 2** is the easiest win. A second chapter turns the lead case from one campaign into a relationship.

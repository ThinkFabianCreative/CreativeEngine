### Pattern (R1)
- Three tiers appear in every sector: networks (pharma/large carriers), large integrated independents (MERGE crosses health plans + MedTech), and sector-only specialists (Carnegie, SimpsonScarborough, Media Logic, ParkerWhite).
- Only MERGE and Maricich were observed spanning more than one of Traffik's three buyer sectors. No agency observed spanning health plans + higher ed + MedTech.
- Southern California specialists in the same buyer pools: Maricich (Medi-Cal plans), Modera (Orange), ParkerWhite (San Diego), Bloom Creative (Costa Mesa).

## R3. Voice of customer (buyer verbatims)

### Higher ed
- Jeff Harmon, CMO, Southern Illinois University: "Show us the data. I've had some agencies that are a little bit hesitant." Also: "We've got to see the good, the bad, and the ugly." https://christianbanach.com/jeff-harmon-cmo-at-southern-illinois-university-prefers-agencies-that-embrace-their-failures/
- Oakland University board document: needs "more than effective advertising campaign management or the generation of impressions and clicks." (institutional voice, Oct 2026; link above)
- College of DuPage evaluator on VisionPoint: "They treated this process as if we did not know them." (link above)
- Suzan Brinker (agency founder, ex-higher-ed marketer; not a current buyer): "Agencies still focus too much on lead volume and not lead quality." https://www.halda.ai/post/building-the-best-relationship-with-a-higher-ed-marketing-agency
- Black Hawk College RFP asks agencies to "Identify risks, limitations, and opportunities candidly" and "Focus on outcomes rather than activity alone." (RFP link below)

### Health plans / healthcare
- Defy Collective survey of 47 healthcare marketers (agency-run, paraphrased by Defy, not verbatim): top pains ranked = lack of partnership ("order-taker"), weak strategic thinking, "bait and switch" pitch teams, insufficient knowledge incl. MLR process, flawed execution. https://thedefycollective.com/defy-surveyed-47-healthcare-marketers-to-identify-pain-points-in-agency-relationships
- Smith & Jones 2013 healthcare survey (dated): 78% saw room for improvement; options included "They could lead more, instead of just taking orders". https://smithandjones.com/resources/newsletters/survey-results-marketing-agency-performance-and-reviews
- Alex Gallegos, L.A. Care (on hiring): agency "shares our commitment to helping Angelenos obtain quality healthcare coverage." (Ntooitive link above)
- Agency-curated client quotes (biased): MVP Health Care director, Media Logic "focused on our business goals". https://www.medialogic.com/

### Life sciences / pharma / MedTech
- GSK marketer (DeLash) in MM+M: "I would love to get an email from you guys that starts off by saying, 'I've been thinking about this and I have an idea,' and then explain that never happens." Also: "If they don't do their homework, even in the first meeting, then it's hard to trust..." https://www.mmm-online.com/features/ai-encroaching-on-established-pharma-agency-partnership/
- ISR healthcare agency benchmark (2015), buyer verbatims on selection: "Know the therapeutic area / experience - have done their homework and know the area"; "Creative - great ideas and concepts that set them apart". https://isrreports.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/ISR-Health-Ad-Agency-Quality-Benchmark-Preview-Oct2015.pdf
- MedTech marketing director (agency-curated, Jairus): "There was a pretty immediate trust because they understand the nuances of marketing for a med device company." https://jairusmarketing.com/medtech-marketing

### Cross-sector surveys (not sector-specific)
- Dalton 2026: 49% cite high costs + unclear ROI as biggest frustration; 27% lack of strategic insight; >1/3 not confident in agencies' AI use. https://daltonagency.com/insights/dalton-survey-what-marketing-leaders-really-want-from-their-agencies
- Setup 2023: value and delivery = top two reasons clients leave; chemistry ranked first in selection. https://setup.us/2023-marketing-relationship-survey

### Pattern (R3)
Recurring across sectors: proof of outcomes (enrollments, not leads/clicks), transparency with data, proactive ideas, sector/regulatory fluency (MLR, CMS), the same senior team after the pitch.

## R6. Review triggers (partial)

- Contract term expiry: public institutions rebid on fixed terms (3 yr + 2x1 yr Ivy Tech; 3+2 College of DuPage; 1+2 Oakland). Covered California rebids ~5-yearly (2014, 2019, 2024).
- New strategic need: Oakland cites new brand, student journey, Slate modernization.
- Budget/value: Setup 2023 budget cuts as reason to leave rose 24%→33%.
- Pharma: reviews rarer; new CMO or procurement cost pressure named as triggers in consumer, longevity valued in pharma (MM+M).
- Higher ed: enrollment cliff and declining applications cited by list publishers (weak source).
- MedTech: stage change (clearance, launch, new market) cited by list publishers (weak source).

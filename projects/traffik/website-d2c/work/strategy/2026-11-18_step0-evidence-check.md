## Research request for creative-researcher

Scope: public sources only. Sourced observations and patterns; no insights or verdicts. Client = Traffik; open no other client workspace as evidence.

**R1. Competitor set per audience (the set prospects actually compare).** For each of the three buyer audiences, identify 5-8 agencies they realistically shortlist: specialist (e.g. health-plan/Medicare agencies, higher-ed enrollment marketing agencies, life-sciences/MedTech agencies incl. network-owned), plus 2-3 generalist independents of Traffik's size in Southern California/West. Method: RFP award notices and public procurement (universities, health plans post these), agency "best of" lists, conference sponsor lists, category search results. Mark each as evidence-based shortlist vs. likely. Andres's list (A1) will overwrite this when supplied.

**R2. Competitor positioning capture.** For each R1 agency: homepage claim, tagline, stated promise, proof style (results, awards, logos), case format, how they handle regulated work. Pattern: what every one says (the category cliché to avoid).

**R3. Voice of customer, per buyer audience.** How health plan marketers, enrollment leaders and MedTech commercial leads talk about agencies: frustrations, switching reasons, what impresses them, RFP evaluation criteria. Sources: Reddit/LinkedIn posts, trade press interviews (e.g. Medicare marketing, higher-ed marketing, MedTech/pharma marketing outlets), published RFP scoring rubrics, conference talk summaries. Quote verbatim with links; label paraphrase as paraphrase.

**R4. Talent and partners VoC.** What creative talent and freelance/production partners look for in a mid-size agency and its site; public sentiment patterns. Plus anything public about Traffik itself (reviews, press, awards).

**R5. Ladder benchmark.** For R1 sites and their showcased work, a rough Creative Ladder rung per sector, with links, so the Bar can be set against the real category.

**R6. Category triggers.** Calendar and market forces that open agency reviews per sector (AEP/OEP, enrollment cliff, device launches/approvals). Factual, sourced.

Priority: R1, R3, R2 first (they gate the swap test and the human truth); R5, R6, R4 next.

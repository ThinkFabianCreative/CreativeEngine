## 4. Core Creative Idea

### CCI-1. Stories where the rule everyone works around becomes the idea.

The specific rule in a case (a regulation, an approval step, a category habit) is the material the idea is made from. PIH's plan confusion became the questions; Netwatch's live operators became the campaign [F: INV].

**Guard: this is not "constraints breed creativity".** *(D)* That truism says limits in general make work better. CCI-1 says something narrower and checkable: in each story, one named rule is the content of the idea. Episode test: **remove the rule and the idea disappears.** If the idea would survive without that rule, it is a good case but not a CCI-1 episode. Never write or show CCI-1 as a general claim about constraints.

**Case-telling rule (CCI-3 folded in).** *(D, WD)* Every episode is told with candor: the brave version, what review changed, what shipped and what it delivered, weak numbers included. This is how cases are told, not a page format, and it applies only where the client allows it [A8].

**Episode anchor guardrail.** *(WD show-test risk)* Every episode anchors in that case's real rule and its real material (the actual disclosure, form, plan-choice question, operator audio). No traffic-signal metaphors (lights, stops, barriers, tape, warning signs) as the house image for "rules" or "approval"; black, red and yellow carry the work's evidence, not a signal. "Stop traffic" stays a separate territory and is never CCI-1's default.

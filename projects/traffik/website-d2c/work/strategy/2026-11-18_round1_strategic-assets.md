## 1. Universal Human Truth (shared by the four audiences)

**Draft.** People who market inside high-stakes, regulated institutions are measured on results but protected by caution. The work that would move their numbers is the work their own approval chain is built to stop, so most of them settle for work that is safe and invisible. [S, built on R3 asks for ideas + R2 sameness; the "protected by caution" half is A4]

**Tension:** to be noticed vs. to be approved. [S]

For talent and partners the same tension runs the other way: good creatives avoid regulated categories because they expect the work to die in review. [A5]

Maslow level: esteem (being the marketer who did something worth pointing at) on top of safety (keeping the job). [S]

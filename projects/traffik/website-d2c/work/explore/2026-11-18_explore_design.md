## P1 case template: three treatments of the deck's fields

Same fields in all three (client, 3 tags, challenge, what we did, how/media, results, quote, related, next step). Each is shown with PIH Health (hero), CMU Featherstone (supporting, one result) and a placeholder regulated health plan (still + quote).

- **A · Ledger.** Field labels visible, dense, Swiss record on dark. Candor sits in "what we did". Unused result slots collapse to a thin rule.
- **B · Headline-led.** The two-line challenge is set huge and carries the rule where the case passes remove-the-rule; one big result figure. A new case can show "results due [month]" and move up later.
- **C · Poster + index card.** The work fills the screen; every deck field sits on a pinned card a validator can copy. The regulated card states why results aren't published.

**How an empty result reads (all three):** never "N/A" or a blank. Say why (pending until a date, or not published at client request) and turn the slot into a next step ("ask us about this case"). Unused positions collapse.

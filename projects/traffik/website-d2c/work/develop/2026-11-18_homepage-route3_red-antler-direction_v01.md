## Reference
Red Antler hero ([supahero.io/hero/red-antler](https://supahero.io/hero/red-antler)): one giant condensed all-caps statement across the first screen, one image behind it, a quiet nav, nothing else.

What we take from it:
- One line carries the first screen.
- The type is the layout.
- The image sits behind the type, not beside it.
- Everything else waits for the first scroll.

What we keep from our own brand:
- Black ground. Red stays the one red on the page.
- Yellow appears only on figures.
- Caslon does not appear on this page (its one job was the rule panel, which is cut). Display type is Acumin Pro in condensed caps.

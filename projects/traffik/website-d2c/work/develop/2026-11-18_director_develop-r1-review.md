## Which route

- **For 2% conversion:** Route 1 as rendered (CTA on first screen at both widths, clearer job statement at the rule). After R2 fixes 1 and 2, roughly even; neither has a conversion advantage in the idea itself.
- **For winning pitches:** Route 2. The hero line is retellable in the room, it is the higher-ceiling idea (a route to 8 GAME CHANGER if the work behind it is real), and its PIH section already contains Route 1's best device.
- **Recommendation: combine in round 2, on Route 2's skeleton.** Keep R2's who line, Caslon hero line and habit-framed cases; keep R2's PIH rule > idea > result section, upgraded with Route 1's rule-panel → work motion (the one place the CCI is performed); take Route 1's CTA discipline, its "How a project runs" step 3 ("We sit with your legal and compliance team…") into R2's how-we-work for health buyers, and its closing CTA ("Got a rule you think kills the idea?") as an A/B against R2's.

## 9. Lesson candidate (critique-learning-loop)

**Recurrence:** the template middle appeared in both v1 routes (two independent builds) and survived the r2 combine, because r1 prescriptions targeted the hero and cases and nothing targeted the lower third. Two builds plus one carry-over is repeat evidence for this project.

**Candidate lesson (DesignChamp, homepage/long-page work):** "Every section below the hero gets its own job and its own layout decision. Before render, list each section's layout pattern; if two consecutive sections share an equal-column pattern, one must change or justify itself." *Counterexample to regression-test:* a deliberately quiet, uniform section used as a pause after a loud one (that is a decision, not a default); the rule must allow a stated, justified repeat.

**Second, smaller lesson for the director (me):** I checked an invented sixth move in r1 because the source was not in my reading. Rule: when a checklist is cited from a source, read the source or mark the check `not verifiable`; never fill it by inference.

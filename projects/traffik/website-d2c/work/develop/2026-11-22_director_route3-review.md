# Director review: Homepage Route 3 "One line" (JUDGE)

CLIENT: Traffik · PROJECT: website-d2c · SOURCE SCOPE: PROJECT-LEVEL · creative-engine-director, 2026-11-22
Object: Figma eLfndwoXdZ0nURgxN8VIhn, frame 695:431 (1440 x 3380), read-only screenshot. Inputs: route3 direction v01, director r1 and r2 reviews, better-interface r1, pack files 10 and 11.

## What the render is
Giant white condensed caps "IDEAS THAT SURVIVE REVIEW." with a red square full stop over a darkened Featherstone still; "See the work" link; nav with filled "Start a project". Yellow proof row (77% / 9.4% / 45,000+, all unv). "The work": 3x2 equal tiles, client + campaign only (PIH, WellCare, Care1st grey). Grey band "What we make: HEALTH. EDUCATION. UNITES." in display caps. CTA "Got a rule you think kills the idea?" + Start a project. One-line footer.

The form is right. Andres was right that r1 talked too much; this reads in eight seconds, the type is the layout, one red, yellow only on proof. Keep the skeleton.

## 1. Does it keep the strategy?
No. The idea is "the rule everyone works around becomes the idea; approval is the second half, not the job" (pack 11). On this page:
- The hero states only the second half. With nothing above or beside it, "survive review" is the whole offer.
- The rule-to-idea story is gone. The only trace is the closing CTA, 2,800px down. A visitor who never scrolls meets a review-survival promise and a university photo.
- Case tiles are names only, so the work cannot argue for the idea either.
The page cut words correctly but cut the argument with them. One line of argument has to come back; that is not the same as bringing back r1's paragraphs.

## 2. Is "IDEAS THAT SURVIVE REVIEW." the hero line?
Not as the sole hero line. The r1 risk is now worse, not better: at this size and alone, it reads as the compliance shop's promise ("we get things through legal"), which is the commodity health buyers already have. It is defensive, it names the obstacle rather than the outcome, and the red full stop lands the emphasis on REVIEW. It is a good sign-off (it was written as one) and works as the payoff once the rule-to-idea move has been seen.
The hero needs a 3 to 5 word condensed-caps line that carries the brave half and the rule, not the approval. WordSmither owns it. Criteria: fits 2 to 3 lines at this size; names the rule or the category's "no" as raw material; no traffic/approval vocabulary; not generic "constraints breed creativity" (pack 10 guardrail). Route 2's "Half of what your category 'can't say' is just what it hasn't said" is the strongest meaning on file but too long for this form; compress its thought, do not paste it.

## 3. Is Featherstone right behind it?
Not yet, for three reasons:
- At 35-40% visibility under the type it reads as "students in front of a Gothic building", a university brochure. It does not show a rule broken, and nothing on screen tells you it is work.
- It reads as education-only on a site whose biggest buyers are health plans.
- It appears twice (hero and tile 2), and clearance and the 9.4% source are still open (r1 decision 1).
The Red Antler move works when the image is unmistakably the work. Either the hero image becomes legible as work (a caption chip "Colorado Mesa University · Featherstone" and a brighter plate), or it rotates/changes to a case whose image carries the rule. Keep Featherstone as the working default only if cleared.

## 4. What must come back / what is still too much
Must come back (three lines, not sections):
- A who-we-are strapline on the first screen (r2 fix, lost again). The footer is the only place that says "advertising for health, education and life sciences."
- One rule > idea line, on the PIH tile: "Rule: you can't tell seniors which plan to pick. Idea: tell them what to ask." (locked line).
- Industry entry (r2 move 4): the three divisions should be links into filtered work, which also justifies their section.
Still too much / wrong weight:
- HEALTH. EDUCATION. UNITES. at display size competes with the hero (two giant-type moments, the second carrying less meaning). "UNITES" means nothing to a buyer without a gloss. Smaller, and each with a 3-word descriptor and a link.
- Featherstone repeated.
- Three grey tiles out of six: the grid reads unfinished; not judgeable as work.
Accepted cuts: how-we-work to About, careers to footer. Content relevancy (r2 move 6) is consciously dropped by the cut; flag it once for Andres, do not re-prescribe.

## 5. Ladder and craft notes
Rung 4-5: competent, clean, borrowed form (the reference is recognisable). It climbs only when the line is Traffik's own. Craft: red full stop at display size passes contrast (large text). Acumin Pro Black is wider than the intended Extra Condensed, so line breaks will change; re-check with the real face before any further judgment of the hero. "See the work" is tiny bottom-left; fine as secondary.

## Verdict: REWORK
Preserve: the one-line form, the proof row, the reduced word count, the closing CTA (now the page's best line). Fix: the page lost the idea and the hero now says the commodity half of it.

## Fixes (prioritized, max 5)
1. **Critical · word-smither.** New 3-5 word hero line carrying rule-to-idea / brave; "Ideas that survive review." moves to the footer or CTA band as sign-off. *Acceptance:* 3+ candidates, each fits 2-3 lines in condensed caps at 1440; a reader of the hero alone could not mistake Traffik for a compliance consultancy.
2. **Major · design-champ + word-smither.** Who-we-are strapline under the hero (one line, ~10 words, body-large). *Acceptance:* visible in the 1440x900 and 390x844 first screens.
3. **Major · design-champ.** PIH tile goes wide (2 columns) and carries the locked rule/idea line in two levels; breaks the equal grid. *Acceptance:* the work section shows one tile larger than the others with readable rule and idea lines.
4. **Major · design-champ.** Hero image legible as work: caption chip with client + campaign, lighter overlay; remove Featherstone from the grid (or swap hero) so no image repeats. Conditional on Featherstone clearance. *Acceptance:* no duplicate image; first screen names the case.
5. **Minor · design-champ.** Divisions section: drop from display size to H2 scale, add a short descriptor per division (Unites explained), each links to filtered work. *Acceptance:* hero is the only display-size type on the page; three links to /health, /education, /unites.

Open for Andres (unchanged): Featherstone clearance and 9.4% source; all figures unv; real condensed face; content relevancy slot dropped by this route (flagged once).

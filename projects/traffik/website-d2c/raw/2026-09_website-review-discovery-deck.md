## Where we are
- **Four sites, not three:** wearetraffik.com, traffikhealth.com, traffikedu.com, **traffikunites.com**.
- **Goal today vs wanted (s6–7):** today "an online brochure", "a passive digital HQ", direct traffic from referrals and RFPs. Wanted: "a passive lead and business generator", "a scalable 24/7 acquisition channel", high-intent organic search. Local SEO: 1,220 searches/month in Orange County for "marketing agency orange county", "advertising agency orange county", "digital marketing agency orange county". "The goal is the lead."
- **Conversion 2025 (s8):** 38,447 users, 423 thank-you visits = 1.10% (B2B benchmark 2–5%). Parent 1.24% (28,305 users), Health 1.03% (5,718), Edu 0.46% (2,585), Unites 0.05% (1,839). **Goal: min. 2% within 6 months of new site launch.**
- **Conversion Jan–Sep 2026 (s9):** all four 1.03%. Parent 1.30%, Health 0.63% (conversions 59 → 37 despite more traffic), Edu 0.50%, Unites 0.09%.
- **Lighthouse desktop, 30 Sep 2026 (s10):** parent performance 69, LCP 4.3 s (target 90 / 2.5 s); average 78.5; Edu 93. Page-weight fix, not rebuild.
- **Bots, Jan–Sep 2026 (s11):** 19% of sessions overall; Health 43%, parent 13%, Edu 11%, Unites 6%. Fix is config (Cloudflare, Turnstile, GA4 baseline).
- **Competitive research, 24 agency sites (s12):** six things the best do: one line says who they are; work leads the homepage; every service has its own page; work navigable by industry; proof right under the hero; content relevancy.
- **Content (s13):** newest post four years old (blogs 2020, Feb 2021, Jun 2022). Same 13 capability paragraphs on every domain; case studies duplicated across 2–3 domains; generic partner list on all four.
- **Sitemap gaps (s14):** no service pages (13 capabilities, 0 pages); 42 work tiles, no filters; no careers page ("Be hired" opens the sales form).
- **Five questions (s15):** Who are you? What do you do? How do you do it? Can we trust you? What do we do next? Current site answers only trust ("real numbers, awards, clients since 2001"). Homepage H1 is a case-study headline. One form for hiring, jobs and partners. Peers that answer all five: monks.com, wearecollins.com, ideo.com, weareenvoy.com, dfy.co.kr.

## Recommendation
- **One site (s20):** 38,447 users split four ways; only 10,142 reach the vertical sites; Health's biggest source is the parent footer link. Parent ranks #1 in Irvine; Health and Edu rank for nothing. Divisions keep their own pages; domains redirect.
- **One CMS (s22):** case studies, services, articles, team; tagged by service, division (/health, /education, /unites) and industry; tag once, pages pick it up. CMS not decided.
- **Sitemap, eight sections (s23):** Homepage; Work (/work/… case study); Services (/services/… detail); Divisions (/health etc.); Blog; Contact (/start project scoping); Careers (/career-start talent form); About.
- **Page IA (s24):** every page answers the five questions; every page ends with a next step.
- **Forms (s25):** split into Project scope (division, service, scope, budget, timeline, then scoping call; buyers only), General contact (partners, press), Career form; plus Book (name, email, company, calendar link). Target 2%+ on project scoping, measured per form.
- **Case study template (s26):** client; three tags (division · service · industry); challenge (two lines); what we did (three lines); how we did it / media; results (three numbers); quote (one line); related work (three tiles by tag); next step (start a project, service preset). Fields in CMS, not designed pages. Goal: one case study a month (39 today, +12 a year).
- **Blog (s27):** one article a week via a random draw at the TRAFFIK Jam; tagged by service.
- **Links (s28):** press, awards, co-branded client case studies, trade campaign stories, events, free tools, podcasts/webinars, partner showcases.
- **Next steps (s29):** decide (one site or four, the blog, design language to keep); measure (key events, bot filtering); wireframe (five-question test per page); model content (tags, migrate 39 case studies). Fix in parallel: consent banner, page weight, security headers.
- **Q&A (s30):** Health brand kept as a division page; redirects carry authority; CMS not decided; no date given.

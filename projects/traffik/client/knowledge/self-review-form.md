# Traffik self-review form (draft)

Companion to the annual review form (`annual-review-form.md`). Drafted by CreativeAdmin for Andres, 2026-10-16. STATUS: DRAFT, not yet adopted by Traffik.

Filled in by the employee **before** they see the manager's review, then compared side by side in the review meeting. Gaps between the two are where the conversation is.

---

**Name** ______ **Role** ______ **Review period** ______ to ______

## 1. Your year

1. The two or three pieces of work you're proudest of this year, and why.
2. Something you learned or got better at.
3. Something that didn't go the way you wanted. What would you do differently?

## 2. Rate yourself (1–5, same scale as your manager)

1 Below expectations · 3 Meets · 5 Exceeds. One line of evidence per score; skip any you can't judge.

## 3. What gets in your way

4. What slows you down or makes your work harder: workload, briefs, tools, process, feedback, people?
5. Is your workload sustainable right now? If not, what would you change?
6. What support do you need from your manager, and from Andres?

## 4. Looking ahead

7. Where do you want to be in a year? Role, skills, kind of work.
8. Two or three goals you'd like to set for next year.
9. Any feedback for your manager?

---

### Notes for running it

- Send it a week before the review. Make clear that it's their voice in the record, not a test.
- The manager doesn't change their scores just to match. Discuss gaps of 2 or more points openly.
- Answers to questions 4–6 and 9 go to CreativeAdmin as STATED entries in the person's profile, and feed advocacy and workload decisions.

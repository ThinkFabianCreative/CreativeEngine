| Element | Observed |
|---|---|
| Logo | White "T" in a circle plus TRAFFIK wordmark in caps (`/wp-content/themes/traffik/img/logo.png`). Shown white on dark imagery. Black version not observed |
| Serif (headlines h1–h3) | Adobe Caslon Pro, 400, sentence case, large (48 px h2/h3) |
| Sans (body) | Acumin Pro, weight 300 body text |
| Wide sans (labels, nav, tags) | Acumin Pro Wide 700, uppercase |
| Also loaded | Maiola (not seen in use on the home page) |
| Base colors | Black #000000 text on White #FFFFFF; White on Black hero; 50% black secondary text |
| Accent red | #D91F1F: section intro labels ("ABOUT US", "CAPABILITIES"), "Learn More" links, PODCAST tags |
| Accent yellow | #F4D803: footer contact block background |

Typefaces are Adobe Fonts. In Figma they need local installation through Adobe Creative Cloud; Google Fonts stand-ins are a production choice, not Traffik identity.

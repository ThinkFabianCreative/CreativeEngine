# Messaging orchestration diagrams: reference scan

Date: 2026-10-07. For: slide 6 ("One story… In 2027 we orchestrate…"). Sources: Google/web, Pinterest (logged-in search), vendor docs. These are links and our own analysis only, not copies of the diagrams.

## The recurring diagram archetypes

| # | Archetype | What it shows | Seen in | Fit for slide 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hub and spoke** | A unified profile or engine in the centre, channels on a ring | Zigment, Indigitall, Adobe AJO marketing | Fits "one orchestration engine on Adobe". Overused, and reads as vendor-ware |
| 2 | **Loop or cycle** | Data, then decision, then execution, then feedback, back to data | Zigment, Grid Dynamics NBA blueprint, IQVIA NBA | Fits "what we learn feeds the next conversation". Abstract |
| 3 | **Before/after silos** | Separate channel columns with their own sends vs. channels feeding one layer | Braze, Worldline, Forrester, IQVIA "converging silos" | Fits Yash's script beat exactly: "each therapy on its own calendar… uncoordinated" vs. orchestrated |
| 4 | **Swimlane timeline** | Channels as horizontal lanes, time running left to right, touches as marks | ABM "surround sound" playbooks (Abmatic, Cargo), pharma rep and digital sequencing (ZS "Orchestrator Rep") | Fits "a sequence for each physician between visits". Most legible for leadership |
| 5 | **Journey stage track** | Awareness to advocacy along a line, touchpoints hanging off it | infoDiagram, Miro, Whimsical, most Pinterest pins | Generic template look. Avoid |
| 6 | **Subway or transit map** | Each journey is a coloured line; stations are touchpoints; interchanges are shared moments | Pinterest ("subway map customer journey"), Starbucks service exploration | Elegant. Could show four physician archetypes as four lines through shared field-visit stations |

## Pharma/HCP-specific patterns (closest to Edwards)
- **NBA engine flow:** 360° HCP profile, then the next-best-action engine, then who/what/when/channel, then rep or digital execution, then a feedback loop. Sources: [Grid Dynamics](https://www.griddynamics.com/blog/operationalizing-next-best-action-in-commercial-pharma-a-blueprint), [IQVIA NBA](https://iqvia.com/solutions/technologies/performance-management-and-insights/next-best), [OptimizeRx](https://optimizerx.com/blog/hcp-marketing-next-best-action-engagement)
- **Rep as orchestrator:** digital touches between visits; the rep walks in knowing what the HCP engaged with. This mirrors Yash's line "the rep walks in knowing what the physician read yesterday". Sources: [ZS / Veeva white paper](https://www.veeva.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/ZS-WP-The-Orchestrator-Rep-in-Pharma.pdf), [PharmaLive](https://www.pharmalive.com/?p=33074), [Pharma Marketing Network](https://www.pharma-mkting.com/?p=19729)
- **Silo problem in pharma:** the rep details product A while headquarters emails the same HCP about product B. Source: [IQVIA, converging silos](https://www.iqvia.com/-/media/iqvia/pdfs/library/articles/pharma-cx-the-solution-lies-in-converging-silos.pdf)

## ABM "surround sound" (the ABX in Yash's notes)
- Each channel plays a different role, not the same pitch repeated. Cadence is 2–3 touches a week, and touches are suppressed during live sales conversations. Sources: [Abmatic, multichannel orchestration](https://blog.abmatic.ai/abm-multi-channel-orchestration-2026), [Cargo, orchestration best practices](https://www.getcargo.ai/blog/abm-campaign-orchestration-best-practices)

## Adobe (Edwards' platform)
- Journey Optimizer "journeys" are the 1:1 canvas: email, push, SMS, in-app, web, code-based. Paid media activation is in the B2B edition. Sources: [AJO journeys](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/journey-optimizer/using/orchestrate-journeys/journey-get-started), [AJO B2B omnichannel](https://business.adobe.com/products/journey-optimizer-b2b-edition/omnichannel-orchestration.html), [Omnichannel orchestration PDF](https://business.adobe.com/content/dam/dx/us/en/products/journey-optimizer/omnichannel-orchestration/Adobe_Journey_Optimizer_Omnichannel_Orchestration.pdf)

## Our read (CreativeEngine, DesignChamp lens)
- The category is saturated with hub-and-spoke wheels and blue icon grids. That is the cliché to avoid.
- Slide 6 already uses archetypes 3 and 4 (Today lanes vs. the 2027 sequence), which matches Yash's script order best.
- **Unclaimed territory: the musical score.** "Orchestration" taken literally:
  - Each channel is an instrument staff, and field visits are the bar lines.
  - Today's version is three separate scores playing over each other; 2027 is one conducted score.
  - It is ownable, it reads instantly, and it extends naturally to motion.
  - Nothing like it turned up in the scan. This is not a "done before" check.
- A second option is a **subway map** with the four physician archetypes as four lines through shared field-visit stations. It visualises Yash's line "the entry point changes; the themes do not".

FOR THE CREATIVE TEAM  /  OCTOBER 2026

Edwards capabilities presentation

Design brief and slide outline

Build a conversational, work-led presentation that introduces TRAFFIK today, reconnects Edwards with our shared history, and opens a useful discussion about what we could do together next.

Audience  Edwards brand, digital and acquisition leaders. Some may know our past work; others may be meeting TRAFFIK for the first time.

The takeaway  They should leave knowing who TRAFFIK is, what we do and why it matters to Edwards.

The story to tell

Start with a little about us. Establish the history through real Edwards work, then let the work and reel create energy. Give a clear view of what we bring today, prove it through two or three short cases, and finish with how we work and where we could help next.

Recommended structure  10 slides across seven sections. The third case is conditional on approved acquisition proof. The slide copy below is a starting point for the creative team; design instructions and open inputs stay out of the client deck.

Design principles

• Use a white background, generous space and a clear hierarchy. Use EB Garamond for headlines and Roboto for body copy, with genuine TRAFFIK logo assets. This brief uses the intact TRAFFIK health lockup from the supplied deck.
• Give the work most of the space. Lead each slide with one idea, one headline and a few short lines. Put detailed context in the conversation.
• Keep headlines, copy, diagrams and labels editable. Use original artwork and high-resolution exports. Headshots are optional; the deck can work without them.
• Use the 2021 deck as a historical asset source. Refresh brand assets as needed and confirm current facts, project roles, permissions and results before client review.

## Introduce TRAFFIK — Section 1 • A little about TRAFFIK

SLIDE 01 / A LITTLE ABOUT TRAFFIK — TRAFFIK and Edwards
Purpose: Open warmly and make the purpose of the conversation clear.
Suggested client-facing copy: TRAFFIK and Edwards / Who we are today, a look at our work, and where we can help next
Design direction: Create a quiet, confident cover. Use the TRAFFIK mark, a restrained Edwards identifier and generous white space. If a work image adds value, choose one strong approved crop. Keep the date or meeting label small.
Assets and checks: Approved TRAFFIK and Edwards marks. Use a verified work asset only. Avoid a stock healthcare montage or an oversized credentials statement.

SLIDE 02 / A LITTLE ABOUT TRAFFIK — A little about TRAFFIK
Purpose: Give the room a simple picture of the agency and the people available to support the work.
Suggested client-facing copy: A little about TRAFFIK / We bring strategy, creative and digital teams together to help brands tell a clear story and build useful experiences. / 40 full-time team members • 20 seasonal team members
Design direction: Keep the introduction human and compact. Let the team-size line support the story. A small strip of real work can introduce range. Avoid an org chart, a long service list or a row of executive headshots.
Assets and checks: Team size was confirmed by Brent in October 2026. Confirm the final agency description and current team-size wording before release. Do not carry over staff names, titles, tenure, partner badges or client logos from 2021.

## Show digital and acquisition capability — Section 5 continued • Short cases

## Make the next conversation easy — Sections 6 and 7 • How we work and where we can help next

SLIDE 09 / HOW WE WORK — How we work with your team
Purpose: Make collaboration feel clear and manageable for Edwards.
Suggested client-facing copy: How we work with your team / Agree on the audience and goal / Build the right team / Create and review together / Launch, learn and improve
Design direction: Use a simple four-step flow with short supporting labels. Show the points where Edwards gives input, reviews work and makes decisions. Keep the emphasis on how the work moves. People can be introduced in the room; headshots are not required.
Assets and checks: This is a proposed description of the working approach. Brent to confirm it reflects current practice. Do not imply a fixed staffing model, named team, timeline, service commitment or review process that has not been agreed.

SLIDE 10 / WHERE WE CAN HELP NEXT — Where could we help next
Purpose: Connect the presentation to Edwards' priorities and invite a concrete next step.
Suggested client-facing copy: Where could we be most useful next? / What needs to be clearer? / Where does the experience get harder? / What action are you trying to increase?
Design direction: End on a clean discussion slide with the three questions easy to read. Leave space for conversation. Use an understated TRAFFIK mark and, if useful, one small work image. The questions should feel open and practical.
Assets and checks: Tailor the questions to any confirmed Edwards meeting context. Frame brand, digital and acquisition opportunities as discussion topics. Avoid presenting assumptions about Edwards' needs as known problems. Agree on the next step in the meeting.

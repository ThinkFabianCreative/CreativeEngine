## The job

Not "what has TRAFFIK done?" but: help complex therapies move from **evidence → understanding → trust → adoption**, and show TRAFFIK is the partner for the next chapter.

The audience should leave thinking: *they know us; they know structural heart; they've already solved adjacent problems; they can do more than we've been asking; they have ideas about where this business is going; we should give them a bigger shot.*

## Five acts

| Act | Job | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| I. We understand the opportunity | TMTT as an emerging structural-heart growth story | Category education, physician confidence, referral, KOL, patients, portfolio (see client portfolio file) |
| II. We've been here before | Institutional knowledge, not nostalgia | One move, not a history lesson |
| III. Here is the proof | Five capability categories, each answering *why this matters to TMTT* | Hero work, very few examples. Three strong beat twelve thumbnails. Each case: `THIS PROVES:` |
| IV. And that was then | **The turn.** "AND THAT'S WHAT WE'VE ALREADY DONE." | Hero moment; pivot from history to possibility |
| V. What's next | Future scenarios Edwards doesn't yet associate with TRAFFIK | Visually stronger than Act III |

Scenario candidates: SHOOT THE BRAND FILM · BUILD THE NEXT PRODUCT BRAND · REIMAGINE THE CONGRESS EXPERIENCE · CREATE THE KOL CONTENT ENGINE · BUILD AN ALWAYS-ON SOCIAL PRESENCE · CONNECT THE REFERRAL JOURNEY · CREATE THE B2B CAMPAIGN · TURN THE PORTFOLIO INTO ONE CONNECTED STORY. Pick the 3–5 that best fit what we learn about TMTT's needs.

## Thesis options (choose from the brief, not by default)

- We understand the science, the audiences, the environment and the organization. Now let us help build what comes next.
- FROM BREAKTHROUGH THERAPY TO BREAKTHROUGH ADOPTION.
- BUILDING THE NEXT CHAPTER OF STRUCTURAL HEART.
- INNOVATION DOESN'T CHANGE LIVES UNTIL PEOPLE ADOPT IT.

WordSmither should test these against the copycraft and specificity checks before one is chosen. Some lean on category language a competitor could also say.

## Shape

About 15–18 primary slides, plus an appendix for deeper evidence. A trailer, not a book.

Possible run: Opening → TMTT opportunity → communications challenge → TRAFFIK + Edwards → proof montage → Events → Brand → Digital → Film → Clinical/sales → the turn → what's next → expanded capabilities → TMTT ecosystem → scenarios → partnership proposition → close. A starting architecture, not a fixed count.

## Pitch questions to answer before the deck is final

1. What business problem are we helping solve?
2. Why does TMTT need an agency partner now?
3. Why TRAFFIK?
4. Which historical work proves our relevance?
5. What can TRAFFIK do that Edwards may not associate with us?
6. What should they want us to do next?
7. Does the deck make the agency feel larger than the archive?
8. Does the final section create desire, not a service list?

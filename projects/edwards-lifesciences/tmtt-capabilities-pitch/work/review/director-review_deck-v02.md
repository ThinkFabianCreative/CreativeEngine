## Ranked problems

### 1. The axis now claims outcomes. (Claims risk, highest)
"Here's where we've already closed them", "the gap closes because Act III lit every station", and a TREATMENT station lit by TRAFFIK work say that agency work closed Edwards' adoption gaps, up to treatment. The ledger has no performance, usage or outcome data for any case, and four of six proof items are ARCHIVE EVIDENCE. This breaks "don't imply marketing alone causes clinical adoption".
- **Fix:** stations become the *communication jobs* TRAFFIK has worked on, not gaps it closed. Light a station = "we have worked here", never "solved here". Rename TREATMENT to a communication job (e.g. PATIENT JOURNEY or ACCESS, word-smither to name) or leave it unlit by proof and make it TMTT's open frontier. Act III line drops "closed": e.g. "Here's where we've already worked." ARCHIVE EVIDENCE cases light a station in outline only on internal versions until confirmed.
- **Owner:** word-smither (station names, Act II/III lines); design-champ (lit vs outline states).

### 2. The turn contradicts Act V. (Concept logic)
If the gap fully closes at slide 12, slide 12's "Now let's close TMTT's" has nothing left to close. The room will feel the conceit wobble at the deck's most important moment.
- **Fix:** the turn closes *Edwards' line* (the archive across aortic, pulmonic, surgical) and, in the same move, a second, open line appears for TMTT on the dark field: "And that's what we've already done" then Act V opens TMTT's gap. The device then carries the pivot from history to possibility, which is what Act IV is for.
- **Owner:** design-champ (motion and static states); word-smither (turn and Act V line pair).

### 3. Proof is not mitral/tricuspid, and the deck doesn't say why it transfers.
Every case is aortic, pulmonic or surgical. Tissue Is Trending and INSPIRIS are surgical; in a TMTT room that blurs surgical/transcatheter unless labelled. The concept test ("only works for mitral/tricuspid") passes for the device, not for the proof.
- **Fix:** each `THIS PROVES:` states the transferable job and names the original category plainly ("Surgical aortic. What transfers: ..."). Cut over-reach such as "the exact job PASCAL Precision, EVOQUE and SAPIEN M3 need" to a proposal ("the job a three-therapy portfolio will face"). Merge slide 11 to one case or move to appendix if time is short; the deck loses nothing.
- **Owner:** word-smither (THIS PROVES lines); design-champ (category label in the mono system).

### 4. Act I chapter line reads clinically.
"A therapy is only as strong as the gap it closes" reads as a statement about valve function in a structural-heart room. Slide 3's "TMTT's challenge at each station" is AS SUPPLIED and could read as telling Edwards its business.
- **Fix:** move the gap to communication explicitly ("between the evidence and the people who need it" territory); phrase station challenges as questions or hypotheses, labelled as our read.
- **Owner:** word-smither. brief-machine flags which challenges are user-supplied vs Edwards-stated.

### 5. Scenarios and thesis are placeholders with no brief.
Act V is meant to be visually stronger than Act III and carry desire, but its three picks are guesses, and no thesis is chosen.
- **Fix:** brief-machine produces a one-page normalized brief from the AS SUPPLIED architecture plus whatever is known about attendees by Sunday; choose scenarios from it. If nothing new arrives, default to REFERRAL JOURNEY + KOL CONTENT ENGINE + CONGRESS, labelled as proposed. word-smither tests the four thesis options against the gap device and picks one.
- **Owner:** brief-machine (brief, decision on scenarios); word-smither (thesis).

### 6. Asset dependencies can sink slides 8, 9, 10, 11.
TCT photos may not exist; video and patient-profile showing rights unconfirmed; patient portal screens need anonymizing.
- **Fix:** design-champ prepares a fallback per slide now (8 becomes specimen plate of the case slide; 9 uses whichever film is cleared; 11 to appendix if consent unclear). Rerun the monotony test only if fallbacks trigger.
- **Owner:** design-champ; account lead for rights (outside the department).

Minor: the red signal test and Edwards guideline question (§12.2, §12.5) should be settled in the first rough, not in debate.

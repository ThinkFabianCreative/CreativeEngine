## 1. Concept (Barry §1)

```
TRUTH:          In mitral and tricuspid regurgitation, the leaflets fail to meet and a gap remains. TMTT therapies exist to close it.
                The commercial version: a breakthrough therapy still has a gap to cross between the evidence and its adoption.
CONCEPT:        The deck draws that gap as one line from evidence to adoption, plots TRAFFIK's real work along it, and closes it at the turn.
AUDIENCE SEES:  A hairline with a measured gap, five marked stations, real work placed on the line, then the line closing.
AUDIENCE GETS:  "They understand our problem as a gap to close, and they have already closed parts of it for Edwards."
```

Concept tests: it uses no style words, it does not depend on a technique, and it only works for a mitral/tricuspid therapy story, so another category could not run it. Risks carried: the seam must stay diagrammatic, never a valve or device drawing (mitral has two leaflets, tricuspid three), and must never imply a clinical outcome. Closing the gap is a claim about communication, not about patients.

## 3. Design system

### Constant / variable (Barry §8, visual-craft §7)

```
CONSTANT:  the evidence-to-adoption axis on a fixed horizon · warm paper field (dark after the turn) · two type roles + mono labels · one signal color with one job · full-bleed real imagery
VARIABLE:  where the work sits on the axis · the crop (wide environment vs macro artifact) · the medium (still, screen, film) · the words
```

Brand-blind test target: with the logo covered, three proof slides should read as one family because of the axis and the plotting, not because of a repeated layout.

### Where the seam appears (device trap fix)

| Slide type | What the seam does |
|---|---|
| Opening, Act I gap map | Introduces the axis and names the five stations; the gap is at its widest |
| Act III opener | Shows the five stations TRAFFIK's work will be plotted against |
| Each proof slide | A thin axis along the foot of the slide, with the case's station(s) lit. This acts as a folio that carries meaning |
| The turn | All stations are lit and the gap closes |
| Ecosystem map (Act V) | The axis becomes a route through the referral journey (Direction B lives here) |
| Close | The closed line is the structure the partnership line sits on |
| Film, scenarios | No seam. Film plays clean; scenarios use the dark field without decoration |

If a slide's seam would not tell the audience anything, it is removed.

### Type (typography.md §1)

```
JOB:       mostly invisible-authoritative; voice only at chapter lines, the turn and scenario titles
SAYS:      precise, calm, confident; a partner who has done this a long time
SURVIVES:  projection in a lit room, a PDF read on a laptop, the smallest case caption
EXCLUDES:  the geometric "tech/health" sans with healthcare blue; thin letter-spaced caps as instant premium
LOCKED:    Edwards identity face if guidelines require it for Edwards-voiced elements (UNKNOWN)
```

- Pairing model: **one family, full range** (a grotesque with real weight and width range) plus a mono with a separate job: stations, status labels, `THIS PROVES:` and captions (the engineering register). Two is a system. The final face is not chosen until LOCKED and licensing are answered. Licensing is a question, not an assumption.
- Hierarchy (§4): three levels at most (line, headline, caption/mono). On proof slides the image leads and the type follows. At the turn, type is the only element.
- Tells to avoid (§9): centered default stack, letter-spaced luxury caps, gradient text.

### Grid

12 columns, 16:9, wide outer margins. A fixed horizon carries the axis at about 0.55 of the height on chapter and map slides, and at the foot on proof slides. Asymmetric compositions with no equal-weight card grids.

### Color (visual-craft §4)

| Color | Job | Proportion |
|---|---|---|
| Warm paper off-white (Acts I–III) | Set temperature: precise but human, not clinical | Dominant |
| Near-black ink; becomes the field after the turn | Separate the past from the future; Act V is heavier and more confident | Secondary |
| One signal red (deep arterial or oxblood; test both) | **Mean + Direct:** used only for the gap, lit stations and the closing moment | Accent, under 5% of any slide |

Value check: the focal point must hold in grayscale. The red never fills a shape, never glows, and never touches a heart form (to avoid the "glowing heart" code). Photography is graded warm-neutral to sit inside the palette. Contrast is checked at projection and PDF sizes.

### Imagery (visual-craft §5, editorial-presentation §9)

- **Priority:** confirmed Edwards/TRAFFIK work as it ran → archive documentation (booth, screens, spreads) → macro crops of real artifacts → typography and diagrams. Generated imagery is limited to clearly labelled Act V future frames, with no people, devices or procedures, and stays at or under about 10% of visuals.
- **Direction for archive re-shoots/crops:** natural or directional light, real environments with people present but unposed (only in genuine archive photography), macro crops of print and screens to show craft, and no stock clinicians.
- **Text on image:** solved by crop and placement, never by a scrim.

### Graphic language

The axis (hairlines, measured gap, five mono station labels), leader-line captions, catalogue numbers per case, mono status labels. Internal versions carry full status; client versions show only claims the evidence supports. No icons, ECG lines, gradients, glass or blobs.

### Motion (editorial-presentation §8)

One explanatory motion: stations lighting as each case is plotted, then the gap closing at the turn in a single move, then the axis bending into the referral route on the ecosystem map. Everything else is a hard cut. Film plays full-bleed with sound. The deck must work as a static PDF, where station states carry the same information.

### Monotony test (editorial-presentation §3)

Thumbnail strip by behaviour: Axis · Statement · Axis · Statement · Axis · Bleed · Specimen · Bleed · Film · Specimen · Specimen · Statement · Scenario · Axis · Scenario · Scenario · Statement.

- **10–11 (two specimen plates).** Flagged. Fix: 10 is screens on the dark-ink ground in a tight crop (close), 11 is print artifacts on paper ground at specimen scale (dense). Same behaviour, different crop, ground and density, so they read as dense → dense with a reason, followed by the sparsest slide in the deck (12). Acceptable; if it still reads flat in roughs, merge 11 into a single INSPIRIS profile and move Tissue Is Trending to the appendix.
- **15–16 (two scenarios).** Separated from 13 by the map. 15 is type-led (a wall of real KOL formats as type), 16 is image-led (labelled future congress frame), so the composition changes.
- **8 → 9.** Wide environment → film: wide → moving close, a deliberate pairing.
- Rhythm overall: Act I builds (statement/axis alternate), Act III runs dense → sparse into the turn, Act V is build → break → build. No three neighbours share a composition without reason, and six behaviours keep it a system.

## 9. Anti-tropes

Healthcare blue · ECG/heartbeat lines · glowing or literal hearts · DNA and molecules · HUDs, holograms, screen-surrounded doctors · stock clinicians and hospital hallways · logo walls and thumbnail case grids · equal-weight capability cards · gradients, glass and fake dashboards · history timelines · invented statistics or tenure · generated people, devices, procedures or case imagery · the axis drawn as a valve or device · the red used as fill or glow · a seam on a slide where it says nothing.

## Frame by frame

### Intro: "Bringing the SAPIEN M3 RESILIA story to life" (166:825)

- **Objective:** generic ("compelling animation… engaging visual storytelling"). It names no audience and no change we want.
- **Pillars:** a fair restatement of R3. It already contains the USP ("Showcase… the docking system") but treats it as one item among three.
- **Approach:** commits the deck to "the red glass heart". That conflicts with your preference (Q37) and should be softened until Edwards answers.
- **v03 fit: weak.** No tension, no proposition. Nothing on the frame tells Edwards which single thing the film will make them believe.

### Overview (166:817)

- **"Core of concepts"** is the closest thing to a proposition in the deck: "controlled, reproducible mitral valve replacement… sets performance in motion". It's a list of attributes, though, and it misses the mechanism that makes reproducibility possible (drawing inward). "Reproducible" appears with no reason to believe it.
- **v03 fit: partial.** It has the benefit half of B ("reproducible") without A's cause.

### T01 Nature meets Science (166:641 and scripts)

- **Premise:** "Edwards has engineered what the body does naturally." That's about mimicry, and v03's proposition is about a motion (drawing in). The nature examples (honeycomb, DNA, tree rings, fingerprints) are about pattern, not about holding or gathering. Only the wingbeat-to-leaflets example connects to the product.
- **Tension:** none. Nature is presented as perfect ("Effortlessly. Efficiently. Perfectly."), so there's no problem for the device to solve. v03's tension (no two anatomies the same) is actually a nature truth, since no two leaves or fingerprints are the same, but the deck uses that variety as wonder, not as a problem.
- **Mechanism:** absent. The dock never appears in the script.
- **Ownability:** low. Any device could run "what the body does naturally".
- **v03 fit: the world is right, the idea isn't yet.** The nature lens is the best vehicle for A. That's what `p1-nature-30s_v02.md` already does: nature that *holds by drawing in*, matched into the dock.
- **Fingerprints:** the deck's own fingerprint image is a gift. "No two are the same" is exactly v03's tension, and it should be repurposed, not cut.

### T02 Precisely Engineered (166:536 and script)

- **Premise:** "Innovation is in the details", with robotic arms building the device and placing it into the glass heart.
- **Against A:** it shows how the device is made, not what it does to the anatomy. Nothing is drawn in. "Precision" is the category's default claim, so it fails the ownability check: any valve maker can say it.
- **Against B:** the closest the deck gets. "Reproducible" and "precision" point at the same benefit. But B's cause is standardizing the *anatomy*, not machining the part more exactly.
- **Glass heart:** load-bearing here. The payoff is literally "placed into a glass heart", so if the glass heart goes (Q37), T02 loses its ending.
- **v03 fit: low.** Right structure for the format (product first, titles only), wrong idea.

## Verdicts (DEVELOP)

- **T01 Nature meets Science: REWORK.** Keep the nature world, the warmth and the wingbeat. Replace the premise with A: nature holds by drawing in. Swap the pattern montage for inward motions (fern, anemone, closing flower), and use fingerprints for the tension.
- **T02 Precisely Engineered: REWORK, leaning KILL as a standalone.** The structure is useful and the idea is off-strategy. Fold its product-first discipline into T01 rather than presenting it as a second route.

If Edwards needs two routes, build the second on **proposition B or C**, not on precision:
- **B, "Every mitral is different. The landing zone is the same."** Many anatomies resolving into one form. Fingerprints, leaves and shells of every shape, each fitting the same dock.
- **C, "For the mitral patients anatomy ruled out."** The emotional route: the heart that was turned away, moving forward again.

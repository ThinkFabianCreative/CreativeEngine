## 04 · R3-2 · Footing · BET THE PITCH (conditional)

**Headline:** Different ground every time. The same footing every time.

**Premise:** The heart is always moving forward, but the ground ahead is never the same twice. Before each move, the ground draws in from its own edges and becomes the same level footing, so every landing is the one you planned. That is what the dock does for the valve.

**Glass-heart variant:** Labelled variant: the heart is the glass heart, gliding in profile at small scale; on the final footing the camera pushes into it and the [APPROVED-REF] dock and valve are visible inside, sitting on the same footing.

**Claim notes:** Claims noted, not gated: standardized landing zone, reproducible, RESILIA tissue, fully transseptal. Heartbeat is sound only, never an ECG line. Conditional BET: if the cold read fails on cute, Footing drops to ADVANCE and The Gather leads.

**v02 changes (per review v04):**

- Wide shots (:30 sc5, :60 sc9): one footing formed ahead, levelled trail sinking flush behind. Never a row of discs.
- Footing is a flat level plane, no rim, never ring-like or concentric.
- Lines: :30 sc3 lands after the second footing; :30 sc5 "Reproducible" removed; :60 sc2 "Force doesn't make footing."; :60 sc8 "Reproducible."; :60 sc9 "Different ground. One place to land."
- Title slide keeps "That is what the dock does for the valve."
- Talking point only (Q43): R4's "steady beat" rhymes with our heartbeat sound. Not adopted as a line.

**Premise:** Every heart team remembers the mitral cases it worked up and ruled out because the anatomy fit no option. This film takes a second look at one of them, on the team's own case-review sheet. The reason is a dock that draws the anatomy in, so the heart can move forward.

**Glass-heart variant:** Labelled variant: the reopened sheet holds the glass heart, small and flat-lit; it lifts off the sheet and the camera moves into it, [APPROVED-REF] dock and valve inside.

**Claim notes:** Highest exposure. "The anatomy didn't change. The answer did." implies newly eligible patients (Q5, Q36, Q42): kept for presentation, claim flagged; lower-exposure alt "Same anatomy. A different way in." Verdict lines carry the anatomy-ruled-out register (Q29, Q38).

- "The anatomy didn't change. The answer did." kept, claim flagged (Q5, Q36, Q42); alt "Same anatomy. A different way in."

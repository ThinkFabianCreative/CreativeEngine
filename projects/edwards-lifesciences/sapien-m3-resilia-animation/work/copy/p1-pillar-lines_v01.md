# P1 · Pillar lines v01 (on-screen supers)

```
CLIENT / PROJECT:  Edwards Lifesciences · SAPIEN M3 RESILIA animation
SOURCE:            R3 message pillars (normalized-brief.md §7.2)
AUTHOR:            word-smither (main session), 2026-10-15
MODE:              EXPLORE. Claims not gated; Edwards will call them out
```

**What's wrong with the originals.** They're positioning statements, not lines. They're built from nouns ("elimination", "replacement", "function", "expertise"), they hedge with "designed to empower" and "optimized", and none of them can be read in the 3–4 seconds a super gets. The job: keep each pillar's meaning, cut each to about six words, and give the three a shared rhythm so they read as one family.

Two sets. **Set 1** fits the nature territory (v02). **Set 2** is plain-spoken and works in any territory.

## Set 1 · Nature territory

| Pillar | Original | Line |
|---|---|---|
| OUTCOMES | Delivering consistent, near MR elimination and significant patient benefits | **Leaks closed. Lives opened up.** |
| INNOVATION | Designed to empower reproducible mitral valve replacement and optimized function | **It draws in what other valves push away.** |
| LEGACY | Built on a legacy of structural heart expertise, engineered for mitral | **Grown from SAPIEN. Built for mitral.** |

"Grown from" is the nature-family version of "built on". The INNOVATION line is the territory's idea in pillar form, and it's the strongest of the three.

## Set 2 · Plain-spoken

| Pillar | Line | Alt |
|---|---|---|
| OUTCOMES | **MR, nearly gone. And it stays that way.** | **Results you can see at two years.** |
| INNOVATION | **One way in. A valve that fits every time.** | **Different anatomies. One reliable result.** |
| LEGACY | **Everything SAPIEN learned, made for mitral.** | **A million SAPIEN procedures behind it.** |

- "One way in" stands for fully transseptal delivery. "Every time" stands for reproducible.
- "Different anatomies. One reliable result." is the closest to R3's "standardize heterogeneous mitral anatomy", and the most likely to survive the client's review.
- "A million SAPIEN procedures" is platform data, not this device's. Expect a note.

## Recommendation

Use Set 1 with the nature :30 and :60. Keep Set 2's "Different anatomies. One reliable result." in your back pocket as the fallback INNOVATION line when Edwards pushes back. Expect client notes on "nearly gone", "every time", "stays that way" and "other valves" (that last one is comparative).

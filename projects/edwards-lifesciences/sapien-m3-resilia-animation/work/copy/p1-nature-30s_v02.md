## The idea

**Nature holds what matters by drawing it in. So does SAPIEN M3 RESILIA.**

T01 had an overarching concept but used nature in general. Here nature is narrowed to what the dock does: things in nature that hold by gathering inward instead of pushing outward. Every nature shot is a match-cut into the mechanism, so there's no preamble. The red glass heart moving forward stays as the opening and closing image.

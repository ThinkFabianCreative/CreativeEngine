Premise: A film made of one motion. Irregular material comes inward toward a single red heart and settles, without anything pulling or tying it. Once it has settled, the heart moves forward, and the mechanism shows us why.

## R3-2 · Footing · BET THE PITCH candidate

| | |
|---|---|
| Proposition | **A + B fused** · Drawn in (A) so that every landing is the same (B) |
| Concept sentence | A red heart moves forward across uneven ground, and before every step the ground ahead draws in to give it the same firm footing. |
| Mechanism | Repetition with a turn (the same miracle, three times, then the reveal of why). Visual analogy: footing = landing zone. |
| How the heart moves forward | Structurally: the film *is* the heart travelling. It cannot take a step until the ground draws in; every scene is one step. The forward motion and the mechanism are the same event, which is why it survives the :30 (three steps is a complete film). |
| Why heart teams get it | Implanters already talk about "landing zone" and "anchoring". Footing is their word in plain language: you don't need a perfect patient, you need a reliable place to land. Reproducibility made visible as rhythm. |
| Ownability | A competitor can't sign a film where the ground comes in to meet the heart; their story is the device pressing out to grip the ground. Pass. The rhythmic step is a property Edwards can carry into stills, booth and social (a step = a frame). |
| Material rules | Soft tan ground, side-on low angle, cut-paper / pressed-linen relief. Each patch ahead is irregular in its own way (dip, ridge, gap, slope). It draws in from its edges and flattens into a level round footing, then the heart lands. No stones, no dots, no spiral, no thread. The footing is flat and level, not a ring. |
| Glass-heart variant | The heart is the glass heart, travelling in profile at small scale; on the final step the camera pushes into it and the [APPROVED-REF] dock and valve are visible inside, sitting on the same footing. |

**Title slide**
Headline: **Different ground every time. The same footing every time.**
Premise: The heart is always moving forward, but the ground ahead is never the same twice. Before each step, the ground draws in and becomes the same level footing, so every landing is the one you planned. That is what the dock does for the valve.

## R3-3 · The Second Look (The Door, rebuilt for the heart team)

| | |
|---|---|
| Proposition | **C, HCP-framed** · For the mitral patients anatomy ruled out, told from the heart team's side |
| Concept sentence | A stack of closed beige case files, each one a patient the team had to turn away, and one of them opens again because the anatomy no longer decides. |
| Mechanism | Context shift: the hope story moved from the corridor (cliché) into the heart team's own artefact, the case review. The physician's "no" becomes the subject. |
| How the heart moves forward | Inside the reopened file is the red heart. It slides out of the folder and moves forward off the desk and across the frame; the case literally moves forward. |
| Audience fit | The lines are the team's verdicts in their own register ("Anatomy unsuitable", "No option"), not patient feelings. It speaks to the human truth in §16.2: the patients a team remembers are the ones it worked up and turned away. |
| Ownability | Medium. Any new mitral device could reopen a file, so the :30 must name *why* (dock draws in). The draw-in beat inside the file is what makes it Edwards. |
| Red discipline | One red only: the heart inside the reopened file. Files, tabs and stamps are beige and tan type. No light change, no glow, no door, no corridor. |
| Glass-heart variant | The reopened file contains the glass heart; it lifts off the page and the camera moves into it, [APPROVED-REF] dock and valve inside. |

**Title slide**
Headline: **The case you closed. Open it again.**
Premise: Every heart team has a drawer of mitral cases it worked up and ruled out because the anatomy fit no option. This film reopens one of them. The reason is a dock that draws the anatomy in, so the heart inside can move forward.

## Design-champ view

- **R3-2 Footing** is the BET THE PITCH candidate: one repeated action carries all three mandates at once (heart moves forward, draw-in mechanism, same landing zone), it is complete in three steps so the :30 loses nothing, it gives Edwards a rhythm system beyond the film, and it uses the heart team's own word. It absorbs T03's procession as its wide shot.
- **R3-1 The Gather** is the purest A and the most beautiful; it now depends on the suture/forward image tests passing.
- **R3-3 The Second Look** keeps C's emotion while fixing all five CD problems; it is the slate's provocation.
- First image test for Footing: three-frame sequence (uneven patch, drawn-in footing, heart landed). Must read as "steady step forward", not cute, not stepping stones, not a ring or coil.

## 1. The rocks / red dot: critique

**What fails.**
- **It shows stillness.** A red dot sitting among rocks is placement, not drawing in. Proposition A is a verb (draws in); the frame has no verb. B's logic (variety resolves to one) also doesn't read: the rocks stay varied and the dot just sits there.
- **The red reads as a target or a wound.** On stone, a single red dot reads "you are here", a map pin, or blood on rock. None of those is "heart moving forward".
- **Zen-garden code.** Pebbles on beige is spa, wellness and mindfulness stock. A competitor's logo fits it, as does a meditation app's.
- **Rocks are the wrong material for the truth.** The tension is soft, irregular, living anatomy. Rocks are hard, inert and permanent: they argue against "draws the anatomy in".

**What's salvageable.** The restraint: beige field, one red, lots of air, a single point of attention. That composition discipline is right for this system; carry it forward. Also the instinct that *many irregular shapes* can stand for anatomy without showing an organ. The irregulars just need to *move and yield*, not sit.

## 2. Three new territories

### R2-1 · The Gather (proposition A)

**Concept sentence:** We never show the anatomy; we show loose, irregular material being gathered inward by one quiet circular motion until it holds, and the held form then moves forward.

**Mechanism:** Pricken *analogy* plus *omission*. The dock's action is played by a gesture everyone understands physically (a drawstring closing, sand pulled into a ring by a turning current, threads cinched), never by a device. The viewer completes "this is what the system does to the anatomy".

**Ownability:** Built on one verb, *draws in*, which is the dock's literal mechanism. Competitors push out (radial force) or clip; their story is expansion or grip. Put another logo on an inward-gathering film and it says something untrue about their device. Moderate-high ownability.

**Heart moving forward:** The gathered material, once held, has a pulse. The ring beats once, then begins to travel across the beige frame left to right; the red accent is the thread that does the gathering, and it leads the forward move.

**:30 beats**

| Time | Picture | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Loose tan fibers and grains drift on beige, asymmetric, never settling | **No two mitrals are alike.** |
| 4–10 s | A single red thread traces a circle around them and slowly cinches. The material gathers inward, folding and conforming, not crushed | **So we don't push against the anatomy.** |
| 10–15 s | The ring closes. Everything is held, quiet, centered | **We draw it in.** |
| 15–21 s | Within the held ring, a soft rhythmic open/close (abstract, leaf-like, no device) | **SAPIEN M3 RESILIA.** Refined leaflet geometry. RESILIA tissue. |
| 21–26 s | The held form pulses once, then begins moving forward across the frame, red thread leading | **[Evidence line, e.g. ENCIRCLE]** |
| 26–30 s | End card on beige | **SAPIEN M3 RESILIA** · It draws the anatomy in. |

**Risk:** "Cinching" can read as constriction or strangling if the motion is too tight or fast; it must feel like gathering, not squeezing. Abstraction can drift into textile ad territory; the type has to carry the link.

### R2-2 · Many Hands, One Imprint (proposition B)

**Concept sentence:** Dozens of different soft, irregular forms are each pressed into the same red-edged mould, and every one comes out carrying the identical clean circle.

**Mechanism:** *Repetition with variation* turned into a *craft analogy*: letterpress, a seal into wax, a ring pressed into clay. Variable input, constant impression. Unlike T03, the constant is not drawn on screen by graphics; it is *made* by a physical act each time, so repetition becomes proof by ritual.

**Ownability:** The specific claim is "your anatomy varies; the landing zone it produces doesn't". Precision brands promise exact parts; this promises a reproducible *result in variable conditions*, which is the dock's job. A competitor without a dock can't say "the landing zone is the same". Moderate ownability; the line does more work than the picture, so keep them welded.

**Heart moving forward:** The pressed forms stack, then begin to roll forward like a line of coins or clay discs, the red circle on each turning like a wheel. A procession moving forward.

**:30 beats**

| Time | Picture | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Overhead: a lumpy, tan, irregular form of wet clay/wax | **Every mitral is different.** |
| 4–11 s | Rapid match cuts: rounder, flatter, lopsided, thicker forms. Each is pressed by the same red-rimmed seal (no device geometry; a plain ring) | (no text; rhythm of the press as sound) |
| 11–15 s | Lift. Every form carries the same perfect circle in the same spot | **The landing zone is the same.** |
| 15–21 s | Within one imprint, a soft three-part opening and closing (abstract) | **SAPIEN M3 RESILIA.** Refined leaflet geometry. RESILIA tissue. |
| 21–26 s | The imprinted forms stand on edge and roll forward in a line across beige | **[Evidence line]** |
| 26–30 s | End card | **SAPIEN M3 RESILIA** · Every mitral is different. The landing zone is the same. |

**Risk:** "Pressing" is pushing, which contradicts A if this ever pairs with "draws in". Keep it on B only. The seal can read as manufacturing (T02's precision trap); the material must stay soft, warm, hand-made. Closest to T03's structure, so it's the least new of the three in logic, though new in image.

### R2-3 · The Door That Opened (proposition C)

**Concept sentence:** A long beige corridor of closed red doorways, each one shut to someone, until one opening finally stays open and the frame moves forward through it.

**Mechanism:** *Context shift* plus *omission*. We never show a patient; we show the experience of being ruled out as architecture: thresholds, closed openings, a light that stays off. The only human trace is movement (a shadow-free point of view walking). The viewer supplies the person.

**Ownability:** C's audience is patients previously untreatable because of anatomy (heavily calcified, MAC, unsuitable for other options). That is a specific population a generic valve can't claim. The corridor is generic; the reason doors were closed (anatomy) is in the text. Moderate; depends on Q36 audience confirmation and on claim support for "ruled out" populations.

**Heart moving forward:** Literal. The camera itself moves forward, and the red stays as the colour of the open threshold we pass through. The forward move is the payoff, not a decoration.

**:30 beats**

| Time | Picture | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Static, symmetrical, Barragán-like corridor: beige plaster, a row of recessed doorways, all sealed | **Too calcified.** |
| 5–10 s | Camera drifts; each door we pass is closed. Text changes per door | **Too irregular. · Too complex. · Not a candidate.** |
| 10–15 s | Ahead, one doorway: its edge is red. It is open | **For the mitral patients anatomy ruled out.** |
| 15–21 s | We pass through. Warm light, open space, a red curve of wall drawing us in | **SAPIEN M3 RESILIA.** Fully transseptal TMVR. |
| 21–26 s | The space keeps opening forward, rhythm picks up | **[Evidence line, e.g. MAC registry]** |
| 26–30 s | End card | **SAPIEN M3 RESILIA** · A way forward for the anatomy that ruled them out. |

**Risk:** Emotional route aimed at a HCP-first audience may read as consumer. "Ruled out" implies eligibility claims that MLR will scrutinize. Architecture-as-metaphor can feel like a real-estate or luxury-hotel film if the type isn't decisive. It is the furthest from the mechanism, so it needs the :60 to show the dock.

## 4. Recommendation

**Take R2-1 The Gather to creative-engine-director first.**

- It is the only one of the three that *dramatizes proposition A*, the recommended proposition, as a visible action: the verb "draws in" is the picture.
- It carries the tension (irregular material) and resolves it inside one motion, which is what the rocks frame wanted to do and didn't.
- The forward move grows out of the idea (held, then travels), so "heart moving forward" is earned without the glass heart.
- It extends the warmth of the nature :30 v02 without its pattern-montage cliché, so it can absorb T01's best parts.

R2-2 is the strong B backup if Edwards wants a reproducibility route, but it overlaps T03 in logic; decide between them, don't present both. R2-3 is the bravest and the most likely to be loved or killed; hold it for the :60 or as the provocative third, pending Q36 (audience) and claim support for "ruled out" populations.

## Premise (deck)

**Title:** One Landing Zone
**Line:** Every mitral is different. The landing zone is the same.
**Premise:** No two mitral valves are shaped alike. We show that, fast: one anatomy after another, each a different shape, size and stiffness. Then the SAPIEN M3 dock draws each one in, and every time it resolves into the same clean landing zone. The variety speeds up. The result never changes. Repetition is the proof.

**Mechanism:** repetition with variation (many inputs, one constant output), carried by match cuts on the same circle.
**Sound:** each anatomy gets its own off-beat note; every dock closure lands on the same tone. By the end, the chaos has become a rhythm.
**Works without the glass heart:** yes. The close is the anatomical heart moving forward.

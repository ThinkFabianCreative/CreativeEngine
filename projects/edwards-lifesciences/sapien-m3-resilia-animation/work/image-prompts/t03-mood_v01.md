## M1 · Different every time (still)

Seven soft organic forms in a single row on a warm beige seamless backdrop, each one a different irregular shape, size and texture, like river stones, clay and seed pods. At the centre of every form sits the same small, perfect red circle, identical in size and position. Still life in the manner of Irving Penn and Karl Blossfeldt: soft north-window light from the left, gentle shadow, matte surfaces, calm and precise. Muted beige, warm grey and a single saturated red. 16:9, no text, no logos, no medical objects.

## M2 · One landing zone (still)

Many translucent, irregular organic rings of different shapes float on a warm beige ground, slightly out of register, converging toward the centre where they align into one perfect, crisp red ring. Minimal, sculptural, quiet, in the tonal restraint of Hiroshi Sugimoto. Soft diffused light, shallow depth of field at the edges, perfectly sharp centre. Beige, cream and one red. 16:9, no text, no logos, no medical objects.

## V1 · Resolve (video, 5 s)

A single red drop falls onto a still beige liquid surface. Uneven, wobbling ripples spread out, then slow and settle into one perfect, still red ring at the centre. Locked-off overhead camera, soft diffused light, calm, slow. 16:9, no text.

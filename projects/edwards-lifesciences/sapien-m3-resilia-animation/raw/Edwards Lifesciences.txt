INDICATION (short form)
For patients with symptomatic moderate-to-severe or severe MR or symptomatic MAC-associated mitral valve dysfunction, including MR and/or MS, who are unsuitable for surgery or TEER.

PRIMARY MESSAGE
The fully transseptal SAPIEN M3 RESILIA TMVR system delivers consistent, near MR elimination with a proven safety profile for patients with mitral valve dysfunction. The next-generation valve with RESILIA tissue and refined leaflet geometry enables optimal performance, building on the ENCIRCLE pivotal trial and trusted SAPIEN and RESILIA technology.

PROMOTIONAL HEADERS / REASONS TO BELIEVE (RTBs)

1. OUTCOMES

Consistent MR elimination with 97% of patients achieving MR ≤1+
- ENCIRCLE main cohort: 97% achieved MR ≤1+ through 2 years
- MAC registry: 98% MR ≤1+ through 1 year

Significant Functional and Quality-of-Life Improvements
- NYHA I/II achieved in 87% of the main cohort at 2 years and 88% of the MAC Registry at 1 year; KCCQ-OS improved by +17 and +18 points, respectively.

Proven Safety Profile
- Main cohort 30-day mortality <1%; significantly outperformed primary endpoint performance goal specified at 1 year, with high freedom from all-cause mortality or HFH through 2 years.

Evidence Demonstrates Efficacy Across Varied Patient Populations
- ENCIRCLE trial consistently demonstrates efficacy across varied populations, anatomies and pathologies, including FMR, DMR, MMR, and MAC-associated MR and/or MS.

2. INNOVATION

Reassurance From Access to Deployment
- The low-profile Edwards 23F Guide Sheath supports fully transseptal delivery; the dock is repositionable and retrievable before release; and the SAPIEN M3 RESILIA valve builds on the design of the trusted SAPIEN platform

Refined Valve Design for Mitral Hemodynamic Performance
- The valve's optimized leaflet dimensions and RESILIA tissue improve EOA when deployed within the SAPIEN M3 dock in the mitral position (*compared to the first-generation SAPIEN M3 valve)

Accommodates Heterogeneous Anatomy for Reproducible Replacement
- The novel dock captures the anterior and posterior mitral leaflets, bringing the mitral apparatus inward to create a stable, standardized landing zone for the SAPIEN M3 RESILIA valve

Designed to Draw Anatomy Inward
- The SAPIEN M3 dock leverages radial force to draw the mitral apparatus inward, rather than expanding outward against the anatomy
- The dock pulls the leaflets and chordae toward the dock center to approximate papillary muscles
- Native leaflet-based anchoring is designed to support natural annular motion
- Demonstrated favorable LV reverse remodeling, with significant reductions in LVEDVi

3. LEGACY

Trusted SAPIEN Platform. Built for Mitral.
- The SAPIEN platform: more than 1 million procedures globally, including more than 11,000 mitral procedures with the SAPIEN 3 platform as of year-end 2025

Advanced with Proven RESILIA Tissue Technology
- RESILIA tissue technology is proven to reduce calcification, with the potential to extend valve longevity.
- More than 500,000 (surgical) RESILIA tissue valves have been implanted worldwide
- In the COMMENCE Mitral Trial evaluating RESILIA tissue in surgical mitral valves, hemodynamics remained stable through 5 years, with 98.7% freedom from SVD and 97.1% freedom from reoperation†

Founded in Deep Structural Heart Expertise
- Edwards' longstanding structural-heart focus, multidisciplinary evaluation, training and hands-on case support bring deep expertise from patient selection through implantation and beyond
- 100% case support with Edwards device and imaging-certified specialists in the cath lab

ELEVATOR PITCH
The fully transseptal SAPIEN M3 RESILIA system advances TMVR with a novel valve-in-dock assembly that provides inward radial force to standardize heterogeneous mitral anatomy. Built for the mitral valve with refined leaflet geometry and RESILIA tissue, the system delivers near MR elimination, significant functional and quality-of-life improvements, and a proven safety profile across diverse patient populations. Edwards supports heart teams across the continuum of care, from patient selection through implantation.

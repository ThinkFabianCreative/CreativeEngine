CREATIVE BRIEF

DATE: 10/6/26

PROJECT: Edwards SAPIEN M3 RESILIA Animation

## Overview

Edwards Lifesciences is a global medical technology company focused on structural heart disease and critical cardiovascular care.

## Assignment

Developing animations to support the launch of the new SAPIEN M3 RESILIA system

## Deliverables

One approved script and storyboard
One 60-second U.S. animation
One 30-second U.S. animation cutdown
Two rounds of consolidated revisions per deliverable
Final high-resolution 16:9 MP4 files

## Creative Direction

The animation will be developed based on the campaign concept below:

The "SM3R\_CampaignConcepts\_WIP" file is now outdated, so I've placed it in the "Archive" folder within the Sharepoint. The campaign agency is in the middle of artwork finalization with the below concept. We want to incorporate the new pillar "icons"/visuals

Here are some thoughts of visuals I associate with each pillar, but please do feel free to advise a different creative direction:

For pillar 1, it'd be show the ENCIRCLE trial graphics in a sleek way (I placed a draft of the ENCIRCLE trial deck in our sharepoint)

For pillar 2, we'd like to show the new RESILIA leaflets, the dock encircling, the dock bringing anatomy inward, valve opening and closing, full valve-in-dock implant

For pillar 3, SAPIEN 3 pipeline/evolution, RESILIA tissue technology, some numerical graphic (ie. Commence trial data or 500,000+ valves) to show longevity/credibility of RESILIA technology, Edwards portfolio

Concept/ Creative Elements:

Red glass heart: Yes please keep

The current color palette, or if we have flexibility to adjust it: Let's use the beige/tan background color as the main color but there's flexibility with using colors within the Edwards brand palette to break up the monotony if needed

The leaflet background pattern: I believe this is no longer applicable with the current concept but please let me know if I'm not referring to the correct pattern. Also, we certainly will want a portion of the animation to highlight the new RESILIA technology which I imagine it would visually focus on the leaflets somewhere during the animation.

The 3 leaflet circle graphic: You don't have to use this graphic in this exact format within it's circle, but as I mentioned above, we would like some visual showcasing the RESILIA leaflets during that portion of the messaging.

The ENCIRCLE trial logo: This logo cannot be changed. Although you have flexibility for the color; for example, on a dark background, we have a logo version that's all white and vice versa.

The word "Evolve": Please do not use "evolve." We are not moving forward with the Evolve concept

The visual of the heart moving forward: Yes please keep. This will be our main concept so we will want to somehow incorporate.

Sound/Narration: The animation can include sound. Text on screen would suffice; no narration needed.

## Messaging

The required messaging is around the main points of the 3 pillars (highlighted in green). The black bulleted text are the reasons-to-believe for each red promotional header; therefore, I'm hoping that the content of the black reasons-to-believe text might be expressed more visually in accompany of the red promotional text.

I'd also like to make the team aware that for each of the three pillars, we are beginning exploration with the campaign agency to develop 3 distinct creative "icons" or visuals that will be associated with each pillar. I would love for these pillar visuals to also be incorporated into the animation but of course that means I do currently have the artwork files for you right now.

Priority outcomes/data to highlight: For pillar 1, MR elimination with the 97% achieved MR \<= 1+ at 2 years is a key point, and the 30-day mortality \<1% at 30 days is another key one we'd want to highlight.

Please reference "SM3R EW Provided messaging\_9-30-26" for the latest version of the messaging.

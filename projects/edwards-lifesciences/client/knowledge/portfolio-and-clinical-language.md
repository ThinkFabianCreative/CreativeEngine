Edwards focuses primarily on structural heart disease.

## Valve basics

A heart valve works like a one-way gate.

- **Stenosis:** the valve is too narrow and flow is restricted. Aortic stenosis is central to TAVR.
- **Regurgitation (insufficiency):** the valve doesn't close and blood leaks backward. Mitral and tricuspid regurgitation are central to TMTT.

Always keep two dimensions separate:

| Dimension | Options |
|---|---|
| Access route | **Transcatheter** (catheter-delivered) · **Surgical** (implanted in surgery) |
| Treatment type | **Repair** (the native valve stays and works better) · **Replacement** (a prosthetic takes over) |

- **SAPIEN 3 Ultra RESILIA:** balloon-expandable frame; eligibility varies by patient, label and market. TAVI is the non-US term.
- **KONECT RESILIA:** preassembled valve-and-graft for aortic-root surgery. **MITRIS:** saddle-shaped sewing cuff, low-profile frame.
- **Alterra** creates a landing zone in suitable RVOT anatomy; SAPIEN 3 supplies the valve.
- **AUTUS:** size-adjustable surgical pulmonary valve; Edwards announced FDA approval on 2026-10-01 (per the deck; verify).
- **Critical Care:** BD completed the acquisition 2024-09-03; now BD Advanced Patient Monitoring.
- A multidisciplinary Heart Team decides the treatment option. Confirm the actual buying group for hospital audiences.

**RESILIA** is a tissue technology used in specific valves. It is not a valve. Evidence for one RESILIA device does not transfer to another.

## TMTT communication challenges

As supplied, TMTT is not only a product-marketing job. Its connected challenges:

- **Category education:** disease, symptoms, severity, patient identification, treatment alternatives
- **Physician confidence:** clinical evidence, procedural understanding, peer confidence, training, expert advocacy
- **Referral development:** referrer → specialist → structural heart team → evaluation → treatment
- **KOL / DOL activation:** interventional cardiologists, structural-heart specialists, surgeons, imaging specialists, investigators, conference faculty. Peer credibility matters enormously
- **Patient understanding:** simple explanations, realistic expectations, questions to ask; never overpromise
- **Portfolio storytelling** (above)

A possible organizing model: **EVIDENCE → EDUCATION → ADVOCACY → REFERRAL → TREATMENT** (data and congress → HCP and patient tools → KOL/DOL and peer content → awareness, referrer education, CRM → patient preparation and follow-up). Use it when useful, not automatically.

Edwards is a regulated medical-device company. These rules outrank every creative and commercial consideration.

## Never invent

Clinical outcomes, approval status, indications, product claims, procedure results, market share, patient outcomes, physician quotes, ROI, booth traffic, leads, sales results or campaign performance. If a metric isn't substantiated, don't estimate it.

Use language that matches the evidence: "archive shows", "scope included", "proposed", "historical", "campaign included", "TRAFFIK developed", "documentation indicates".

Don't imply that marketing alone causes clinical adoption.

## Proposed vs delivered

A scope of work is not proof that the deliverables were completed. Classify every piece of archive material:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CONFIRMED DELIVERED | Evidence the work existed or launched |
| PROPOSED SCOPE | Appears in a proposal, estimate or unsigned scope |
| ARCHIVE EVIDENCE | A project folder exists; delivery status unclear |
| UNKNOWN | Not enough evidence |

Never silently upgrade one status to another.

## Verify before anything Edwards-facing

Exact product, anatomy, therapy type, audience, market, indication, approval status, evidence source, study name, device generation and terminology. Watch especially:

- repair vs replacement
- surgical vs transcatheter
- mitral vs tricuspid vs aortic vs pulmonary
- RESILIA vs ThermaFix
- current vs legacy (divested) businesses

### The team's four checks for any Edwards brief, asset or adaptation

From the team orientation deck, slide 21 (`client/sources/2026-10-03_gslides_edwards-traffik-team-orientation_LINK.md`, added 2026-10-14):

1. **Product and anatomy.** Name the exact device, valve location and route. Keep repair, replacement, tissue technology and monitoring distinct.
2. **Audience and purpose.** Decide who needs the asset and what they need to understand. A surgeon, referrer, patient and purchaser need different detail.
3. **Market and evidence.** Confirm geography, device version, label and source study. Approval, launch and investigational status are different facts.
4. **Archive and review.** Separate delivered work from proposed scope. Treat historical examples as creative references and recheck claims, permissions and review requirements.

Healthcare adds evidence, regulation, clinical ethics and peer credibility, and those differences must be respected.

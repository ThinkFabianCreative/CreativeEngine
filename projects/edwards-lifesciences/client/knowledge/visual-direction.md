**The intersection:** humanity + engineering + clinical precision. Sophisticated, intelligent, optimistic, precise, human, credible. Never sterile. Never sci-fi for its own sake.

## Prioritize

Real Edwards product photography · macro material and tissue detail · structural-heart anatomy and anatomical diagrams · engineering detail · physician environments · real congress photography · real historical TRAFFIK work · clinical evidence and visualization · authentic patient storytelling · product animation and procedure visualization · typography

## Category codes to avoid (unless an idea deliberately turns one)

Generic "healthcare blue" · ECG/heartbeat lines as decoration · glowing hearts · DNA and generic molecules · holograms, HUDs, doctors surrounded by screens · fake futuristic medicine or labs · superficial "innovation" symbolism · smiling doctor with clipboard, generic hospital hallway · card grids, equal-weight boxes, gradients, fake dashboards

## Generated imagery

Stricter than the global default. **Never** generate patients, physicians, clinical procedures, Edwards products or case-study imagery, even in comps. Generation only for clearly labeled future-state scenarios (an imagined congress space, a film mood frame), and never in a way that could read as evidence.

## Future-state sections

In any pitch, the "what's next" section should be visually stronger than the archive section. The goal is to make Edwards imagine what TRAFFIK could become.

## Color (p. 24–27)
- "Red and titanium brand." Lead with primary red + grays; other colors only as light backgrounds or accents.

## Typography (p. 29–30)
- Primary Vesta Std. Digital: Merriweather Sans. PowerPoint/office: Arial.
- Hierarchy by size OR weight, not both. Regular for titles, subtitles and body; Bold for subheads and emphasis; Italic for pull quotes and emphasis.
- Flush left; centered text only in tables and infographics. Sentence case preferred (Title Case allowed for headlines).
- Color on light backgrounds: titles red or red + gray; subtitles dark gray or black; other copy black or gray.

## Secondary graphic (p. 33–37)
- Red squares, 100% PANTONE 186, never tinted. Always a small red square at lower left; the large square may be cropped at the top or right edge.
- Over images: red square with white type, 25% light-gray square with red headline, or dark-gray square with white type.

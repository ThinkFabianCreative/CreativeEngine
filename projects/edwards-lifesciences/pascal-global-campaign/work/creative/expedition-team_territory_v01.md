# Territory: The expedition team (under "Tighten your grip.")

## Andres's idea
A heart team is an Everest expedition crew, gearing up to do what looks impossible. Reliable carabiners and tools you can depend on decide whether the climb succeeds. Like climbers, heart teams hold lives in their hands: the patient's. The territory raises heart teams and specialists to that level.

## Concept sentence
The procedure is the expedition. The heart team is the rope team, and PASCAL is the carabiner: the one piece of gear that closes, locks and holds when everything depends on it.

## The procedure as a climb (Andres, 2026-11-11)
| Climb | TEER procedure |
|---|---|
| Base camp, route planning | Pre-procedure imaging, case planning |
| The approach | Transfemoral access, transseptal crossing |
| Navigating the icefall | Steering through the chords (elongation, slim profile) |
| The crux | Grasping the leaflets |
| Clip in, lock off | Clasp closes; reclasp if the hold isn't right |
| Summit | MR reduced, seen before release |
| The descent | Release, retrieve the system, patient home |

Lines this opens: "Every TEER is a climb." / "The crux is the grasp." / "Plan the route. Trust the gear." / "Most accidents happen on the way down." (predictable release)

## Why it works
- **The carabiner is the device.** Its gate opens, clips and locks shut, the same action as PASCAL's clasp. The metaphor is mechanical as well as emotional.
- **It makes heroes of the team, not one doctor.** Interventionalist, imager, surgeon and nurses are a rope team: tied in together, each one's grip protecting the others.
- **It ties into the umbrella.** Climbers tighten their grip. "Tighten your grip." works on a rope as well as on a valve.
- **It's competitive.** Climbers retire old gear, and nobody summits on equipment from 2013.

## Lines

- "You don't take 2013 gear up Everest." (competitive)

## Visual world
- Close detail, not the summit postcard: gloved hands clipping a carabiner, the gate snapping shut, frost on metal, rope running through.
- The rope team at dawn on an ice ridge, all tied into one line. That's the heart team.
- Gear laid out before the climb, flat-lay style: carabiners, rope, ice axe, with PASCAL among them at the same size, as one more piece of gear.
- Visual match: the carabiner gate and the PASCAL clasp as the same gesture, side by side.

## Watch
- "Summit" and "peak" imagery is a stock pharma and med-device cliché (flag once). Win it with gear-level detail and the clip-in moment, not flags on peaks.
- Edwards brand: imagery stays light. Snow and dawn light help (white, bright, cold blue). Avoid dark storm shots.

## Executions
- Billboard: a carabiner gate clicking shut in a gloved hand, with the PASCAL implant at the same size beside it. "Clip in." / "Tighten your grip."
- Brochure: the cover is the rope team on the ridge. The spread is the gear flat-lay, with the device facts as gear specs (spacer, nitinol paddles, elongation, reclasp).
- Conference stand: a "base camp" booth. A gear wall with a demo station styled as a gear check, and a team photo wall: "Who's on your rope?"
- Event idea: name the PASCAL training program "Base Camp".

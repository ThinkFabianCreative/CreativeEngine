The job, in one line: the routine case goes to the habit. Every territory below is an episode of the CCI, *stories where what a heart team has proved with PASCAL becomes how it works*. The hard case appears only as provenance, never as subject or opening beat. No territory leans on "certainty", "confidence", "control", "precision" as a benefit, or "first" (A7 clichés; Abbott owns certainty at TCT 2026).

## 1. EXPLORE: eight territories

### T1 · Precedent
- **Concept:** A heart team's past PASCAL cases work like case law: once you've ruled on it, it governs what comes next.
- **CCI episode:** the moment a program realizes its own record has already decided the question.
- **Lines:** "Your cases set the precedent." / "You've already ruled on this."
- **Chapters:** *US tricuspid:* "Precedent travels. Your mitral record now counts on the tricuspid side." (US indication only ⚑) · *Capture Clarity:* "Now the precedent is on screen. A visible indicator when the leaflet is captured." (US, DMR, PASCAL Ace only ⚑) · *Next-gen:* "Precedent is how the next system arrives: into a record you already wrote." (no device in action, investigational ⚑) · *EU:* "Since 2020, European teams have been setting tricuspid precedent ⚑." Real EU teams, real case counts.
- **Key visual (concept sentence):** A program's own case register, bound like a law report, open on a page of ordinary scheduled cases, with one earlier hard case cited in small type as the authority.
- **Watch:** legal metaphor could feel cold; keep it peer, never courtroom kitsch.

### T2 · Tuesday
- **Concept:** The campaign lives on the routine case day, not the hero case. Tuesday is where a default is made.
- **CCI episode:** the proof from the hard day showing up on the normal one.
- **Lines:** "Bring it to Tuesday." / "What you proved on the hard day belongs to the normal one."
- **Chapters:** *US tricuspid:* "Tuesday now has a tricuspid list." ⚑ · *Capture Clarity:* "A Tuesday-morning view: the indicator tells the whole room the leaflet is captured." (US, DMR, Ace only ⚑) · *Next-gen:* "Built for the Tuesdays ahead." (team planning, not device) · *EU:* each market gets its own routine day ("Dienstag", "Mardi") shot in real labs.
- **Key visual:** A cath-lab whiteboard schedule for an ordinary weekday, PASCAL written in the device column on every routine row, in the team's own handwriting.
- **Watch:** "every row" can read as "every case". Show the rows the anatomy leaves open, not the whole list. Day naming translates well; check the umbrella doesn't already use weekdays.

### T3 · The Logbook
- **Concept:** The team's own case log is the hero; the device company is only the publisher of its proof.
- **CCI episode:** the log turning from record into routine.
- **Lines:** "Your proof is already written." / "Read your own logbook."
- **Chapters:** tricuspid = a new section in the same book; Capture Clarity = "an entry the device now helps you write" ⚑; next-gen = the pages ahead; EU = European logbooks since 2020 ⚑.
- **Key visual:** A worn procedure log, the latest pages filled with routine PASCAL entries, the first page (a hard case) closed behind them.
- **Watch:** close cousin of T1, weaker metaphor. Depends heavily on Edwards granting case-history access.

### T4 · Same Hands
- **Concept:** The skill on one valve is the skill on the other; the team crosses valves as itself.
- **CCI episode:** a one-valve program discovering it is already a two-valve team (J2).
- **Lines:** "Same hands. Other valve." / "You don't start over. You start as you."
- **Chapters:** US tricuspid is the lead ⚑; Capture Clarity explicitly mitral-only, "and on the mitral side, now you can see the grasp" ⚑; next-gen "the hands you'll bring"; EU tricuspid since 2020 ⚑.
- **Key visual:** One operator's gloved hands on the delivery handle, photographed twice, identical, captioned "Mitral" and "Tricuspid".
- **Watch:** "hands" excludes the imager and the wider team; "same" edges toward a one-skill-set claim ⚑ (different anatomy, different approach angles).

### T5 · Read Together
- **Concept:** The whole room reads the same moment on the same screen; the default is a team decision, not one operator's hand.
- **CCI episode:** proof becoming shared practice between operator and imager.
- **Lines:** "Seen by the whole room." / "Everyone reads it the same way."
- **Chapters:** strongest in Capture Clarity (US, DMR, Ace ⚑); tricuspid "the team reads a new valve together"; next-gen thin; EU through real imager-operator pairs.
- **Key visual:** Operator, imager and nurse caught in one frame looking at the same monitor, faces lit by it, the screen out of shot.
- **Watch:** collides with DragonFly "readable indicators" and edges into Abbott's certainty; carries one chapter well and three weakly. Better as an execution inside another territory.

### T6 · House Style
- **Concept:** Great programs have a way they do things. PASCAL becomes the program's house style, not a specialty tool.
- **CCI episode:** best work codified into how a program works.
- **Lines:** "Make it house style." / "The way your program does TEER."
- **Chapters:** tricuspid "house style, second valve" ⚑; Capture Clarity a new house standard on the mitral side ⚑; next-gen "house style keeps evolving"; EU house styles by center.
- **Key visual:** A program's own laminated protocol card, pinned in the lab, with PASCAL steps in its own words.
- **Watch:** "style" can sound cosmetic to interventionalists. Good language, flimsy for a clinical audience.

### T7 · Called In
- **Concept:** The team others call on one valve becomes the team others call on both.
- **CCI episode:** standing earned in mitral carried into tricuspid (J2 status barrier).
- **Lines:** "They already call you. Now for both valves."
- **Chapters:** good on tricuspid and EU; weak on Capture Clarity and next-gen.
- **Key visual:** A referral pager screen showing two incoming requests, one mitral, one tricuspid, to the same team.
- **Watch:** "called in" implies referred hard cases: breaks the hard-case rule by implication. KILL-adjacent; kept only as a line source.

### T8 · Second Nature
- **Concept:** The default is what you no longer think about; make PASCAL the thing you don't have to decide.
- **Lines:** "Make it second nature."
- **Chapters:** generic across all four.
- **Key visual:** A setup tray laid out before anyone asked.
- **Watch:** cliché ("second nature" is everywhere), says the job not the idea. Honest filler; not recommended.

**ET, bluntly:**
- **Confidence, Captured** is the most showable thing on the table: Capture Clarity hands it a real, visible moment and a pitch-room "ah". But it is a product feature wearing a campaign coat. It is US-only, DMR-only, Ace-only, so it cannot carry tricuspid or EU (hub review point 2). "Confidence" is an A7 cliché and the whole idea now sits on Abbott's "certainty is essential" and DragonFly's readable indicators. It also answers the wrong problem: the occasional user doesn't lack confidence on the hard case; they lack a reason on the easy one.
- **Every Case** breaks two mandatories by name: "every case" is banned for PASCAL and collides with the TMTT umbrella, and its line opens on the hardest case. It should not be shown.
- **Fluent in Both** is ET's best and it is genuinely good. It is the clearest answer to J2, "fluency" travels across EU languages, and nobody in A6 owns it. Where it falls short: it speaks to J2 and barely to J1, the core audience; "fluent" implies the team had to learn, which nudges toward the novice framing the brief bans; and "one skill set" is a claim ⚑ MLR will test. It also doesn't dramatize the CCI's key move (proof becoming habit); it describes a capability.

**Where ET is stronger than us:** Confidence, Captured beats every one of ours on instant showability in the Capture Clarity chapter, and Fluent in Both beats T4 Same Hands on the second valve because it includes the whole team, not just hands.

## 3. Self-DEVELOP: reworked picks

### Pick 1 · Precedent (rework of T1, absorbs T3)
- **Concept:** What your team has done with PASCAL is precedent, and precedent governs the ordinary case.
- **Platform line:** "Your cases set the precedent." Support: "You've already ruled on this."
- **Why it beats Fluent in Both:** it serves J1 and J2 in one move (precedent on one valve governs the other), it is the CCI almost word for word, and it stands where A6 shows nobody: operator craft identity, not certainty or indicators. Abbott's scale ("300,000 patients, 20 years") becomes irrelevant: the only precedent that counts is yours.
- **Chapters:** tricuspid, precedent travels ⚑; Capture Clarity, the precedent made visible on screen (US/DMR/Ace) ⚑; next-gen arrives into a record already written; EU, six years of European tricuspid precedent ⚑.
- **Key visual:** the program's own case register, open on routine scheduled cases, an earlier hard case cited in the margin as the authority.
- **Risk to manage:** register must be the team's real record (Edwards access ask). Fallback: real team, real handwriting, no generated proof.

### Pick 2 · Tuesday (rework of T2)
- **Concept:** The default is made on the routine day. The campaign never shows a hero case; it shows the normal list.
- **Platform line:** "Bring it to Tuesday." Support: "What you proved on the hard day belongs to the normal one."
- **Why it beats Fluent in Both:** it names the behavior change exactly (routine cases) and makes the hard-case rule a creative strength rather than a restriction. It is the most visual of our ideas and localizes naturally: every market has a Tuesday.
- **Fix from EXPLORE:** never "every row". Show the open-anatomy rows only, so it cannot read as "every case" or all-anatomy.
- **Chapters:** "Tuesday now has a tricuspid list" ⚑; Capture Clarity, a Tuesday-morning grasp the whole room can see (US/DMR/Ace) ⚑; next-gen, the team planning future Tuesdays; EU, Dienstag/Mardi/Martedì in real labs.
- **Key visual:** an ordinary weekday whiteboard in a real cath lab, PASCAL in the team's handwriting on the routine rows.

### Pick 3 · Home Valve (rework of T4 + T5, answering Fluent in Both directly)
- **Concept:** The second valve isn't foreign ground. The team crosses as itself, operator, imager and nurse together, with standing intact.
- **Platform line:** "Both valves. Same team." Support: "You don't start over. You start as you."
- **Why it beats Fluent in Both:** fluency is something you acquire; standing is something you already have. It meets the J2 status barrier head-on (no beginners in our own lab) and fixes T4's hands-only problem by casting the whole team (T5's strength).
- **Chapters:** US tricuspid is the lead ⚑; Capture Clarity explicitly the mitral side only (US/DMR/Ace) ⚑; next-gen, the team that will meet it; EU, teams on both valves since 2020 ⚑.
- **Key visual:** the same heart team photographed twice in the same lab, same positions, one frame labeled "Mitral", one "Tricuspid", nothing changed but the label.
- **Risk:** "same team" must not imply identical technique ⚑; it promises continuity of people and standing, not procedure.

### What stays from ET
- **Fluent in Both** should stay in the mix as the benchmark Pick 3 must beat in the room; if the director prefers it, Pick 3's "whole team" casting and "start as you" belief should be grafted onto it.
- **Confidence, Captured** should not survive as a territory, but its Capture Clarity moment is the best chapter execution anyone has: keep it as the mitral chapter inside whichever territory wins.
- **Every Case**: drop. It breaks mandatories.

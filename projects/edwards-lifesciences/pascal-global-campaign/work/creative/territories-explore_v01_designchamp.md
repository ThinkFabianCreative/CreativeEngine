Ground rules carried from the brief: every territory is an episode of the CCI (*what a heart team has proved with PASCAL becomes how it works*). The hard case is never subject or first beat. Capture Clarity: US, degenerative mitral, PASCAL Ace only. Next-gen: investigational, never shown performing. Edwards red, dark grey (never black, never orange). Classy and futuristic, not sci-fi. Real teams; generated imagery is pitch rough only and never stands in for clinical proof (fluoro, echo, case history). No arrows, roads, bridges.

**Evidence caveat.** Abbott's visual codes are LOW confidence (captions and page structure, no screenshots). The clichés I design against are the ones the research lists: isolated device render on a plain field, mm size line-ups, system animation, KOL headshot plus quote, booth/simulator photos. Plus the general structural-heart set: glowing blue anatomical heart, beating-heart CGI, surgeon-hero in blue light, ECG line, hands-holding-heart. ## Part 1. EXPLORE: eight territories

### T1. The Room Remembers
- **Concept sentence:** A cath lab keeps the shape of the work a team has done there, and PASCAL is part of that shape.
- **Mechanism:** Exaggeration (accumulation made visible) + context shift (long-exposure art photography in a clinical room).
- **Who carries it:** Image. Line only names whose room it is.
- **Key visual:** A real hybrid lab, dark grey, shot as a multi-hour long exposure (or a composite of real case-day frames). The equipment is pin-sharp; the team is a soft, layered presence where they always stand, worn into the room like a path in grass. One thin Edwards-red trace where the operator's hands have worked over the table, the only saturated colour. Calm, gallery-quality, almost architectural. Nothing generated in the final; pitch rough can be generated as a mood only, labeled.
- **Chapters:**
  - US tricuspid: same room, same trace, new case on the board: the team's accumulated presence is already there on day one of T-TEER.
  - Capture Clarity (US, DMR, Ace): the one moment in the exposure that is sharp is the imager's screen, the instant the team saw capture. Imaging must be real.
  - Next-gen (investigational): the room at dawn before the lab opens, the trace of past work, an empty table. Forward-looking without the device.
  - EU: European labs (named cities, real teams) with deeper exposures: they've been doing both valves since 2020.
- **Avoids:** device-on-field, surgeon-hero, glowing heart. No path/arrow: the trace is a record, not a direction.

### T2. The Ordinary Board
- **Concept sentence:** The day you know PASCAL has become how you work is the day nobody remarks on it.
- **Mechanism:** Omission (the drama is left out) + literal language (the case board says it).
- **Who carries it:** Image, with the line as a quiet caption.
- **Key visual:** The real whiteboard or scheduling screen of a cath lab day, shot flat and editorial: patient initials redacted, times, room numbers, imager, and "PASCAL" written in an ordinary hand on ordinary rows. Nothing is circled. The red is the marker colour of one entry, or a single Edwards-red magnet. It looks like documentary photography of an institution, like an annual-report spread.
- **Chapters:**
  - US tricuspid: a "T" row appears on the board in the same handwriting as the mitral rows.
  - Capture Clarity: the board reads "Ace" on a DMR row; companion frame is the real fluoro indicator, US-only asset.
  - Next-gen: a future date on the board, the row left blank, labeled investigational. Nothing shown performing.
  - EU: European boards in their own languages and formats; both valves on one day.
- **Avoids:** every TEER visual cliché, because it has no device and no heart in it. Risk: it is so quiet it can read as a stock-board shot unless the details are a real program's.

### T3. Same Hands
- **Concept sentence:** On the second valve, the team is already the team it is.
- **Mechanism:** Fusion through repetition (two moments that should differ, made identical).
- **Who carries it:** Image (a diptych); the line names the valves.
- **Key visual:** A strict two-panel diptych, same lab, same framing, same team positions: left is a mitral day, right is a tricuspid day. Only the monitor and one small detail differ. Shot locked off like a scientific comparison plate, dark grey grade, red used only for the panel rule and type.
- **Chapters:** US tricuspid is its natural home. Capture Clarity: diptych of before/after the indicator on a DMR case (US only; never paired with tricuspid). Next-gen: left panel today, right panel a blank, lit lab, investigational. EU: diptych dated 2020 and 2026, same team, both valves.
- **Avoids:** novice framing; it is a status image. Risk: overlaps ET "Fluent in Both".

### T4. Read by Everyone
- **Concept sentence:** A default lives in the whole team's eyes, not only the operator's hands.
- **Mechanism:** Context shift (the monitor as a mirror).
- **Who carries it:** Image.
- **Key visual:** Shot from behind the boom monitor: the real imaging on screen, and in the dark glass around it the reflected faces of operator, imager, nurse and fellow, all looking at the same thing. The team appears inside the image of its own work.
- **Chapters:** Capture Clarity is the strongest chapter (the reflection at the moment the indicator reads, US/DMR/Ace). US tricuspid: same composition, tricuspid team. Next-gen: monitor dark, the team's reflection only. EU: European teams in reflection.
- **Avoids:** surgeon-hero; puts imagers and nurses in frame (brief hypothesis on team default). Risk: closest to ET's "Confidence, Captured" moment.

### T5. Standing
- **Concept sentence:** A team's PASCAL experience is a credential, and it deserves the portrait a credential gets.
- **Mechanism:** Context shift (the formal institutional portrait: faculty, orchestra, expedition team).
- **Who carries it:** Image + a dated caption ("PASCAL since 2021").
- **Key visual:** Large-format, posed, unhurried group portraits of real heart teams in their own lab, shot like a museum commission. No scrubs drama, no blue light. Caption plate in Edwards red with the year the program began PASCAL.
- **Chapters:** US tricuspid: same portrait, a second date added. Capture Clarity: weaker; becomes a portrait caption only. Next-gen: weak. EU: very strong (CE teams since 2020).
- **Avoids:** stock doctors. Risk: congratulatory, static; testimonial-adjacent.

### T6. Where You Stand
- **Concept sentence:** The lab is worn in the places where a team's habits live.
- **Mechanism:** Omission (show the trace, never the person).
- **Who carries it:** Image.
- **Key visual:** Macro still lifes of real lab wear: floor tape where the operator plants, a pedal polished by use, a boom handle worn matte. Dark grey, raking light, one red detail.
- **Chapters:** good for tricuspid ("same spot"), poor for Capture Clarity, fine for next-gen (fresh unmarked floor), fine for EU.
- **Avoids:** all category clichés. Risk: can read as melancholy or dirty; too abstract for a medical panel.

### T7. The Grasp Archive (provocation)
- **Concept sentence:** A program's own grasps, collected, are the most persuasive image PASCAL has.
- **Mechanism:** Accumulation / typology (Becher-style grids).
- **Who carries it:** Image.
- **Key visual:** A typological grid of one program's real fluoro or echo frames of PASCAL captures, catalogued with case number and date, archive style.
- **Chapters:** all, but only with Edwards imaging access and consent. Capture Clarity grids are US/DMR/Ace only.
- **Avoids:** device renders. Risk: total dependency on access; can drift toward hard-case imagery. Keep as the Showtime (rung 7) layer on top of another territory.

### T8. The Red Line (killed in EXPLORE, kept for the record)
- **Concept sentence:** One continuous red line runs through a team's work.
- **Mechanism:** visual metaphor of continuity.
- **Why it's out:** reads as progress line, ECG, or road. It is the cliché the brief warns against. Not a verdict on anything else.

## Part 3. Rework and select three

### Pick 1. The Room Remembers (T1, reworked)
Rework: fold in T4. The long exposure keeps one sharp element per chapter: the screen. The team's accumulated presence is the soft layer; the shared moment on the monitor is the sharp layer. That answers "the default lives with the whole team", gives Capture Clarity a legitimate US-only home, and leaves tricuspid, next-gen and EU fully covered. Showability fix: present one generated mood rough clearly labeled, plus a real-photography reference board and the shoot plan, so the panel never mistakes the rough for proof.
**Pairs with:** a verbal territory about memory, experience carrying, or "the room already knows". Strongest pairing candidate.

### Pick 2. The Ordinary Board (T2, reworked)
Rework: make it a system, not a single photo. Every asset is a real artifact from the working day (board, schedule screen, case-log line, handover sheet) shot as editorial still life, with one Edwards-red mark. Lines sit as handwriting or captions on the artifact. It is the purest statement of the job to be done: the routine case, not the hard one. Cheapest to rough honestly for Nov 13.
**Pairs with:** a deadpan, understated verbal territory (the anti-hero line). Can also carry a WordSmither headline written *on* the board, a true visual-verbal 1+1=3.

### Pick 3. Same Hands (T3, reworked)
Rework: widen it from "two valves" to "same team, new chapter". The locked-off diptych becomes the campaign's grammar: left panel what you've proved, right panel the new news (second valve, Capture Clarity on DMR, a lit and empty next-gen lab, a European team's 2020 and 2026). It is the head-on visual answer to ET's "Fluent in Both", and it beats it because the image proves the claim without the line.
**Pairs with:** J2-focused verbal work about standing and continuity; can absorb ET's "Fluent in Both" thinking if Andres wants to merge.

Held in reserve: T7 Grasp Archive as the rung-7 layer if Edwards grants imaging access (it sits on top of Pick 1 or 3). T5 Standing for EU only.

## Verdict vs ET (DesignChamp view, for creative-engine-director to judge)
Picks 1 and 2 are more distinctive and travel across all four chapters better than any of ET's three. Pick 3 beats "Fluent in Both" on showability. ET's "Confidence, Captured" remains the stronger Nov 13 moment for the Capture Clarity chapter alone; my recommendation is to absorb its strongest asset (the moment on screen) into Pick 1 rather than fight it. "Every Case" should not go forward under the brief's mandatories.

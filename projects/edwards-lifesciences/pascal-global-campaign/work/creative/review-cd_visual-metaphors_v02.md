## 4. Distinct territories? Stronger than ET's originals?

**Distinctness.** C is clearly its own territory (J2, anatomy, two valves). A and B are both J1 and, as currently drawn, both are *supply storage showing use*. In a lineup they will look like two crops of one idea. Strategically they differ (A = authority from your history; B = the ordinary day), so the difference must show in the image. That is the second reason B moves to option 1 or 2 above, with option 2 the better differentiator.

**Against ET's originals.**
- *Confidence, Captured* is built on Capture Clarity, which is US-only, degenerative mitral, Ace-only. It cannot be a global or two-valve platform. It is a chapter.
- *Every Case* breaks a mandatory ("never every case") and starts from the hard case. Not presentable as is.
- *Fluent in Both* is a capability description; good J2 strategy, no image.

The new set is stronger: all three sit inside the CCI and the mandatories, and A and C have real visual mechanisms rather than claims. That is a prediction on paper; it becomes true only if the roughs pass the cold read. Keep *Fluent in Both*'s J2 logic in C's chapter copy; it is not lost.

## What round 2 changed

My round-1 mechanism was "your own proof turned on yourself". v02 kept it but moved its engine. The hard case used to supply the reversal: the exception becomes the rule. Now the hard case is evidence only, and the reversal has to come from "further". The question for this round is whether "further" can carry tension by itself. My answer is that it can in V1, and only when every episode names a real starting point.

## Drift check: "further" into "next-generation" category language

Risk: **REAL, medium.** "Further" has a neighbor in the category's progress words: next-generation, advancing, the future of TEER, pushing boundaries (EP 4). Three places in v02 lean that way:

1. **Umbrella row, "PASCAL is where the work keeps going" / "leading where TEER is going"** (§7). This is the drift point. Here the subject of "further" changes from the team's experience to the technology's progress, and once that happens the phrase is the category's. Flag it.
2. **E5 next-gen episode** (§4). "Experience carries into the next system" is fine as written because the team stays the subject. The danger is in execution: it will be pitched as product news.
3. **Long-lived test** (§4): "every indication, device, data release is another 'further'". Strategically this is true. Verbally it teaches the team to hang "further" on product milestones.

Test to apply: **whose "further" is it?** If the team's experience moves, it is owned. If the device, the pipeline or "TEER" moves, it is category language. The test should be written into the never-say list.

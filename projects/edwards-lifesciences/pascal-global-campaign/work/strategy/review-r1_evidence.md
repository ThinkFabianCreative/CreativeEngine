## 2. New evidence that bears on the strategy (observations, not verdicts)

**A2 (heart team, not operator, sets the default): weakened.**
- The only public statement found on why operators default to MitraClip attributes it to the operator's own comfort implanting it (Lim, CLASP IID lead; reporter paraphrase, EP 5.4d). That locates the habit with the operator.
- Named physicians frame device choice by anatomy (EP 5.4a, b, e), not by team.
- The heart team appears in the US tricuspid label for **patient selection** (EP 5.1b), not device choice.
- Counter-evidence that keeps A2 alive: imaging literature treats grasp confirmation as hard and dependent on team coordination (EP 5.4i); a rescue case report credits same operator and same imager (EP 5.4j). These show the imager matters to the grasp, not that the team sets the default.
- No source found on who sets the device default inside a program. A2 is still untested, not refuted. The Phase 1 interview test in §10 stands.

**Recommended CCI (C2, "hardest case → every case, both valves"): two pressure points.**
- Physician voices treat "different devices for different anatomies" as normal good practice (EP 5.4a, b, c). That is the habit C2 wants to move, stated as clinical wisdom by the trial's own lead. Audience may read "every case" as against good practice. Strategist to decide whether this is the tension or a problem.
- Lim's paraphrase that thicker or complicated leaflets might suit MitraClip (EP 5.4e) contests that "hardest case" belongs to PASCAL in every anatomy.
- C1 launch episode: Capture Clarity is US, DMR, PASCAL Ace only (EP 5.2a). It cannot launch a global or tricuspid chapter on current evidence, and it is a mitral-only feature inside a "both valves" CCI.
- Support for C2's both-valve half: Edwards trade coverage frames the approvals as completing a mitral-to-tricuspid portfolio (mddionline headline, EP 5.2a source). Abbott MitraClip→TriClip transfer messaging (A3) still not found.

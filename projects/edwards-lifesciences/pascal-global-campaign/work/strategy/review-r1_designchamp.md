Question for each candidate: does it hand us an image, a demonstration or a visual proof that could carry ONE global system across congress (large, social, live), detail aid (close, sequential, rep-led) and digital (small, fast)?

## Promise candidates

| # | Rating | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| P1 certainty in the decisive seconds | **SHOWABLE** | The capture moment is a real, filmable proof (echo/fluoro read, independent clasping); it scales from congress screen to detail aid sequence. Narrow: one image, not a system with range. |
| P2 hardest case to every case | **THIN** | "Hardest case" is visual (complex anatomy, imaging); "every case" has no picture of its own. Risks re-anchoring the visual to complex anatomy, the thing the RFP wants operators to stop thinking. |
| P3 skill carries further (case to case, valve to valve) | **THIN, leaning SHOWABLE** | "Skill" and "carries" are abstract; the system would rest on metaphor. Its showable halves are owned facts: one device across mitral and tricuspid (same form, both sides of the heart) and the team's shared read. Needs a concrete visual anchor named before it can carry a system. |
| P4 operator in control | **NOT (as ownable)** | Showable in theory (hands, clasps), but the image is the category's: control/precision pictures are what Abbott shows too. Fails visually for the same reason it fails the swap test. |

## Category visual clichés to avoid

Device floating on gradient blue; glowing or translucent heart; ECG lines as decoration; generic "healthcare blue"; HUD/hologram overlays and doctors surrounded by screens; smiling physician with clipboard, hospital hallway; abstract "precision" macro of metal with lens flare; hands-clasping or puzzle metaphors for "team"; bridge/path/arrow metaphors for "carries"; side-by-side device comparison as hero; fake dashboards and card grids. Per client visual direction: no generated physicians, patients, procedures or Edwards product, even in comps.

## Note for the gate

Visual weight sits in C1's proof moment and C2's both-valves structure. P3 is the right promise strategically but is the weakest to look at; the visual system should come from C2 plus C1, not from illustrating "skill that carries." Every showable proof depends on real imaging and real case access (claims and consent ⚑, O3).

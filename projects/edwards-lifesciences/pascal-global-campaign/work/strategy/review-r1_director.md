## The four risks the strategist raised

1. **C2 length.** Confirmed. It fails Simple. Fix above.
2. **"Hardest case to every case" re-anchors PASCAL to complex anatomy.** Partly confirmed. As provenance ("what you already proved") it is the strongest owned fact we have and Abbott cannot use it; as the opening of every story it reinforces the old belief. Rule for round 2: the hard case may appear as evidence, never as the subject or the first beat. The routine case is the hero.
3. **Weight on A2 (team sets the default).** Too much weight today for a pitch claim, acceptable for a strategy hypothesis. P3 and the rewritten CCI do not need A2 to be true; only the imager half of the creative target does. Keep the pair as the target, add the operator-only fallback, and put A2 at the top of the Phase 1 research plan.
4. **Departure from the proposal's "Confidence, Captured" lean.** Endorsed. Building the brand idea on Capture Clarity would stake a global, multi-year idea on a feature with LOW-confidence evidence (EP 2.5), unknown markets and dates (O3), and a narrowing device gap (EP 3.4). It survives as the launch episode, so nothing Andres liked is lost. Flag once to Andres as a divergence from the draft for his decision; do not relitigate after.

## RFP fit

- **Four belief shifts:** three clearly answered (first choice, carries across, leading via next-gen and US tricuspid). The fourth (straightforward to move) is answered only in the pillar table and has no proof. Must be closed in round 2.
- **25% strategic thinking:** strong. Explicit belief-shift mapping, owned-fact swap test, labeled assumptions with tests, and measurement tied to RFP §6. Weakest visible point for evaluators is the CCI sentence itself; fix it and this section leads the pitch.

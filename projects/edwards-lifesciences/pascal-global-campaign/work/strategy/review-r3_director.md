## One note for the brief (not a fail)

"Proved" is a strategy word. In copy, to an MLR reviewer, "proved/proven" next to a device name reads as a clinical claim. The CCI is strategy, not a line; WordSmither should not inherit "proved" as a line by default, the same way "further" was handled. Carry this as a writing rule in the brief, not as a change to the CCI.

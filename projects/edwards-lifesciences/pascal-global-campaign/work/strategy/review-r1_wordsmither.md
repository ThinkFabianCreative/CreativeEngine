## Talking down to experts

- C4 and the insight's word "habit" are the main risk. To an interventional cardiologist, "habit" can read as "you haven't thought about it." Prefer framings where the operator's own evidence does the persuading (P2/C2 logic).
- "Readable" (C1) and "straightforward to move" must never slide into "easy": implying a device is easier for them signals we think the procedure or their skill is simple.
- "Skill carries" can read as us grading their skill. Keep the subject the team's proof, not our assessment of their ability.
- No condescension found in P1, P2 or C2 as written.

## Recommendation to the gate

Verbally, the stopping power sits in the hard-case reversal (P2 inside P3, C2's first half). Keep P3/C2 strategically, but consider tightening C2 to its first movement and carrying both valves as proof. C1 is the best launch episode. Hold C4 as tone only.

## Slide 1 · Familiarity is earned through experience
- In a U.S. registry of 14,923 mitral TEER procedures, optimal procedural success rose from 63.9% in operators' first 25 cases to 75.1% after 50 cases.
- Improvements continued through approximately 200 cases.
- Strategic takeaway: Asking an operator to reconsider a familiar device also means addressing confidence in the team's workflow.
Source: Chhatriwalla et al., 2019 (pubmed 31568867). Historical MitraClip data, not a PASCAL learning-curve study or evidence of brand preference.

## Slide 2 · Confidence depends on what the team can see
- Mitral TEER requires close coordination between the operator and interventional echocardiographer.
- Imaging helps the team position the device, assess leaflet insertion and evaluate the result.
- Strategic takeaway: Clarity gives confidence a concrete meaning in the procedure.
Source: ASE intraprocedural imaging guidelines for mitral TEER.

## Slide 3 · Evidence creates a reason to reconsider
- At two years, 95.0% of evaluable PASCAL patients in CLASP IID had moderate-or-less mitral regurgitation.
- 77.2% had mild-or-less regurgitation.
- Strategic takeaway: Clinical evidence gives the heart team a foundation for considering PASCAL.
Source: CLASP IID, JACC CI 2025 (10.1016/j.jcin.2025.07.014). Prohibitive-surgical-risk DMR; 101 evaluable PASCAL patients. MR outcomes, not retention or superiority.

## Slide 4 · Make the connection visible
- Edwards announced FDA approval of PASCAL Ace with Capture Clarity for degenerative mitral TEER in October 2026.
- Fluoroscopic indicators provide confirmation when leaflets have been grasped.
- Creative opportunity: Connect a procedural feature to the human value of knowing what has been achieved.
Source: Edwards newsroom, 2026-10-21. Claims appropriate to approved device, indication and market.

## Slide 5 · Confidence in Every Connection
- Confidence: Trust supported by evidence and procedural confirmation.
- Clarity: "I can see and confirm what I'm doing."
- Precision: "I can position and execute with control."
- Creative platform: Know the Connection.
Note: behavioral takeaways are strategic interpretations to validate with physicians. Capture Clarity is not a guarantee of permanent attachment.

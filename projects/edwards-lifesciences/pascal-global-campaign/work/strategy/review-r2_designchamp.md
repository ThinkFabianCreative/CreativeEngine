## Can "further" carry a global visual system without bridge/path clichés?

**Yes, with one condition: "further" must never be drawn.** It is shown by repetition with change, never by direction. The system's constant is something real that stays the same (the PASCAL device's form, the team, the imaging read); the variable is a real context that is new (an everyday case, the other valve, a new site). The viewer infers "further" from sameness across difference, which is the mechanism WordSmither named (their own proof turned on themselves) expressed visually.

Banned for "further" in addition to the round-1 list: arrows, vectors and chevrons; roads, horizons, paths, stairs, bridges; motion blur and speed lines; zoom-out reveals; progress bars; "journey" maps; forward-tilted type or layouts that lean right. Any of these makes "further" mean "next-generation", the category language the red team flagged (EP 4).

Dependencies unchanged: real imaging, real teams, real case access (claims and consent ⚑, O3, O5; Bar ask in §6). No generated physicians, patients, procedures or Edwards product, even in comps.

## 1. Human / professional truth and audience insight

### Universal (professional) truth [S] — unchanged

**Under pressure, experts repeat what they can read.** Habit is how a team buys certainty. The tension: the more skilled the team, the more its skill is locked into one routine.

Grounding unchanged from v01 [F: RFP §1, §4; EP 3.5], now with verbatims on anatomy-led choice [F: EP 5.4a, b] and operator comfort [F: EP 5.4d, paraphrase, MEDIUM].

### The field norm: tension, not problem [S] (new, R1)

Physicians, including the CLASP IID lead, describe "different devices for different anatomies" as good practice [F: EP 5.4a, b, c]; Lim's paraphrase suggests some thicker or complicated leaflets may suit MitraClip [F: EP 5.4e, MEDIUM].

Decision: **this is the tension the campaign works inside, not a problem to argue against.** Read honestly, the norm says device choice should follow the anatomy. The habit we want to move is a different thing: that in the large middle of cases where both devices are suitable, the default goes to the device the operator has implanted most (EP 5.4d). "Right device for the anatomy" is a principle PASCAL can respect; "the same device by default" is a habit it can challenge.

Consequences, binding on everything below:
1. Nothing says or implies PASCAL is right for all anatomy. "Every case" is retired from the promise and CCI; "first choice" (RFP §3) is read as *first considered*, not *only*.
2. The campaign never argues with anatomy-led choice. It argues that where anatomy leaves the choice open, the team's PASCAL experience should count.
3. The hard case remains PASCAL's provenance [F: RFP §1] but is not claimed universally: some complex anatomy is contested (EP 5.4e). Use specific case types only with evidence ⚑.

### Audience insight (creative target) [S + A]

**Primary insight (PROPOSED, operator-led, holds without A2):** the occasional user has already proven PASCAL to themselves on demanding cases. Routine cases go to the device they have implanted most, because comfort is the rational choice when the anatomy leaves it open [F: EP 5.4d, paraphrase]. Their own PASCAL experience is the argument; it has simply not been allowed to travel.

In their register (PROPOSED synthesis, not VOC): PASCAL is what I reach for when it is hard; when it isn't, I go with what my hands know.

**Team extension (PROPOSED, depends on A2, Phase 1 hypothesis):** the default may also sit with the team (the imager's familiar read, lab prep, shelf). Public evidence leans the other way: the one explicit statement places the habit with operator comfort (EP 5.4d); the team appears in the US label for patient selection, not device choice (EP 5.1b). What keeps A2 alive: grasp confirmation depends on team coordination (EP 5.4i), and a rescue case credits the same operator and imager (EP 5.4j). Status: **untested hypothesis, presented in the pitch as what Phase 1 research tests first.** If it fails, the imager stays an influencer audience (S2), and nothing in the promise or CCI changes.

**Creative target (PROPOSED):** the operator who uses PASCAL some of the time [F: RFP §4]. The imager beside them is a co-target if A2 holds and a supporting audience if not. Primary market US and Europe interventional cardiologists [F: RFP §5].

**Deficit check.** Unchanged: comfort is framed as rational, never lazy; the operator's own experience does the persuading.

## 4. Core Creative Idea

### Recommended CCI (PROPOSED)

**Stories where a heart team takes its PASCAL experience further.**

One movement, team as subject, ends on the destination. "Experience" is theirs (WordSmither's mechanism: their own proof turned on themselves). "Further" hosts every chapter without naming complexity: from a demanding case to an everyday one (J1), from one valve to the other (J2), from a first tricuspid case to a program (J3), from one team member's read to the whole team's (S2), from one site's experience to a new site's, with Edwards support (belief shift 4).

### Variants generated (round 2)

| # | PROPOSED CCI | Note |
|---|---|---|

**Swap detail (V1).** Name-swapped, "Stories where a heart team takes its MitraClip experience further" is sayable by Abbott in shape. It fails on substance in two places: (a) for mitral, MitraClip experience is already the default [F: RFP §1], so "further" has nowhere to go; (b) for the other valve, Abbott's experience must change brand to TriClip [F: EP 1.1], whereas PASCAL stays one name [F: RFP]. G5's heart-team ease framing [F: EP 1.1, MEDIUM] sits at handling, not at experience travelling. A3 (Abbott transfer messaging) remains unfound (EP 5.3e); if it appears, (b) weakens and (a) carries the ownership. **Condition**: the episodes, not the sentence, carry the owned facts. Every episode must show PASCAL experience moving somewhere the habit does not already own. Honest status: this CCI is owned by context and proof, not by its wording alone.

**Hard-case rule (binding).** The hard case may appear as evidence a team already has, never as a story's subject or first beat. The routine case is the hero.

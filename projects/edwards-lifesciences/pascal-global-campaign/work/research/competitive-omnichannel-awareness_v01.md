- Face-to-face remains the largest stated HCP preference; email is rising and now split by sender, with rep-sent email expected to lead.

- Country variance in preference alignment is large inside Europe.
- Published device examples measure referral behaviour or patient routing rather than impressions.
- Public, quantified structural-heart HCP omnichannel cases are scarce; most benchmarks are pharma or vendor-sourced.

### Patterns observed (C)
- The stethoscope check recurs across UK, Canadian, global and US efforts as both symbol and call to action.
- Awareness is staged at live, everyday venues (football grounds, libraries, city centres) and anchored to fixed calendar moments (Feb 22; a September week).
- Coalitions of charities, societies and device-adjacent sponsors carry most visible awareness work; manufacturers appear mainly as sponsors or through data-targeting.
- Referrer-side material found is academic (undertreatment, referral rates) rather than campaign creative.
- Reported results are counts of checks or reach, not referral or treatment outcomes.

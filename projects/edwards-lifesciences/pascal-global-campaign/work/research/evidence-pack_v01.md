## Patterns (observational)

- Both brands' public language centres on procedure ease, control and generation numbers (G5, Precision, Ace).
- Head-to-head evidence is reported as comparable; the evidence-volume gap favours MitraClip.
- Independent clasping is the most consistently cited mechanical difference, and also tied to "challenging anatomy" framing.

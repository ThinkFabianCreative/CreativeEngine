## Patterns (observational)

- Named physicians in trade press frame device choice by anatomy (5.4a, b, e), not by team or habit.
- The one explicit statement on why operators default to MitraClip attributes it to operator comfort with implanting (5.4d, paraphrase).
- Imaging sources treat grasp confirmation as a known hard step that depends on team coordination (5.4i); Edwards frames Capture Clarity at the same step (5.2b, c).
- Capture Clarity is a US, DMR, PASCAL Ace story as reported; it is not yet evidenced as a tricuspid, European or Japanese story.

## Patterns that matter most for a PASCAL operator campaign (observations, not strategy)

1. **Operator codes cluster on control and confidence.** Abbott (control, precision, certainty), Edwards (grasp, accurate, Capture Clarity confirmation) and Valgen (readable indicators, accurate positioning) all talk about procedural control at the moment of grasp. 3 makers, primary pages.
2. **Scale and firsts are the claim currency.** Abbott: years and patient counts; Abbott and Edwards both use "first and only" constructions for tricuspid; Valgen counts countries. Every ⚑ number above is the owner's own.
3. **Congress presence is portfolio-wide and education-led.** Booths sell demos, hands-on training, KOL case discussion; registration lists for follow-up come with the booth. Abbott, Edwards (PCR sessions, v01), Valgen, TCT terms.
4. **Referral is fought through third parties.** Both Abbott and Edwards fund awareness alliances, referral meetings, CME and referral research rather than branded referrer ads. 6+ sources.
5. **Evidence of what works is reach, not outcomes.** Awareness and omnichannel sources report impressions, open rates and vendor-claimed lifts; none found ties a campaign to referrals or device adoption in structural heart.

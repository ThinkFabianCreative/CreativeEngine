Brand: PASCAL Precision (TEER, mitral and tricuspid), a product brand inside Edwards TMTT. Markets: global, led by US and Europe. Ask for Nov 13: 2–3 early creative territories, shown as equals. Italics = assumption or proposal. ⚑ = needs Edwards MLR approval.

## The problem
- Many TEER programs use PASCAL for hard anatomy only.
- The routine case goes to the device the operator has implanted most.
- Background only, never for copy: Abbott was first to market in mitral and US tricuspid. That is why MitraClip became the habit.
- This year's news (US tricuspid, Capture Clarity, next-gen, EU) reopens nearly every account.
- Edwards wants to use that news to change the default. A short launch flight won't do it.

## Brand platform
- **Brand promise (proposed):** *PASCAL exists so a heart team's best work goes further: into its everyday cases, across both valves.*
- **Core Creative Idea (proposed):** *Stories where what a heart team has proved with PASCAL becomes how it works.*
- **DECIDED (Andres, 2026-11-05):** every territory must be an episode of the Core Creative Idea.
- **Human truth (proposed):** *I want certainty under pressure. But the certainty I trust is the routine I already know.*
- **Positioning (Edwards house frame, proposed):** **To** interventional cardiologists who perform TEER, **who** use PASCAL some of the time but default elsewhere when the anatomy leaves the choice open, **PASCAL Precision is** the TEER system for mitral and tricuspid **that** lets their team's experience carry into everyday cases and across both valves, **because** one system, with independently operable clasps and staged leaflet capture, works on both valves in indicated patients ⚑, **so that** their best work becomes how their program works. *Check the US tricuspid indication against the FDA label.*
- **Umbrella:** TMTT line "Patient-matched & Advancing with every case." Never "every case" for PASCAL.

## The job to be done

### J1 · The occasional user (core)
- **Persuade:** TEER operators who already use PASCAL some of the time.
- **Behavior to change:** PASCAL on demanding cases; the familiar device for everything else.
- **Insight (proposed, not voice of customer):** *PASCAL is what I reach for when it's hard. When it isn't, I go with what my hands know.*
- **New belief:** our PASCAL experience counts in everyday cases, not only hard ones.
- **USP:** the team's own proof. *Pre-emptive on device facts. Owned through the team's history.*
- **Key takeout (a belief, not a line):** our own PASCAL experience is the reason to use it every day.
- **Proof:** independently operable clasps and staged leaflet capture ⚑. PASCAL Ace's narrower paddles ⚑. CLASP IID, a randomized noninferiority trial in degenerative mitral patients at prohibitive surgical risk ⚑ (no figures until Edwards' wording). US registry (~2,000 patients), no major learning curve ⚑.
- **Visual proof (Edwards access ask):** *the program's own PASCAL case history, fluoroscopy and echo of its own grasps, and the real operators and imagers.* Consent and claims ⚑. *If access is refused: show the team's process and real people, never generated stand-ins for its proof.*
- **Barrier:** Aspirational, through operator comfort. *Whether the default also sits with the imager and the team is a Phase 1 hypothesis.*

### J2 · The one-valve program
- **Persuade:** programs using PASCAL on one valve.
- **Behavior to change:** they treat the second valve as a fresh start, with a new device and a new learning curve.
- **Insight (proposed, not voice of customer):** *On the valve we know, we're the team others call. On the other valve, we'd be beginners again in our own lab.*
- **New belief:** on the second valve we start as the team we already are.
- **USP:** *Parity in the US, where the comparator also spans both valves. Pre-emptive in CE markets, on tricuspid experience since 2020 ⚑. Ownership comes from the team's history (J1).* The idea must carry the distinctiveness here.
- **Key takeout (a belief, not a line):** the standing we earned on one valve comes with us.
- **Proof:** US: FDA approval Oct 21, 2026 for T-TEER, symptomatic severe TR despite optimal medical therapy, intermediate or greater surgical risk, judged suitable by a multidisciplinary heart team ⚑. CLASP IITR one-year: 80% moderate-or-less TR vs 30% with medical therapy alone, +18 KCCQ ⚑ (Edwards-approved wording only). EU: CE Mark for TR since October 2020 ⚑.
- **Barrier:** Credential, with a status cost. Lead with proof. Respect the standing.

## By stage: what each chapter must make the team believe
- **US tricuspid:** we can begin tricuspid TEER with the PASCAL experience we have. US indication only.
- **Capture Clarity:** the device itself now indicates when a leaflet has been captured ⚑. **US only, degenerative mitral, PASCAL Ace only.** Never on tricuspid, "both valves" or non-US assets.
- **Next-generation system:** PASCAL will keep pace with where our work goes. **Investigational, not approved; no performance claims ⚑.** Show the team moving forward; never show the new device in action.
- **EU news:** there is something new to act on now: the second valve under CE, shown through real European teams.

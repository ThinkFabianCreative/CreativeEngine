## 2B: fix before the file goes out on Nov 10

1. **CLASP IID numbers don't match.** The hub says "77% vs 71% at one year." Our evidence pack has 83.7% vs 71.2% at six months. The line also names MitraClip, which breaks the hub's own rule against naming Abbott. Pull the exact Edwards-approved wording, or drop the line.
2. **"I know it holds. On every case, on both valves" promises more than the label allows.** Capture Clarity is FDA-approved on PASCAL Ace for degenerative mitral only. "Both valves" and "every case" attach a confirmation story to tricuspid and to anatomy the indication doesn't cover. Keep confirmation as the mitral chapter, or reword the takeout so it doesn't depend on Capture Clarity.
3. **The "Every Case" territory collides with the umbrella line.** The TMTT portfolio line is "Patient-matched & Advancing with every case." A PASCAL territory called "Every Case" either echoes the umbrella or competes with it. Its line "From the hardest case to every case" also opens on the hard case, which brings back the complex-anatomy label we're trying to move past.
4. **"Nobody owns the moment of capture" isn't quite true any more.**
   - Abbott's TCT 2026 line is "Complexity is inevitable. Certainty is essential." That moves into certainty.
   - Valgen DragonFly claims "readable indicators" in Europe.
   - The white space is narrower than the hub says: it's what the team sees on screen, not certainty as a feeling.
5. **The fourth belief shift has no proof.** "Edwards makes moving to PASCAL straightforward" has no proof point in 2B. It needs Edwards' switch support: proctoring, case support, training. The portfolio brochure's "100% case support" is a lead, but it needs checking.
6. **Abbott's patient count differs between sections.** 5D says 250K+ and 5A/5B say 3,000+ registry patients. Abbott's TCT 2026 page says 300,000+ patients and "20+ years." Use one number, and say it's Abbott's figure.
7. **Missing mandatories:**
   - Capture Clarity's indication limits (US, degenerative mitral, PASCAL Ace).
   - The rule against putting "first" in headlines outside the US.
   - Heart-failure doctors in the heart team (05C says they're required by the coverage rule).
   - The rep and non-personal versions.
8. **A positioning statement may be expected.** Edwards' house frame is To / Who / Is / That / Because / So that. The brief has none, and the panel may look for it in that format.
9. **The brief carries the answer.** 2B includes a takeout line and three territories. That's fine for a pitch hub, but the version that goes to the creative team should hold the strategy and leave the lines to the team.

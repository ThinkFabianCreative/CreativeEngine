## The four questions

1. **Strategy and USP sharpness.** J1 is sharp and ownable. J2 should be honest parity in the US (Abbott also spans both valves), with ownership carried by J1's team history. It is pre-emptive at most in CE markets, where PASCAL has had tricuspid since 2020.
2. **Giving the answer away.** No headlines and no territories, apart from the By stage questions and the "Test" line, which work as headline drafts. The CCI is a strategic asset, not a territory, so it stays.
3. **Claims safety.** Capture Clarity limits are correctly stated (US, degenerative mitral, PASCAL Ace only). The issues are listed in fixes 5–8.
4. **Length.** Cuts are named in fix 1.

   - b. Problem: trim the Abbott background paragraph to one clause, "Background only: Abbott was first to market" (about 20 words saved).
   - c. J1 Proof: move "Contrary data for MLR" and the CLASP IID figure conflict to Open items as one line each ("CLASP IID: no figures until Edwards wording; conflict logged in evidence pack v02"). About 60 words saved.
   - d. Open items: keep one line each. Move O5's detail (brochure "100% case support" lead, inventory list) to the loop log. About 50 words saved.
   - e. Success: keep the three measure names and drop the parentheticals (about 25 words).
   - f. Sources footer: replace the list with "Sources: see brief-loop-log.md" (about 40 words).
   - g. Heart team: two sentences maximum (about 20 words).
2. **J2 insight.** Replace the RFP characterization with a labeled operator-voice proposal that carries tension, for example the cost of restarting expertise, without writing copy. Mark it *proposed, not voice of customer*.
3. **J2 USP.** Delete "the comparator changes brand name between valves." Label J2 honestly: *Parity in the US; pre-emptive in CE markets on years of tricuspid experience ⚑. Ownership comes from the team's own PASCAL history (J1), not the device.*
4. **By stage.** Rewrite each chapter as the audience's question in plain, flat prose, or as "what this chapter must make them believe", not as a finished line. Remove "Can the whole team see the grasp?" outright (see fix 5). Recast the "Test" line as a judging criterion: "An occasional user recognizes their own program."
5. **Capture Clarity wording.** The feature indicates when a leaflet has been captured (RFP). "The whole team see the grasp" implies an imaging or team-visibility claim not in the indication. State the function in Edwards' words, add ⚑, and keep the US / degenerative mitral / PASCAL Ace limit attached.
6. **"First" mandatory.** Rewrite as: "PASCAL makes no 'first to market' claims. Any 'first' language needs Edwards MLR and is US-scoped only if approved ⚑." Check this against the claims rules before the file goes out.
7. **Positioning "because" line.** Add "in indicated patients" next to "works on both valves" and note the US tricuspid label is still to be checked against the FDA label, not only Edwards wording (strategic assets O1, E12).
8. **Next-generation chapter.** Add "investigational / not approved; no performance claims" ⚑. "Forward-looking, framed by MLR" is too soft for a device not yet cleared.
9. **Success.** Add one line: "Marketing supports, not causes, clinical adoption" (claims rules), so share-of-TEER measures are not read as campaign-caused outcomes.
10. **Tone "Never" list.** Keep the vocabulary bans ("every case", "easy", all-anatomy, stock words as claims, hard case as the opening beat). Move the "arrows, roads, bridges" imagery ban to a DesignChamp note, or keep it as one clause, so the brief does not art-direct.

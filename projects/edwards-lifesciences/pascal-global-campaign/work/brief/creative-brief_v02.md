## The problem
In many TEER programs the routine case goes to the device the operator has implanted most, and PASCAL is kept for the hard anatomy where it earned its name. This year's news (US tricuspid, Capture Clarity, next-gen, EU) reopens nearly every account, and Edwards wants to use it to change the default, not run a launch flight.

Background only: Abbott was first to market.

## Brand platform
- **Brand promise (proposed):** *PASCAL exists so a heart team's best work goes further: into its everyday cases, across both valves.*
- **Core Creative Idea (proposed):** *Stories where what a heart team has proved with PASCAL becomes how it works.* At least one territory is an episode of it; others may test a different reading of the promise.
- **Human truth (proposed):** *I want certainty under pressure, but the certainty I trust is the routine I already know.*
- **Positioning (Edwards house frame, proposed):** **To** interventional cardiologists who perform TEER, **who** already use PASCAL some of the time but default elsewhere when the anatomy leaves the choice open, **PASCAL Precision is** the TEER system for mitral and tricuspid **that** lets the experience their team has built carry into everyday cases and across both valves, **because** one system, with independently operable clasps and staged leaflet capture, works on both valves in indicated patients ⚑, **so that** their best work becomes how their program works. *US tricuspid indication to be checked against the FDA label, not only Edwards' wording.*
- **Umbrella:** it sits under the TMTT portfolio line "Patient-matched & Advancing with every case." Never "every case" for PASCAL.

## The job to be done

### J1 · The occasional user (core)
- **Persuade:** TEER operators in programs that already use PASCAL some of the time to make it their routine choice where the anatomy leaves the choice open.
- **Behavior to change:** they reach for PASCAL on demanding cases and go back to the device they have implanted most for everything else.
- **Insight (proposed, not voice of customer):** *PASCAL is what I reach for when it's hard. When it isn't, I go with what my hands know.*
- **New belief:** my team's PASCAL experience counts in our everyday cases, not only the hard ones.
- **USP:** the team's own proof. *Pre-emptive on the device facts; owned through the team's history.*
- **Key takeout (a belief, not a line):** our own PASCAL experience is the reason to use it on everyday cases.
- **Proof:** independently operable clasps and staged leaflet capture ⚑; PASCAL Ace's narrower paddles ⚑; CLASP IID, a randomized noninferiority trial in degenerative mitral patients at prohibitive surgical risk ⚑ (no figures until Edwards' wording); US registry (~2,000 patients), no major learning curve ⚑.
- **Visual proof the team owns (Edwards access ask):** *the program's own PASCAL case history, fluoroscopy and echo of its own grasps, and the real operators and imagers who did them.* Consent and claims ⚑.
- **Barrier:** Aspirational, through operator comfort. *Whether the default also sits with the imager and the team is a Phase 1 hypothesis.*

### J2 · The one-valve program
- **Persuade:** programs using PASCAL on one valve to treat the other valve with PASCAL.
- **Behavior to change:** they treat the second valve as a fresh start, with a new device and a new learning curve.
- **Insight (proposed, not voice of customer):** *On the valve we know, we're the team others call. Starting the other valve, we'd be beginners again, and nobody wants to be the beginner in their own lab.*
- **New belief:** on the second valve we start as the team we already are.
- **USP:** *Parity in the US, where the comparator also spans both valves. Pre-emptive in CE markets, on tricuspid experience since 2020 ⚑. Ownership comes from the team's own PASCAL history (J1), not the device.* The idea has to carry the distinctiveness here.
- **Key takeout (a belief, not a line):** the standing we earned on one valve comes with us to the other.
- **Proof:** US: FDA approval Oct 21, 2026 for T-TEER, symptomatic severe TR despite optimal medical therapy, intermediate or greater surgical risk, judged suitable by a multidisciplinary heart team ⚑. CLASP IITR one-year: 80% moderate-or-less TR vs 30% with medical therapy alone, +18 KCCQ ⚑ (Edwards-approved wording only). EU: CE Mark for TR since October 2020 ⚑.
- **Barrier:** Credential, with a status cost. Lead with proof, and respect the standing.

## By stage: the chapters the campaign has to carry
- **US tricuspid:** the team believes it can begin tricuspid TEER with the PASCAL experience it already has. US indication only.
- **Capture Clarity:** the team believes the device itself now indicates when a leaflet has been captured ⚑. **US only, degenerative mitral, PASCAL Ace only.** Never on a tricuspid, "both valves" or non-US asset.
- **Next-generation system:** the team believes PASCAL will keep pace with where its work goes. **Investigational, not approved; no performance claims ⚑.** The team moves, never the device.
- **EU news:** a program already on both valves believes there is something new to act on now: the second valve under CE, shown through real European teams.

## Tone
- **We are:** peer-level, exact, quietly confident, classy and futuristic without sci-fi.
- **Never:** "complex anatomy device" as our description; "every case"; "easy" about the procedure or their skill; anything implying PASCAL suits all anatomy; the category's stock words used as benefit claims. The product name PASCAL Precision stays; "precision" is never a benefit claim. *Note for DesignChamp: avoid arrow, road and bridge metaphors for progress.*

## 1. Objective (RFP)

Make PASCAL "the brand operators think of first for TEER" across mitral and tricuspid, today and moving forward (§Purpose, §1). Build one lasting global campaign that can grow and evolve over several years (§Purpose). Success = "PASCAL becomes the default, not just the alternative" (§6).

## 2. Challenge (RFP §1)

- PASCAL has momentum in TEER programs worldwide, but Abbott is the more recognized brand.
- Abbott was first to market in mitral and, in the US, in tricuspid; MitraClip became "the habit" in many programs. Habits "rarely shift on features alone."
- PASCAL has moved beyond its "device for complex cases" reputation, though that perception persists in some accounts.

TRAFFIK-DRAFT reading (not client fact): the competitor is a habit, not a device; defaults are set by heart teams, not a single operator.

## 3. Opportunity: news chapters, next 12 months (RFP §2)

| # | News | RFP wording (abridged) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | US tricuspid indication | Opens tricuspid TEER with PASCAL to US programs |
| 2 | PASCAL with Capture Clarity | New feature that indicates when a leaflet has been captured |
| 3 | Next-generation system | One next-gen system for both mitral and tricuspid |
| 4 | Fresh news for established markets | New device and feature news where PASCAL is already used on both valves |

Client intent: not "a short launch flight" but a reset of how operators think about PASCAL for years, staying relevant as product news, evidence and indications evolve (§2). "Chapters, not flights" framing is TRAFFIK-DRAFT. Status, dates and markets of each chapter: see MISSING.

## 4. Belief shifts (RFP §3, verbatim)

| What many operators believe today | What we want them to believe |
|---|---|
| Abbott is the established name in TEER. | PASCAL is leading where TEER is going. |
| PASCAL is the device for complex anatomy. | PASCAL is my first choice for TEER. |
| Treating the other valve means a new learning curve. | My PASCAL experience carries across mitral and tricuspid. |
| Changing my default costs my team time and effort. | Edwards makes moving to PASCAL straightforward. |

RFP: "To drive this shift, PASCAL needs a refreshed campaign idea and visual system." The draft's "what has to be true to switch" column is TRAFFIK-DRAFT.

## 5. Audiences

**RFP (§4, §5):**
- Core: TEER operators (interventional cardiologists) and the heart teams around them, including imagers.
- Biggest opportunity: programs already using PASCAL some of the time; move operators from occasional to routine use.
- Secondary: select programs new to PASCAL.
- Messaging focus: interventional cardiologists in the US and Europe, "with archetypes to be defined."
- Edwards will share segmentation and account data with the selected partner (not available now).

**TRAFFIK-DRAFT working archetypes (hypotheses, to be defined with Edwards segmentation):** The Occasional User (core), The Loyalist (selective pursuit), The Tricuspid Builder (US tricuspid window), The One-Valve Program (second-valve adoption), The Imager (heart-team influence).

**TRAFFIK-DRAFT hypotheses to pressure-test:** (1) a default is a team decision; (2) confidence is earned at the moment of capture; (3) the complex-anatomy label is "a compliment in a cage"; (4) the second valve is the easiest new habit.

## 6. Deliverables (RFP §5)

Ask: one lasting campaign idea; a messaging framework with tailored messaging per audience segment; a launch toolkit for the regions.

| Workstream | Example deliverables (RFP) |
|---|---|
| Strategy and messaging | Campaign strategy; messaging framework; key messages per segment (US and Europe ICs, archetypes TBD) |
| Campaign creative | Campaign idea and visual system; key visuals; style guide |
| Activation | Campaign activation playbook |

Final deliverables confirmed with the selected partner. Proposal must show priorities within budget.

**Proposal deliverable (RFP §9):** PowerPoint; no slide limit, "clarity over volume." Must include: agency overview + 1–2 relevant case studies with results; read on the challenge and what to explore further; approach and phased plan with timing; team and capabilities; budget; conflicts (TEER or mitral/tricuspid device work, past 24 months). Early creative thinking welcome, not required.

The proposal must also address how we would approach (§5): understanding customers and pressure-testing Edwards' thinking; a distinctive, ownable idea; messaging framework and segment messaging; a global campaign across regions and channels; keeping it fresh as evidence, indications and devices evolve; measuring success.

TRAFFIK-DRAFT shape (example, not decided): three territories, each shown as a working campaign (line, chapter roll-out, congress banner, field detail aid, HCP email); a territory comparison and an early lean. Its territory names and lines are TRAFFIK proposals and carry no claim approval.

## 8. Measures of success (RFP §6)

- PASCAL share of TEER inside priority accounts, by valve and region
- Operators moving from occasional to routine PASCAL use
- Second-valve adoption among accounts using PASCAL on one valve
- How operators describe PASCAL: a first choice for TEER, not only a device for complex anatomy

Agency to propose how to measure progress; final targets set with the partner. No baselines provided.

## 12. Mandatories and regulatory

**RFP:** PowerPoint format; conflicts disclosure; fees separate from third-party costs; transparency on partnered capabilities ("Partnering is not a disadvantage"); experience with medical, legal and regulatory review (§8). In-market timing "subject to approvals."

**CLIENT-KB (claims-and-evidence-rules.md):** never invent clinical outcomes, approval status, indications, product claims, market share or physician quotes. Before anything Edwards-facing verify product, anatomy (mitral vs tricuspid), repair vs replacement, market, indication, approval status, device generation, evidence source. Approval, launch and investigational status are different facts. "HCPs" is never one audience. PASCAL tricuspid repair is available in some markets; check local indication (portfolio-and-clinical-language.md). PASCAL = repair (TEER); EVOQUE and SAPIEN M3 = replacement; keep distinct.

**Pitch-stage note (standing preference):** at territory stage, creative is written for the idea; claims are called out for the client. Any claim-like line (e.g. Capture Clarity benefits, "both valves", "first choice") still carries a review flag in the deck.

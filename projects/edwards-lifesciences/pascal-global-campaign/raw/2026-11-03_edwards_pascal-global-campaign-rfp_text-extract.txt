Edwards Lifesciences | Transcatheter Mitral and Tricuspid Therapies (TMTT) 
About Edwards TMTT 
Patients with mitral and tricuspid heart valve disease often have complex conditions and limited treatment 
options. Edwards Lifesciences Transcatheter Mitral and Tricuspid Therapies (TMTT) offers repair and 
replacement solutions for these patients, with three therapies now commercially available: 
• PASCAL Precision: Transcatheter Edge-to-Edge Repair (TEER) for mitral and tricuspid 
• EVOQUE: Transcatheter Tricuspid Valve Replacement (TTVR) 
• SAPIEN M3: Transcatheter Mitral Valve Replacement (TMVR) 
Purpose 
Edwards is looking for an agency partner to build a new global campaign for PASCAL with us, one that 
can grow and evolve over several years. PASCAL has built real momentum, and our innovation and 
pipeline set it up to lead TEER into the future. Our ambition is to make PASCAL the brand operators think 
of first for TEER across mitral and tricuspid, today and moving forward. 
This RFP shares the challenge, the opportunity and what we know about our customers. We want to 
understand how you think, how you would approach building this campaign with us, who would do the 
work and what you can deliver within the budget.

1. The challenge 
PASCAL has built real momentum in TEER programs around the world, but Abbott is still the 
more recognized brand.  
Abbott was first to market in mitral and, in the US, in tricuspid. That head start made MitraClip the habit in 
many programs, and habits rarely shift on features alone.  
PASCAL has moved well beyond its reputation as the device for complex cases, although that perception 
still holds in some accounts. Now we want to build on that momentum, our innovation and our pipeline to 
lead the category.  
The challenge: make PASCAL the brand operators think of first for TEER.

2. The opportunity 
The next 12 months give us a reason to reopen the conversation in nearly every TEER account. 
• US tricuspid indication: opens tricuspid TEER with PASCAL to US programs 
• PASCAL with Capture Clarity: a new feature that indicates when a leaflet has been captured 
• Next-generation system: one next-gen system for both mitral and tricuspid 
• Fresh news for established markets: new device and feature news for markets already using 
PASCAL on both valves 
We could spend this moment on a short launch flight. We would rather use it to reset how operators think 
about PASCAL for years to come, with a campaign that stays relevant as product news, evidence and 
indications evolve. 
3. What needs to change 
PASCAL's strength today is only part of the story. With Capture Clarity, a next-generation system for 
mitral and tricuspid, and expanding indications, PASCAL is shaping the future of TEER. We want 
operators to see it that way too.

4. Who we need to reach 
Core audience: TEER operators (interventional cardiologists) and the heart teams around them, 
including imagers. 
Our biggest opportunity is inside programs that already use PASCAL some of the time, helping operators 
move from occasional to routine use. We will also pursue select programs that are new to PASCAL. We 
will share our segmentation work and account data with the selected partner. 
5. What we’re asking for 
We are looking for a partner to build this global campaign with us: one lasting campaign idea, a 
messaging framework with tailored messaging for each audience segment and a launch toolkit to deliver 
to the regions. 
In your proposal, tell us how you would approach: 
• Understanding our customers and pressure-testing our thinking 
• Developing a distinctive, ownable campaign idea 
• Building a messaging framework from PASCAL’s strengths and developing the messaging for each 
audience segment 
• Creating a global campaign that works across regions and channels 
• Keeping the campaign fresh as evidence, indications and devices evolve

6. How we’ll measure success 
Success means PASCAL becomes the default, not just the alternative. Over time, we will look at: 
• PASCAL share of TEER inside priority accounts, by valve and region 
• Operators moving from occasional to routine PASCAL use  
• Second-valve adoption among accounts using PASCAL on one valve  
• How operators describe PASCAL: a first choice for TEER, not only a device for complex anatomy 
Share how you would measure progress. Final targets will be set with the selected partner.

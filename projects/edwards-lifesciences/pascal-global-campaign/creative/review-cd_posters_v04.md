## 2. Judgement

### 1 · The Grasp + "One grasp. Two valves." → **ADVANCE** (lead)
- **Ward poster (PREDICTION):** passes. One white object on red, at a scale no one in the category uses. Read in passing: "PASCAL, holding on, both valves." The risk is a viewer counting leaflets (two drawn edges against "two valves"). Fix: the leaflet edges stay quiet, the line stays large.
- **Against Abbott:** as clear as Abbott and less generic. Abbott shows a device; this shows a device doing its job. That is the difference, and it holds only if the leaflets are visible from across the corridor.
- **Against "Not How I Go Out":** plain enough. No metaphor, no pun, four words.
- **Fix before roughing:** the crop has to be bigger than Edwards' existing implant-on-red visual (paddles off the edges), or it reads as a refresh of their own ad.

### 2 · Open, for Both + "Mitral. / Tricuspid." → **REWORK**
- **Ward poster (PREDICTION):** passes on words, at risk on image. The two words read at once. The open V with visible barbs may read as a mouth or a claw from across a corridor, and families pass this wall too.
- **Against Abbott:** the closest of the three to Abbott in kind (device large, simple field). The rule and the two words are what separate it, so they have to carry the whole idea.
- **Against "Not How I Go Out":** the plainest of the three. Its strength is also its ceiling: it is a label, and it does not reach the grasp moment the award winners name.
- **Fix:** rotate to 3/4 and grade the barbs down; rough a version where the rule visibly passes *through* the device so the object is the hinge between the two words. If it still reads as Abbott with fewer words, it drops to a chapter system, not a lead.

### 3 · The Imager's Frame + "The grasp your imager knows." → **KILL as a poster direction** (keep the line and the imager insight for social and digital)
- **Ward poster (PREDICTION):** fails. A dark grey echo silhouette is static from ten metres; nurses and passersby see a dark screen. This is the round 3 decoding problem again.
- **Against Abbott:** less clear than Abbott, the reverse of the round 4 brief.
- **Against "Not How I Go Out":** technical, not plain.
- It also depends on an echo still Edwards may not give us.

## 3. Are the three genuinely different?
Not fully. 1 and 3 collapse: both are the closed implant, both are about the grasp, and 3 is a darker, harder-to-read version of 1. The line builds the grasp, and so does the image. Keep 1, drop 3.

**Replacement · 3 · IN HAND** (for DesignChamp to rough; WordSmither to beat the line)
- **Frame:** the implant at true scale, held between a gloved thumb and forefinger, on warm light grey, filling the centre third. Monumental by crop, not by size. No face, no operating room, no case.
- **Line:** **The grasp you already know.** (low risk, builds on "Grasp the difference" through the team's own history, J1 in the brief).
- **What it owns:** human scale and the team's familiarity. The other two are object posters; this one is about the people who use it (Getinge "Heart Surgeon's Cookbook" honours the hands; ICU Medical honours the team's calm). Distinct in look (light, human) and in job (habit, not news).
- **Ward poster (PREDICTION):** passes, provided the device stays bigger in the frame than the fingers. Patient check: a gloved hand holding a small object reads calm, not clinical.
- **Watch:** stock-like "hand holds product" look. It works only if the crop is extreme and the grade is Edwards-specific. Not novice casting: the hand is assured, not tentative.

The three are now **the act** (The Grasp), **the reach across both valves** (Open, for Both) and **the people** (In Hand).

## 4. Campaign line
**Whichever valve. PASCAL.** is the campaign line across all three directions and all four chapters. It is the positioning with no claim word, it works globally, and it sits under each poster's own headline without fighting it. Each direction keeps its own headline (above); the campaign line is the sign-off above the logo.

- **Lead:** The Grasp. It names the professional moment, builds Edwards' equity and passes the corridor test most clearly.
- **TMTT umbrella:** The Grasp + "Whichever valve. PASCAL." carries it. The red field and monumental implant become the system; chapters swap the headline and what the implant holds (three leaflet edges for US tricuspid; Ace with an indicator mark for Capture Clarity; outline only for next-gen).
- "First choice. Either valve." is held back. Show it only if Edwards MLR signals that "first choice" is defensible in the US.

## 5. Rules and MLR
- **"First":** none in any lead headline or the campaign line. "Grasped. First choice." retired. Any "first" option shown to Edwards is preference-only, US-scoped and marked ⚑⚑. Never time or market order (Abbott was first to market: background only).
- **Indication by market:** "Two valves", "Mitral. / Tricuspid." and "Whichever valve" all depend on each market's label ⚑. The US tricuspid chapter carries US label wording. Where tricuspid is not indicated, the both-valve posters do not run; the market gets a mitral-only variant.
- **Capture Clarity:** US, degenerative mitral, PASCAL Ace only. Never on The Grasp's "two valves" frame, never on Open, for Both, never outside the US. Its chapter runs a mitral-only headline ("Grasped. And it tells you." or WordSmither's "Now it tells you it's grasped.") with the campaign line removed, because "Whichever valve" breaks the indication.
- **Abbott look-alike (device on a field):** highest in Open, for Both. Guardrails for all three: flat Edwards red or warm grey, never a gradient; no magenta or blue; never a single claim paragraph; the device always shows a state (held, ready, in hand), never just a device.
- **"Grasp the difference":** Edwards' line, live since 2023. We build on it, we do not replace or parody it. "One grasp. Two valves." and "The grasp you already know." extend it; neither repeats it. Recommend presenting it as Edwards' brand line kept in place, with ours as the campaign line under it. Confirm with Edwards that they still want it live before any lockup puts the two together ⚑.
- **Imagery:** Pascal.png and any closed-pose render are Edwards CAD for pitch use. Drawn leaflet edges are diagrams, not anatomy, and must not imply a clinical result.

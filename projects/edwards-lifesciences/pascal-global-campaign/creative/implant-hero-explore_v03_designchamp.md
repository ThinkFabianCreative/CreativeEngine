# PASCAL · Implant-hero exploration v03 · DesignChamp

CLIENT: Edwards Lifesciences · PROJECT: PASCAL Global Campaign · SOURCE: work/brief/creative-brief_v03.md + pitch-team hub notes (via creative-engine-director) · MODE: EXPLORE (no verdicts)

Fixed for every idea: the implant is the image, comped from Edwards' own product photography of today's PASCAL / PASCAL Ace. Edwards red as the only accent, dark grey ground or warm neutral, never black-with-glow. No hand holding the device up, no blueprint lines, no arrows, roads or bridges. Every idea is an episode of the CCI: what the team has proved with PASCAL becomes how it works.

Frameworks: Pricken mechanisms (omission, fusion, context shift, repetition, analogy, literal language, reversal); Barry concept sentence + cold read (headline covered).

★ = my top 5.

## A. The moment of capture and the imager (8 of 20)

**1 ★ Two screens, one shape.** Two echo monitors side by side, one mitral, one tricuspid; on both, the same crisp PASCAL silhouette at the moment of grasp, while every other structure differs.
Mechanism: repetition/analogy. Cold read: "Different valve, same thing on my screen. I already know how this looks." Valves: both, by construction.

**2 ★ The preset.** The imager's echo console, macro: a row of hardware preset keys (MV, TV, LAA...) and the first key is replaced by a small, real PASCAL implant seated in the key well.
Mechanism: fusion/context shift. Cold read: "It's the first thing the team reaches for, before thinking." Valves: both (key row carries MV and TV).

**3. Freeze.** The imager's finger on the FREEZE key; on screen, the implant held at the instant the clasp closes. Nothing else in focus.
Mechanism: literal language (freeze the moment you'd want to keep). Cold read: "That's the frame I want to see every time." Valves: flexes, swap the frozen view.

**4. Caliper marks.** Product shot of the implant overlaid only with the imager's yellow measurement calipers and a typed annotation, as if measured on echo.
Mechanism: context shift (brand photo read as clinical image). Cold read: "We know this device to the millimetre." Valves: flex by annotation (MV / TV).

**5. Biplane.** The same implant shown in the two orthogonal panes of a biplane display; pane labels read MITRAL and TRICUSPID instead of view angles.
Mechanism: analogy. Cold read: "One device, two views of my practice." Valves: both.

**6. The imager's note.** Echo still of a grasp, with a grease-pencil scrawl in the corner: "Got it." The implant is the only sharp object.
Mechanism: omission (no claim, just the team's word). Cold read: "My imager and I agree on this one." Valves: flex.

**7. Capture Clarity chapter (US, DMR, PASCAL Ace only).** The Ace implant in close-up at closure; the lab's room light behind it shifts from neutral to Edwards red as the only signal.
Mechanism: reversal (the device tells you, not the screen). Cold read: "It lets me know." Valves: mitral only. Chapter, not spine.

**8. Shared glance.** Over-the-shoulder of operator and imager looking at one monitor; the monitor fills the frame and shows only the implant. Faces cropped out.
Mechanism: omission. Cold read: "We see the same thing at the same moment." Valves: flex.

## B. The default, the habit, the team's proof

**9 ★ The clip.** The cath lab's daily case board: a stack of case sheets (mixed MV and TV TEER) held together by a real PASCAL implant used as the clip.
Mechanism: fusion/context shift. Cold read: "It's what holds our day together, both valves." Valves: both.

**10 ★ Case history.** A grid of 40 identical implant photographs, each captioned with a date and a valve; mostly MV, then TV starts appearing.
Mechanism: repetition. Cold read: "Our record already made the choice." Valves: both. CCI in its purest form; real program data if Edwards grants access.

**11 ★ Two shadows.** The implant lit from two sides on warm neutral: one shadow reads as M, the other as T.
Mechanism: fusion. Cold read: "One device carries both valves." Valves: both. Watch for gimmick in DEVELOP.

**12. Slot one.** Overhead of a sterile TEER back-table, every instrument in its place; the implant sits in position 1, top left, where the eye starts.
Mechanism: literal language (first choice = first position). Cold read: "It's laid out first." Valves: both.

**13. Actual size.** The implant printed 1:1 on a full page of white, a single small ruler at the edge.
Mechanism: exaggeration by understatement. Cold read: "Everything we've learned fits in this." Valves: both.

**14. The mirror.** The implant on a mirrored surface; the reflection carries a small tricuspid label, the object a mitral one.
Mechanism: analogy. Cold read: "The second valve is the same device seen from the other side." Valves: both.

**15. Signed.** The implant photographed resting on the lab's procedure log, the signature line under it filled by many operators' initials.
Mechanism: context shift. Cold read: "The whole team has put its name to it." Valves: flex.

**16. Muscle memory.** Long-exposure photo: the implant sharp at centre, a faint repeated ghost trail of the same implant behind it, like a motion practised hundreds of times.
Mechanism: repetition. Cold read: "My hands already know it." Valves: both. Avoid it reading as a path.

**17. The keyring.** The implant hangs as the only fob on the lab's key ring, beside the room key.
Mechanism: context shift. Cold read: "It belongs to how we run this room." Valves: both. Risk: trivializing; keep the shot clinical.

**18. Scrub label.** The implant pinned through a scrub-cap name tag where the team writes its first choice; label reads "PASCAL" in a nurse's handwriting.
Mechanism: fusion. Cold read: "It's part of who we are in the lab." Valves: both.

**19. Next chapter (next-gen, no performance shown).** Today's PASCAL in the foreground, sharp; behind it a closed, labelled Edwards shipping case, unopened.
Mechanism: omission/imply-don't-reveal. Cold read: "What's coming builds on what we use now." Valves: both. No claims.

**20. The quiet one.** A single implant on dark grey, perfectly lit, with the case board's whiteboard handwriting behind it out of focus: today's list, MV, TV, MV.
Mechanism: context shift. Cold read: "Nothing special: just our everyday." Valves: both.

### Killed in my own pass (clever, decodes)
- "First in view. First in mind." (round 3): two firsts to parse, and "in view" needs the echo picture to make sense. Corridor fail.
- "First, grasp this." (round 3): reads as a gag on the brand line, not a building step.
- "Your record already chose.": needs a grid to decode; poster has one implant.
- "Hold everything." / "Got it?" / "Valve-agnostic.": puns or jargon; "got it" also flirts with ease.
- "Grasp the difference, twice.": decoding plus implies comparison.

### Rules check
No "every case", "easy", complex-anatomy framing, precision as benefit, competitor name, hard case, or novice casting. "First" only as preference (choice, reach), never time or market. Tricuspid lines follow each market's label; Capture Clarity is never paired with both-valve lines.

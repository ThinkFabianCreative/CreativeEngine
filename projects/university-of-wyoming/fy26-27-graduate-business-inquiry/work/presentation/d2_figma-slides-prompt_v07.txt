DESIGN SYSTEM (apply to every slide)
Canvas: 1920 x 1080.
Grid: 12 columns, 120 px outer margins, 24 px gutters. Everything aligns to the grid, flush left.
Colors: UW Brown #492F24 (default background), White #FFFFFF (type; background on slide 9 only),
  UW Gold #FFC425 (one emphasis per slide, never large areas), Black #000000 (not used unless stated).
Type: Montserrat only.
  DISPLAY  160 px Bold, line height 0.95, tracking -2%   (slide 9 only)
  H1        88 px Bold, line height 1.0,  tracking -1%
  H2        56 px Bold, line height 1.1
  BODY      34 px Regular, line height 1.35, max 3 lines per block, max 60 characters per line
  LABEL     22 px SemiBold, ALL CAPS, tracking +8%, White at 70%
Section marker (top-left, every slide except 1 and 5): LABEL style, Gold, e.g. "02  AUDIENCE".
Page number (top-right, every slide except 1 and 5): LABEL style, White 50%, e.g. "04 / 13".
Footer (bottom-left, every slide except 1 and 5): 20 px, White 50%:
  "UW College of Business · Graduate Meta campaign · Work in progress · Placeholder imagery"
Space: minimum 96 px between any title and the body below it. When unsure, add space, not elements.
Never: gradients, shadows, icons, emoji, bullets with symbols, rules or dividers (except the short
  gold rule where stated), device mockups, stock photos, rounded cards with fills, animations.
Images: only COPIES of existing "Too stubborn to stop?" ad frames and their placeholder photo. Never
  redraw frames. Never use FAR / FURTHER, RUNNER or MARGIN NOTES frames.

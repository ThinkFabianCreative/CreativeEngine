DESIGN SYSTEM · TRAFFIK
Style: editorial and calm, like the Traffik website. White pages, big classic serif headlines,
small wide caps labels, one small red label per slide, bold full-bleed color only where it earns it.
Canvas: 1920 x 1080. Grid: 12 columns, 120 px outer margins, 24 px gutters. Flush left.

Colors
  WHITE        #FFFFFF  default background
  BLACK        #000000  type on white; background on slide 6 left half
  GREY         #7F7F7F  secondary text, page numbers, footer, captions
  TRAFFIK RED  #D91F1F  section labels only (small caps). Never headlines, never large areas
  TRAFFIK YELLOW #F4D803 slide 13 background only
  UW BROWN     #492F24  slides 8 (left half), 9 (full bleed), 11 (left half) only
  UW GOLD      #FFC425  only inside ad frames and on slide 9

Type (Adobe Fonts; install locally through Creative Cloud so Figma can use them.
If unavailable, use the Google stand-ins in brackets and say so in your report)
  HEADLINE XL  Adobe Caslon Pro Regular [Libre Caslon Text], 120 px, line height 1.0, tracking -1%, sentence case
  HEADLINE     Adobe Caslon Pro Regular [Libre Caslon Text], 80 px, line height 1.05, sentence case
  HEADLINE S   Adobe Caslon Pro Regular [Libre Caslon Text], 48 px, line height 1.15
  BODY         Acumin Pro Light [Archivo Light], 30 px, line height 1.45, max 3 lines per block, max 55 characters per line
  LABEL        Acumin Pro Wide Bold [Archivo Expanded Bold], 20 px, ALL CAPS, tracking +6%
  CAMPAIGN     Montserrat Bold: ONLY for campaign lines on slide 9, matching the ads
Headlines are Regular weight, never bold. Emphasis in headlines is a line break or scale, not color.

Page furniture (all slides except 1, 5, 9 and 13)
  Section label: top-left, LABEL, TRAFFIK RED, e.g. "AUDIENCE"
  Page number: top-right, LABEL, GREY, e.g. "04 / 13"
  Footer: bottom-left, 18 px Acumin Pro Regular, GREY:
    "TRAFFIK for the University of Wyoming College of Business · Work in progress · Placeholder imagery"
  Traffik logo (T in a circle + TRAFFIK wordmark): black version top-left on slide 1, white on slide 5,
    black on slide 13. Use the supplied logo file; if none is in the file, leave a labelled empty
    frame "TRAFFIK LOGO" and ask for the file. Never redraw the logo.

Split rows (Traffik signature layout)
  Half the slide (960 px) is a full-bleed color or image field; the other half is white with the
  headline and body set in columns 8–12 (or 1–5), vertically centered.

Ad frames
  Flat copies, never recolored, cropped, bordered, shadowed or rounded. On UW Brown fields, center them
  with at least 96 px clear space. Never use FAR / FURTHER, RUNNER or MARGIN NOTES frames.
Never: gradients, shadows, icons, emoji, symbol bullets, dividers, device mockups, stock photos,
  rounded filled cards, animations, bold serif, red headlines.
Space: at least 96 px between a headline and the body under it. When unsure, add space.

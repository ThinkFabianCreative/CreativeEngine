## The split

| Tier | Metrics | Owner | Decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Creative performance** (Traffik controls) | 3-second view rate · CTR · cost per landing-page view · cost per qualified inquiry · comment sentiment | Traffik | Which creative wins, scales or is cut |
| **Funnel conversion** (shared, mostly UW) | inquiry → application start · start → complete · complete → admit · admit → enroll | UW web, Salesforce portal, admissions operations | Where the funnel leaks and what UW must fix |

## Why it is tracked internally
- The agency controls the ad, not the program pages, the application portal, document requirements, advisor follow-up or deadlines.
- S7 GTM funnel (Fall 2025): MBA-MBA 329 starts → 137 completed → 31 enrolled; MS Accounting 92 → 43 → 17; MBA-MBAX 142 → 93 → 53. The largest losses occur after the click.
- Creative decisions use the controllable tier so engagement cannot be won by broad targeting or provocation that sends worse prospects.

## Guard against misuse
- Engagement is easy to inflate; never select creative on likes or comments alone. Cost per landing-page view and 3-second view rate are the primary creative decision metrics.
- The brief's success measure stands. This split is how Traffik reads performance internally, not a substitute presented to the client.
- Imagery test (people from Canto vs object scenes): judge on 3-second view rate and cost per landing-page view; monitor inquiry and start rates as a secondary signal.

## To make it real
- Reporting access needed: Meta Ads Manager (in place, assumed), GA4 for landing pages, Salesforce funnel counts by source (not supplied; Q11, Q12).
- Until source-level funnel data is available, only the creative tier is measurable.

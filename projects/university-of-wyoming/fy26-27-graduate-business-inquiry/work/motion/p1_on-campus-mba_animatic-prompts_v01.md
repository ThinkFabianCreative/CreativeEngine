## 1 · Camera pull-out (picture COMP)

Start frame: P1-A Firefly still-life (our own generation, PLACEHOLDER), exported as JPEG
from Figma. Draft 5 s at 480p first; the 15 s pass comes only if the draft reads.

```
@Image1 is the first frame. Start tight on the table: the black server's apron over the
chair back, the marked-up presentation pages, the small wrapped gift with a cream ribbon.
The camera pulls straight back slowly and steadily, one continuous move, revealing the
closed restaurant dining room with chairs upturned on every other table. Nothing in the
scene moves. No cut. Constant speed, no ease at the end.
Sound: quiet room tone, a refrigerator hum from the kitchen, nothing else.
Look: warm tungsten documentary still life, 35mm film look, fine grain, deep warm brown
shadows, cream highlights, low saturation. The top 40% of the frame stays dark and plain.
Exclude: people, hands, faces, text, numbers, logos, light fixtures, windows, camera shake,
morphing objects.
```

## 2 · Temp music bed (audio TEMP)

```
Instrumental. Sparse modern Americana with a dry, deadpan edge. Muted palm-muted electric
guitar pulse, brushed kick, one upright bass note per bar, a low warm organ pad underneath.
Around 96 BPM, steady. Confident and understated, not heroic, not corporate, no swells.
Structure for a 15 second social ad: bars 1–3 a ticking pulse that adds one layer per bar
(a to-do list filling up); bar 4 holds tension; then a full one-beat stop; then one
decisive low hit and a warm, resolved two-bar tag that ends cleanly on the downbeat.
No vocals, no whistling, no banjo, no rodeo or Western-movie clichés.
```

Settings: Lyria 3 · instrumental · 30 s (only option) · genre folk · mood auto · theme
commercial · tempo slow_med. The silence at 7.0–7.6 s is cut in the edit whatever the
model does.

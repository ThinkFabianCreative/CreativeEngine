## Key judgment: on-campus variants

S1 wants a campus environment for P1 and P3. The idea needs the viewer's current life before enrolling. Putting current-life objects (apron, internship badge) in a campus room muddles both and suggests working through an in-person program. Resolution: cards 1–2 and the static show current life; the end card carries a UW campus photograph with the logo and program label. The ad then shows both the life they have and the place they would go, without implying the program fits around a job.

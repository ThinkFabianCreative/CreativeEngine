### Decision chain (§2)

The idea's ownability sits in a brand property the College may not control. S2 p8 and S4 p19 name **UW Institutional Marketing** approvals for headlines and videos (applicability to this campaign OPEN, Q15), and Blackhawk is licensed through them (S2 p11). The person we present to may not be able to approve the one word the idea depends on.

- **Arm the contact.** Give the College contact one sentence and one reason they can take to Institutional Marketing without us. Suggested: *"It turns the thing that makes people think they can't do a master's into the thing UW already calls a Cowboy."* Plus: it extends the university's own campaign to a graduate audience rather than competing with it.
- **Ask, don't argue.** Cowboys permission is a brand-approval question (Q1). Put it to UW as a question in the room, not as something to win with creative argument. Slides prompt v09 already carries it as an ask ("Approval to use Cowboys language and the Blackhawk font"); whether that slide made it into v1.0 was not checked.

### B2 · Conversion layer design v01

The system is sound, and the framework mostly confirmed it:

- Contrast checked: White on Brown **12.24:1**, Gold on Brown **7.68:1**, matching §3's figures. Gold on White is **1.59:1**, so the spec is right to ban it.
- Display tracking is 0 to −1% and caps labels are +6%, in the right directions (§6). Scale steps are 2.2 / 1.8 / 1.5, which is contrast, not increments (§4). Line breaks are written, with no orphaned "TOO." (§6). The 9:16 layout maps onto the safe area (§8).

Two findings:

1. **Straight apostrophes will reach the comps.** The §7 build block quotes "KID'S RECITAL." and "YOU'VE STARTED" with straight marks (7 instances, 0 typographic), and line 12 of the block says "Do not change the words in quotes." A Figma agent following it will set primes. Fix in the source: KID’S, YOU’VE. The same applies to copy v01 wherever lines are handed to production.
2. **The cleverest word is typographically invisible.** Word-smither's bridge hides the task inside the week ("…KID'S RECITAL. **APPLICATION.** SAME WEEK."). The system sets the week line at the smallest message size, all White, with Gold moved to the stage line, so nothing marks APPLICATION as the odd one out. That may be the intended deadpan, since the discovery is the joke. But at 48 px on a phone, in a feed, most viewers will read the stage line and skip the week. Test both: as specced, and with APPLICATION alone in Gold and nothing Gold on the stage line (still one Gold word per frame). This is a design call, not a defect.

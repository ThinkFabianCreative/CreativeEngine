Restricted proof is upside only, never the load-bearing wall |
| Is the proposition focused? | **Mostly** | S1's message architecture and "What they need to hear" focus each route. P2's line leans on "value", which is restricted (C27–C29, C58), so P2 is the route most at risk of an unsupportable proposition. CP3's shared concept cannot rest on STEM, on-campus experiences, the GRE/GMAT waiver, exam outcomes or prep materials, because each is route-specific or restricted. Those belong in executions, not in the shared idea |
| Is there enough creative space left? | **Yes, with a squeeze to watch** | Concept space is open. Executional constraints are tight: immediate UW and program ID, one proof point, minimal on-image copy, Apply Now only, approved photography, 0–3 / 3–10 / 10–15 structure. The risk is mistaking S1's message architecture (need → benefit → proof → Apply Now) for the idea and producing a template. Treating S2/S3 themes as provisional keeps space open. A later mandatory theme could narrow it, which is why selection waits on Q1 |
| Are we solving the real problem rather than the first articulation? | **Partly. Name it for exploration** | S1's articulation is "identify fast, one proof, Apply Now". The underlying problem is heavier: one short ad must move someone, possibly on first contact, to a high-commitment action (starting a graduate application), and the idea must hold whether the click lands on a program page or the application portal (Q11). Identification is necessary, not sufficient. This is the exploration question, not a brief defect. If exploration shows the first-exposure-to-application ask cannot be credibly carried, that is a strategic question for the user and account team. Creative does not substitute another goal (user direction) |

**Result.** No answer is a plain no. The two partial answers (tension; real problem) are problems EXPLORE exists to solve, and are carried as explicit exploration conditions. The brief is not returned to `brief-machine`.

## Restrictions summary

**1. Internal exploration allowed.**

- CP1–CP4. Outputs stay internal.
- S1 is the working execution direction. S2 and S3 themes and headlines are provisional references (H5–H10), usable as stimulus, neither compulsory nor excluded.
- Source-suggested territories (H1–H4) are input, not the starting set. The director may withhold them in round one.

**2. Final concept selection pending: Q1.** Covers the roles of S1, S3 and university brand guidance, whether any theme or headline language is mandatory, and which approvals apply. Q20 (identity of S3) and Q21 (missing GTM and guidelines materials) are inputs. No direction is selected, presented to the client, or developed toward approval before Q1 is answered.

**3. Claim-specific restrictions (Q3–Q10; ledger §9, including §9.7).**

- No outcome, factual-benefit, comparative, ranking, ROI, price, access or urgency statement is used externally without support. Hedged wording is not permission.
- No unsupported benefit promise replaces a blocked fact.
- STEM never leaves P3.
- CPA content is preparation only.
- CFP means education requirement only.

**4. Production and launch requirements** (Q11, Q12, Q14–Q19). Destinations and test design; measurement and Meta setup; delivery-format routing and alumni separation in media; video brand standards; approvals; brand currency and assets; placements; schedule. None blocks unrelated exploration. Q15 may later require a UW intro or exit graphic, so video thinking should not depend on frames the brand might claim.

## Exploration conditions set by the director

- Establish a credible tension per concept problem before generating territories. AP1–AP4 are hypotheses to test, not answers.
- Explore genuinely different territories. Multiple executions are not multiple ideas. The number explored is set at exploration time, not by the brief.
- Every direction must survive on confirmable proof, with restricted proof as upside only.
- Every direction must work at first exposure and hold for either destination (program page or portal).
- Flag, do not reject, generic language in EXPLORE (role exclusion list; S1 p6 prohibited phrases).
- Taste profile: consult during development and review, not for this gate.

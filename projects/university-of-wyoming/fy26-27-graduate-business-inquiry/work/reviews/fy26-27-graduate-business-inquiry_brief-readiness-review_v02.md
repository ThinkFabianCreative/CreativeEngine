## Conditions for round 02

- **Shared-claim rule.** Umbrella-level statements must hold for all five variants. Only C01 does. No universal STEM, online flexibility, Spain, CPA or CFP.
- **Not a template.** A shared layout, palette, slogan or headline pattern is not an umbrella. Each territory needs an organizing thought that would still exist without its design system.
- **Variants do real work.** The adaptation must show five variants that are the same idea, not five ideas in the same frame.
- **Program identity.** UW branding is always clear. Program identity rides a restrained label, an end frame or post text. Program proof may live in primary ad text, with claim IDs and the same restrictions.
- **Provisional references.** S3 themes and "The World Needs More Cowboys" may be explored or answered, not treated as compulsory.
- **Taste profile.** Specialists read it for this round.

## Summary

The idea satisfies S1's voice, CTA, claims and minimal-copy rules. It does not yet satisfy S1 on four substantive fronts: program proof (none written), program fit (one working-parent week for all five), imagery (AI still life, no people, wrong environment for on-campus), and deliverables (no videos, no other programs, carousel outside S1's condition). The umbrella architecture itself remains a user-directed divergence awaiting client acceptance (Q37).

## To close the gaps without changing the idea

1. **Program-specific weeks.** Five week lines, each true to that program's audience (P1 early-career and immersive, P3 accounting graduates, P5 financial planning). The question and answer stay constant.
2. **Post text with one verified proof point per variant**, from the claim ledger (for example C25 for P2, C10/C12 for P1, C36 for P3/P4, C44/C47 for P5), with required caveats.
3. **Imagery by delivery format.** On-campus variants: UW photography of campus life mid-week. Online variants: work/life context. Prefer a person in action; still life only if the client accepts the divergence.
4. **Program label larger and in frame 1 / card 1.**
5. **15s videos to S1 timing:** 0–3s week collision + UW + label; 3–10s question lands; 10–15s answer + UW + Apply Now; captions.
6. **Static + video as core deliverables;** carousel only as an agreed extra.
7. **Ask UW:** umbrella acceptance (Q37), Cowboys language and Blackhawk approval (Q1, Q16), photography access (Q17, Q27), destinations (Q11).

## Working

- **The argument reads in five swipes.** Week → question → reframe → promise → proof. Card 2 → 3 is a real reveal.
- **Cards 2 and 3 type is right:** set line owns the dark top, one gold word each, answer line at full size (fixes review 2026-09-13 #2).

- **"ONE-YEAR"** is an approved P1 proof (pack 12).

## Fix, in order of weight

1. **The person is a current student, not our audience.** Library study hall + "Mid terms" + pen on notes = someone already in school. P1's audience is working adults *before* applying (program set v02); the week should be closing shift, pitch deck, a wedding — life that hasn't made room for school yet. "Midterms" was flagged on 2026-09-13 for exactly this. Recast the scene as current life (restaurant booth after close, kitchen island), which the "Latest" frame already holds.
2. **Visible face on an AI placeholder.** Imagery v02: placeholders are face-free (backs, hands, bowed heads); pack 12 wants a person-free still life for T2. And no "PLACEHOLDER IMAGE - not for external use" label on any card (pack 10).
3. **No logo or program label on cards 1–3.** Pack 12: logo + label on every card. Same fix as review 2026-09-13 #1, still open. This is first exposure.
4. **Card 1 week line drifted from copy and from the device.**
   - Copy: P1 is CLOSING SHIFT. PITCH DECK. BEST FRIEND'S WEDDING. SAME WEEK. Comp has four items, two different ("Long shift", "Sister wedding"), one off-audience ("Mid terms"). "Sister wedding" is ungrammatical.
   - Device: checkbox graphics are ruled out in both systems (planner-page v01: "never a checkbox"; "no handwriting fonts pretending to be handwriting"). Every item is ticked, so there is no unfinished line, which was the planner page's whole argument.
   - Gold: four ticks + four strikes + "same week." = nine gold events. Rule is one.
   - "All in the same week." loses the signature "SAME WEEK." (caps, set line).
   → Either pack 12 card 1 (set week line, SAME WEEK. in gold) or the planner-page page with one unticked line. Not a hybrid.
5. **Cards 2 and 3 are the same frame.** Pack 12: card 2 the whole scene, card 3 the dark top of the photo. Right now the swipe changes only the words. Move closer on each card.
6. **Cards 4–5 are Gold-dominant.** Pack 10: Brown dominant, Gold accent only. Pack 11/12: card 4 flat UW Brown, no photo; card 5 campus photo band over flat Brown with the white logo. Brown logo on Gold is not a listed logo pairing either.
7. **Card 5 photo is a building exterior.** Pack 11 on-campus end card: "people in action, no exteriors or landmarks." The classroom photo on card 4 is the right *kind* of image for card 5; swap them and make card 4 flat Brown.
8. **Card 5 proof and label.** Proof should be the one line "ONE-YEAR MBA." with a gold rule under it (pack 12), plus the program label ON-CAMPUS MBA at ≥32 px. "MBA" set huge reads as the label, not the proof. Card 4 also needs the label.
9. **Lockup.** Confirm the "College of Business / MBA Program" lockup is the supplied UW Lockup Placeholder component, not a rebuild (pack 10).

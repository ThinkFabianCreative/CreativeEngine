# Internal response: Estimate 945 V2 Option B (Program Video Package)

CLIENT: University of Wyoming · PROJECT: fy26-27-graduate-business-inquiry
Source: `~/Downloads/Est_945_V2_Option_B_University_of_Wyoming_Video_10-26-26.pdf` (account team, 2026-10-26, $8,000, expires 2026-11-18). Not copied into raw/.
Status: draft from Andres to the account team, not sent.

## What the estimate proposes
- 3 AI-assisted videos (MBA, MS Finance, MS Accounting), up to 90 s each, scripted, AI-generated visuals.
- 1 alternate opening hook per video, 1 × 15 s cutdown per video: 9 outputs.
- Captions, 2 aspect ratios, 2 revision rounds. No filming, no Instapage.
- Positioned as an alternative to the Instapage landing-page journey.

## Andres's position
The landing-page journey (email → program page → short video inside the page) is the recommendation. A standalone 90 s program video is a presentation few prospects will finish, and with no page behind it there is no next step.

## Viability issues
1. **Length.** 90 s straight presentation vs. a 15 s / 30 s attention reality. Most viewers leave in the first seconds; the message has to land early.
2. **No destination.** Without the landing pages the video has nowhere to send people. Where does it run, and what does a viewer do after?
3. **AI-generated visuals vs. UW rules.** UW discourages stock and our working rules exclude AI-generated people, faces and students. AI visuals must stay to graphics, type and motion over UW-supplied footage and photos.
4. **3 programs vs. 5.** Estimate covers MBA, MS Finance, MS Accounting. Campaign has 5 (on-campus and online MBA and Accounting, online Finance). Confirm whether the online variants share a video.

## Draft message (to account team)

Hi team,

Thanks for putting Option B together. I get the logic: a simpler package with one clear price, and video is what UW asked for first. I want to make it work, and I think we can with a few changes inside the same budget.

My concern is the 90-second format. A program video that runs a minute and a half as one piece is a presentation, and most people won't watch it to the end, especially on a phone coming from an ad or an email. We'd be spending most of the effort on the part almost nobody sees.

Here's what I'd suggest instead, same price, same number of outputs:

- **Make each program video modular.** Instead of one 90-second piece, build three 30-second chapters per program (for example: why this program, what it's like, how to apply). They can still play back to back as a 90-second version, but each one also works on its own. That gives UW a full-length video and pieces people will actually finish.
- **Keep the 15-second cutdowns and the alternate hooks.** Those are the most useful part of the package for ads.
- **AI only for graphics and motion, not people.** UW's brand rules discourage stock imagery, and we shouldn't put AI-generated students or faculty in front of prospective students. We build the videos from UW's own footage and photos, with AI handling type, graphics and motion.
- **Give the videos a place to send people.** Even if UW doesn't go with the landing pages now, each video needs a clear next step: the program page, a request-info form, something. Can we agree where these will run and where they point?
- **Check the program count.** The estimate covers 3 programs; the campaign has 5. Do the online MBA and online Accounting share the same video as on campus?

If UW later comes back to the landing pages, these chapters drop straight into them, so nothing we make here is wasted.

Happy to talk it through.

Andres

Landing pages excluded; chapters reusable if commissioned later.

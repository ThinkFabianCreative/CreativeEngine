## Board 1 · FAR → FURTHER

**The type mechanism doesn't read.** v02's idea was "further" visibly keeping what "far" started with: aligned letters, unmoved letters in Brown, added letters in Gold.

- The static drops it completely: all brown, "FAR" and "FURTHER" left-aligned at different sizes, so no letters line up.
- The carousel golds "URTHE" and keeps F and R white. With FAR set larger above, the shared letters don't sit over each other, so the gold reads as a two-tone accent, not as growth.
- Honest limit of the mechanism: "further" shares only F…R with "far", not the word. Even perfectly aligned, the relationship is subtle. It probably needs the motion (the v02 snap) to land; a static can't show it.

Result: as comped, this is the line set large. Taste-profile note: type chosen for drama without a working conceptual role is decoration.

**Hierarchy points the wrong way.** In the static, "FAR" is the largest word. That makes distance, the thing the line dismisses, the focal point. Either make FURTHER dominant or set both at one size and let alignment do the work.

**The carousel doesn't progress.** Cards 1 and 3 are the same line in near-identical layouts; card 2 is the same line again with a rule. Three swipes, one message. Give each swipe a job, or run it as a single image.

**New copy:** card 2's footer "WHAT CHANGES. WHAT STAYS." is not in the record, and it explains the idea. If the type works, it isn't needed; if it doesn't, the footer won't save it.

## Board 2 · NOT FOR EVERYONE

**The strongest board.** The copy carries it, and it's the one place where Gold means something: on card 2, the second "FRIDAY." in gold is the kept promise. That's colour doing argument, not decoration.

**Copy order.** The four acts get weaker as they go:

1. "Said Friday. Friday." Best: specific, deadpan, and the repetition is the proof.
2. "Told them the real number." Strong.
3. "Read the whole contract." Fine, closer to generic diligence.
4. "Put your name on it." Weakest: a stock "ownership" idiom, and it sits right before the verdict.

Cut to three, or reorder so the verdict follows a strong act. WordSmither can supply a sharper fourth.

**Verdict size.** The static sets "NOT FOR EVERYONE." larger than the list. v02 specified the same size. Same size is more deadpan: the verdict is just one more line of the record. Worth trying both.

**Q10 risk made worse.** Card 2 is "SAID / FRIDAY. / FRIDAY." with a huge gold "FRIDAY." In a feed, from an education advertiser with an Apply Now button, that can read as a deadline. v02 flagged this; the comp enlarges it. Keep "Said" at equal weight, or never isolate "Friday" at display size.

**Regressions from v02:**

- FRIDAY was a photographic diptych (sticky note Wednesday, tick Friday), revised so carousel card 1 worked as a thumbnail with an image in it. The comp is type only.
- THE RECORD and FRIDAY are merged into one carousel. That decision isn't recorded, and card 1 already says "Said Friday. Friday.", so card 2 repeats it.

**Spec breach:** card 1 uses a brown-to-black gradient, which the board 4 sheet prohibits. It isn't the S2 campaign gradient either.

## Board 3 · RUNNER

**The metaphor is lost in the illustration.** v02 specified a real spider plant and a rooted plantlet on an unbroken runner. The whole idea depends on recognizing that biology. As flat brown silhouettes, a dome with radiating strokes on a block reads as a sunrise, crown, cupcake or chef's hat, not a plant. The gold line reads as a connector or progress bar, pushing it toward a generic "network" infographic.

**The line argues against the line.** The runner is straight and taut, with a midpoint node and, on card 4 (end card), an arrowhead. Arrows mean travel. "You don't have to go far" shouldn't have an arrow pointing away.

**New one-word supers.** "ESTABLISHED. CONNECTED." and "UNBROKEN." aren't in the record. They're adjective positioning: the three-adjective habit the exclusion list and anti-slop rules warn about.

**Brand check.** Flat illustration as the lead visual isn't specified in v02, and S1 p6 asks for people, action and real experiences. Check against S4 (Q17) before developing further in this style.

**Recommendation.** Either shoot it as v02 specified, or, if illustration is wanted, draw a recognisable spider plant (arching striped leaves, plantlet with its own roots) with a slack, curved runner, no node and no arrow.

## Board 4 · Production sheet and components

- **Font stand-in:** the sheet uses Inter for Gotham. S4 p15 names the approved substitute for Gotham: Montserrat (brief §10.4). Swap it, so comps aren't judged on a face UW wouldn't allow.
- **Apply Now button:** the sheet says Apply Now is restrained copy, with the CTA in Ads Manager. The components include a filled gold button and an outline button, and the board 1 end card and board 4 end card use the gold one. A drawn button in an image that doesn't function risks reading as fake UI (also prohibited on the sheet) and doubles Meta's real CTA button. It also looks lightly graded; check against the no-gradient rule.
- **Double branding on end cards:** set type "UNIVERSITY OF WYOMING / ONLINE MBA" plus the logo lockup that already says University of Wyoming. One of them is redundant; the program label is the part that must stay.
- **Micro type:** footer lockups ("UNIVERSITY OF WYOMING · ONLINE MBA · APPLY NOW") are tiny, letterspaced caps: roughly 10–12 px on a 1080 canvas, about 4 pt on a phone. Illegible in feed. That undermines Q38 (program identity per placement) and S4 accessibility. Program label must survive at phone size.
- **Missing:** 9:16 masters (v02 rule 5) and any video frame. Carousel is spec'd 1:1; v02 assumed 4:5 feed masters. Resolve once placements are set (Q18).

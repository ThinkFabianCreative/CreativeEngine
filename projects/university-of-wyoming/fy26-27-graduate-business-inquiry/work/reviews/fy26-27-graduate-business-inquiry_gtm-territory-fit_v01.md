## What the GTM establishes for creative
- Situation: accreditation, cost and convenience are commoditized (p2). Distinctiveness must come from UW's character story.
- Journey (p6): Awareness "Is graduate school relevant to what I want to do next?" → Consideration "Is this program a good fit?" → Preference "Why UW?" → Conversion "What do I need to do next?". Brand story persuades at "Why UW?" and "should not replace the information needed to establish fit".
- Messaging framework (p7): prospect need → program outcome → proof → UW differentiation → next action.
- Priorities (p10): 1 convert existing demand (CRM, recruiter, CRM-matched Meta retargeting with deadline messaging); 2 build future demand (search, flighted Meta, grassroots).
- Meta role (p19–20): flighted around application deadlines; build consideration; retarget known prospects. Paid media about $70K for the year across search, Meta and grassroots.
- Program weighting (p9, p14–17): Online MBA 200 (always-on scale, Meta key); On-Campus MBA 35 (grassroots-led, Meta supports); MS Accounting 50 (search-led); MS FP&WM 30 (partnerships first; Meta retargeting only once claims are confirmed; "broad paid-media scale should not be assumed").
- Markets (p12): multi-state (e.g. Online MBA core WY, SD, NE, ND; growth CO; test TX).

## Fit: the strategy
Strong at Awareness. "Change what they believe about themselves" answers the GTM's awareness question and fills the gap left by commoditized proof. It covers Priority 2 (build future demand). It does not cover Priority 1 (convert existing demand), which needs a separate, practical retargeting and CRM layer.

| T3 Students as influencers | Nurture "Student, alumni and faculty perspectives" (p22); On-Campus MBA proof experiences (p15) | Good as consideration and retargeting proof, weaker as cold prospecting | Needs real students and approved quotes; production cost vs budget |

## Architecture implied
One platform across the journey rather than three competing options:
1. Prospecting hook (Meta, flighted): T2.
2. Consideration proof (retargeting, CRM nurture, program pages): T3 with real students.
3. Conversion (retargeting, CRM): practical application and deadline creative, not yet made.
T1's thought survives as Online MBA prospect-need copy ("advance without stepping away from work"), not as a platform.

## Gaps and divergences (OPEN)
- Markets: S5 targeting (Wyoming counties, two Colorado counties) vs GTM multi-state markets.
- Program name: S1 "M.S. in Finance" vs GTM "MS Financial Planning & Wealth Management" (Q7).
- Meta job: S1 "application starts" inquiry campaign vs GTM consideration plus deadline retargeting.
- Deadline messaging in retargeting vs S1 "FROM CLIENT" deadlines and start terms (Q10).
- GTM framework step 2 "program outcome" and proof items (tuition below $30K, STEM, college-funded CPA prep, Jackson Hole ecosystem) are restricted or pending in the claim ledger (C27, C34, C38, C48, C57–C59).
- Program weighting: five equal Meta variants vs GTM weighting (Online MBA priority; FP&WM not scaled in paid media).
- Enrollment target conflict noted by the GTM itself (MS Accounting 50 vs 60; FP&WM 30 vs 20).

## Working

## Fix
1. **Branding missing in Version A carousel.** No logo or program label on any card. Mandatory for a first-exposure ad (S1 p7; U1).
2. **Answer line too small.** "SOUNDS LIKE A COWBOY." is roughly a quarter of the headline size in statics; it is the payoff and vanishes at phone size. Target line 2 at 1/2 to 2/3 of line 1. On its own carousel card it should be large.
3. **Pendant light competes.** The brightest spot in every frame sits beside the headline and pulls the eye first. Darken it or crop it out.
4. **Dotted lines (Version B).** Decoration with no job; reads as a progress or route line (Direction B vocabulary).
5. **Gold logo box (Version B 9:16).** Badge-like, and sits in the top 14% unsafe zone.
6. **9:16 safe zone (Version A).** Logo and label sit in the bottom 35%, under Reels/Stories UI.
7. **Two brush underlines (Version B).** Underline on "SAME WEEK." and "COWBOY." dilutes both. Gold color on SAME WEEK only; brush underline on COWBOY only.
8. **Version B end card.** Washed brown overlay on the photo looks muddy; logo tiny.
9. **Copy drift (Version B).** "DAUGHTER'S RECITAL" and "MID-TERMS"/"MIDTERMS" differ from the working copy and from each other. "Daughter's" adds good specificity; "midterms" implies an already enrolled student (tension lines v01 flag). Needs Andres's call.
10. **Photo is generic.** The prompt's specific life objects (recital program, hair clip) did not render; the result reads as a stock "studying late" image, so image and copy are 1 + 1 = 2. Add one visible non-work object.
11. **Same photo on every card.** Acceptable as a continuous scene, but a crop progression (wide → laptop → mug) would give the swipe a visual reason.

## Recommended build
Version A structure (question card → answer card) + Version B static scale + no dots, no logo box, end card added.

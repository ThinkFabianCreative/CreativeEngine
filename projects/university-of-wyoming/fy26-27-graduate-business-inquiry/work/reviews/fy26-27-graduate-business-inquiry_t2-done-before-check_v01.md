## Conclusion

No SAME or CLOSE precedent on Ads of the World. "Stubborn as a virtue" has been said before, most plainly by DDB Mozambique's 2012 self-promotion, so the word alone is not what makes T2 ownable. What this archive shows no precedent for is the combination: the prospect's own week as the evidence, the question that makes them admit it, and UW's Cowboy as the name for the trait.

**Implication for defense:** if UW or a reviewer says "stubborn has been done", the answer is the combination above. The planner-page week and the Cowboy answer carry the originality; keep both.

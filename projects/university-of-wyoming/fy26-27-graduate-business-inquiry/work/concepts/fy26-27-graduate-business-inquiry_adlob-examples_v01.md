**Purpose.** Eight ADLOBs, two per provocation lens, written so a designer can build rough 4:5 Meta feed comps in Figma. They are not the 50-idea wall and not umbrella territories. Each is one advertising thought that could act as the umbrella, shown here in one program variant.

**Rules applied.**
- Creative rule: **RECOGNIZE THEM. DON'T RESCUE THEM.**
- Headlines are six words or fewer.
- None of "future", "potential", "next level" or "unlock" appears in creative copy.
- No graduation caps, mountaintops, heroic distance-staring, stock meetings or cowboy cosplay.

**Standing flags for every comp.**
- **UW lockup:** placeholder until approved logo files and current brand rules arrive (Q17). Advertising use needs Trademark Licensing approval (Q16).
- **Program identity:** a restrained label in the asset plus primary text. Where the program name isn't immediately visible, that is the recorded S1 divergence (§11.6; Q37). Visibility of primary text by placement is Q38.
- **CTA:** Apply Now in Meta's CTA button. An in-image CTA is optional and a production choice.
- **Photography:** approved UW/Canto images or a new shoot with releases (S1 p6). Hands and people are cast talent, never testimony. Documents, books and labels are generic, with no real titles, brands, names or data.
- **Canvas:** 1080×1350 as directed. The Meta knowledge base documents 1440×1800 at the same 4:5 ratio for feed; scale up for production.
- **Accounting variants:** primary text carries the S1 caveat "CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state."
- **Primary text:** word counts are given in brackets. Claim IDs sit on a separate line and are not part of the ad text.

## CURIOSITY

### A01 · CURIOSITY · "You kept the book."

- **POST-IT:** Curious people keep the textbook long after the class ends, and they're still adding sticky flags to it.
- **HEADLINE:** You kept the book.
- **SUPPORT LINE:** none
- **VISUAL (photo):**
  - A narrow shelf above a lived-in desk: a plant, a charging cable, a stack of unopened mail with no addresses visible.
  - Among them sits one heavy undergraduate accounting textbook with a cracked spine and a generic cover (no real title or publisher).
  - A fan of **faded** sticky flags sticks out of it, plus three **new, bright** ones. The contrast in the flags is the idea: the curiosity didn't end with the course.
- **LAYOUT NOTE:**
  - The book, with its two generations of flags, is the focal point, at eye level in the middle third.
  - The headline sits on the wall space in the top third, left-aligned and medium weight. The flags are the loudest color in the frame.
  - The UW lockup and label "M.S. in Accounting · On campus" sit small in the bottom band.
- **PROGRAM VARIANT:** M.S. in Accounting, on-campus (P3)
- **PRIMARY TEXT [38 words]:** Some subjects stay with you. The University of Wyoming's on-campus M.S. in Accounting prepares students for the CPA exam. Full- or part-time; one- or two-year completion. AACSB-accredited College of Business. CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state.
  - Claims: C36 (preparation only), C35, C01 · all SOURCE-STATED · caveat per C39 row
- **CTA:** Apply Now
- **RISK / OPEN:**
  - Skews to recent graduates and may miss early-career accountants who didn't keep the book.
  - STEM (C34) is deliberately left out, pending Q3.
  - The book must not read as a CPA prep product (C38 PENDING APPROVAL).

  - "M.S. in Finance" naming is Q7.

### A05 · POSSIBILITY · "Numbers person. People person. Both."

- **POST-IT:** People who are good with spreadsheets and good with people never had to pick which one they are.
- **HEADLINE:** Numbers person. People person. Both.
- **SUPPORT LINE:** none
- **VISUAL (typographic):**
  - "Numbers person." is set small in tabular, monospaced-feeling figures style.
  - "People person." sits beneath it, small, in a warm humanist face.
  - "Both." is enormous below them and fuses the two treatments, e.g. a structured "B" and warmer "oth". The fusion is the whole idea, so it must be one word, not a font collage.
- **LAYOUT NOTE:**
  - The two small lines sit top left like a question on a form.
  - "Both." dominates the lower 55%, bleeding close to the side margins.
  - The label "Online MBA" and the UW lockup sit in the bottom-right corner.
  - Brown and Gold may carry the two treatments, subject to brand rules (Q17).
- **PROGRAM VARIANT:** Online MBA (P2)
- **PRIMARY TEXT [33 words]:** Keep both. Online MBA from the University of Wyoming: 100% online and asynchronous. 30 credits, full- or part-time, completion in as few as three semesters. Fall, spring and summer entry. Nine elective credits.
  - Claims: C25 · SOURCE-STATED · "as few as" kept as the condition · no dated start (Q10)
- **CTA:** Apply Now
- **RISK / OPEN:**
  - Leans toward an implied leadership or people-skills outcome (C30, OUTCOME CLAIM). It must stay a statement of identity, never a promise of development.
  - "People person" may read as HR cliché.
  - The font fusion is a craft risk at phone size.

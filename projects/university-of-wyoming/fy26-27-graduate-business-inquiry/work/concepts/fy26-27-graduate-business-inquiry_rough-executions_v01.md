# DIRECTION 1 · YOU DON'T HAVE TO GO FAR TO GET FURTHER

## D1-1 · FAR → FURTHER  *(DC D1-A, with WS D1-1 merged)*

**THE AD (static).**

- **Subject:** type only. "far" is set large, lowercase, in a quiet serif. Directly beneath it, "further" is set so its **f** and **r** sit in exactly the same positions as in "far". The **a** has become a **u**, and "ther" runs on to the right margin.
- **Crop:** "further" runs nearly edge to edge; "far" sits inside its width.
- **Focal point:** the unmoved **f** and **r**, in UW Brown. The changed and added letters are in UW Gold (brand rules, Q17).
- **Type relationship:** the small connector words sit in the gaps. The two big words are the image.
- **Reading order:** "You don't have to go" → **far** → "to get" → **further.** → UW lockup → program label.
- **Absent:** photography, people, place, distance imagery.

**COPY.** You don't have to go · far · to get · further. · University of Wyoming · [program label]

**15 SECONDS.**

1. "You don't have to go far" builds. UW mark in the corner.
2. The **a** flips to a **u**.
3. "ther" slides out while the **f** and **r** never move.
4. "to get" settles. Hold on the full line.
5. End frame: UW lockup · program label · Apply Now.

**META BEHAVIOR.** A three-second transformation, complete by about 3 seconds, so autoplay carries the whole thought. Autoplay-dependent.

**VARIANT STRIP.** Visual change: label only, in every row.

| Program label | Rough primary ad text |
|---|---|
| On-Campus MBA | Further is about what you want, not how far you move. A one-year, in-person MBA in Laramie, with applied case work and real-world problem solving. |
| Online MBA | Further is about what you want, not how far you move. 100% online and asynchronous; full- or part-time; completion in as few as three semesters. Transcripts do not identify the delivery format. |
| M.S. in Accounting · On campus | Further is about what you want, not how far you move. The program prepares students for the CPA exam. Full- or part-time; one- or two-year completion. CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| M.S. in Accounting · Online | Further is about what you want, not how far you move. 100% online and asynchronous; GRE/GMAT not required. The program prepares students for the CPA exam. CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| M.S. in Finance · Online | Further is about what you want, not how far you move. 30-credit, 100% online; one- or two-year completion. Topics include investment management, tax planning, estate planning, insurance and retirement planning. |

**WHY IT WORKS.** The letters act out the thought: "further" keeps what "far" started with and adds reach. The double meaning lives inside the line, not in a picture explaining it.

**WHERE IT FAILS.**

- It can feel like a clever type trick, with no human truth and no Wyoming.
- The glyph alignment only works in some typefaces and must survive phone size.
- If the added **u** reads as UW's U, it slides toward "UW produces further".

**CLAIM FLAGS.** Asset: none. Primary text: C10, C12, C25, C02, C36, C35, C44, C45.

**COWBOYS.** Weakens it: a second headline crowds an all-type frame.

## D1-2 · RUNNER  *(DC D1-B)*

**THE AD (static).**

- **Subject:** a spider plant on an ordinary indoor sill, with no view outside. One runner arcs out of the pot into a second, smaller pot, where its plantlet has rooted. The runner is still unbroken.
- **Crop:** tight on the arc of the runner. The parent plant is cut by the left edge; the rooted plantlet sits at the right.
- **Focal point:** the unbroken stem between the two pots.
- **Type relationship:** the line sits on the plain wall above the arc, left-aligned. The type never touches the plant.
- **Reading order:** the plantlet → back along the runner → the parent plant → the line → UW lockup and program label along the sill.
- **Absent:** people, window scenery, soil mess, any sign of "moving out".

**COPY.** You don't have to go far to get further. · University of Wyoming · [program label]

**15 SECONDS.**

1. Macro on the plantlet rooting in its own pot. It looks independent. UW mark in the corner.
2. The camera follows the runner back along its arc.
3. The parent plant is revealed, still attached. The line appears.
4. Hold on both pots and the unbroken runner.
5. End frame: UW lockup · program label · Apply Now.

**META BEHAVIOR.** A three-second reveal. The first frame reads as starting over alone; the pull-back shows it never left.

**VARIANT STRIP.** Visual change: label only, in every row.

| Program label | Rough primary ad text |
|---|---|
| On-Campus MBA | Reach further without letting go of what grounds you. A one-year, in-person MBA in Laramie, with applied case work and real-world problem solving. |
| Online MBA | Reach further without letting go of what grounds you. 100% online and asynchronous; full- or part-time; completion in as few as three semesters. Transcripts do not identify the delivery format. |
| M.S. in Accounting · On campus | Reach further without letting go of what grounds you. The program prepares students for the CPA exam. Full- or part-time; one- or two-year completion. CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| M.S. in Accounting · Online | Reach further without letting go of what grounds you. 100% online and asynchronous; GRE/GMAT not required. The program prepares students for the CPA exam. CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| M.S. in Finance · Online | Reach further without letting go of what grounds you. 30-credit, 100% online; one- or two-year completion. Topics include investment management, tax planning, estate planning, insurance and retirement planning. |

**WHY IT WORKS.** One living thing is close and expansive at the same moment. The reveal overturns the assumption that growing further means cutting ties.

**WHERE IT FAILS.**

- Houseplant metaphors tip easily into greeting-card sentiment.
- There is no Wyoming, and no challenge or pride.
- The primary-text opener must never read as a promise that the program makes someone "reach further".

**CLAIM FLAGS.** Asset: none. Primary text: C10, C12, C25, C02, C36, C35, C44, C45.

**COWBOYS.** Weakens it: the tone clashes with the plant's quiet metaphor.

## D1-3 · NOT GOING ANYWHERE  *(WS D1-2)*

**THE AD (static).**

- **Subject:** a cast adult sitting on their own front step at dusk, relaxed, turning a house key in their fingers.
- **Crop:** waist up, with the front door behind them. They are off-center right.
- **Focal point:** the settled posture and the key.
- **Type relationship:** "Not going anywhere." is set large in the open space at left. The line sits smaller beneath it and changes what the first phrase meant.
- **Reading order:** "Not going anywhere." (sounds like staying put) → the person → the line (now it also means staying with it) → UW lockup → program label.
- **Absent:** moving boxes, roads, destinations, books, laptops, distance-staring. The gaze is down, at the key.

**COPY.** Not going anywhere. · You don't have to go far to get further. · University of Wyoming · [program label]

**15 SECONDS.** Different cast people in each scene. Scripted captions, never testimony.

1. On a front step with a friend. Caption: "Not going anywhere." (meaning staying)
2. At a workplace counter, a coworker nods to them. Caption: "Not going anywhere." (meaning loyalty)
3. Daylight, alone and focused on a hard page at work. Caption: "Not going anywhere." (meaning staying with this)
4. Caption: "You don't have to go far to get further."
5. End frame: UW lockup · program label · Apply Now.

**META BEHAVIOR.** Repetition changes meaning. It needs the video; the static carries only the reframing pair.

**VARIANT STRIP.** Visual change: label only, in every row. The same cast works across variants.

| Program label | Rough primary ad text |
|---|---|
| On-Campus MBA | Staying put and staying with it can be the same decision. A one-year, in-person MBA in Laramie, with applied case work and real-world problem solving. |
| Online MBA | Staying put and staying with it can be the same decision. 100% online and asynchronous; full- or part-time; completion in as few as three semesters. Transcripts do not identify the delivery format. |
| M.S. in Accounting · On campus | Staying put and staying with it can be the same decision. The program prepares students for the CPA exam. Full- or part-time; one- or two-year completion. CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| M.S. in Accounting · Online | Staying put and staying with it can be the same decision. 100% online and asynchronous; GRE/GMAT not required. The program prepares students for the CPA exam. CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| M.S. in Finance · Online | Staying put and staying with it can be the same decision. 30-credit, 100% online; one- or two-year completion. Topics include investment management, tax planning, estate planning, insurance and retirement planning. |

**WHY IT WORKS.** A phrase that usually means standing still is repeated until it means commitment. Staying connected and moving forward happen in the same three words.

**WHERE IT FAILS.**

- The static is weak without the repetition, and done warmly it turns sentimental.
- "Not going anywhere" can read as "stuck".
- For P1 it strains for viewers outside commuting range of Laramie.

**CLAIM FLAGS.** Asset: P1 geography assumption (Q14). Primary text: C10, C12, C25, C02, C36, C35, C44, C45.

**COWBOYS.** Neutral: the steadiness fits the modern Cowboy, but a sign-off crowds the phrase.

# DIRECTION 2 · NOT FOR EVERYONE

**Primary text rule for this direction.** Facts only, with no opener. No "Some people…", no reassurance, no explanation of the line. The asset makes the challenge; the text states the program.

## D2-1 · THE LINE, ALONE  *(DC D2-A; act relocated by CreativeDirector)*

**THE AD (static).**

- **Layout:** the top 60% is a plain type field holding only "Not for everyone." The bottom 40% is a documentary crop of a printed team schedule taped to a breakroom wall.
- **The act:** a hand in pen fixes one misspelled letter in a colleague's name on the schedule.
- **Crop:** tight on the schedule row and pen tip. Other names are blurred and generic.
- **Focal point:** the single corrected letter, deliberately small.
- **Type relationship:** the provocation is large and alone; the evidence caption is small, below the photo.
- **Reading order:** "Not for everyone." → the search for what changed → the corrected letter → "Corrected it. Nobody noticed." → UW lockup → program label.
- **Absent:** faces, the colleague, thanks, meeting rooms, anyone watching.

**COPY.** Not for everyone. · Corrected it. Nobody noticed. · University of Wyoming · [program label]

**15 SECONDS.**

1. 0–3s: "Not for everyone." alone on a plain field. UW mark in the corner.
2. The breakroom schedule. A hand fixes one letter in a colleague's name.
3. The pen caps. People pass behind in soft focus; nobody looks.
4. Caption: "Corrected it. Nobody noticed."
5. End frame: UW lockup · program label · Apply Now.

**META BEHAVIOR.** A three-second hold on the line alone creates tension before any evidence. Autoplay-dependent; placements that judge the first 3 seconds see only the line (Q38).

**VARIANT STRIP.** Visual change: label only, in every row.

| Program label | Rough primary ad text |
|---|---|
| On-Campus MBA | University of Wyoming On-Campus MBA. One year, in person, in Laramie. Applied case work and real-world problem solving. |
| Online MBA | University of Wyoming Online MBA. 100% online and asynchronous; full- or part-time; completion in as few as three semesters. Transcripts do not identify the delivery format. |
| M.S. in Accounting · On campus | University of Wyoming M.S. in Accounting, on campus. The program prepares students for the CPA exam. Full- or part-time; one- or two-year completion. CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| M.S. in Accounting · Online | University of Wyoming M.S. in Accounting, online. 100% online and asynchronous; GRE/GMAT not required. The program prepares students for the CPA exam. CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| M.S. in Finance · Online | University of Wyoming M.S. in Finance, online. 30 credits, 100% online; one- or two-year completion. Topics include investment management, tax planning, estate planning, insurance and retirement planning. |

**WHY IT WORKS.** The biggest provocation meets the smallest act. Getting a colleague's name right when nobody would notice sets a standard of character, not status, and nothing praises it.

**WHERE IT FAILS.**

- Three seconds of type may lose the scroll.
- The corrected letter can vanish at phone size.
- Seen alone, the line still reads as admission scarcity (Q38).

**CLAIM FLAGS.** Asset: none. Primary text: C10, C12, C25, C02, C36, C35, C44, C45.

**COWBOYS.** Strengthens it: "The World Needs More Cowboys" answers "Not for everyone" and blocks the admission misreading.

## D2-3 · FRIDAY  *(DC D2-D)*

- **Subject:** a diptych from one fixed camera, as a single frame.
  - Left: a sticky note reading "Friday" on the edge of a desk shelf, with a small desk calendar showing Wednesday.
  - Right: the same shelf, with the calendar showing Friday, the note gone, and a small pen tick on Friday.
- **Crop:** identical in both halves: the shelf edge and the calendar.
- **Focal point:** the empty spot where the note was.
- **Type relationship:** "Not for everyone." sits above the diptych; "Said Friday. Friday." sits small beneath the right panel.
- **Reading order:** line → left panel → right panel → caption → UW lockup → program label.
- **Absent:** people, anyone checking up, praise, night light.

**COPY.** Not for everyone. · Friday · Said Friday. Friday. · University of Wyoming · [program label]

**15 SECONDS.**

1. "Not for everyone." alone. UW mark in the corner.
2. The note on the shelf; the calendar shows Wednesday.
3. Match cut: the calendar turns to Friday and the note is gone.
4. A small pen tick. Caption: "Said Friday. Friday."
5. End frame: UW lockup · program label · Apply Now.

**What was wrong in v01.** Several executions used film grammar:

- slow pull-backs and follow-backs
- a three-second type-only hold before any evidence
- a three-scene cast narrative
- five-beat sequences that only make sense watched start to finish

In a feed, most people see the first frame or a static, often muted.

**Meta-native rules applied to all six.**

1. **Frame 1 is the ad.** The video's first frame is the static, and the idea is readable in it.
   - *Basis:* shared Meta knowledge base, derived guidance: "Show the central idea early enough to survive a short encounter"; avoid "a long logo pre-roll that delays the actual idea".
2. **No slow camera moves.** Motion is one small action or a hard cut: a push, a swap, a snap.
3. **One setup.** Tabletop or a single location, with no multi-scene cast stories.
4. **Sound-off by design.** On-screen type does all the work; captions are required (S1 p6).
5. **Two masters: 4:5 feed and 9:16 Stories/Reels.** Key type sits inside the Stories/Reels safe zone: roughly the top 14%, bottom 35% and sides 6% clear (knowledge base production guidance; verify per placement, since placements are still open, Q18).
6. **15 seconds, because S1 requires it.**
   - The idea lands by 3s.
   - 3–10s repeats or tightens it as a loop, not a story.
   - 10–15s is the UW lockup, program label and Apply Now.
   - Program facts stay in primary text (brief §11.6).
7. **Carousels:** card 1 must work alone as the thumbnail.

**Unchanged from v01.** The copy, the five-variant strips and the rough primary text all carry over. Program labels change only the label; primary text is the vetted facts, with the same claim IDs and restrictions.

## DIRECTION 1 · YOU DON'T HAVE TO GO FAR TO GET FURTHER

### D1-1 · FAR → FURTHER

**Was it Meta-native?** Mostly. v01 built the line slowly over five beats; now the static is complete in frame 1.

**Static, 4:5 / 9:16.**

- **The idea:** "far" sits above "further", with the **f** and **r** aligned, so "further" visibly keeps what "far" started with.
- **Colour:** unmoved letters in UW Brown, added letters in UW Gold (brand rules, Q17).
- **Connector words:** "You don't have to go" and "to get" are set small.
- **Layout:** in 9:16 the stack sits in the middle band, clear of the top 14% and bottom 35%. The UW lockup and program label sit just above the bottom safe line.

**15 seconds.**

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–1s | Frame 1 is the full static. UW lockup visible. |
| 1–2s | "further" collapses back to "far" in one snap: "ther" retracts and "u" flips to "a". |
| 2–3s | It snaps open again to "further". The move *is* the idea. |
| 3–10s | Hold. At about 7s, the snap repeats once as a loop. |
| 10–15s | End card: UW lockup · program label · Apply Now. |

**Production.** Type animation only; no shoot.

### D1-2 · RUNNER

**Was it Meta-native?** No. v01 opened on a macro and followed the runner back, so the idea didn't exist until about 3s.

**Static, 4:5 / 9:16.**

- **The idea:** both pots are in one frame from the start. The parent spider plant is on the left; the rooted plantlet sits in its own pot on the right, joined by an unbroken runner.
- **Crop:** tabletop, straight on, plain wall. The line sits above the arc.
- **9:16:** the pots stack diagonally, with the runner crossing the middle band.

**15 seconds.**

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–1s | Frame 1 is the static. |
| 1–3s | A hand slides the small pot a few inches further along the sill. The runner stretches and holds. The line lands. |
| 3–10s | One more small slide. The runner still holds; nothing snaps. |
| 10–15s | End card: UW lockup · program label · Apply Now. |

**Production.** One tabletop setup with a hand-model release. The live runner must stretch safely, so a rigged runner may be needed.

**New risk.** The stretched runner can read as strain. Keep the slides small and the runner relaxed.

### D1-3 · NOT GOING ANYWHERE

**Was it Meta-native?** Least of the six. The v01 video depended on the same phrase repeated across three cast scenes, a mini-film. That repetition device is **cut**.

**Static, 4:5 / 9:16.** Unchanged in v01's composition.

- **The idea:** the pairing does the work. "Not going anywhere." sounds like staying put, and the line beneath reframes it as staying with it.
- **Scene:** a person on their own front step, turning a house key.
- **9:16:** the person sits in the middle band; "Not going anywhere." goes in the upper band below the 14% line, and the working line sits above the bottom 35%.

**15 seconds.** One locked shot, one location.

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–1s | Frame 1 is the static with only "Not going anywhere." |
| 1–3s | "You don't have to go far to get further." lands beneath it. |
| 3–10s | Same shot: they stand, pick up a work bag from the step and head down the steps into their day. The type stays. |
| 10–15s | End card: UW lockup · program label · Apply Now. |

**Production.** Dropped from three cast scenes to one location, one talent and one continuous take.

**Honest flag.** Without the repetition, this is the thinnest of the six in motion. The static carries it; the video mostly re-states it. It's a candidate to replace if Direction 1 needs a stronger third master. The P1 geography flag (Q14) still applies.

## DIRECTION 2 · NOT FOR EVERYONE

### D2-1 · CORRECTED  *(was THE LINE, ALONE)*

**Was it Meta-native?** No. v01 held "Not for everyone." alone for three seconds before any evidence, which is exactly the delay a feed doesn't allow.

**Static, 4:5 / 9:16.**

- **The idea:** "Not for everyone." and the act share frame 1.
- **Layout:** the line is large in the top band. Directly below, a tight crop of the breakroom schedule shows the pen tip on a colleague's name, one letter visibly corrected. "Corrected it. Nobody noticed." sits small beneath.
- **Hierarchy:** the line still reads first, by scale. The tension comes from the line, not from a pause.

**15 seconds.**

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–1s | Frame 1 is line plus schedule, pen touching the misspelled letter. |
| 1–2s | The pen corrects the letter. Tight enough to read on a phone. |
| 2–3s | "Corrected it. Nobody noticed." lands. |
| 3–10s | Hold on the corrected name. At about 6s, a blurred passer-by crosses behind without looking. |
| 10–15s | End card: UW lockup · program label · Apply Now. |

**Production.** One wall, one hand, one pen, with a hand-model release. The corrected letter must be legible at phone size, so crop tighter than v01.

**Architecture.** One campaign umbrella delivers five program/delivery variants: P1 On-Campus MBA, P2 Online MBA, P3 M.S. in Accounting (on campus), P4 M.S. in Accounting (online), P5 Online M.S. in Finance (FP&WM / CFP pathway). The working baseline is 5 statics + 5 fifteen-second videos.

**Placeholders.** One static and one video per territory are illustrated, using different variants to show range. Copy is placeholder. Post-text rows are rough.

**Claims and approvals.**
- Claim IDs are SOURCE-STATED facts for internal exploration only. None is cleared for external use.
- Shared umbrella statements use only C01 (AACSB-accredited College of Business), the one claim true of all five variants.
- The College themes and "The World Needs More Cowboys" remain provisional references (Q1). Final selection is pending Q1 and Q37.

**How the round ran.**

1. **Seeds.** DesignChamp contributed 8 umbrella seeds and WordSmither 7, each stress-tested against all five variants.
2. **Shortlist and first build.** Three territories were shortlisted and built.
   - *The Hover* was dropped: it duplicated Straight Answers (one doubt, one fact), and "Almost." presumed interest the brief says can't be assumed.
   - Its replacement, *The Marked Route*, was built and dropped: four of its five "Not ___" lines only restated format, which makes it a template.
3. **Final build.** The third territory, The Still Mark, was rebuilt from DesignChamp's *Stay On* seed. Both specialists independently removed the riding metaphor, because it implied the programs are hard.

**Program identity, all territories.**
- UW branding is always clear.
- The program rides a restrained label, an end frame or post text; the central idea never changes.
- Where a video's program name isn't in 0–3s, that is the treatment change recorded in brief §11.6 row 4. Placement visibility of post text is Q38.
- Accounting post text carries the S1 caveat "CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state." (C39 row).
- Apply Now is the only action.

## A · NOT WHERE YOU PICTURED

**1. Umbrella idea.** People have a picture of Wyoming but none of its graduate business school, so the campaign puts the school inside the picture they already have.

**2. Observation / tension.**
- *Evidence (S1 p1, p5):* the ad may be a prospect's first exposure to UW.
- *Hypothesis:* the state's image is vivid; the College's image is blank.

**3. Mechanism (visual + verbal).**
- The word **WYOMING**, set heavy across the frame, becomes a window. Its letters hold a photograph of the program's real setting: the picture viewers don't have yet.
- Verbally: "Wyoming." then "Also Wyoming:" and the College, then the program.
- "Wyoming" names who UW is, never where an online student studies.

**4. Constant.**
- The WYOMING window
- The "Also Wyoming" turn
- C01, "AACSB-accredited College of Business"
- UW lockup
- Apply Now

**5. Changes by program.** The photograph inside the letters (campus for on-campus variants; credible work/life for online) and the program named after "Also Wyoming:".

**6. Rough static (P2 Online MBA).**
- **Image:** "WYOMING" fills the frame. Inside the letters is an approved work/life photo (a working professional in a real workplace), not a hero laptop.
- **Copy beneath:** "Also Wyoming: an AACSB-accredited College of Business."
- **Label:** "University of Wyoming · Online MBA" · Apply Now.
- **Reading order:** word → scene → C01 → label → Apply Now.

**15-second outline (P1 On-Campus MBA).**

| Beats | Picture | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Tight shot of applied case work (approved footage), UW mark present | "Wyoming." |
| 3–10s | Pull back: the shot sits inside the letters of WYOMING | "Also Wyoming: an AACSB-accredited College of Business." (C01) · "And a one-year, in-person MBA in Laramie." (C10) |
| 10–15s | End frame: label "On-Campus MBA" | UW lockup · Apply Now |

**7. Adaptation.**

| Variant | Program identifier | In-asset line | Rough post text (primary ad text) |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | On-Campus MBA | "Also Wyoming: a one-year, in-person MBA in Laramie." (C10) | Applied case work and real-world problem solving (C12). |
| P2 | Online MBA | "Also Wyoming: an Online MBA." | 100% online and asynchronous; full- or part-time (C25). Transcripts do not identify the delivery format (C02). |

**8. Weakness / dependency.**
- **Tone:** "Also" can sound surprised that Wyoming has a business school. That is self-deprecation by implication; test "And".
- **Audience fit:** most targeting counties are in Wyoming, so the "no picture of the College" gap may be truest for the Colorado audience.
- **Craft and rules:** photo-in-type is a familiar device and can fail on a phone. Brand rules on setting the state name are unconfirmed (Q17).

## B · STRAIGHT ANSWERS

**1. Umbrella idea.** Each ad asks the blunt question a prospect hesitates to put to a school they don't know yet, and answers it with one fact UW can back up, including "No" when "No" is true.

**2. Observation / tension.**
- *Evidence (S1 p1, p5):* first exposure, and a high-commitment ask.
- *Hypothesis:* one practical doubt nobody asks aloud decides whether people keep looking.

**3. Mechanism (typographic + verbal).**
- **Scale is the stance:** the question is small and light, half-asked. The answer is one enormous word, "Yes." or "No.", surrounded by space. One restrained line underneath gives the fact behind it.
- **Not an FAQ:** no Q/A labels, no lists.
- **Internal stance only:** "nothing we can't back up". As ad copy it would itself claim universal substantiation.

**4. Constant.**
- The small question
- The giant one-word answer
- One sourced fact line
- UW lockup and a restrained program label
- Apply Now

**5. Changes by program.** The question, the answer and the fact that backs it. Each answer must be true for that variant only.

**6. Rough static (P5 M.S. in Finance).**
- **Question (small, top left):** "Is this CFP certification?"
- **Answer:** "No." fills about 60% of the frame.
- **Fact line:** "The CFP graduate certificate fulfills the education coursework requirement for CFP certification." (C47)
- **Label:** "University of Wyoming · M.S. in Finance · Online" · Apply Now.
- **Image:** mostly type, with an optional thin work/life photo band.

**15-second outline (P4 M.S. in Accounting · Online).**

| Beats | Picture | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | UW mark and label present; question types small | "GRE or GMAT required?" |
| 3–10s | "No." lands full-scale, then shrinks into a caption over a work/life shot | "GRE/GMAT not required." · "100% online and asynchronous." (C35) |
| 10–15s | End frame | "AACSB-accredited College of Business" (C01) · UW lockup · Apply Now |

**7. Adaptation.**

| Variant | Program identifier | In-asset question / answer | Rough post text |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | On-Campus MBA | "An MBA in one year?" · "Yes." (C10) | A one-year, in-person MBA in Laramie (C10), with applied case work and real-world problem solving (C12). |
| P2 | Online MBA | "Held to the same standards as in person?" · "Yes." (C02) | Same learning expectations and accreditation standards as in-person programs; transcripts do not identify the delivery format (C02). 100% online and asynchronous (C25). |
| P3 | M.S. in Accounting · On campus | "Full-time only?" · "No." (C35) | Full- or part-time; one- or two-year completion (C35). The program prepares students for the CPA exam (C36). CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| P4 | M.S. in Accounting · Online | "GRE or GMAT required?" · "No." (C35) | 100% online and asynchronous (C35). The program prepares students for the CPA exam (C36). Requirements vary by state. |
| P5 | M.S. in Finance · Online | "Is this CFP certification?" · "No." (C47) | 30-credit, 100% online (C44). Topics include investment management, tax planning, estate planning, insurance and retirement planning (C45). |

The umbrella is the question-and-answer act; the five rows change only the question, answer and fact.

**8. Weakness / dependency.**
- **"No" at first contact:** a giant "No." beside Apply Now can read as rejection, and the GRE "No." can imply admission is easy.
- **Voice:** questions must sound like a prospect without being attributed to anyone, and a series can slide into a quiz.
- **P5 depends on Q7.** The answer concerns the certificate, while the ad sells the M.S.
- **P2 depends on C02** being confirmed for this route.
- **P3 was changed.** WordSmither's first P3 question ("More undergrad accounting?") was replaced because it invited an unsourced comparison.

## C · THE STILL MARK

**1. Umbrella idea.** Graduate study arrives in a life that's already moving, so every ad holds UW perfectly still while everything around it moves.

**2. Observation / tension.**
- *Hypothesis, for all five audiences:* the program would join something already in motion (a first career year, a job, a move), never a still moment.
- The idea must not imply the programs are hard or the viewer's life is chaotic, and it says nothing about workload.

**3. Mechanism (visual; nearly wordless).**
- **Picture:** slow-shutter photography. The world in the frame is motion-blurred; only the UW lockup (Steamboat untouched, never animated, cropped or merged), the program label and Apply Now are optically sharp.
- **As a static:** a long exposure is motion already frozen, so the still carries the full idea and reduced-motion loses nothing.
- **Words:** at most one sourced word, used only where stillness gives it a second meaning.

**4. Constant.**
- Blur everywhere; sharpness only on UW's identity
- The lockup in a fixed position
- Near-wordlessness
- Apply Now

**5. Changes by program.**
- **Motion:** campus movement between classes (P1, P3); a workday (P2); an evening at home (P4); a financial-services workplace (P5).
- **Words:** the optional still word and the label.

**6. Rough static (P4 M.S. in Accounting · Online).**
- **Image:** a long exposure of an evening work/life setting (figures blurred; releases still required).
- **Sharp elements:** UW lockup · "Asynchronous." (C35) · label "M.S. in Accounting · Online" · Apply Now.

**15-second outline (P2 Online MBA).**

| Beats | Picture | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Blurred workday; UW lockup locked-off and pin-sharp | "University of Wyoming" |
| 3–10s | Commute, meetings and evening streak past; the lockup never moves | "Online MBA." · "Asynchronous." (C25) |
| 10–15s | Motion continues; still end frame | "AACSB-accredited College of Business" (C01) · UW lockup · Apply Now |

**7. Adaptation.**

| Variant | Program identifier | In-asset word (optional) | Rough post text |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | On-Campus MBA | "In person." (C10) | A one-year, in-person MBA in Laramie (C10), with applied case work and real-world problem solving (C12). |
| P2 | Online MBA | "Asynchronous." (C25) | 100% online; full- or part-time; completion in as few as three semesters (C25). Transcripts do not identify the delivery format (C02). |
| P3 | M.S. in Accounting · On campus | none | The program prepares students for the CPA exam (C36). Full- or part-time; one- or two-year completion (C35). CPA education and licensing requirements vary by state. |
| P4 | M.S. in Accounting · Online | "Asynchronous." (C35) | 100% online; GRE/GMAT not required (C35). The program prepares students for the CPA exam (C36). Requirements vary by state. |
| P5 | M.S. in Finance · Online | "Planning." (S1 p3 program focus; not a claim) | 30-credit, 100% online (C44). Topics include investment management, tax planning, estate planning, insurance and retirement planning (C45). |

The umbrella is the stillness; the rows change only what moves and the post text.

**8. Weakness / dependency.**
- **Stock trope:** blurred "busy life" footage is a category cliché, and the idea lives or dies on photographic craft. That likely means a new long-exposure shoot beyond existing Canto material.
- **Program work shifts to text:** the copy contributes little, so post text does all the program work. Visibility across placements (Q38) is its largest dependency.
- **Mark approval:** using Steamboat within the lockup follows brand rules (S4 p12), but approval is Q16/Q17.
- **Viewer knowledge:** the Steamboat resonance needs viewers to know the mark; without it, stillness alone must carry the idea.
- **Unsourced line:** "UW's oldest image" isn't sourced; C55 says only "first used … in 1921".

## Cross-territory notes (for calibration, not decisions)

- **Kinds of idea:** A is identity (who UW is), B is voice (how UW answers), C is a picture of a life (what the program joins).
- **Overlap.** A and C both draw UW's role from identity: Wyoming and Steamboat. If both advanced, the campaign would lean on region twice.
- **Precision voice.** B continues round 01's "say exactly what's true" voice, now as a whole-portfolio mechanism.
- **Where program work sits.** B carries it most inside the asset; C least.
- **Provisional references.** None of the three uses "The World Needs More Cowboys" or the S3 themes as a headline. A is closest to them in spirit (Wyoming identity), and the Q1 answer could reshape it.
- **Also explored, not presented:**
  - The Hover and The Marked Route (dropped, reasons above)
  - Frame One (application first screen)

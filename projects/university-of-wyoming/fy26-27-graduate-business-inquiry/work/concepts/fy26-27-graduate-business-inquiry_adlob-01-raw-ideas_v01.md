```
PROJECT:     University of Wyoming · FY26–27 Graduate Business Inquiry Campaign (handle)

DIRECTION:   exploratory user direction recorded in normalized-brief.md §11.4 (rev 3)

STATUS:      RAW · unranked · not selected · no refinement · not client-facing
```

**Rule:** RECOGNIZE THEM. DON'T RESCUE THEM.

**Architecture:** one campaign umbrella, later expressed through five program/delivery variants. Each idea below is a portfolio-level lens. Program facts come later, through labels and accompanying post text. No idea uses a program claim.

**Standing flags for every idea:**

- Any person shown is cast talent or approved UW/Canto photography with releases, never testimony.
- Screens, apps, receipts, reviews and documents are generic mock-ups, not real brands or real data.
- Carousel swipe order, autoplay and sound are placement-dependent and must be verified later.

**Count:** Curiosity 13 · Independence 13 · Possibility 12 · Momentum 12 = **50**.

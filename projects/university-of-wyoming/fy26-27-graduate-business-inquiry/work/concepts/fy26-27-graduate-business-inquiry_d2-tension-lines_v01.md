## The problem with the current lines

"Laptop open after the kids went to bed. Again." and the busy-week line are recognition: they show the viewer what they already know about their life. Recognition is not revelation. The tension has to turn something the viewer dismisses about themselves into evidence they never counted.

**Tension (hypothesis):** They read "Not for everyone" and assume it means not for them. Their evidence against themselves (no time, too much going on, an ordinary job) is the proof of the one thing a graduate degree on top of a life actually takes.

**Continuity flag:** "Midterm" in the selected week line describes someone already enrolled. For a prospect, the week must be their current life.

## Four tension mechanisms

### 1. The reframe: you already passed boot camp and called it normal
- Month-end close. Recital. Sick kid. Same week. **You called it a normal week.**
- Most people would call that a hard year. **You called it March.**
- Nobody gave you a medal for last Tuesday.

### 2. The flip: "not for everyone" and you assumed it meant you
- Not for everyone. **You assumed that meant you.**
- You read "not for everyone" and counted yourself out. Read the list again.

### 3. The misallocation: your grit works for everyone except you
- You've never missed anyone's deadline. **Except yours.**
- You finish everything you start. For other people.

### 4. The excuse inside out: the reason you think you can't
- Too much going on? That's the qualification.
- Your excuse is your résumé.

## Recommended sequence for comps (mechanisms 1 + 2)

| Card | Copy | Viewer's move |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | NOT FOR EVERYONE. | "Probably not for me." (self-exclusion) |
| 2 | MONTH-END CLOSE. RECITAL. SICK KID. SAME WEEK. | "That's my week." |
| 3 | YOU CALLED IT A NORMAL WEEK. | The flip: what they dismissed is the proof |
| 4 | UW lockup · program label | Apply Now (Meta CTA) |

Static: card 2 + card 3 + gold rule + NOT FOR EVERYONE.

Alternate with a harder edge: mechanism 3, "You've never missed anyone's deadline. Except yours."

## Guards

- **No program claims.** Nothing says UW is hard to get into, has attrition, or fits a busy schedule (flexibility is a claim only the online variants can support).
- **Parental guilt:** mechanism 3 and any kid-based act must not suggest the viewer is failing their family. Vary the week items so not every act is parental.
- **Presumption:** "You assumed that meant you" tells the viewer what they think. It is the most challenging and the most likely to irritate. Test it.
- **Meta personal attributes policy:** "you" statements must not assert or imply protected personal attributes (for example financial status or parental status as a targeting signal). Check before launch.
- **Variant fit:** "month-end close" is accounting; swap per variant. P1 on-campus students may not be working.

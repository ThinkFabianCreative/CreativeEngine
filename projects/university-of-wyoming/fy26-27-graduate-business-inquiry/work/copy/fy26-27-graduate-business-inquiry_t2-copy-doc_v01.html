Copy rules

  • Week lines: three items, then SAME WEEK. Caps, full stop after each item. No “my” or other possessive pronouns (KID'S, GRANDMA'S are fine). One work item, one life item, one that makes it absurd.
  • No school items in T2 weeks, except P1-B and P3-B (seniors) and P2-D (already studying).
  • Answer line is YOU SOUND LIKE A COWBOY. Never on the same card as the tagline.
  • Voice: flush left, deadpan, no exclamation marks.
  • Never in the proof slot: STEM, tuition or cost, rankings, job placement, salary, pass rates, duration in semesters or months, “Meets CPA requirements”, “AACSB-accredited MBA” (it is the College that is accredited).
  • Rotation: first flight runs the A weeks; B, C (and P2-D) rotate in. Build one version per proof line until Meta dynamic text is verified.

## The problem
People who have inquired about a UW business master's stall before they apply, or apply and never enroll. To them every program looks the same, so UW never gives them a reason to choose it this cycle.

## Brand platform
- **Brand promise:** Wyoming-Built. Globally Prepared. Entrepreneurially Driven. (College strategy document; currency OPEN. It reads as a line; a competitor could not sign "Wyoming-Built", but the need it meets is MISSING.)
- **Core Creative Idea:** *Stories where UW graduate business students do the real work before they graduate.*
- **Human truth:** *"I want my career to move, but I can't stake time and real money on a degree that only adds letters after my name."*

## The jobs to be done
### On-Campus MBA
- **Persuade:** *early-career graduates* to apply to UW's MBA.
- **Behavior to change:** *They treat MBAs as interchangeable and default to a bigger-name or closer program, or put it off a year.*
- **Insight:** *"Every brochure says hands-on. None show me what I'd do on a Tuesday."*
- **New belief:** *At UW the MBA happens in real rooms in Wyoming and beyond, not only in a classroom.*
- **USP:** Experiences tied to Wyoming's places: Jackson Hole Leadership Summit ⚑, Sheridan ⚑, the State Capitol ⚑, Spain. Unique in place, not in "experiential"; defensible because place can't be copied. No STEM, no value claim.
- **Say this:** *You'll do the real work before you graduate.*
- **Proof:** Global Business course in Spain · Friday Executive Speaker Series ⚑ · Jackson Hole, Sheridan, Capitol experiences ⚑ (not every cohort or term).
- **Barrier:** Credential. Lead with what they would actually do.
### Online MBA
- **Persuade:** *working professionals* to start this term.
- **Behavior to change:** *They have inquired at three or four online MBAs and stall at "not this term".*
- **Insight:** *"If I wait for a quiet year, I'll never do it."*
- **New belief:** *I can do this now, around the job I already have.*
- **USP:** **Parity.** AACSB-accredited College, fully asynchronous, 30 credits, multiple starts; competitors say the same. Price (under $30K ⚑) may differentiate once approved. Distinctiveness must come from the idea.
- **Say this:** Advance your career without stepping away from it.
- **Proof:** AACSB-accredited College of Business · 100% online, asynchronous · 30 credits · multiple starts · under $30K ⚑
- **Barrier:** Aspirational ("not for me, not now"). Lead with emotion.
### M.S. in Accounting
- **Persuade:** *accounting graduates and early-career accountants* to choose UW for their CPA-route master's.
- **Behavior to change:** *They treat the master's as credit-collecting to reach 150 hours, and buy the cheapest credits.*
- **Insight:** *"Credits make me eligible. They don't get me through the exam."*
- **New belief:** *This program is built around preparing me for the CPA exam.*
- **USP:** **Pre-emptive.** Many programs prepare for the CPA; UW can be first to dramatize preparation as the point. College-funded CPA prep materials ⚑ and on-campus STEM ⚑ may make it real once approved. No passage, eligibility or licensure promise.
- **Say this:** *Built to get you through the CPA, not just to it.* ⚑ (implies outcome; needs approved wording)
- **Proof:** AACSB · prepares students for the CPA exam · CPA prep materials ⚑ · STEM, on-campus only ⚑. Online and on-campus executions stay separate.
- **Barrier:** Credential. Lead with proof.
### M.S. in Finance (Financial Planning & Wealth Management)
- **Persuade:** *early-career finance professionals* to enter wealth management through a degree.
- **Behavior to change:** *They plan to build credentials on the job and don't see a master's as the faster way in.*
- **Insight:** *"Clients trust a network. I don't have one yet."*
- **New belief:** *This degree puts me close to the wealth industry, not only its theory.*
- **USP:** The Jackson Hole wealth ecosystem: family offices, trust companies, advisory firms ⚑. Unique and defensible (place); unusable until substantiated.
- **Say this:** *The degree that sits next to the Jackson Hole wealth world.* ⚑
- **Proof:** CFP graduate certificate meets the CFP education requirement (not certification) · Jackson Hole ecosystem ⚑
- **Barrier:** Aspirational. Lead with the world they want to enter.

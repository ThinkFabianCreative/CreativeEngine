## The problem
People who have already inquired about a UW business master's stall before they apply, or apply and never enroll. To them every program looks the same, so UW never gets a reason to win.

## Brand platform
- **Brand line:** Wyoming-Built. Globally Prepared. Entrepreneurially Driven.
- **Core Creative Idea:** *Stories where UW graduate business students do the real work before they graduate.*
- **Human truth:** *"I want my career to move, but I can't stake time and real money on a degree that only adds letters after my name."*

## The jobs to be done
### On-Campus MBA
- **Persuade:** early-career grads to choose the experience over the name.
- **Behavior to change:** *They treat MBAs as interchangeable and default to a bigger-name or closer program, or put it off a year.*
- **Insight:** *"Every brochure says hands-on. None show me what I'd do on a Tuesday."*
- **New belief:** *UW's MBA is lived in the real world, not just taught in a classroom.*
- **USP:** Applied experiences no classroom MBA offers: Jackson Hole Leadership Summit, Sheridan, Spain, State Capitol. Affordable and immersive, with close faculty access.
- **Say this:** *You'll do the real work before you graduate.*
- **Proof:** Jackson Hole Leadership Summit · Sheridan · Spain · State Capitol · Friday executive speakers.
### Online MBA
- **Persuade:** working professionals to stop waiting for a quiet year.
- **Behavior to change:** *They've inquired at three or four online MBAs and stall at "not this term".*
- **Insight:** *"If I wait for a quiet year, I'll never do it."*
- **New belief:** *I can grow my career without pausing it, and the value makes it realistic now.*
- **USP:** Accredited, fully asynchronous, 30 credits, under $30K ⚑. Value-led rather than unique: competitors also offer online MBAs.
- **Say this:** Advance your career without stepping away from it.
- **Proof:** AACSB · 100% online · 30 credits · multiple starts · under $30K ⚑
### MS Accounting
- **Persuade:** accounting grads to choose a program that gets them through the CPA.
- **Behavior to change:** *They treat the master's as credit-collecting to reach 150 hours, and choose the cheapest credits.*
- **Insight:** *"Credits make me eligible. I need to pass."*
- **New belief:** *This program is built to get me through the CPA, not just make me eligible.*
- **USP:** College-funded CPA prep after 50% completion ⚑, STEM designation (on-campus) ⚑, AACSB.
- **Say this:** *Built to get you through the CPA, not just to it.*
- **Proof:** AACSB · STEM (on-campus) ⚑ · college-funded CPA prep ⚑
### MS Financial Planning & Wealth Management
- **Persuade:** finance professionals to discover a degree that is also a network.
- **Behavior to change:** *They plan to earn credentials on the job and don't see a master's as the faster way in.*
- **Insight:** *"Clients trust a network. I don't have one yet."*
- **New belief:** *This degree gives me expertise and a way into the wealth industry's network.*
- **USP:** The Jackson Hole wealth ecosystem: family offices, trust companies and advisory firms ⚑. Few universities can replicate it.
- **Say this:** *The degree that walks you into the Jackson Hole wealth world.*
- **Proof:** family offices, trust companies, advisory firms ⚑

⚑ Needs UW approval before it runs in an ad. No salary or ROI figures without verified data.

## By stage
- **Inquired:** Should I apply? → **Apply Now**
- **Started an application:** Is it worth finishing? Use real deadlines only → **Complete Your Application**
- **Applied, not enrolled:** Why UW? → **Visit Campus** (MBA) · **Enroll Now** (online)

## Tone
Confident, grounded, direct. Show the opportunity; don't claim it.
- **Never:** "unlock your potential", "next level", "dream big", graduation caps, laptop-in-a-café, stock cowboys.

## Ambition
- **Rung:** *Fresh (6 of 10) in every cell. Showtime (7) for the On-Campus MBA and FP&WM inquiry ads.*
- **Benchmarks:** *The Economist red posters · Apple "Shot on iPhone" · Spotify data billboards.*
- **Test:** would a current UW student repost it?

## Deliverables
- **Assets:** 4 programs × 3 stages = 12 statics (carousel optional) + 12 fifteen-second videos. *Two headline variants per static.*
- **Production:** Canto imagery, *no live-action shoot.*
- **Media:** Meta retargeting, with an A/B test of the program page against the ERx portal.
- **Timing:** *Wave 1 in market mid-November 2026; Wave 2 February–June 2027.*
- **Success:** application starts → completions → enrollment, by program and stage. *+15% / +10% vs last cycle.*

# UW Graduate Business · Creative Brief

TRAFFIK · v03 · 2026-10-26 · Draft for UW approval · *Italics = assumption, not yet validated*

## The problem
People who have already inquired about a UW business master's stall before they apply, or apply and never enroll. To them every program looks the same, so UW never gets a reason to win.

## Brand
**Wyoming-Built. Globally Prepared. Entrepreneurially Driven.**
**Core Creative Idea:** *Stories where UW graduate business students do the real work before they graduate.*

## Human truth
*"I want my career to move, but I can't stake time and real money on a degree that only adds letters after my name."*

## The jobs

| Program | Persuade… | Insight | Say this | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **On-Campus MBA** | Early-career grads to choose the experience over the name | *"Every brochure says hands-on. None show me what I'd do on a Tuesday."* | *You'll do the real work before you graduate.* | Jackson Hole Summit · Sheridan · Spain · State Capitol · Friday executive speakers |
| **Online MBA** | Working professionals to stop waiting for a quiet year | *"If I wait for a quiet year, I'll never do it."* | Advance your career without stepping away from it. | AACSB · 100% online · 30 credits · multiple starts · under $30K ⚑ |
| **MS Accounting** | Accounting grads to choose a program that gets them through the CPA | *"Credits make me eligible. I need to pass."* | *Built to get you through the CPA, not just to it.* | AACSB · STEM (on-campus) ⚑ · college-funded CPA prep ⚑ |
| **MS FP&WM** | Finance professionals to discover a degree that is also a network | *"Clients trust a network. I don't have one yet."* | *The degree that walks you into the Jackson Hole wealth world.* | Family offices, trust companies, advisory firms ⚑ |

⚑ = needs UW approval before it runs in an ad. No salary or ROI figures without verified data.

## By stage

| Stage | The prospect's question | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Inquired | Should I apply? | Apply Now |
| Started an application | Is it worth finishing? Use real deadlines only | Complete Your Application |
| Applied, not enrolled | Why UW? | Visit Campus (MBA) · Enroll Now (online) |

## Tone
Confident, grounded, direct. Show the opportunity; don't claim it. **Never:** "unlock your potential", "next level", "dream big", graduation caps, laptop-in-a-café, stock cowboys.

## Ambition
*Fresh (6 of 10) in every cell. Showtime (7) for the On-Campus MBA and FP&WM inquiry ads.*
*Benchmarks: The Economist red posters · Apple "Shot on iPhone" · Spotify data billboards.*
**Test:** would a current UW student repost it?

## Deliverables
4 programs × 3 stages = 12 statics (carousel optional) + 12 fifteen-second videos. *Two headline variants per static.* Canto imagery, *no live-action shoot.* Meta retargeting, with an A/B test of the program page against the ERx portal.

**Timing:** *Wave 1 in market mid-November 2026; Wave 2 February–June 2027.*
**Success:** application starts → completions → enrollment, by program and stage. *+15% / +10% vs last cycle.*

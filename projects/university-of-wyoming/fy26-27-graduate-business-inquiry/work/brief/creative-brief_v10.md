## The problem
People who have inquired about a UW business master's stall before they apply, or apply and never enroll. To them every program looks the same, so UW never gives them a reason to choose it this cycle.

- **Observed (live ads):** UW's own live Meta ads already say "applied case work and real-world problem solving", "One year" and "We get you", words CSU, Daniels and Eccles also use.

## Brand platform
- **Brand promise:** Wyoming-Built. Globally Prepared. Entrepreneurially Driven. (College strategy document S3; currency OPEN. A competitor could not sign "Wyoming-Built"; the need it meets is MISSING.)
- **Core Creative Idea (CCI):** *Stories where UW graduate business students do the real work before they graduate.*
- **Human truth:** *"I want my career to move, but I can't stake time and real money on a degree that only adds letters after my name."*

## The jobs to be done
### On-Campus MBA
- **Persuade:** *early-career graduates* to apply to UW's MBA.
- **Behavior to change:** *They treat MBAs as interchangeable and default to a bigger-name or closer program, or put it off a year.*
- **Insight:** *"Every brochure says hands-on. None show me what I'd do on a Tuesday."* Assumption: no VOC for or against.
- **New belief:** *At UW the MBA happens in real rooms in Wyoming and beyond.* Serves **Wyoming-Built** (and Globally Prepared via Spain).
- **USP:** Specific Wyoming experiences: the Capitol during session, Sheridan. No competitor claims these (0 found); study abroad is also claimed by CU Denver (2 programs), and "real-world/applied" by 4 advertisers incl. UW. Ownability rests on the named places. No STEM, no value claim.
- **Where students are shown working (S1, S3):** consulting and industry projects on real business challenges ⚑ · engaging elected officials at the State Capitol during session ⚑ · Sheridan economic-development experience ⚑ · the Global Business course in Spain · questioning executives at the Friday Speaker Series ⚑. Show the work, not the scenery.
- **Say this:** *You'll do the real work before you graduate, at the Capitol, in Sheridan.* Without the places, CSU, Daniels and Eccles could sign it.
- **Proof:** Spain (S1, cleared to highlight) · the rest ⚑, not every cohort or term.
- **Barrier:** Credential. Lead with what they would actually do.
### Online MBA
- **Persuade:** *working professionals* to start this term.
- **Behavior to change:** *They have inquired at three or four online MBAs and keep pushing the start: fall becomes spring becomes summer.* UW offers fall, spring and summer starts (S1); dates MISSING (Q10).
- **Stall moment:** *the start-term deadline they let pass "until work calms down".*
- **Insight:** *"If I wait for a quiet year, I'll never do it."* Assumption: 0 quotes for it; the evidence leans to a network motive ("networking … severely reduced", #24).
- **New belief:** *I can start a Wyoming-built MBA now, around the job I already have.* Serves **Wyoming-Built**.
- **USP:** **Parity on format**, honestly labeled: AACSB, async, ~30–45 credits and 3–5 starts are claimed by 4 of 4 competitors checked. **Potential differentiator: price.** Under $30K ⚑ would be lowest among the 4 regional competitors checked ($32,400); national online programs (e.g. WGU) not checked.
- **Say this:** *Do the real work of a Wyoming MBA this term, inside the job you already have.* CSU says nearly the same; only price ⚑ or Wyoming experiences open online make it ownable.
- **Proof:** AACSB-accredited College of Business · 100% online, asynchronous · 30 credits · fall, spring, summer starts · under $30K ⚑. No on-campus experiences unless confirmed available online.
- **Barrier:** Aspirational ("not for me, not now"). Lead with emotion.
### M.S. in Finance (Financial Planning & Wealth Management)
- **Persuade:** *early-career finance professionals* to enter wealth management through a degree.
- **Behavior to change:** *They plan to build credentials on the job and don't see a master's as the faster way in.*
- **Insight (VOC #18, #23):** Practitioners say clients don't care about the master's; career changers ask how to get in at all. Tension: *"I need a way in, and the people inside say the degree isn't it."*
- **New belief:** *This degree is a way into the wealth industry, not only its theory.* Serves **Wyoming-Built** (Jackson Hole) and **Entrepreneurially Driven**.
- **USP:** The Jackson Hole wealth ecosystem ⚑. No competitor claims place-based wealth access (0 of 3). Unique and defensible; unusable until substantiated.
- **How students meet that world:** **MISSING.** Sources say only "exposure to family offices, trust companies, and advisory firms" (S3). UW must supply who, where, how often, and whether online students get it.
- **Say this:** *Get inside the Jackson Hole wealth world while you study.* ⚑ (access)
- **Proof:** CFP graduate certificate meets the CFP education requirement (not certification) · Jackson Hole ecosystem ⚑
- **Barrier:** Aspirational. Lead with the world they want to enter.

## The problem
People who have inquired about UW's M.S. in Accounting stall before they apply, or apply and never enroll, because they see the master's as interchangeable credit hours toward 150, and UW never gives them a reason to choose it.

## Brand platform
- **Brand promise:** Wyoming-Built. Globally Prepared. Entrepreneurially Driven. (S3; currency OPEN; the need it meets is MISSING.)
- **Core Creative Idea (CCI):** *Stories where UW graduate business students do the real work before they graduate.*
- **Human truth:** *"I want my career to move, but I can't stake time and real money on a degree that only adds letters after my name."*

## The job to be done
### M.S. in Accounting (on-campus and online, executed separately)
- **Persuade:** *accounting graduates and early-career accountants* to choose UW for their CPA-route master's.
- **Behavior to change (sourced):** They treat the master's as credit-collecting and buy the cheapest credits ("cheap community college/online courses", #1; "about $10,000", #3; `2026-10-26_voice-of-customer.md`). The 150-hour logic is weakening: a 120-credit + experience route now exists (DU, 2026-07-01), and USU says its MAcc cuts Utah's experience requirement to one year (`2026-10-26_competitor-map.md`). UW's position on both is MISSING.
- **Insight (sourced):** "Credits make me eligible. They don't get me through the exam." Supported by 6 of the 7 exam-readiness quotes (#7–9, #11–13; #10 says prep is common, so it complicates rather than supports; the research summary counts all 7) ("I have the credits in theory … I feel underprepared", #12; "to be certain I pass", #8; `2026-10-26_voice-of-customer.md`). Contradicted by 2 ("nobody gives a damn about a masters", #6). A second motive, not used here: recruiting and connections (3 quotes, #14–16), which CU Denver and USU already claim (Big Four, `2026-10-26_competitor-map.md`).
- **New belief:** *This program is built around preparing me for the CPA exam.* Weaker link, stated plainly: no promise word is served directly. The tie to the platform is the CCI (exam preparation as the real work) and the human truth (more than letters after a name).
- **USP:** **Pre-emptive, with one possible unique.** "Prepares you for the CPA exam" is said by 5 of 7 competitors (CSU, CU Denver, MSU Denver, Montana, Utah). College-funded prep materials ⚑ appear on none of the 7 pages; a Reddit commenter names Auburn and Tennessee Tech as including prep (`2026-10-26_competitor-map.md`). No passage, eligibility or licensure promise.
- **Showable artifact:** College-funded CPA preparation materials, provided after 50% completion (S3) ⚑ wording. The materials themselves can be shown if UW confirms what they are (provider, format). If not approved, UW must supply another concrete artifact (a prep session, a faculty review, a study plan). "Most students pass one or more sections" (S1) is an outcome claim and stays out.
- **Say this:** *Built to get you through the CPA, not just to it.* ⚑ (implies outcome; needs approved wording). Any of the 5 could sign it until the funded materials are named.
- **Proof:** AACSB-accredited College · prepares students for the CPA exam · CPA prep materials ⚑ · STEM, on-campus only ⚑ · online: fall, spring, summer starts, GRE/GMAT not required.
- **Barrier:** Credential. Lead with proof.

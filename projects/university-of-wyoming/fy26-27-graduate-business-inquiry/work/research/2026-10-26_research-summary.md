# Research summary · UW Graduate Business · 2026-10-26

CLIENT: University of Wyoming · PROJECT: FY26–27 Graduate Business · by creative-researcher. Observations, not insights. Files: `2026-10-26_competitor-map.md`, `2026-10-26_voice-of-customer.md`, `2026-10-26_benchmarks.md`.

## Patterns (sources supporting)
1. **Format parity is real.** AACSB, async/self-paced, ~30–45 credits and 3–5 starts a year are claimed by every online MBA checked (4 of 4). Bears on **std 6, 8**.
2. **No competitor in this sample is under $30K for an online MBA.** Lowest: CU Denver $32,400 in-state; then CSU ~$42K, USU $45K, Utah $72K (4 sources; WGU not checked). Bears on **std 8** (the ⚑ price hook).
3. **"Prepares you for the CPA exam" is said by 5 of 7 accounting competitors;** college-funded prep materials are claimed by none of the 7 pages, though a Reddit commenter names 2 other schools whose MAcc includes prep. Bears on **std 6, 8**.
4. **Accounting audience splits.** Cheapest-credits view: 6 quotes. Exam-readiness reason: 7 quotes. Recruiting/network reason (not in the brief): 3 quotes. Bears on **std 5**.
5. **CPA route is changing.** A 120-credit + experience pathway is now described by DU (2026-07-01) and USU says its MAcc cuts Utah's experience requirement from two years to one from 2026-07-01 (2 sources). Bears on **std 5, 6** (the "credits to 150" framing).
6. **Finance: master's value is contested by practitioners.** 3 quotes say clients/hiring don't care; 2 career-changer posts ask whether the MS is the way in; 1 says "build your own book". No competitor claims place-based wealth access (0 of 3). Bears on **std 5, 8, 12**.
7. **Online MBA felt gap is network, not format** (3 quotes). No quotes found on postponing a start term. Bears on **std 5**.
8. **Place.** No competitor claims Wyoming-like place experiences (Capitol, Sheridan); study abroad is also claimed by CU Denver (2 programs). Bears on **std 6, 8**.
9. **Benchmarks.** One new verified higher-ed rung-7 candidate (Monash, VML, D&AD/Cannes); one higher-ed retargeting example at a lower rung (Uni Compare, 360 OM). No awarded retargeting work found. Bears on **std 10**.

10. **Live Meta ads (ad-library MCP; 7 advertisers including UW).** Flexibility (5 of 7), speed (4), "real-world/applied" (4), no GMAT/GRE (3), ranking (3), persona flattery (3). UW's own 5 ads already say "applied case work and real-world problem solving", "One year" and "We get you", so the brief's "real work" proposition is currently stated in UW's ads in words that CSU, Daniels and Eccles also use. Bears on **std 6, 8**.
11. **Competitors run labeled retargeting.** CSU (7 `rtg` ads, agency VisionPoint) and Daniels (`retarget` ads with an Apply now CTA, `prospect` ads with Learn more). This is the closest thing found to a lower-funnel benchmark: a working practice, not an awarded one. Bears on **std 10**.
12. **Visual.** Only 2 creatives viewed: campus or landscape (CSU) and a laptop at night (UW, close to the brief's "laptop-in-a-café" ban). Bears on **std 12**; the sample is too thin to count.

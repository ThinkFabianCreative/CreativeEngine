THE CAMPAIGN · NOT FOR EVERYONE (grit and perseverance)
====================================================================
THE IDEA
Adults who go back to school are often told they are doing too much, or that they are
too stubborn. That "flaw" is the grit it takes. The ad makes the viewer recognise their
own chaotic week, then flips "stubborn" into a Cowboy trait.
The reader should think: "That's my week... and yes, I'm too stubborn to stop."

PHOTOGRAPHY
- One photo per program, cropped progressively: card 1 tight on the key object, card 2 the whole
  scene, card 3 the dark top of the same photo. All current-life scenes are off campus.
- Placeholder images must carry "PLACEHOLDER IMAGE - not for external use".

DESIGN RULES
- Two versions: TYPE ONLY, and PHOTO (a person-free still life of the week, supplied
  as an internal placeholder image, labelled "PLACEHOLDER IMAGE - not for external use").
  Never icons or cowboy imagery.
- PHOTO version: keep the photo in its planned area; set type on the solid UW Brown or
  darkest area only. Grade must stay warm and dark so Brown still dominates.
- Headline in Montserrat Bold (Gotham stand-in). Line 1 about 1.5x the size of line 2.
- Emphasis: one Gold word per card (SAME WEEK. / STUBBORN / COWBOY).
- Answer line wording follows the client deck (2026-09-14): "YOU SOUND LIKE A COWBOY."
    Version A: those words in UW Gold.
    Version B: headline all White; a rough dry-brush UW Gold underline under those two
    words only, slightly wider than the word, slight tilt, not touching the letters.
- Deadpan layout: flush left, generous but purposeful space, no exclamation marks.
- The question mark stays. It turns a statement into a question to the reader.
- No single-image static. Carousel or animated video only (11_uw_campaign.txt).

DO NOT
- Re-add old lines ("SAID FRIDAY. FRIDAY.", "TOLD THEM THE REAL NUMBER.",
  "READ THE WHOLE CONTRACT.", "PUT YOUR NAME ON IT.", "REAL COWBOYS NEVER QUIT.").
- Use gradients or a black fade.
- Make "COWBOY" look like a rodeo poster.

T2 · "TOO STUBBORN TO STOP?"
====================================================================
THE IDEA
The viewer recognises their own impossible week, then "stubborn" is flipped into a
Cowboy trait. The reader should think: "That's my week... and yes, I'm too stubborn
to stop."

FORMAT: each post is a 5-CARD CAROUSEL or a 15s ANIMATED VIDEO. No single static.
  Card 1:  WEEK LINE                                        Gold: SAME WEEK.
  Card 2:  TOO STUBBORN TO STOP?                            Gold: STUBBORN
  Card 3:  YOU SOUND LIKE A COWBOY.                         Gold: COWBOY
  Card 4:  GRADUATE PROGRAMS THAT WORK AS HARD AS YOU DO.   No Gold word
           + program label
  Card 5:  ONE PROOF LINE (see PROOF LINES) + logo + program label.
           Gold rule under the proof line. No Gold word.
- Logo + program label on EVERY card. One Gold word per card, never more.
- Carousel 1080 x 1080. Card 1 tight on the week's key object, card 2 the whole scene,
  card 3 the dark top of the same photo, cards 4-5 flat UW Brown (card 5 on-campus
  programs: campus photo band, see 11_uw_campaign.txt END CARD).
- VIDEO 15s, 4:5 and 9:16: one continuous camera pull-out on the week scene.
    0.0-4.0   week builds one item at a time, then SAME WEEK. (logo + label from frame 1)
    4.0-7.0   TOO STUBBORN TO STOP?
    7.0-7.6   silence, camera stops
    7.6-10.0  YOU SOUND LIKE A COWBOY.
    10.0-12.5 tagline + program label, on flat Brown
    12.5-15.0 proof line + logo + label
  No voiceover. All words are on-screen type. Must work with sound off.

  M.S. IN ACCOUNTING

    B  TWO FINALS. PART-TIME TAX PREP. ROOMMATE MOVES OUT. SAME WEEK.
    C  FIRST BUSY SEASON. BANK RECS DUE. BABY SHOWER. SAME WEEK.

    A  MONTH-END CLOSE. INVENTORY COUNT. REC LEAGUE FINAL. SAME WEEK.
    B  PAYROLL FRIDAY. QUARTERLY FILINGS. SCIENCE FAIR. SAME WEEK.

- Break week lines only at full stops. Use typographic apostrophes (KID’S, WON’T,
  GRANDMA’S), never straight quotes.

  pass rates, duration in semesters or months, "Meets CPA requirements".

  Brown dominates). Until the shoot exists, use a placeholder labelled
  "PLACEHOLDER IMAGE - not for external use".
- Online programs: work or home settings, never campus. On-campus programs: off-campus
  week scene; campus appears only on the card 5 photo band.

DESIGN RULES
- Two versions: TYPE ONLY (all cards on flat UW Brown) and PHOTO.
- Montserrat Bold (Gotham stand-in). Flush left, deadpan, no exclamation marks.
- The question mark on card 2 stays.
- Emphasis variants for STUBBORN (card 2) and COWBOY (card 3):
    Version A: the word in UW Gold.
    Version B: word in White with a rough dry-brush UW Gold underline, slightly wider
               than the word, slight tilt, not touching the letters.
    Version C (test): COWBOY in Blackhawk Italic, lowercase glyph setting, real font only.
- Never icons or cowboy imagery. No gradients, black fades, contour or topographic
  textures, dotted lines, drawn buttons.

DO NOT

T3 · STUDENTS (REAL UW STUDENTS AND ALUMNI)
====================================================================
THE IDEA
T2 asks the question. T3 is someone who already answered it: a real student saying
their own week, and the thing they thought would rule them out.

FORMAT: 15s video, spoken to camera. 4:5 and 9:16.
    0-3s    student says their week; super: the week in T2's style
            (SAME WEEK. in Gold); logo + program label from frame 1
    3-10s   student's turn line; ONE fact super, about 6-10s (T2 proof list, lead line)
    10-15s  last line on the student; end frame: logo, program label,
            FIRST NAME · CURRENT STUDENT or FIRST NAME · ALUM
- Captions are required (they transcribe the speech). Supers and captions must not collide.
- No "YOU SOUND LIKE A COWBOY." and no tagline card on T3.

RULES
- Real students only. NO AI-generated people, faces or voices, ever, including comps.
  Until the Laramie shoot exists, use a grey frame labelled
  "STUDENT FOOTAGE - LARAMIE SHOOT - PLACEHOLDER".
- Spoken lines come from the approved script for THAT student only. Never put a line
  from one student's script on another student's footage.
- Online programs: work or home settings, never campus. On-campus programs: campus
  interiors, people in action, no exteriors or landmarks.

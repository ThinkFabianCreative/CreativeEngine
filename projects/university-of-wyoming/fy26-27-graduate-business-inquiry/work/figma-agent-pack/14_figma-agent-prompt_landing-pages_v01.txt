======================================================================
ROLE AND GOAL
======================================================================
You are building static, high-fidelity landing page mockups in Figma for five University
of Wyoming College of Business graduate programs. The pages exist to drive inquiries
(request-info form submissions) from people who arrived from Meta ads.

This is a round-1 approval mock. It is NOT a prototype and NOT production. No
interactions, no prototype links, no variants beyond what is asked. The pages will later
be rebuilt natively in Instapage, so build structure Instapage can reproduce: stacked
full-width sections, simple 12-column grid, no effects Instapage can't make.

STRUCTURAL REFERENCE (structure only, never its look)
The section rhythm of the Miami Ad School Art Direction Master's page: a long scroll of
full-bleed color-blocked sections, each one job, each with a big stacked uppercase
headline, a short paragraph and at most one action. Borrow that rhythm and modularity.
Do NOT borrow its pinks, purples, collage art, playful illustration or type. The look
is UW: Brown dominant, Gold as accent, Gotham/Montserrat, flat fills.

======================================================================
THE TWO NON-NEGOTIABLES
======================================================================
A) VIDEO IS PROMINENT
- The video player sits directly under the hero, full content width (desktop: 8 cols,
  form occupies the right 4), 16:9, large play button (Gold circle, Brown triangle),
  duration chip "0:60 PLACEHOLDER", caption line under it.
- Poster frame: grey fill labelled "VIDEO POSTER – UW FOOTAGE PLACEHOLDER". No
  AI-generated people, faces or students, ever.
- On mobile the video is the first thing after the hero headline, full bleed.

B) THE FORM IS PERSISTENT
Static mock, so show persistence explicitly:
- Desktop: Form Card pinned in the right 4 columns from the hero down. Show it in the hero
  frame AND repeat it, identical, beside the curriculum section and in the final CTA
  section, with a small annotation outside the artboard: "STICKY – stays in view on
  scroll (Instapage sticky block)". Put a red dashed annotation line showing its scroll
  range.
- Mobile: form inline after the video, PLUS a Sticky Mobile CTA Bar fixed at the bottom of
  every mobile screen-height section ("REQUEST INFO" Gold button, program label left).
  Annotate: "Sticky bar opens/jumps to form".
- Form Card fields (keep it short, max 5): First name · Last name · Email · Phone
  (optional) · Program of interest (dropdown, pre-filled with this page's program) ·
  Button "REQUEST INFO" · micro-line "We'll send program details. No spam." in Caption.
  White card on Brown, 24px radius max 8, no shadow, Brown labels, 1px Brown 30% field
  borders, 56px fields.
- Field values are placeholders. No real personal data.

SECTIONS (top to bottom; alternate backgrounds Brown / Cream / White / Brown 90)
======================================================================
Each section = one job, one big stacked headline, ≤60 words body, ≤1 action.
Copy is placeholder: write [BRACKETED SLOT DESCRIPTIONS] unless approved copy is supplied.
Never invent program facts, costs, rankings, salaries, outcomes, deadlines or "STEM".

01 NAV (Brown): UW lockup placeholder component left; anchor links (Program · Curriculum ·
   Faculty · Admissions · FAQ); Gold "REQUEST INFO" button right.
02 HERO (Brown): eyebrow = program label; Display XL headline [CAMPAIGN HEADLINE – FROM
   APPROVED AD LINE] 3 stacked lines; one sentence sub; form card right. Image slot
   (labelled placeholder) optional behind left half, never mountains, horizons, caps.
03 VIDEO (Brown 90): Video Player as above, + one-line caption [WHAT THE VIDEO SHOWS].
04 PROGRAM AT A GLANCE (Cream): 4 Stat Slots in a row: [FORMAT] [CREDITS] [LENGTH]
   [START TERMS] — big numerals area, label under. Values = "[CLIENT TO SUPPLY]".
05 WHY THIS PROGRAM (White): Display L headline + 3 short reason blocks, left-aligned
   numbered 01/02/03, thin Brown rule above each.
06 STUDENT VOICE (Brown): one large Quote Card: grey photo slot "STUDENT PHOTO – LARAMIE
   SHOOT – PLACEHOLDER", quote "[REAL STUDENT QUOTE – SUPPLIED BY UW]", first name +
   CURRENT STUDENT/ALUM. Gold quotation mark is the single Gold moment of the section.
07 CURRICULUM (Cream): headline + 2-column course list (8 slots "[COURSE NAME]") +
   secondary button "DOWNLOAD PROGRAM GUIDE". Form card repeats on the right (desktop).
08 FACULTY (White): 3 portrait slots (grey, labelled), name/title slots.
09 HOW TO APPLY (Brown): 3 numbered steps in a vertical stepper (Miami's "three steps"
   pattern): Step 1/2/3 with [STEP] text. Gold step numbers.
10 DEADLINES & COST (Cream): two cards. All values in dashed boxes "[CLIENT TO SUPPLY]".
    Not gold, not the largest element.
11 FAQ (White): 5 FAQ Rows, collapsed state, + / – icon. First one shown open.
12 FINAL CTA (Brown): Display XL [CLOSING LINE], Form Card centered (mobile) / right
    (desktop).
13 FOOTER (Black or Brown 90): lockup, address slot, legal slot, social icons.

Section padding: 160px top/bottom desktop, 80px mobile. Content max 1200px.

======================================================================
ROLE AND GOAL
======================================================================
You are building static, mid/high-fidelity WIREFRAMES in Figma for five University of
Wyoming College of Business graduate program landing pages. The pages exist to drive
inquiries (request-info form submissions) from people arriving from Meta ads.

The client is approving: section order, what each section does, content hierarchy,
the prominence of the video, and the persistent form. They are NOT approving color,
typography, imagery or brand look. Make that obvious: everything must read as a
wireframe, so nobody gives feedback on style.

Not a prototype, not production. No interactions, no prototype links. Pages will later
be designed, then rebuilt natively in Instapage, so keep structure Instapage can
reproduce: stacked full-width sections on a 12-column grid.

STRUCTURAL REFERENCE (structure only)
The section rhythm of the Miami Ad School Art Direction Master's page: a long scroll of
full-width sections, each with one job, a big headline, a short paragraph and at most one
action. Borrow the rhythm and modularity. None of its look.

======================================================================
CONVERSION LOGIC (why the page is short)
======================================================================
Visitors come from a Meta ad and decide fast. Keep the page SHORT: 7 sections on desktop,
roughly 5–6 screen-heights. Anything that is "nice to know" moves into a carousel (swipe
sideways instead of scrolling down) or an overlay (opened on demand). The vertical scroll
holds only: promise → video → proof → reasons → how to apply → form.
Every overlay and every last carousel card ends with a "REQUEST INFO" button.

HOW TO SHOW MOTION IN A STATIC FILE
- Carousels: draw card 1 fully, card 2 peeking 20% at the right edge, arrows (desktop) and
  dots (all). Next to the artboard, lay out ALL cards side by side in a strip labelled
  "CAROUSEL 05 – ALL CARDS", connected with an annotation arrow.
- Overlays: draw each overlay as its own frame beside the page (desktop 1440 with a 60%
  Black scrim over a greyed page; mobile as a full-screen sheet). Annotation arrow from the
  trigger button to the overlay frame: "OPENS OVERLAY 07A".

======================================================================
SECTIONS (top to bottom)
======================================================================
Each section = one job, one headline, ≤40 words body, ≤1 primary action.
All copy is bracketed slot descriptions: [SLOT – WHAT GOES HERE]. Do not write marketing
copy or invent facts, numbers, costs, rankings, outcomes or deadlines.
Label each section on the artboard edge ("01 HERO") so the client can reference it.

00 NAV (slim, 64px): logo box left; "REQUEST INFO" button right. No anchor links (fewer
   exits). Mobile: logo + button only.
01 HERO + VIDEO (one screen): label = program name; H1 [CAMPAIGN HEADLINE] (2 lines max);
   one-line sub; video player 8 cols beside the form card (4 cols). Above the fold on
   1440x900. Mobile: headline, then full-width video, then the sticky bar.
   The video thumbnail opens OVERLAY 01A: full-screen video player, with an end-state
   card "[CLOSING LINE] + REQUEST INFO".
02 AT A GLANCE (a strip, not a section): 4 Stat Slots in one row directly under the hero:
   [FORMAT] [CREDITS] [LENGTH] [START TERMS], values "[CLIENT TO SUPPLY]". Mobile:
   horizontal swipe strip.
03 WHY THIS PROGRAM – CAROUSEL: H2 left, carousel right. 4 cards, each: image box,
   H3 reason, 2 lines. Card 4 = REQUEST INFO card.
04 STUDENT VOICES – CAROUSEL: 3 Quote Cards (photo box "STUDENT PHOTO – TBD", quote
   "[REAL STUDENT QUOTE – SUPPLIED BY UW]", name + CURRENT STUDENT/ALUM). Each card may
   have a small play icon if UW supplies student video (opens OVERLAY 04A).
05 PROGRAM DETAILS – TABS + OVERLAYS: one compact block with 4 tabs: CURRICULUM ·
   FACULTY · COST & AID · DEADLINES. Show the CURRICULUM tab active (8 "[COURSE NAME]"
   rows, 2 cols). Other tabs drawn as separate frames beside the page.
   Secondary button "SEE FULL CURRICULUM" opens OVERLAY 05A (full course list +
   "DOWNLOAD PROGRAM GUIDE" + REQUEST INFO). Values "[CLIENT TO SUPPLY]".
   Mobile: tabs become a horizontal scrollable tab bar.
06 HOW TO APPLY + FINAL FORM (one section): left: 3-step stepper (Step 1/2/3 + [STEP]);
   right: Form Card. H2 [CLOSING LINE]. Mobile: stepper, then form.
   Under the steps, a text link "QUESTIONS? READ THE FAQ" opens OVERLAY 06A (5 FAQ rows,
   first open, REQUEST INFO at the bottom).
07 FOOTER (minimal): logo box, legal slot, privacy link.

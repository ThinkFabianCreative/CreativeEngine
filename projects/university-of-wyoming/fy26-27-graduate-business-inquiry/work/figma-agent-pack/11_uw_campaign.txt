11 · CLIENT: UNIVERSITY OF WYOMING · THE CAMPAIGN
Updated 2026-09-17.

WHAT IT IS
Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) for UW College of Business graduate programs.
Goal: application starts. The action is "Apply Now", which is the Meta CTA button set in
Ads Manager. Do not draw it in the ad.

The ad may be the viewer's FIRST exposure to UW. UW branding and the program name must be
clear and legible on every ad.

ONE CAMPAIGN IDEA, FIVE PROGRAM VERSIONS
The same creative runs for five programs. Only the program label changes:
  ON-CAMPUS MBA
  ONLINE MBA
  M.S. IN ACCOUNTING
  ONLINE M.S. IN ACCOUNTING
  ONLINE M.S. IN FINANCE
Use "ONLINE MBA" as the sample label in comps unless told otherwise.
The label is restrained but must be readable at phone size (min 32 px on 1080 wide).
Program facts go in the post text, EXCEPT one proof line per ad (T2: card 5 / last video
frame; T3: one fact super), chosen from the PROOF LINES list in 12_uw_directions.txt.
One line only. Never bullet points.

VOICE
Confident, direct, credible. Never "unlock your potential" or "take your future to the
next level".

DO NOT PUT ON ANY FRAME
- Any claim about rankings, cost, salary, careers, outcomes, job placement, pass rates.
- Any claim that the programs are hard to get into, or that students drop out.
- Any schedule or flexibility claim ("fits your schedule") on a shared frame.
- Any date, deadline or urgency. EXCEPTION: conversion-layer frames (retargeting) may carry
  marked placeholder slots "[DEADLINE: CLIENT TO SUPPLY]" etc. inside a dashed box, per
  work/concepts/…_conversion-layer-design_v01.md. Never a real-looking date, never relative time
  ("today", "days left"), never gold, never the largest element.
- "STEM" on any frame, any program (UW sources disagree on which formats are STEM).
- Laramie, campus buildings or state imagery on shared or online frames (Online MBA also runs
  in Colorado). EXCEPTION: On-Campus MBA and M.S. in Accounting (on campus) end cards use a UW
  campus photograph (people in action, no exteriors or landmarks); these two target Wyoming only.

FORMATS (Andres, 2026-09-17: each post is a carousel OR an animated video; no single static)
  T2 carousel  1080 x 1080, 5 cards
  T2 video 15s 4:5 1440 x 1800 and 9:16 1080 x 1920, camera pull-out, no voiceover
  T3 video 15s 4:5 and 9:16, student to camera, captions required
  Safe zones per 05_meta_ad_craft.txt
Placements are not final (Q18).

END CARD (T2 card 5 / last video frame; T3 last frame)
UW College of Business logo + program label. T2 adds the proof line; T3 adds the student's
first name and CURRENT STUDENT or ALUM. No button. No typed university name.
- Online programs: solid UW Brown.
- On-campus programs: UW campus photograph in the top 670 px (1080x1080 card), flat UW Brown band
  below holding the white logo and program label. Photo from the UW Photo Database only.

CONVERSION LAYER (retargeting frames)
- The stage line is the largest type; the prospecting headline is not repeated.
- ONE GOLD EVENT PER FRAME. It may be a word (STARTED, PAPERWORK, DAY ONE) or the highlighter stripe
  under the untouched planner line — never both. When the stripe is in frame, the words stay White.
  "SAME WEEK." is White.
- Logo and program label on every carousel card; no separate end card.
- Never state or imply the viewer's status ("your application", "you started", "admitted").

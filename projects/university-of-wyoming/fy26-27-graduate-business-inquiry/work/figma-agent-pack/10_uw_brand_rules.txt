10 · CLIENT: UNIVERSITY OF WYOMING (COLLEGE OF BUSINESS) · BRAND RULES
Attach only for University of Wyoming work. Internal comps; not client-approved.

COLORS
  UW Brown  #492F24   dominant color
  UW Gold   #FFC425   accent only, used deliberately
  White     #FFFFFF
  Black     #000000
- Brown and Gold must stay dominant in every design.
- No other accent colors on these ads. Never use accent colors on the logo.
- Flat fills. No gradients (an official campaign gradient exists but has not been supplied).

FONTS
- Official: Gotham (sans serif). If Gotham is not available, the ONLY approved substitute
  is Montserrat. Do not use Inter, Helvetica, Arial or other lookalikes.
- Blackhawk Italic is UW's licensed campaign headline font ("The World Needs More Cowboys").
  Use its lowercase glyph setting. Headlines or single-word emphasis only, limited use.
  Only use it if the font is installed; never substitute a lookalike brush font on
  client-facing work.
- Script or specialty fonts: limited, for emphasis only.

LOGO
- Use the supplied "UW Lockup Placeholder" component only. Never redraw or recreate the logo.
- White logo on Brown; Brown logo on White.
- One brand mark per frame: do not also type "UNIVERSITY OF WYOMING" next to the logo.

PHOTOGRAPHY
- Stock photography is strongly discouraged by UW. Real imagery comes from the UW photo
  library or a new shoot with model releases.
- For internal comps, a placeholder image is allowed only with a visible label:
  "PLACEHOLDER IMAGE - not for external use".

PROHIBITED ON THESE ADS
gradients · decorative shadows · glass effects · device mockups · fake Meta UI ·
drawn buttons · graduation caps · mountains · trails · roads · horizons · maps ·
cowboy costumes, hats or horses · stock business meetings · heroic poses ·
prices · rankings · testimonials · outcome claims · unsupported program facts ·
deadlines, dates or urgency language ("apply by", "last chance", "starts soon")

ACCESSIBILITY
Text must pass contrast at phone size. Gold text on White fails for small type: use Gold
on Brown, or Brown/White for small text.

University of Wyoming · FY26-27 Graduate Business Inquiry (internal comps only)

WHAT THESE ARE
One person-free still life per week line (15 weeks: 5 programs x A/B/C).
INTERNAL COMP PLACEHOLDERS ONLY. They are replaced by the Laramie shoot before anything
runs. Label every image "PLACEHOLDER IMAGE - not for external use" in Figma.
Source: work/concepts/…_t2-executions_v01.md (weeks and scenes), work/image-prompts/
d2_program-imagery_v02.md (grade, light, zones), visual-craft.md §5.

NEVER GENERATE
- People, hands, faces or body parts. T2 is person-free by design.
- Readable text, numbers, logos or brand names. Firefly garbles text, and the week line
  is set in Figma, not in the photo.
- Mountains, landscapes, windows to the outdoors, cowboy imagery, graduation caps,
  money, glowing screens facing the camera.
If Firefly adds any of these, regenerate. Do not fix it by retouching.
- Light fixtures, bulbs, lamps or windows in frame. The light source is always out of frame
  (lesson from the v1 set: pendant bulbs and doorways landed in the type zone).

FIXES AFTER THE v1 REVIEW (2026-09-18): use t2_firefly-fix-prompts_v01.txt. It has the
regeneration prompts for P1-B, P1-C, P2-C, P3-B and P5-C (they replace the versions below),
the Photoshop fixes and the grade match to P3-A. For any new generation, upload P3-A as the
style reference.

FIREFLY SETTINGS (use what your version offers)
- Content type: Photo.
- Aspect ratio: Portrait 3:4 for the main image (crop to 4:5 feed and 1:1 carousel in
  Figma). Vertical 9:16 for the Stories and Reels version. Generate each ratio separately;
  don't crop 9:16 out of 3:4.
- Visual intensity and style strength: low to medium. Effects: none.
- If photo settings are available: shallow depth of field, eye-level camera.
- Generate 4, pick the one whose TOP THIRD is darkest and emptiest. That is the type area.

PHOTOGRAPHIC STYLE: DOCUMENTARY STILL LIFE
The evidence of a week, minutes after someone left it. Objects mid-use, not arranged for a
shop window: a bag half unzipped, papers out of line, a cold cup. One practical light source
just out of frame, low and to the side, as if it's the lamp or doorway in the room. Low-key:
deep shadows at roughly 8:1 against the lit objects. Grade "Brown hour": shadows in UW Brown
(about #2A1C15 to #492F24), warm cream highlights (about #F3E6CF), saturation down about a
quarter, fine 35mm film grain, no bloom or vignette. Nothing glossy, nothing styled.
Not editorial product still life, not a flat lay, not a mood-board "cozy desk".

OPTIONAL FILM-STOCK LINE (add to the end of any prompt if the grade comes out too clean)
"Shot on Kodak Portra 800, underexposed by about one stop, fine grain."
Portra's warmth sits with UW Brown. Don't use CineStill 800T (red halation) or Ektachrome
(too cool and clean) on this set.

MODEL CHOICE
- Adobe Firefly for the whole set. One model for all 15 scenes keeps the grade and the
  "hand" consistent; mixing models makes a set look assembled.
- If a 9:16 comes out with the objects too high, open the 3:4 in Photoshop and use
  Generative Expand upward to add dark wall, instead of regenerating.
- If Firefly can't place the objects as described after 3 to 4 attempts, a model that
  follows long prompts more literally (for example OpenAI's image generation, used for
  earlier UW comps) can make that one scene. Put the model in the file name and match the
  grade in Photoshop.
- Never a stylized model (for example Midjourney) for this set: it must read as
  documentary, not art-directed.
- Model characteristics: DesignChamp/knowledge/image-direction-reference.md section 6.
  These are comps only. The UW brief requires real UW photography for release, so no
  generated image here is proposed for external use.

HOW EACH IMAGE IS USED (carousel)
Card 1: tight crop on the key object. Card 2: the whole scene. Card 3: the dark top of the
same image. Generate at the highest resolution available so the tight crop holds.

9:16 VERSION OF ANY PROMPT
Paste the same prompt, set Vertical 9:16, and replace the composition sentence with:
"Vertical composition: the objects sit in the middle band of the frame, slightly closer;
the top 40% and the bottom 30% are dark, plain shadow."

SHARED STYLE (already included at the end of every prompt below)
====================================================================
Documentary still-life photograph, 35mm film look, fine grain, real and unposed.
A single warm practical light from out of frame, low and to the side. Deep warm brown
shadows, cream highlights, low saturation, no blue or green cast, no pure white. The top
40% of the frame is dark, plain and low in detail. No people, no hands, no faces. No
readable text, numbers, logos or brand names. No lens flare, glow or heavy vignette. No
windows, no light fixtures or bulbs visible in frame. Warm tungsten grade.

OPTIONAL · VIDEO PULL-OUT (only if your Firefly plan includes image-to-video)
====================================================================
Upload the chosen 9:16 or 3:4 image, then:
"A slow, continuous camera pull-back, starting close on the [key object] and ending on the
whole scene. Smooth, steady, no shake. Nothing in the scene moves. No people appear. The
light stays warm and constant. 5 seconds."
The T2 video is a single camera pull-out (pack 12). If the generated motion adds anything
that moves, a person or a flicker, discard it and animate the still in After Effects or
Figma instead.

AFTER GENERATING
- Check each image at phone size: the key object reads in under a second.
- Reject: plastic textures, impossible objects, garbled paper, extra limbs or hands
  anywhere, anything that looks like a real brand.
- File the chosen images in Figma, labelled, with the prompt ID (for example P2-A 3:4 v1).

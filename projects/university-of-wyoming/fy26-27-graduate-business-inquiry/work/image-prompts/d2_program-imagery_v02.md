## Set rules carried from v01

These are unchanged. They apply to every lead, alternate, placeholder and final photo, including how the campus end-card photo is chosen.

**Moment.** The seam of a week: the minutes between two commitments. Something is on the way in or on the way out.

**The image's job.** It is 1 + 1 = 3 with the week line. It shows one or two named items, never all three, with one collision object large enough to read at phone size. The person is mid-action, never looking at the lens, never still or exhausted. The picture is not a pose of stubbornness and not someone "studying late". No laptop is needed. The degree isn't in this week yet.

**Light.** One warm practical source, out of frame, below or beside the subject. The brightest point is the lit task, below the middle of the frame, **never beside the headline**. Nothing bright in the type zone. Key-to-shadow about 8:1. Any screen is side-on or angled away.

**Grade ("Brown hour").** Shadows in the UW Brown family (about #2A1C15 to #492F24), never neutral or blue-black. Highlights warm cream (about #F3E6CF), never pure white. Gold only through warm highlights or one ochre or rust object. Saturation about −25%. Fine 35mm grain. No bloom, flare, heavy vignette or gradient fade. Darkness must be in the photograph, not an overlay (pack file 10 forbids gradients). If a type zone is too bright, recrop or reshoot. The fallback is a flat solid Brown panel.

**Lens and camera.** 35mm equivalent for wide and mid; 50mm for close. Standing or seated eye height. No top-down flat lay, drone, Dutch tilt or wide-angle distortion. f/2–2.8 (f/4 where a wide shot needs the object and person both sharp).

**People.** Cast for "Trailblazers come in all shapes and sizes": varied ages within 21–35, genders, ethnicities, body types. Ordinary work clothes with no logos or name tapes. **Placeholders are face-free:** backs, hands, over-the-shoulder, bowed heads. Finals may show released faces in profile or three-quarter, eyes on the task.

**Set-wide exclusions.** Mountains, trails, roads, horizons, maps. Graduation caps or gowns. Cowboy hats, boots as costume, horses, antlers or western decor. Stock business meetings, heroic poses, glorified exhaustion (yawning, eye-rubbing, heads down on tables, energy drinks, late-hour clocks), parental guilt, tears. Device mockups. Readable text, numbers, logos or brand names on anything. Money or luxury items.

### Zones (unchanged from v01)

**4:5 feed · 1440×1800.** Type zone 0–45% (dark, plain, no highlight above about 20% brightness, including the first 150 px below the headline) · subject band 45–88% (subject center-right, brightest point below 55%) · lockup strip 88–100% (dark, lockup bottom-left from x = 96 px). Generate at ChatGPT 2:3 portrait (1024×1536) and trim the top and bottom 8%.

**9:16 Stories/Reels · 1080×1920.** Top unsafe 0–14% (dark) · type zone 14–43% · subject band 43–65% (lockup at 1150–1240 px, left, over a dark left third) · bottom unsafe 65–100% (dark mass only, never hands, object or lockup). Keep key content inside the middle 74% of the width. **Frame one step closer than 4:5** (a wide lead becomes mid in 9:16, a mid becomes close).

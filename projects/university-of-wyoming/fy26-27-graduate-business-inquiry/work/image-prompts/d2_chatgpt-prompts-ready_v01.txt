INTERNAL COMP PLACEHOLDERS ONLY. Final imagery must be UW photography or a released shoot (S1 p6, S4 p17).
Faces are deliberately hidden. Headline, logo and labels are added in Figma, never generated.
Campus end cards (P1, P3 card 4) come from the UW Photo Database: no AI prompts for campus.

HOW TO USE
- Paste one prompt per ChatGPT message. Generate the 4:5 first, then the 9:16.
- If ChatGPT offers only square/portrait/landscape, choose portrait; crop in Figma (4:5: trim top and bottom; 9:16: trim sides).
- Reject and regenerate if you see readable text or logos, a visible face, a bright light near the top, or a styled "stock" look.
- Carousel card 1 needs a tight crop: ask ChatGPT for a second, closer frame of the same scene if the crop goes soft.

PROMPT 01 · P1 · ON-CAMPUS MBA · "Chairs up, deck down" · FEED 4:5 (1440 x 1800)
==============================================================================
Create a photorealistic image, vertical 4:5 aspect ratio. If 4:5 is not available, use 2:3 portrait and keep every important element between 10% and 85% of the height, because the top and bottom will be trimmed.

Scene: the dining room of an ordinary neighborhood restaurant about an hour after closing. The floor has just been mopped and still has a faint sheen. On every table, wooden chairs are turned upside down with their legs in the air, except one small two-person table near the middle of the room that has been cleared. A young adult in their mid-twenties stands at that table, still wearing the black server's waist apron from the closing shift, tied over a dark work shirt with the sleeves pushed up, a folded bar towel tucked into the apron string and an order pad in the apron pocket. They lean on one hand and, with a pen in the other, mark up a row of printed presentation slide pages laid out edge to edge across the table: the pitch deck for a business idea of their own. A worn backpack sits on the one chair that is still down. A mop bucket stands in shadow further back.

Camera: from across the dining room at standing eye height, about four meters behind and to the left of the person, so the room reads clearly around them. We see their back, one shoulder, the apron ties, the working hand and the pages. No face is visible: the head is turned down and away. 35mm lens, about f/4: the person, apron and pages are sharp; the nearest upturned chairs are slightly soft.

Composition: a wide shot. The person and the lit table sit right of center in the band from 50% to 86% of the height; the person is about a third of the frame height. Upturned chairs on nearer and farther tables sit in the band from 45% to 88%; their legs point up but never rise above 45% of the height. The top 45% of the frame is the dark upper wall and ceiling of the dining room in deep shadow: empty and low in detail, with no light fixtures, menu boards, signs, TVs, shelves, windows or artwork, so a headline can be placed there later. The lower left corner is a dark near tabletop and floor in shadow. The brightest area is warm light spilling from the kitchen pass-through, out of frame to the right, across the pages, the hands and the apron, below the middle of the frame and right of center. Nothing bright in the upper half.

Light and color: warm tungsten spill light, deep warm brown shadows, cream and soft gold highlights on paper and wood, the apron a soft charcoal, muted low-saturation colors, no cool blue cast, no pure white. Documentary photography, 35mm film look, fine grain. A real, unposed moment.

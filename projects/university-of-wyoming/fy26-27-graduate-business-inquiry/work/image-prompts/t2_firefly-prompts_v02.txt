WHAT THESE ARE
One person-free still life per week line (15 weeks: 5 programs x A/B/C).
INTERNAL COMP PLACEHOLDERS ONLY, replaced by the Laramie shoot before anything runs.
Label every image "PLACEHOLDER IMAGE - not for external use" in Figma.

FIREFLY SETTINGS
- Content type: Photo. Aspect ratio: Portrait 3:4 (crop to 4:5 and 1:1 in Figma).
  Vertical 9:16 generated separately (see below).
- STYLE REFERENCE: P3-A 3:4 v1, strength about 50-60%.
- Visual intensity and style strength: low to medium. Effects: none.
- Generate 4; pick the one with the darkest, emptiest top 40% (the type zone).

OPTIONAL LINE (add to the end of any prompt to push the look)
"In the style of Todd Hido and Stephen Shore."
If Firefly refuses or ignores the names, remove the line; the look is already described.

OPTIONAL FILM LINE (if the grade comes out too clean)
"Shot on Kodak Portra 800, underexposed by about one stop, fine grain."

9:16 VERSION OF ANY PROMPT
Same prompt, Vertical 9:16, and replace the composition sentence with:
"Vertical composition: the objects sit in the middle band of the frame, slightly closer;
the top 40% and the bottom 30% are dark, plain shadow."

NEVER GENERATE (regenerate if it appears; don't retouch it away)
People, hands, faces · readable or fake text, numbers, logos, brand names · windows,
ceiling lights, lamps or bulbs in frame · mountains, landscapes, cowboy imagery,
graduation caps · money · glowing screens facing camera.

AFTER GENERATING: grade to match P3-A and run the check list in
t2_firefly-fix-prompts_v01.txt (Part A and the final check).

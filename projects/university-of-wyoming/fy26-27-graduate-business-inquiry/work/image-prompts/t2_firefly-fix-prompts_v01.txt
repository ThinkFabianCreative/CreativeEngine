PART A · GRADE ALL 15 TO MATCH P3-A
====================================================================
Reference: P3-A 3:4 v1 (warm tungsten, deep brown shadows, cream highlights).

Option 1 (fastest): Photoshop > Filter > Neural Filters > Color Transfer, target image
P3-A, strength about 60%, then tune by eye. Or Image > Adjustments > Match Color,
source P3-A, fade about 30%.

Option 2 (Camera Raw, per image, match by eye to P3-A):
  White balance   warmer until whites read cream, never pure white
                  (the cool ones, P1-B, P1-C, P2-A, P2-C, need the most)
  Tint            slightly toward magenta to remove the green cast (lockers, kitchen)
  Color Grading   Shadows: hue about 25-30 (brown), saturation 15-20
                  Highlights: hue about 40 (cream), saturation 8-12
  Saturation      down about 20-25 overall
  HSL             Orange saturation down 20-30 on P4-C (blaze vest) so it doesn't fight
                  UW Gold; keep P2-A's tulle a muted dusty rose
  Grain           amount about 15, size 25 (fine 35mm)
  Target          shadows in the #2A1C15 to #492F24 range; highlights near #F3E6CF;
                  no blue-black blacks; nothing brighter than the lit objects in the
                  top 40% of the frame

P4-B only: the upper wall is a little lit. In Camera Raw add a Linear Gradient mask from the
top edge down to about 40%, exposure -0.7 to -1.0. It must look like natural light
falloff in the photo, not a graphic overlay.

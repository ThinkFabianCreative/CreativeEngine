## The image's job (1 + 1 = 3)

The headline asks "Too stubborn to stop?" The picture must not show stubbornness as a pose or a metaphor (no tree growing from rock, no gritted teeth). It shows the evidence: the table where the week collided, late at night, still going. The viewer supplies the person, which is themselves.

- **Scene 1 (lead):** kitchen table, 11:45 pm. Work spreadsheet, textbook, a middle school recital program, a child's hair clip, reheated coffee. Laptop still on.
- **Scene 2 (alternate, not parent-coded):** pickup truck cab before dawn in a work lot. Hi-vis vest, textbook with a highlighter in the cup holder, laptop on the passenger seat.

## Ratio handling

ChatGPT image output is usually square, 2:3 portrait or 3:2 landscape. Prompts ask for the target ratio and compose for a crop:

| Target | Canvas | Generate | Crop in Figma |
|---|---|---|---|

| Stories/Reels 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Portrait 2:3 | Trim left and right |

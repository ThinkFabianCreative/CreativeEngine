## Derived layer

- **Derived communication hypothesis.** Address what the one in-person year concretely contains, using only confirmable or conditionally confirmed experiences, for people weighing a year of study against starting or continuing work. Basis: AP2, C10, C12, C13. Confidence MEDIUM.
- **Relevant media signals** (S5 p1–2):
  - Psychographic: "Recent business graduates; early-career professionals seeking applied leadership; UW degree completers; entrepreneurship-oriented students"
  - Age 22–30; Albany and Laramie counties
  - Education level, fields, industries and interests as in the Online MBA set
- **Potential motivations / barriers.** Acceleration into leadership versus the opportunity cost of a year. Uncertainty about what is included (AP2).
- **Creative stimulus:**
  - What would a recent graduate need to see to believe one year changes their trajectory?
  - What does "applied" mean here that a classroom cannot give?
  - How does being close to campus change the decision?
- **Confidence / evidence limits.** Targeting plus S1 only. No cohort data.
- **Do not assume.** Age, alumni status or entrepreneurial intent as admissions or personal facts.

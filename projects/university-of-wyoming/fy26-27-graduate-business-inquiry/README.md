## Scope and workstreams *(summary)*

- **One campaign umbrella** for UW graduate business Meta ads (user direction, 2026-09-12). Alternative umbrella concepts are explored before one is selected; selection is pending Q1 and Q37.
- **Five program/delivery variants** of the selected umbrella: On-Campus MBA · Online MBA · M.S. in Accounting (on-campus) · M.S. in Accounting (online) · Online M.S. in Finance (FP&WM / CFP pathway).
- **Working production baseline:** 5 statics + 5 fifteen-second videos, one of each per variant, unless revised. A carousel is conditional on the idea.
- **Program identity** rides a restrained label, end frame or post text; program benefits and proof may live in primary ad text. Where this changes the V2 brief's treatment: brief §11.6.
- Program-page vs application-portal destination A/B test.
- Brief: [§2](normalized-brief.md#2-assignment--scope), [§4](normalized-brief.md#4-offering-architecture).

Workstreams: lower-funnel campaign. "Inquiry" is the campaign label; the entry condition is OPEN (Q13).

## Audience and purpose *(summary)*

Program-specific prospective graduate students in Wyoming counties (plus two Colorado counties for the Online MBA). The ad may be their first exposure to UW. Brief: [§5](normalized-brief.md#5-audience-architecture), [§8](normalized-brief.md#8-offering-briefs).

## Funnel coverage *(summary)*

"Inquiry" campaign label only. No behavior-defined stage. Brief: [§6](normalized-brief.md#6-funnel-architecture).

## Conversion goals *(summary)*

Goal: application starts. Success measure: application starts and completed applications. CTA wording: "Apply Now". Destinations, measurement and Meta setup are OPEN. Brief: [§12](normalized-brief.md#12-deliverable-matrix).

## Channels and deliverables *(summary)*

Meta; placements not supplied. One umbrella delivered as five variants. Baseline: 5 statics + 5 fifteen-second videos, before conditional carousels (quantity unconfirmed), placement adaptations and destination-test variants. Post text per variant. Umbrella selection is pending Q1 and Q37. Deliverable matrix: [§12](normalized-brief.md#12-deliverable-matrix).

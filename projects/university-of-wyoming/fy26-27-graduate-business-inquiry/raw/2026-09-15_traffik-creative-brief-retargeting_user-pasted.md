Overview
The University of Wyoming's graduate business portfolio provides prospective students with multiple pathways to advance their education, careers, and professional futures. The portfolio includes:
On-Campus MBA
Online MBA
Online MS in Accounting
Online MS in Financial Planning & Wealth Management
Each program serves a different audience and offers a distinct value proposition. The campaign should communicate those differences while maintaining a cohesive University of Wyoming brand presence.
The goal of this campaign is not simply to create awareness. Creative will primarily reach prospective students who have already demonstrated interest or intent and should therefore provide a compelling reason to take the next step toward application and enrollment.
Messaging should answer the questions prospective students are asking at each stage:
Why this program?
What makes UW different?
What will this degree help me achieve?
Is this program worth the investment? (Include the excellent value-for-money proposition)
What proof supports the value of this program?
What should I do next?
The campaign should balance the practical value of each program—including flexibility, affordability, career relevance, specialization, and professional outcomes—with the distinctive experiences and opportunities available through the University of Wyoming.
A key opportunity across the portfolio is demonstrating that graduate education is an investment in a student's career and future, including potential career advancement and increased earning potential where supported by verified University data.
Brand Objectives
Increase consideration of the University of Wyoming's graduate business programs among qualified prospective students.
Move existing prospects further through the enrollment funnel.
Increase completed applications and ultimately enrollment across the graduate business portfolio.
Establish a clear reason for prospective students to choose UW over other graduate business programs.
Differentiate each program through its specific benefits, experiences, and proof points.
Demonstrate the practical value of graduate education through affordability, flexibility, career relevance, specialization, and professional development.
Position UW's graduate business programs as more than simply a credential by highlighting the experiences, connections, and opportunities students gain.
Demonstrate the potential return on investment of graduate education through career advancement and, where supported by verified data, increased earning potential.
Give prospective students a clear and compelling reason to take the next step.

Agency Assignment
Develop a cohesive suite of static and carousel creative for the University of Wyoming's graduate business portfolio that moves prospective students toward application and enrollment.
Creative should combine:
Prospect Need → Program Benefit → Proof → UW Differentiation → Next Action
Rather than using one broad message across all four programs, creative should identify and communicate what makes each program uniquely valuable.
The campaign should also adapt messaging based on where a prospect is in the enrollment journey:
Inquiry → Apply Now
Application Started → Finish Application

The creative should use the University of Wyoming's existing visual assets available through Canto as the primary source of photography and imagery.

TRAFFIK Strategic Approach
Program + Funnel-Based Messaging
The campaign should use a combination of program-specific messaging and audience-stage messaging.
Rather than delivering the same message to every prospective student, creative should acknowledge where the individual is in their decision-making journey and provide the most relevant next step.
The progression should be:
Build Interest → Encourage Application → Create Urgency → Drive Enrollment
However, this is not an awareness campaign. The available budget is focused on audiences who have already demonstrated interest or intent.
The objective is to move those audiences closer to application and enrollment.
Stage 1: Inquiry — APPLY NOW
These prospects have already inquired about a program and demonstrated an initial level of interest.
Creative should move beyond basic program awareness and encourage the prospect to take the next significant step.
Messaging should remain specific to the program and communicate a compelling reason to apply.
Stage 2: Application Started — FINISH YOUR APPLICATION
These prospects have already started their application but have not completed it.
This is a high-intent audience, so creative should focus less on explaining the program and more on creating urgency and encouraging completion.
Where appropriate, legitimate application deadlines or upcoming start dates can be incorporated into the messaging.
Stage 3: Applied / Not Enrolled — TAKE THE NEXT STEP
These prospects have completed their application but have not yet enrolled.
The messaging should now shift from:
"Should I apply?"
to:
"Why should I choose UW?"
Creative should reinforce the program's value and give the prospective student a reason to continue moving toward enrollment.
For the On-Campus MBA, this is an opportunity to encourage a campus visit and allow prospects to experience UW firsthand.
For online programs, messaging should focus on program value, career opportunity, flexibility, specialization, and the experience of becoming a UW graduate student.
MS FINANCIAL PLANNING & WEALTH MANAGEMENT: INTRODUCTORY MESSAGING WITHIN RETARGETING
The MS Financial Planning & Wealth Management program has a differentiated story but is newer and less established than the other programs.
Because there is no dedicated awareness budget, we should not create a separate awareness campaign.
Instead, the retargeting creative should include somewhat more introductory/value-focused messaging to help prospects understand the opportunity.
The goal is:
Introduce the value → Establish credibility → Create interest → Drive action
Once the prospect demonstrates stronger intent, messaging should follow the same funnel:
Inquiry → Apply Now → Application Started → Finish Application → Applied → Take the Next Step
TRAFFIK Creative Direction
Creative should follow:
Prospect Need → Program Outcome → Proof → UW Differentiation → CTA
The most important change from generic graduate program advertising is that each program needs to have a reason to believe. Including the excellent value-for-money proposition.
The PROOF identified for each program should serve as the foundation for messaging and storytelling.
ON-CAMPUS MBA
Core Positioning
An affordable, immersive MBA built around applied experiences, executive access, and close faculty relationships.
The On-Campus MBA is positioned as the clearest expression of the UW brand. The opportunity is to demonstrate that the MBA experience extends well beyond a traditional classroom.
Key Proof
Creative should leverage:
Jackson Hole Leadership Summit
Sheridan Business & Economic Development Experience
Global Business Experience in Spain
Wyoming State Capitol and Legislative Experience
Friday Executive Speaker Series
Applied consulting and industry projects
Creative Territory
The central creative question should be:
What will I actually experience as a UW MBA student?
Rather than simply saying:
Earn your MBA at UW.
Creative should show the experiences that make the program different.
Potential territories:
More Than a Classroom.
Lead. Experience. Connect.
Get Your MBA in the Real World.
An MBA Built Around Experience.
The proof points should be brought to life visually wherever possible using Canto imagery.
ONLINE MBA
Core Positioning
An AACSB-accredited MBA that fits around a full-time career, with the flexibility and value to make advancement attainable.
Key Proof
AACSB accredited
Fully online and asynchronous
30 credits
Tuition below $30K
Multiple start terms
Full-time or part-time pathways
Creative Territory
The strongest creative opportunity is connecting the program to career advancement without requiring students to leave their careers.
Potential territory:
Advance Your Career Without Stepping Away From It.
Then support that message with proof:
100% Online
 30 Credits
 Under $30K Tuition
 Multiple Start Terms
The creative should make the value proposition immediately understandable while maintaining a strong, professional UW brand presence.
MS ACCOUNTING
Core Positioning
A STEM-designated, AACSB-accredited master's degree designed to strengthen CPA readiness and career advancement, with online and on-campus options.
Key Proof
AACSB accredited
STEM designation
Online and on-campus formats
CPA-focused curriculum
College-funded CPA preparation after 50% completion
Employer engagement and mentorship
Creative Territory
The strongest messaging territory is:
CPA Readiness + Career Advancement + Value
The College-funded CPA preparation after 50% completion is a particularly compelling proof point and should be explored in creative, pending UW approval of the exact advertising language.
MS FINANCIAL PLANNING & WEALTH MANAGEMENT
Core Positioning
A specialized graduate degree connected to Wyoming's Jackson Hole wealth ecosystem and the careers that surround it.
This program has perhaps the strongest opportunity to establish a distinctive niche.
The story is not simply:
"Earn a master's in financial planning."
The story is:
Gain specialized expertise and access to an industry ecosystem.
Key Proof
Jackson Hole wealth ecosystem
Exposure to family offices, trust companies, and advisory firms
Applied, industry-focused curriculum
Career paths in financial planning, trust, estate, and investment advisory
Employer and industry relationships
Strong value relative to competing programs
Creative Territory
The creative should help the audience understand why this program is different, particularly the access to family offices, trust companies, advisory firms, and other industry relationships.
The goal is to communicate specialization + industry access + professional network, which are identified as the program's primary student motivations.
ROI / CAREER OUTCOMES
A key opportunity across the graduate business portfolio is demonstrating that a graduate degree is an investment, rather than simply an educational expense.
Prospective students are likely to ask:
"What will this degree do for my career?"
Creative should connect graduate education to potential outcomes such as:
Career advancement.
Increased responsibility.
Leadership opportunities.
Specialized expertise.
Career mobility.
Increased earning potential.
If UW can provide verified alumni or graduate outcome data, those statistics should be explored as creative proof points.
For example, if UW can substantiate salary progression or career outcomes, those figures could become particularly powerful retargeting messages.
Tone of Voice
The tone should reflect the University of Wyoming brand while speaking directly to ambitious graduate business prospects.
Tone
Confident
Authentic
Aspirational
Grounded
Intelligent
Professional
Empowering
Entrepreneurial

Messaging should feel credible rather than overly promotional.
Avoid generic higher-education language such as:
"Take your future to the next level."
"Unlock your potential."
"Dream big."
"Transform your future."
Instead, messaging should use specific, meaningful proof to demonstrate what students can actually gain from each program.
Show the Opportunity. Don't Simply Claim It.
The creative should communicate that UW is ambitious without feeling corporate, prestigious without feeling inaccessible, and distinctive without relying on empty superlatives.
Deliverables
Creative should be developed as a modular system, allowing messaging to change based on both program and audience stage.
The primary creative formats will be static and carousel ads.
We are requesting 1–2 creative options per program and audience stage.

## Curriculum

- "The program consists of a minimum of 30 semester credit hours of graduate coursework."
- Required (minimum 12 credit hours): ACCT 5030 Advanced Financial Accounting · ACCT 5040 Seminar in Managerial Accounting · ACCT 5060 Auditing II · ACCT 5070 Tax II. Plus six approved electives.
- **Pathway track:** "Students without an accounting undergraduate degree may pursue a Master of Science in Accounting degree by following a rigorous curriculum consisting of 2 prerequisite accountings courses taken prior to starting the M.S. coursework (waived if taken in undergraduate studies), 8 core accounting courses, and 2 accounting or business elective courses over a period of two years". Pathway core: ACCT 4060 Auditing I · ACCT 5010 Individual Taxation · ACCT 5030 · ACCT 5040 · ACCT 5070 · ACCT 5230 Financial Reporting I · ACCT 5430 Financial Reporting II · ACCT 5610 Accounting Systems and Analytics. Prerequisites ACCT 2010, 2020 (no degree credit).

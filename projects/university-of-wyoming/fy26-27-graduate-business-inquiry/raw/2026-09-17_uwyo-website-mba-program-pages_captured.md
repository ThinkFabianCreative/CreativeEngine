**On-Campus MBA**
- W6: "Campus MBA - The Campus MBA program offers admission during the Fall semester only and is typically completed in 1 year (Fall, J-Term and Spring semesters). Class sizes are small and diverse."
- W6: "Thirty-six (36) credit hours of graduate coursework, including participation in all MBA Program activities (orientation, Jackson Leadership Summit, MBA Executive Speaker Series, Professional Development, etc.)."
- W6: "Participation in the International trip experience is required for all students in the Campus MBA program."
- W6 Campus MBA core (27 credit hours): MBAM 5101 MBA Foundations (1; "1 week orientation and principles course that occurs the week before classes start") · MBAM 5104 Organization Behavior and Human Resource Management (3) · MBAM 5107 Executive Leadership I (1) · MBAM 5201 Data & Business Analytics (3) · MBAM 5204 Financial Management (3) · MBAM 5207 Marketing Management (3) · MBAM 5208 Accounting for Managers (3) · MBAM 5209 Executive Leadership II (1) · MBAM 5305 Strategic Management (3) · MBAM 5330 Global Business Environment (3) · MBAM 5434 Macro Economic Analysis (3). Electives: 9 credit hours, 5000 level or above, director-approved.
- W6 required Campus MBA activities: "MBA Executive Speaker Series - Students participate in weekly meetings (primarily on Fridays) with business leaders…" · "International Business Trip … associated with MBAM 5330 Global Business" · "Jackson Leadership Summit - Exclusive leadership development event for MBA students… Attendance is required."
- W2: "Our students work on cross-functional teams with real companies on real projects." Deadlines: "Final Application Deadline: June 1 · Priority Application Deadline: November 1" (fall admission only).
- W1: "2:1 Student to faculty ratio."

**Online MBA**
- W3: "fully AACSB International accredited, 100% online program … The cohort-based program can be started in the Fall, Spring or Summer term and consists of 30 credit hours of required coursework. The program can be completed part-time or full-time in as few as three semesters."
- W3: "100% Online: No requirement to visit campus with 100% online instruction - no live-streaming or on-site proctored exams."
- W3: "Our online MBA students are invited to optional on-site networking events such as our annual Jackson Leadership Conference."
- W4: "The Online MBA is an asynchronous program that has rolling admission and can be started at anytime - summer, fall, or spring. The program typically takes 23-months (or 30 credit hours) to complete and due to an integrated curriculum the program is not designed to accommodate starting and stopping once students enroll in the program."
- W4: "The course sequence is designed to take two years to complete, on a case-by-case basis, students can complete the program in as little as one year or it can be extended."
- W6: "Online MBA - The Online MBA program offers admission 5 times a year and can be completed in one year, full-time or in two years, part-time. … 100% online and asynchronous"
- W4/W6 Online MBA core (21 credit hours): MBAX 5104 Organizational Behavior & HRM (fall) · MBAX 5202 Data and Decision Modeling (fall) · MBAX 5204 Financial Management (spring) · MBAX 5207 Marketing Management (summer) · MBAX 5208 Accounting for Managers (spring) · MBAX 5330 The Global Business Environment (spring) · MBAX 5305 Strategic Management (summer). Electives: 9 credits.
- W6: Jackson Leadership Summit: "Online students are invited during their last Fall semester in the program." Speaker Series and Summit "optional for Online MBA students."
- W3 deadlines: "Summer Admission … May 1 · Fall Admission … August 1 · Spring Admission … January 1."

**Concentrations and tracks (all MBA, W6)**
- "All MBA students must declare a dual program, concentration, or track."
- Energy Business concentration: three energy-industry classes instead of electives.
- Accounting concentration: "4 graduate level accounting classes aimed at preparing students to successfully take the CPA exam … ACCT 5030, ACCT 5040, ACCT 5060, and ACCT 5070." Bachelor's must be in Accounting (Advanced Accounting Concentration).
- Tracks: General · Marketing · Financial Planning ("Must take FIN 5750 and choose 2 from FIN 5070, FIN 5780, FIN 5720, FIN 5310") · Non-Profit · Venture (Campus only).
- W1: "Certified Financial Planner Certificate … 18 credit hour graduate certificate … 100% online."

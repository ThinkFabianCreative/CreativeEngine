# Working with Andres: standing rules

These apply to every role, on every machine. Each rule records when and why Andres set it.

## Communication

- **Humanize everything that goes out under Andres's name** (emails, replies, messages, pre-reads). Run the `humanizer` skill per its record in `WordSmither/skills/humanizer.md`. Plain, direct, short. Facts unchanged. (2026-10-10)
- **Executive updates:** "10,000-foot" style. One line per item: the ask, plus a one-clause why. No status detail, no internal file numbering; use creative territory names. Never invent wins, team news or numbers; mark the gaps for Andres to fill. (2026-09-18)

## Briefs and strategy

- **A brief carries strategy, never the creative answer.** No core idea invented in the brief, no campaign vessel, no sign-offs, headlines or lines, and messages written as plain beliefs, not copy. The brief gives the problem, the audience and creative target, the key takeout, messages with reasons to believe, and asks round one for 2–3 campaign ideas. An approved Core Creative Idea may be carried into a brief as a strategic asset; one that doesn't exist is developed first by CreativeStrategist through the `strategy-loop`, not made up inside the brief. Ideas found while briefing go to `work/concepts/` as territories. (EWU graduate pilot, 2026-10-26)
- **Briefs read as plain human prose.** No source codes, internal jargon or agent shorthand in the body; sources listed at the end. (2026-10-26)
- **Fill gaps with labeled proposals, never blanks.** Draft the most plausible option from the evidence and label it proposed, for the client to confirm; never present it as fact. (2026-10-26)

## Design work

- **Never remove Andres's own design work.** Images, 3D renders, gradients, photo backgrounds, logo sizes and positions he added stay, even if a review flags them. Flag the concern and propose; he decides. (Edwards TMTT deck, 2026-10-08)
- **"Elevate this deck" means verbatim.** No slide text changes at all: no added words, labels, colons or periods, no placeholder copy. Copy ideas go only in speaker notes, marked as suggestions. Read the source deck's speaker notes first and carry them over verbatim. Diff slide text before delivery. (Edwards TMTT XLT deck, 2026-10-07)
- **Avoid the AI-slop look.** One variable font family, restrained color, real icons (no emoji), specificity over templates.

## References and ideas

- **Referencing existing work is expected.** Grab what's proven (campaigns, photographs, films, styles, mechanisms), then combine and remix into something new. Judge the result on whether it's new and on brief, not on its distance from sources. No caveats about referencing. (2026-09-18, 2026-10-05)
- **Image prompts use artist, photographer and director names,** plus the technical translation (light, lens, grade, film stock) as a fallback. No ethics caveats; Andres owns the call. Generated images and video are pitch and inspiration only, never commercial; credit referenced artists in pitch documents. Rules: `DesignChamp/knowledge/image-direction-reference.md` and the `artlist-production` skill. (2026-09-18, 2026-09-24)

## How we think

- **Pete Barry's *The Advertising Concept Book* is the default working approach:** briefs, concepts, critique. See `CLAUDE.md` and `shared/knowledge/barry-concept-book-reference.md`. The capture is partial; flag gaps instead of inventing the author's intent. (2026-09-30)

## What we build

- **Hi-fi prototypes, never production code.** Slideshows, landing pages, rich media and Meta ad units, digital OOH, email, Figma, Webflow and HTML mockups. The one exception is native Instapage pages through the `instapage-production` skill. (2026-09-13, 2026-09-24)
- **Global kits stay client-agnostic.** Shared skills, kits and templates define how to build, never what a client looks like. Each client's look lives only in its own client layer. (2026-09-24)

## Accounts and tools

- Each person connects their own Google Drive, Figma, Slack and other accounts on their own machine. Don't store sign-ins here.

# Creative best practices

Read on 2026-09-12. Practices are hypotheses and craft guidance, not guaranteed performance outcomes or universal creative rules. Source-specific recommendations remain separate from CreativeEngine judgment.

## Meta-sourced practices

### Images

[Meta image practices](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/388369961318508) recommend placement-specific ratios, high-resolution imagery, a clear focal point, recognizable branding, readable contrasting text and previewing the result. The same page says the former image-text quantity limit no longer applies. That does not make dense, unreadable typography useful. Test the actual audience response rather than treating a composition convention as proven effectiveness.

### Video and Reels

[Meta Reels guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads) emphasizes vertical 9:16 creative, audio, key messages inside the safe zone, placement customization and testing. Its reported study outcomes are specific research results, not a forecast for a client. No uplift percentages are imported into this knowledge base as promises.

### Carousels

[Meta carousel practices](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/927639917355563) recommend cohesive media, relevant card destinations, using text and CTA components to support the story, and checking available optimization options. It recommends square video. Catalog eligibility and stock affect what displays; a depleted product set can change delivery. Its 4:5 guidance conflicts with the broader Instagram Feed entry, so verify the actual setup. If the concept depends on sequence, inspect card-order or presentation automation before launch.

### Slideshow and playable

[Meta slideshow guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/slideshow-ad-format) supports building a sequence from images, keeping text/product positioning consistent and using each frame to communicate a useful step or benefit. Do not turn it into a compulsory formula.

[Meta playable guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/playable-ad-format) recommends a demo that accurately matches the app, a simple tutorial, a readily available CTA and creative testing. A polished teaser that misrepresents the playable experience is not a successful execution.

### Lead forms

The [official lead-generation guide](https://about.fb.com/ltam/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2023/11/LeadGenerationGuide.pdf) recommends explaining what follows submission and reducing unnecessary questions. This is an older official source (2023 URL), useful for qualitative design guidance, not proof that every historical form feature is still available. The [current lead-ad overview](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/ad-objectives/lead-generation) distinguishes instant forms, website forms, calls and messaging, and emphasizes lead quality and follow-up.

### Blueprint lesson

Andres's notes on Meta Blueprint's *Creative best practices* lesson (added 2026-10-27) cover mobile-first framing, seven video and Reels techniques, creative diversification, Advantage+ creative and placement preview, with an audit checklist. Read [the notes](blueprint-creative-best-practices.md), including how CreativeEngine reads them.

## CreativeEngine recommendations — derived, not Meta mandates

| Format or experience | Useful creative job | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Single image | One clear visual-verbal idea, proposition or proof | Trying to fit an entire brochure into a small image |
| Video | Demonstration, transformation, story, testimony or a temporal reveal | A long logo pre-roll that delays the actual idea |
| Carousel | Meaningful sequence, comparison, offering range or distinct evidence | Repeating the same headline on interchangeable cards |
| Collection | Browseable cover-to-product relationship | A compelling cover leading to irrelevant tiles |
| Instant Experience | A richer, focused continuation of the ad | Decorative screens that hide the next action |
| Lead form | A clear value exchange and proportionate qualification | Optimizing only submission volume while ignoring downstream quality |
| Click to message | An invitation that makes the conversation's purpose clear | An ad promise that the conversation cannot fulfill |
| Playable | A truthful sample of the product experience | A minigame unrelated to the advertised app |

Show the central idea early enough to survive a short encounter. The first seconds matter as a design consideration, but this library does not declare an unsupported universal three-second performance law. Choose duration for the idea and placement, not because the upload limit permits a long file.

Create materially different concepts or executions when testing; changing only a color may not answer the strategic question. Specify the hypothesis, relevant measured event and comparison conditions. A click, a completed view, a submitted form and a qualified lead are different outcomes. No format is automatically best for enrollment or any other industry.

For education, keep the ad and destination consistent about program, mode, intake, costs, eligibility and offer. Any scarcity, deadline, accreditation or career claim must come from the current client's reviewed evidence. This is application guidance, not shared client knowledge.

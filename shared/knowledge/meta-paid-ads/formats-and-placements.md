# Format map and coverage

Research date: 2026-09-12. Source: [Meta Ads Guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update), its visible selectors and source-linked records in this folder.

## Separate the layers

| Layer | Examples | What to specify |
|---|---|---|
| Platform | Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Threads, WhatsApp, Audience Network | Where the ad is served; Meta is the broader platform family |
| Placement | Feed, Stories, Reels, Marketplace, Search, Explore, Status, rewarded video | Actual surface and rendering behavior |
| Base creative format | Image, video, carousel, collection | Media structure and required assets |
| Experience / destination | Website, Instant Experience, instant form, chat, call, app | What opens or happens after interaction |
| Buying or identity feature | Catalog, partnership ad, automated creative enhancement | Additional setup, permission, rendering and eligibility rules |
| Goal / measurement | Brochure download, completed lead form, event registration, video completion | User goal, actual event and platform optimization setting remain separate |

A lead ad is not one universal image size. A Reels placement is not always a video-only format. A catalog is not a canvas dimension. A partnership ad inherits the actual placement's format requirements plus partner permissions.

The Ads Guide menus also expose legacy-sounding names such as video feeds, Groups Feed and in-stream video. A visible guide route is not sufficient proof that a placement can currently be purchased. Do not mark it discontinued solely from its name either. Record account evidence before deciding.

## Threads

[Official Threads guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/655859553779869), read with all three format sections expanded. Image, video and image-only carousel are documented. Collection and partnership ads are listed as incompatible in the inspected page. Although it says all campaign objectives are supported, its conversion-location/performance-goal matrix contains exclusions; do not translate that into universal instant-form, messaging or engagement support. Use the exact matrix for an assignment.

## WhatsApp

[Official Status guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/723817683917948), read with image/video/carousel sections expanded. Rollout is country-dependent. Image/video and image-only carousel are documented; collection is incompatible. The [setup page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1074444721456755) says Instagram Stories must also be included for WhatsApp Status. Confirm the current setup before launch.

Status advertising, ads involving Channels, promoted Channels and ads on Facebook/Instagram that click to WhatsApp are different products or delivery paths. [WhatsApp's overview](https://faq.whatsapp.com/491963319630082) discusses Status and Channels; that does not supply complete Channels creative specifications. Keep the latter OPEN rather than borrowing Status dimensions.

## Education and conversion goals

CreativeEngine's existing goal list still applies: brochures, free guides, information requests, landing-page navigation, lead forms, conversations, campus visits, physical/virtual events, newsletter subscriptions, video completion, engagement, application starts/completions and enrollment.

For each assignment establish: the requested goal; exact approved CTA wording; eligible Meta CTA button; destination; event definition; tracking source; and platform optimization setting. Never infer one from another. A platform need not offer a button matching the client's wording exactly; resolve that separately from the message in the ad.

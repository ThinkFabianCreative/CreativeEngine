## What this version establishes

The reference covers every placement option shown in the four public Ads Guide menus during this pass: **17 image, 17 video, 18 carousel and 8 collection entries**, plus image/video/carousel guidance for **Threads Feed and WhatsApp Status**. These are 66 observed combinations, not 66 guaranteed available products. Seven guide entries are quarantined because their content appears to fall back to unrelated Feed specifications or contains a material anomaly. Others carry narrower warnings.

Additional official pages were read for safe zones, image and carousel practices, lead advertising, Instant Experience, playable assets, slideshow, Threads and WhatsApp. Public documentation cannot establish all availability in every market, ad account, objective, conversion location or rollout. The [gaps register](source-conflicts.md) makes that boundary explicit. This is a broad V1 reference, not a claim that every purchasable variant has been independently validated.

## How to use specifications correctly

1. Identify the creative **format**, **platform**, **placement**, **objective**, **conversion location**, **destination**, **creation path** and whether a **catalog or existing post** is involved.
2. Retrieve the corresponding record and inspect its status and date. Do not use QUARANTINED entries as production instructions.
3. Separate a recommendation from a hard requirement. Recommended resolution is not the minimum upload size; recommended copy length is not automatically a character limit; upload duration is not uninterrupted display time or recommended ad length.
4. For production, recheck the official page and the exact account setup. Record an observed rejection or rendering conflict without silently replacing source history.
5. Inspect the actual placement previews after upload, including CTA, crop, captions and destination. No ad account was inspected in this research pass.

**DOCUMENTED** means visible in official public documentation on the research date, not delivery-tested. **SOURCE_ISSUE** means at least one field or interaction needs confirmation. **QUARANTINED** means the entry must not guide production until resolved. Account availability is separately **NOT_TESTED** for every record. Missing values mean not published in the inspected section; they never mean zero, unlimited or unsupported.

Public platform facts are reusable; client goals, offers, claims, budgets and performance are not. The education extension supplies assignment-specific intake fields when relevant. It does not change Meta's technical rules, and this knowledge base establishes no educational outcome claim.

## Useful findings

- Facebook and Instagram Feed **image** entries currently recommend 1440 × 1800 at 4:5. Their **video** entries differ: Facebook Feed recommends 1440 × 1800 at 4:5, while Instagram Feed recommends 1080 × 1920 at 9:16. See the corresponding source-linked records.
- Stories/Reels specifications are not interchangeable with Feed or with each other. Meta's own pages contain differing safe-zone descriptions; see [production guidance](production.md).
- Instagram Feed carousel guidance distinguishes image-only from video-containing carousels, but another Meta help page narrows 4:5 eligibility to catalog images. That conflict remains open.
- The old image-text percentage limit is not a current rule in the inspected Meta guidance. Read [Meta's image practices](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/388369961318508).
- An Instant Experience view is not a website landing-page view. See [Meta's Instant Experience guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/183469315334462).

Nothing was staged or committed. No campaign, automation, subscription or account setting was created.

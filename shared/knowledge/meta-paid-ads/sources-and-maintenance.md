# Sources, evidence and maintenance

Research date: 2026-09-12 · primary-source-only factual reference · English (US).

## Retrieval method

Public web search initially returned login/temporary-block screens for the Ads Guide and conflicting third-party summaries. Those summaries were not used as specification evidence. The public guide loaded successfully through the browser. Its visible format and placement menus were traversed, and rendered main-page text was read after navigation. No private Ads Manager account, campaign or client workspace was accessed.

All 60 guide combinations have source URLs in specifications.json and the four format documents. Each record captures the observed objective context. Eight selected pages automatically chose **Traffic / Website** (META-008, 013, 017, 029, 033, 045, 050 and 057); the other guide pages showed **Awareness / On your ad**. Do not mislabel the entire scrape as one objective. Threads and WhatsApp specifications came from expanded sections in their help articles.

The stored data is a curated transcription of facts, with notes and status, not a complete archive of every source page. Retrieval evidence is present in this task's browser tool results. Full raw page snapshots were not saved to this folder. The source URL and date establish provenance, not that the page remains unchanged after that date.

## Refresh procedure — proposed operating practice

This is a maintenance recommendation, not a scheduled automation.

1. Recheck the relevant official source before producing or publishing an assignment. Review the overall library monthly if it is being used actively; rollout-dependent surfaces deserve closer attention when selected.
2. Record URL, check date, objective/conversion context, locale, creation path, published value and whether it is a requirement or recommendation.
3. Preserve the previous record or diff before replacing a value. Never erase an unresolved source conflict.
4. Compare live help articles, Ads Guide and the exact account setup. A public page and an account-specific restriction may both be valid at different scopes.
5. Update specifications.json and its readable format page together. Keep unavailable fields explicit.
6. Move an entry out of quarantine only when the discrepancy is resolved with evidence. Record the reason.
7. When a product disappears from a menu, mark availability unconfirmed rather than declaring it retired without evidence.

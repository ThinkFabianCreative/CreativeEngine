# Meta Blueprint: creative best practices (lesson notes)

Added 2026-10-27. Andres's notes on the Meta Blueprint lesson *Creative best practices* (path *Exploring best practices for ad creative*). User-authored summary of an official Meta course; not re-checked against the course itself. Public platform guidance, no client facts.

## How CreativeEngine reads this

- **Meta's advice, not CreativeEngine law.** These are platform practices. They inform DesignChamp's and WordSmither's judgment on Meta work; they do not replace the idea, the brief or the client's brand (CLAUDE.md principles 5, 6, 11).
- **The idea still comes first.** Hooks, text overlays, jump cuts and lo-fi styling are delivery techniques. A Reel that uses all seven on a weak idea is still a weak idea. Choose the techniques that carry the concept; Meta says the same (never force all 7).
- **The 3-second hook is Meta's guidance.** Cite it as such. `creative-best-practices.md` still holds: show the idea early because the encounter is short, not because of a universal performance law.
- **Casual and lo-fi is an option, not a default.** It suits some brands and ideas, and works against others. Decide per concept, with the brand.
- **Diversification means different ideas or angles, not resizes.** The four mindsets (benefit, offer, social proof, results) are examples from a skincare case. Derive a campaign's angles from its own brief and audience, never from this list by default.
- **Advantage+ enhancements can change the craft.** Automatic brightness, contrast, crop and text-placement changes can break a composition, a type layout or a brand look. When the idea depends on exact framing or type, flag which enhancements to switch off, and check them in the advanced preview before calling the work final.
- **No performance numbers.** The lesson says these practices *can* improve results, never by how much.
- **Specs live elsewhere.** Dimensions, safe zones and limits come from the records in this folder (`README.md`), not from these notes.

## 1. Lesson scope and learning objectives

The lesson covers a set of high-impact creative practices, moving from **how to design for mobile**, to **techniques for building ads (especially video and Reels)**, to **Ads Manager tools that diversify ads across placements and formats**.

After applying this knowledge you should be able to:

1. Apply best-practice strategies to ad creation that improve campaign results.
2. Diversify ad creative across placements and formats in Ads Manager.

The underlying logic of the whole lesson:
**Match creative to how people actually consume mobile content → make video/Reels creative stop the scroll → give the ad delivery system many varied assets → let Advantage+ creative expand that variety → preview everything before launch.**

## 2. Design ad creative for mobile (foundational practice)

**Why it matters:** Mobile viewing is *vertical* and *fast*, and most people scroll with the *sound off*. Creative that matches that behavior performs better.

The four mobile-first practices:

| Practice | What to do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Vertical framing** | Frame ads for vertical viewing; use vertical formats like **9:16**. | Matches how people hold phones; maximizes screen space, especially in **Reels and Stories**. |
| **Quick pacing** | Keep ads shorter; **grab attention within the first 3 seconds**. | People scroll quickly on mobile, so interest must be earned immediately. |
| **Sound-off visuals** | Communicate the message with **text and graphics**. | Most people scroll without audio enabled. |
| **Sound-on audio** | Add audio (music, voiceover) that makes the content entertaining. | For people who do watch with sound, audio draws attention and enhances storytelling. |

**Key nuance:** Design for sound-off *and* sound-on at the same time. The message must be fully understandable silently; audio is an enhancement layer, not a dependency.

### Mobile checklist
- [ ] Is the primary asset vertical (9:16) for Reels/Stories?
- [ ] Does something compelling happen in the first 3 seconds?
- [ ] Is the ad as short as it can be while still delivering the message?
- [ ] Can the full message be understood with the sound off (text/graphics)?
- [ ] Does the audio add entertainment or story value for sound-on viewers?

## 3. Enhance videos and Reels with creative techniques

**Context:** Video ads grab attention through **motion, sound, and storytelling** across Meta placements. **Reels are a type of video ad**, but their full-screen, scrollable format supports techniques that other video placements don't (standard video simply plays straight through).

**Important rule:** You do **not** need all 7 techniques in every ad. Choose the ones that fit the brand and message.

The 7 techniques:

1. **Audio techniques**
   Import your own audio, or browse **trending and royalty-free** options directly in Ads Manager. Audio sets the tone and helps the ad blend naturally into the Reels experience.

2. **Editing techniques**
   Use **transitions, jump cuts, and fast edits** to keep the pace moving. Meta's **Edits app** supports timeline editing and background replacement for quick production.

3. **Filters and effects**
   Layer options from the **effects gallery** to create a signature look for the brand. Filters add visual consistency and personality to Reels content.

4. **Hooks**
   Grab attention **in the first 3 seconds**. Examples: a **bold question**, a **surprising fact**, or a **quick before-and-after**, so people stop scrolling immediately.

5. **Text overlays**
   Reinforce the message visually and make content accessible to people watching without sound. Overlays communicate key information regardless of audio settings.

6. **Human presence**
   Show a **real person** on screen: a customer, a creator, or an employee. People connect more naturally with content featuring a human face or voice.

7. **Casual content**
   Swap the polished look for something **unscripted / lo-fi**. Casual content feels more authentic and blends into the Reels environment, where people expect organic content.

### How the techniques relate to mobile principles
- Hooks ⇄ Quick pacing (the 3-second rule appears in both)
- Text overlays ⇄ Sound-off visuals
- Audio techniques ⇄ Sound-on audio
- Editing techniques ⇄ Quick pacing
- Casual content + human presence + trending audio ⇄ Looking native to Reels

## 4. Diversify ad creative and placements

**Core idea:** Different people respond to different messages, visuals, and formats. Combining **multiple placements** with a **variety of ad creative** gives the ad delivery system more options to find the right match for each person.

**Target state:** **Mixed-asset, multi-placement campaigns.**

- The more variety provided, the more flexibility the delivery system has to find the best combination **for each impression**.
- The system uses differences between creatives to match relevant creative to the people likely to be interested in it.

### Four example motivations creative can speak to
The lesson illustrates diversity with four different audience mindsets. Each implies a different creative angle:

| Audience mindset (as voiced in the lesson) | Creative angle it suggests |
|---|---|
| "This moisturizer is exactly what I need for my skin." | Product-benefit / problem-solution creative |
| "I can't pass up this great deal!" | Offer / price / promotion creative |
| "This product was recommended by a creator I trust." | Creator / social-proof creative |
| "I'm looking for dramatic results." | Results-focused / before-and-after creative |

*(The right-hand column is an interpretation of the examples, not verbatim lesson text.)*

**Takeaway:** Don't produce one "best" ad. Produce several meaningfully different ads (different messages, visuals, formats, and messengers) and let delivery match them to people.

## 5. Generative optimization with Advantage+ creative

**What it is:** **Advantage+ creative** provides generation and enhancement tools that create new ad media or improve existing assets.

**How it relates to diversification:**
- *Creative diversification* = variety from **your own** assets.
- *Advantage+ creative* = **generates new variations** to increase diversity further.

Together these features give the delivery system more creative variations to work with, helping it find what resonates with different audiences **without manually producing each asset**.

The three feature groups:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Image generation** | Adds images generated from your existing assets, tailored to where the ad appears. Expands creative variety without extra production work. |
| **Video generation** | Adds generated videos to diversify creative. Each is tailored to fit different placements (such as Reels) so content looks native to each surface. |
| **Enhancements** | Automatically adjusts creative and shows different variations based on what performs well, including adjustments to **brightness, contrast, text placement, and more**. |

## 6. Preview your ad before delivery

Before publishing, preview how creative appears across placements. The **advanced preview** section in Ads Manager shows how ads appear across formats and placements before they go live.

**Preview features:**
- See how the ad shows up across placements, **including how enhancements appear**.
- By default you see the **most representative previews**; you can **filter by placement** or use **Advanced preview** for a full view.

**Preview tabs:**
- Feeds and in-stream ads for Reels
- Stories, Status, Reels
- Search results
- Apps and sites
- All (an array of predicted top-performing variations)

**Why:** Placement-tailored assets and Advantage+ enhancements can change how an ad looks; previewing catches cropping, text-placement, and safe-zone problems before spend.

## 8. Key takeaways (lesson summary)

1. **Design for mobile:** vertical framing, keep it short, design for sound-off.
2. **Use video/Reels techniques** like hooks, text overlays, and human presence.
3. **Diversify ad creative** to give the delivery system more options.
4. **Use Advantage+ creative** to generate variations automatically.
5. **Preview ads across placements** before publishing.

## 9. Applying this as a skill

### When reviewing / auditing an ad
Score each item Pass / Fix / N/A and give a specific fix:
1. **Format:** Vertical 9:16 available for Reels/Stories?
2. **First 3 seconds:** Is there a hook (bold question, surprising fact, before/after)?
3. **Length/pacing:** Short, with fast edits/transitions where relevant?
4. **Sound-off:** Message clear from text overlays/graphics alone?
5. **Sound-on:** Audio (trending, royalty-free, own, or voiceover) adds value?
6. **Human element:** Is a real person (customer, creator, employee) present where it fits?
7. **Native feel (Reels):** Would casual/lo-fi content suit the brand better than polished?
8. **Brand look:** Consistent filters/effects?
9. **Diversity:** Does the campaign include multiple distinct creatives (different angles: benefit, offer, social proof, results) across multiple placements?
10. **Advantage+ creative:** Are image/video generation and enhancements considered to expand variety?
11. **Preview:** Has every placement (Feeds, Stories/Status/Reels, Search, Apps and sites) been previewed, including enhancements?

### When generating ad concepts
- Produce **several variants with different angles** rather than one polished concept.
- For each video/Reel concept, specify: hook (0–3s), on-screen text, audio choice, human presence, editing style, polished vs. casual, and target aspect ratio.
- Pick only the techniques that fit the brand. Never force all 7.

### Guardrails
- Don't claim specific performance numbers. The lesson states these practices *can* improve results, not by how much.
- Ads Manager UI and feature names may change as products update. Treat tool names (Advantage+ creative, Edits app, advanced preview) as current as of this lesson.

## 10. Glossary

- **Placement:** Where an ad appears (e.g., Feeds, Stories, Reels, Status, Search results, Apps and sites).
- **Reels:** Full-screen, vertical, scrollable short video; a type of video ad that supports techniques standard video doesn't.
- **Ad delivery system:** Meta's system that decides which creative to show to which person for each impression.
- **Creative diversification:** Supplying varied messages, visuals, and formats across placements so delivery can match creative to people.
- **Mixed-asset, multi-placement campaign:** A campaign with many different creative assets running across many placements.
- **Advantage+ creative:** Ads Manager tools that generate new image/video variations and automatically enhance creative.
- **Edits app:** Meta's video editing app with timeline editing and background replacement.
- **Effects gallery:** Library of filters/effects for building a signature visual look.
- **Advanced preview:** Ads Manager view showing ads across all formats and placements before launch.
- **Hook:** The opening (first 3 seconds) designed to stop the scroll.

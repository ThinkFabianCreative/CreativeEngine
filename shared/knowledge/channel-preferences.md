# Channel preferences

> CreativeEngine shared knowledge. A record of the user's own workflow preference. Not global method, not client knowledge, not project evidence, and not a default for any assignment.

## The preference

The user identifies **Meta** and **ChatGPT** as their primary advertising channels.

- This is where the user most often works. It is a preference, not an instruction about any particular client, brief or creative problem.
- It does not make either channel the default, the recommended or the assumed channel for an assignment. Channel comes from the assignment: what the source establishes, or what the user asks for on that project. Neither channel is mandatory for any client.
- It is not evidence about any client, audience, market or category, and it creates no expectation that a client's work should run there.
- Naming a channel here establishes nothing about what that channel currently offers. Platform facts are external facts; see "Verification" below.

## Keep these separate

A channel name answers one question out of eight. Record each separately, only as far as it is established. The intake rule and packet fields are in `shared/frameworks/brief-normalization.md`, section 8 ("Channel, placement and format", "Goal, CTA, destination and measurement").

```
CHANNEL / PLATFORM     where the work runs
PLACEMENT              the specific surface within it
CREATIVE FORMAT        what is actually made, with its specification
CAMPAIGN GOAL          what the communication should achieve
CTA                    the exact words the audience sees
DESTINATION            where the action happens
MEASURED EVENT         what counts as success, and how it is defined
PLATFORM OBJECTIVE     the platform's own optimization setting; only where supplied or verified
```

**Neither "Meta" nor "ChatGPT" is a creative format.** Neither establishes a placement, a ratio, a duration, a character limit or an objective.

## Meta

- A Meta assignment records its actual placement and creative format where the assignment establishes them.
- Where it does not, they are recorded as not supplied and become an open question when they would change the work. Do not select a placement or format on the client's behalf, and do not infer one from the goal or the CTA.

## ChatGPT

**The user's intended use is display advertising inside ChatGPT.** Here "ChatGPT" names an advertising channel, as the user has clarified.

- This records user intent, not a verified platform capability. It does not establish current availability, placements, formats, targeting, specifications or measurement options for advertising inside ChatGPT.
- A ChatGPT assignment records its actual placement and creative format where the assignment establishes them. Where it does not, they are recorded as not supplied and become an open question when they would change the work.
- Do not invent or describe an ad format, specification, placement, targeting option, objective, measurement option or availability for ChatGPT. When an assignment needs any of them, verify it against current official documentation (see "Verification" below).

## Verification

Any claim about a platform's current specifications, placements, formats, targeting, objectives, measurement options or availability must come from that platform's official documentation, cited with its source and date. Otherwise it is labeled UNVERIFIED and treated as a hypothesis to check.

Do not reconstruct platform specifications from memory, and do not research platform detail a task does not need: a generic field structure is usually enough at intake.

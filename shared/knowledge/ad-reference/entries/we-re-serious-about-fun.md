## What it does
A national theme-park platform after the Six Flags / Cedar Fair merger, built for daily tickets and season passes, with a deadpan spokes-character (a "Funsultant") who treats fun as serious professional work.

## Our read
Deadpan seriousness applied to fun: the joke is the calm, official tone against the chaos of a park. A spokes-character carries it across TV, social and email. Any leisure idea that uses an official, calm label or a straight-faced authority figure against over-the-top fun sits in this territory and needs its own distinct device.

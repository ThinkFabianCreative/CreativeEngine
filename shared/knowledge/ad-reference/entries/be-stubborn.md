## What it does
An agency recruiting and self-promotion ad that recasts stubbornness, usually a complaint, as the trait behind good ideas, and invites stubborn people to join.

## How it works
MECHANISM:  reversal
IDEA TYPE:  reclaimed-word
DEVICE:     imperative
CARRIER:    headline (typography does the emphasis)
LENS:       sullivan

## Our read
Type-only page: a large headline with the first half in yellow and "be stubborn." in dark grey, a column of body copy that argues the case, and a small logo. The reversal is stated rather than dramatized, so the headline and body copy do all the work; there is no picture or situation that makes the viewer feel their own stubbornness. Worth knowing as the plainest version of "stubborn is a virtue": any new work in that territory needs a situation, a named audience or a second meaning that this ad doesn't have.

## What it does
A theme-park spot that moves an ordinary, buttoned-up adult from everyday life (an office) into the park, where the brand's characters and rides give them permission to react without restraint.

## Our read
The structure is everyday restraint, then release at the destination: the most common theme-park storyline. The line coins a verb from a reaction word. Useful as the benchmark for any "behave vs. let go" leisure idea: new work in that territory needs a sharper device than the cut from office to park, because this is the category default.

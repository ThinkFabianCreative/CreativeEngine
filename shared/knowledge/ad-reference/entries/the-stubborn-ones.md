## What it does
Floss framed as a fishing line: in the execution viewed, a school of red fish swims above one straggler, and the line claims the floss gets the pieces that won't come loose.

## Our read
Student work; stock photography per the credits. Here "stubborn" is the enemy (the thing that won't come loose), the everyday sense of the word in cleaning and hygiene. Useful mainly as evidence that "stubborn" in advertising usually means a stain, a residue or a problem to beat; work that makes stubbornness a human virtue sits in different territory.

## The Creative Ladder
- **10 · Cultural Movement:** sets a new socio-cultural trend. *An outcome, never a pre-launch score.*
- **9 · Contagious:** earned outpaces paid; enters pop culture. *An outcome.*
- **8 · Game Changer:** breaks the category's codes and creates new ones.
- **7 · Showtime:** people enjoy it for its originality, craft and entertainment.
- **6 · Fresh:** a new spin on known solutions, distinctively done.
- **5 · Ownable:** within category codes, but clearly ours. **The floor.**
- **4 · Deja Vu:** stereotype work any brand could run. ✗
- **3 · Puzzling:** confusing; the message gets lost. ✗
- **2 · Disconnected:** borrowed interest with no brand meaning. ✗
- **1 · Calamity:** reckless; damages the brand. ✗

## Three pitstops, ten questions
### A · Branding
- **1.** Is it an expression of the Brand Promise?
- **2.** Does it feel like an episode of the Core Creative Idea?
### B · Strategy
- **3.** Is the insight central to the story?
- **4.** Will it deliver the job in the brief?
### C · Creative
- **5.** Does it make impact?
- **6.** Is the insight brought to life brilliantly?
- **7.** Is it emotionally engaging?
- **8.** Does the craft make it more creative?
- **9.** Will people talk about it?
- **10.** Does it reach the rung agreed in the brief?

## The rules
- Answer **yes / partly / no**, each with one reason. Never add up a score.
- **Judge in order:** great creative that's off-brand is still off-brand.
- **Judge the potential, not the presentation.** A rough can hide a great idea.
- **If it's too easy to approve, it isn't creative enough.**
- **Three meetings on one idea, then produce it or drop it.**

# Organic social

Covers brand posts, captions, multi-card posts (carousels as organic content), and replies and community copy. Platform-agnostic craft. Character limits, truncation points, link behavior and hashtag or mention rules differ by platform and change often: cite current official documentation, or label UNVERIFIED.

## The encounter

The viewer follows the brand, or is shown it by an algorithm alongside people they know. Organic copy has no paid placement to fall back on. It earns attention by being worth reading, sharing, saving or answering. It is a voice in a conversation, not an ad in a slot.

## The format's job

Give people a reason to stop and a reason to feel something, learn something or respond, in a voice that sounds like the brand and like the platform.

## Craft

**The first line is the post.**
- Captions are cut before people choose to expand them. The first line must stop the scroll on its own: the observation, the surprising fact, the joke, the question worth answering.
- Never open with "We're excited to announce", "Happy [day]!" or a restatement of the image.

**Caption and image make one thought.**
- The caption adds what the image cannot: context, a twist, the story behind it, the voice.
- It does not describe what everyone can already see.

**Voice is the brand's, spoken on this platform.**
- Organic copy is where brand voice is most visible. Use the client's approved voice.
- Native register (lower case, humor, brevity) only where the voice allows it.
- Do not borrow memes, slang or trend formats the brand could not credibly use.

**Earn interaction, don't bait it.**
- A question people actually want to answer beats "Comment below!".
- Participation should come from the idea (a prompt, a challenge, a debate the brand can host), not from reach-gaming instructions (verify platform policy on engagement bait).

**Shape for reading.**
- Short paragraphs and line breaks for rhythm.
- A long caption is allowed when the story earns it, and its first line still has to stop someone.
- Avoid headings, bullet walls and bold labels unless the platform and content genuinely call for structure.

**Hashtags, mentions and emoji are functional.**
- **Hashtags:** use few and relevant, where they help discovery or join a real conversation on that platform. Use camel case for readability and screen readers (#BikeToWork, not #biketowork). No hashtag stuffing.
- **Mentions:** only accounts with a genuine connection, and only with the client's permission where required.
- **Emoji:** only if the brand voice uses them. Never as bullet decoration or a replacement for words.

**CTA fits organic behavior.**
- Organic CTAs are usually softer: save, share, reply, visit the link where the platform supports it.
- Use the approved CTA wording where one is set. Do not promise a link the platform will not render.

**Multi-card posts (organic carousels).**
- The cover card is a headline worth swiping for. Each card carries one point in sequence, and the last card pays off.
- The caption frames the set without repeating every card.
- Card type is DesignChamp's composition; the words are written as a sequence.

## Replies and community

- Replies are brand voice in real time: specific, human, brief. Answer the actual comment.
- Never argue, speculate about a person's circumstances, or make a claim or promise in a reply that the claim ledger and client policy do not support.
- Customer-service, complaint, crisis and legal-sensitive replies follow the client's approved escalation process. Draft them for review; do not publish.
- Humor in replies follows the brand's tolerance, not the platform's.

## Accessibility

- **Alt text** describes the image for people who cannot see it: what matters in the image, briefly, not "image of".
- **Video captions** for all speech, where the platform supports them.
- **Camel-case hashtags.**
- **Plain words** over decorative Unicode "fonts", which screen readers cannot read.

## Fidelity check additions

- [ ] The first line stops the scroll without the rest of the caption.
- [ ] The caption adds to the image instead of describing it.
- [ ] The voice matches the client's approved voice.
- [ ] Interaction is earned, not baited.
- [ ] Hashtags, mentions and emoji are functional and minimal.
- [ ] Alt text and captions are provided.
- [ ] Platform limits and link behavior are cited, or UNVERIFIED.

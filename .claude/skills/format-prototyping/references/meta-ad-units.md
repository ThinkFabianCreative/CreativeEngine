# Meta ad units

Covers single image, video, Reels and Stories, carousel, collection, Instant Experience and playable, across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Threads and WhatsApp placements where applicable.

## Specifications

All dimensions, ratios, safe zones, copy recommendations and experience rules come from `shared/knowledge/meta-paid-ads/`. Read its README first, then only the record the placement needs. Do not use QUARANTINED entries. Safe-zone guidance differs between Meta's own pages; follow `production.md` and check the actual Ads Manager placement preview before calling anything final. This reference holds craft only. For Meta's own creative practices (mobile-first, Reels techniques, diversification, Advantage+ enhancements, preview), read `shared/knowledge/meta-paid-ads/blueprint-creative-best-practices.md`, and check which enhancements would alter a composition before calling it final.

## The encounter

A thumb moving fast through content chosen by people the viewer cares about. The ad competes with friends, creators and news, usually sound-off at first, often for a fraction of a second.

## The format's job

Stop the thumb with the idea itself, be understood before the scroll resumes, and earn the tap or the watch.

## Craft

**The first frame is the poster.** For video and Reels, design frame one as a still that could run alone. A logo sting or slow fade-in spends the only moment most people give.

**Belong to the feed without disappearing into it.** Native grammar (vertical, close, human, handheld or typographic) earns attention; a resized TV spot or print ad reads as an interruption. But native does not mean generic: the campaign mechanism must still be recognizable.

**Sound-off comprehension, sound-on reward.** The idea must land silently through image, motion and on-screen type. Sound should add, not explain.

**Compose for the overlay.** Stories and Reels carry interface at the top, bottom and right edge. Place key content inside the cited safe zones, and design the edges knowing they will be covered, not left empty.

**Type belongs to the image.** On-screen text should be part of the composition, sized for a phone at arm's length, few words per beat. Captions for speech are a separate layer from designed type.

**Carousel is a sequence.** Card one is the hook and must work alone. Give a reason to swipe: an unfinished thought, a sequence, a reveal, a composition that continues across card edges. Interchangeable cards with repeated headlines waste the format.

**Collection and Instant Experience continue the ad.** The cover makes a promise the tiles and the experience keep. An Instant Experience is its own designed space, not a landing page.

**Playable is a truthful sample.** The demo must match the real product experience.

**Design for the copy fields Meta shows.** Primary text may truncate and headline or CTA may be hidden in some configurations. The creative must carry the meaning without them.

## What to prototype

- Each required placement at its real canvas, inside a simplified interface frame that shows overlays accurately.
- Video and Reels at true timing, with sound-off and sound-on versions when sound matters.
- Carousel as a swipeable sequence, including the peek of card two.
- Collection cover-to-tile relationship and the Instant Experience flow.
- A small-scale feed view with neighboring posts, to judge stopping power honestly.

Interface frames are presentation aids. Say so, and do not present a mockup as a live ad. The production check is Meta's own placement preview.

## Buildability flags

- Placement or format choices the assignment has not established (record as open question per `channel-preferences.md`).
- Card counts, durations or media mixes outside the cited record.
- Music, effects or media types excluded for the placement in the knowledge base.
- Automated enhancements that could alter the approved creative.

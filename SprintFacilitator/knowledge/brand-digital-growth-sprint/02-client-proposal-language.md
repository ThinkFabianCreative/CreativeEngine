## Why we're proposing this

[CLIENT] has changed a lot in the last [N YEARS]. [Two or three specifics, confirmed with the client: e.g. new product lines, new capabilities, a new customer group, a new market.] Most of what people see of [CLIENT] online was built before those changes.

That doesn't make the website a problem. It may be doing its job well. What we want to check with you is narrower: does what a customer sees, reads and does online today match the business you run today?

We don't know yet, and we'd rather find out than guess. A redesign is one possible answer. Others are sharper messaging, a clearer way of presenting your products, fixing a few places where customers get stuck, or leaving the site as it is and spending the effort somewhere else. We want to work out which one with you before anyone scopes a project.

## What we're proposing

A Brand + Digital Growth Sprint: a [one / two]-day working session with [CLIENT]'s leadership and customer-facing team, with two to three weeks of preparation beforehand. Andres Fabian, Creative Director at TRAFFIK, will facilitate.

**Before the session, we'll gather evidence:**
- Conversations with [5–8] people on your team about what has changed and what customers tend to misunderstand.
- A review of your site analytics and content against what the business offers now.
- A look at how [4–6] competitors present themselves.
- If we can arrange them in time, short conversations with [around 5] customers or prospects about how they choose, what they're trying to do and where they get stuck. If that isn't possible, we'll use recorded sales calls or support material instead where you have it, and we'll say plainly in the Blueprint which findings rest on direct customer input and which don't.

**In the session, we'll look at four things:**
- Brand: who [CLIENT] is today, what it stands for and how it wants to be seen, put into words concrete enough to guide design and writing.
- Customers: what they're trying to get done, and where the experience gets in their way or leaves out something they need to know.
- Business: which offerings and capabilities should get the most prominence, given where you're growing.
- Experience: whether your site and other digital touchpoints support those priorities, and where they don't.

Your team brings the knowledge of the business. We run the process and bring the evidence. Each exercise starts with people writing on their own before any discussion, so the result reflects the whole room and not just its most senior voice. We'll keep what the evidence shows separate from what the team believes, and we'll write down disagreements instead of smoothing them over.

The session ends with your team ranking the opportunities by likely impact and effort, and your decision-maker setting the order.

## What you get

A Brand & Digital Opportunity Blueprint, delivered within [5–10] working days of the session. It covers:
- A short statement of who [CLIENT] is now: values, priority audiences, personality and competitive position.
- What your customers care about most, and where the current experience makes things harder for them.
- How your offerings should be ordered and presented, set against how they're presented today.
- Gaps in your content, and openings your competitors have left.
- A Digital Experience Roadmap: what to do now, next and later, what to hold off on, and what to test before spending money on it.
- An honest account of what we learned, what we couldn't find out, and what's still open.

The Blueprint is yours, whatever you decide next. It's written so your team can act on it, whether you work on it with us, with another partner or on your own.

## What we need from you

- A decision-maker who can attend the whole session.
- [6–7] participants from leadership, sales or customer service, product and marketing.
- Read access to your analytics.
- If possible, introductions to [around 5] customers. We'll handle scheduling and keep the calls short.
- [One / two] days with no competing commitments.

## Timing and investment

- Preparation: [2–3] weeks
- Session: [1 / 2] days, [on site / remote]
- Blueprint: [5–10] working days after the session
- Investment: [FEE], fixed, and separate from any work that follows.

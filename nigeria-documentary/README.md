# Nigeria Documentary — Project Handoff

This folder is a handoff workspace for continuing the documentary story-development work in Claude or another editor. It is not a claim that the footage has been fully reviewed or that all project facts are verified.

## Current editorial premise

A California team led by Anthony Trimino visits a displaced community in Nigeria. Anthony may provide a first-time viewer’s point of view; Gia brings an experienced advocacy perspective. The residents must remain protagonists in their own story, with their accounts, agency, and future defining the stakes. A three-act adaptation of Christopher Vogler’s Hero’s Journey is a provisional structure to test across all three days, not a template to force onto events.

## Important review limit

The Day 1 MOV has **not** been watched continuously or heard in full by the previous reviewer. The initial story map used visual sampling across the 3h 34m file and review of the supplied machine-generated transcripts and notes. Treat all scene selections and arcs as leads until verified against source picture and audio.

## Keep project evidence separate from advocacy and research

Social posts and national reports can show Gia’s perspective or provide carefully attributed context. They do not independently establish what happened to the specific people in the film, who attacked them, or why. Keep direct testimony, partner explanation, visitor interpretation, and externally verified facts distinguishable in notes and any eventual narration or graphics.
